# Script: Tamil voiceover (draft 3, the team talking to a devotee)

Written natively in simple, warm spoken Tamil, not translated line by line, to the same beat timings as `en.md`. The speaker is the development team ("நாங்கள்") talking to the devotee ("நீங்கள்", respectful form). Captions on this cut are still the **English** lines from `en.md`.
Terms follow the app's own Tamil UI where it has one: அயிலவன் அணி (the team credit in the app), பாராயண முறை, அறுபடை வீடு, பிடித்தவை, குறிப்பு, வகைகள், தினசரி அறிவிப்பு.

**Needs your native review before it goes to ElevenLabs.** Tone and word choice are yours to settle. I used "செயலி" for app because it is more natural in speech than the UI's "பயன்பாடு".

## Things to check

- **Timing:** Tamil runs longer than English. Beats 0, 3, 8, 10 and 14 are the likeliest to overrun; we measure after the first ElevenLabs render and trim or lengthen the beat.
- **Beat 0:** "அயிலவன் அணி" is the credit the app itself shows. "சுற்றிக் காட்டட்டுமா?" ("shall we show you around?") is a question, to keep the conversational feel.
- **Beat 6:** I used verbs for "highlight" and "note" rather than the app's labels (சிறப்பம்சங்கள், குறிப்புகள்).
- **Beat 9:** "தற்செயலாக" for random; the app's own label "சீரற்ற பாடல்" sounds stiff aloud.
