The Periya Puranam continues the story of the milk without a pause. Sivapadahridayar finished his ritual bath and came up out of the tank to find his three-year-old son glowing with a new understanding, and milk on his lips (Periya Puranam, Sambandar Puranam 72). Angry, and afraid that the child had been given food that would pollute him, he took up a small stick and demanded, "Who gave you this milk? Show me who it was" (vv. 72–73).

The child, his eyes full of tears of joy, raised one hand and pointed to the sky above the shrine, where Shiva stood on the bull with the Goddess, praised by the Vedas (vv. 73–74). Then, the Puranam says, from the wisdom that had filled him there poured out a hymn in Tamil (v. 74). It began "Todudaiya Seviyan", "He whose ear bears the woman's earring", and in it the child gave the signs by which the Lord could be known and named him as the Lord who dwells at Brahmapuram, one of Sirkazhi's twelve names. At the end he turned to his father and said, "This is the Lord who did this for me" (vv. 75–76).

This hymn is the first of the Tevaram: it stands at the head of the first Tirumurai (Tevaram 1.1.1). Its opening words describe Shiva as wearing a woman's earring in one ear, riding the bull, and with the pure white crescent in his hair, so that in a single line the hymn speaks of Shiva together with Uma, who had just fed the child. The text of the hymn is not reproduced in the film; its first words and the fact that the child sang it are the event. The father's kneeling at the end and the falling stick are added for the screen; the Puranam itself tells of the father's question, the pointing, the song and the child's reply.

In the Thiruppugazh, song 776, sung at Sirkazhi, recalls that Murugan, coming as Sambandar, sang in that very town; the hymn that began the Tevaram is, for Arunagiri, the first word of the Tamil Veda that Murugan gave the world. Other songs, collected under the milk and under Sambandar as Murugan, join the two moments together: the child who drank the Mother's milk and at once began to sing.
