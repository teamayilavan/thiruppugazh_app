# Shiva as Sarabha

`deeds-sarabha` | Deeds of Shiva | 2 songs | primary text + no confirmed DP

After Narasimha killed Hiranyakashipu his rage did not cool. Shiva took the form of the fierce Sarabha (part lion, part bird) and calmed him. The story is told in later Puranas and Shaiva tradition.
