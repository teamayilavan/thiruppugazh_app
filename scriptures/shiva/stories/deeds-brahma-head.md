# Brahma's fifth head cut off

`deeds-brahma-head` | Deeds of Shiva | 11 songs | primary text + Tamil Shaiva texts

Brahma's fifth head spoke proudly, or the head claimed equality with Shiva. Shiva, as Bhairava, cut it off with his fingernail; the skull stuck to his hand as a begging bowl and he was driven to wander until it fell away.
