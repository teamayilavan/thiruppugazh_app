# Shiva drinks the Halahala poison

`deeds-poison` | Deeds of Shiva | 15 songs | primary text + Tamil Shaiva texts

When the gods and demons churned the milk ocean, the Halahala poison rose and threatened the worlds. Shiva drank it at the gods' plea; Parvati held his throat and the poison stayed there, turning it blue (Neelakantha).
