# Manikkavasagar under the kurundam tree

`saints-kurundam` | Saints and the sacred sports | 3 songs | no primary text + Tamil Shaiva texts

The Pandya's minister Manikkavasagar went to buy horses and met Shiva as a guru under the kurundam tree at Tiruperunthurai. He gave the money to the temple and his life to Shiva.
