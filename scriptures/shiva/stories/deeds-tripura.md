# Burning of the three cities (Tripura)

`deeds-tripura` | Deeds of Shiva | 60 songs | primary text + Tamil Shaiva texts

Three flying cities of the demons Tarakaksha, Kamalaksha and Vidyunmali made them unconquerable. The gods made Shiva a chariot: Meru the bow, Vishnu the arrow, Vasuki the string. When the cities aligned he destroyed them with one smile or arrow. A Tamil tradition has Ganesha breaking the chariot's axle before the battle (song 1).
