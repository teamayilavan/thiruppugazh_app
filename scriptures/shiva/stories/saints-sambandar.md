# Sambandar and the Jains

`saints-sambandar` | Saints and the sacred sports | 20 songs | no primary text + Tamil Shaiva texts

The child saint Sambandar of Sirkazhi, nursed with Uma's milk, went to Madurai, healed the Pandya's fever with holy ash and defeated the Jains in debate and contests (a fire test, a palm-leaf floating against the Vaigai). Tradition says the Jains were then impaled on the stake; the Thiruppugazh often adds this.
