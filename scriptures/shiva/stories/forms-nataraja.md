# Shiva the dancer (Nataraja)

`forms-nataraja` | Forms and dance of Shiva | 45 songs | primary text + Tamil Shaiva texts

Shiva dances: the Ananda Tandava in the golden hall at Chidambaram (Thillai), the contest with Kali at Tiruvalangadu, the dance in the burning ground, with his foot on the dwarf Muyalakan. Vishnu, Brahma and the serpent Adisesha watch.
