# Shiva sources: what was checked

Generated references live in `refs-sanskrit.json`, `tirumurai-deeds.json` and `refs-saints.json`; details and numbering
caveats are in the matching `*-notes.md`. Research was done by three agents on 2026-10-26; I spot-checked 4 random Tevaram entries
(first words found on the cited thevaaram.org page, 4 of 4). The gists were not re-verified one by one.

| Source | How reached | Numbering | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tevaram (Tirumurai 1-7), Tiruvasagam, Tirumandiram | thevaaram.org, all pathigams downloaded and searched | Tirumurai.pathigam.verse as the site shows | confirmed (page opened); keyword search is a good sample, not exhaustive |
| Periya Puranam | thevaaram.org | verse number runs within each puranam | confirmed for Sambandar, Appar, Sundarar, Kannappar, Siruthondar |
| Kanda Puranam | Tamil Wikisource raw wikitext | running verse inside each kandam | confirmed (birth of Skanda, Pranava) |
| Tiruvilaiyadal Puranam (Paranjothi) | Tamil Wikisource prose retelling only | padalam numbers | story-level only, no verses |
| Valmiki Ramayana | valmikiramayan.net (Bala); wisdomlib for Uttara | sarga.shloka | Bala confirmed; Uttara 16 (Ravana) from wisdomlib |
| Bhagavata Purana | vedabase.io | canto.chapter.verse | confirmed |
| Mahabharata | GRETIL Critical Edition | parva,adhyaya.shloka | confirmed; some are allusions or name lists only |
| Linga, Shiva, Skanda Puranas | wisdomlib English translations | chapter only, section labels differ from other editions | confirmed at chapter level |

## Gaps (honest list)

- No confirmed source: Shiva kicking Yama for Markandeya (Sanskrit; the Tevaram has it, Appar 4.107), Dakshinamurti teaching sages
  (Sanskrit), Bhikshatana in the Daruka forest (Sanskrit; Sambandar 3.104 is the nearest parallel), Murugan teaching Shiva at
  Swamimalai (the Kanda Puranam gives Brahma's imprisonment and the whisper but not the place), and Sarabha (no Tamil verse).
- Not reached: shaivam.org (403), sacred-texts.com (blocked), Periya Puranam on Wikisource, Perumparrap Puliyur Nambi's
  Tiruvilaiyadal, Tirumurai 9, 11, 12 and Tirukkovaiyar, the Kurma, Matsya, Vamana, Brahmanda and Vayu Puranas.
- The Skanda Purana version of Skanda's birth (Agni and semen) differs from the forehead-eye version in the Kanda Puranam
  that the Thiruppugazh lines match.
- Primary-text reference counts in `STORIES.md` count Sanskrit references only; the Tamil Shaiva texts are counted separately.
