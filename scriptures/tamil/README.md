# Tamil edition

The 142 English story texts (`pdf/narratives/<slug>.txt`) translated into Tamil, for a **separate Tamil edition** of
the four volumes of *Stories in the Thiruppugazh* (owner's decisions, 2026-11-23). The songs and their meanings are
already Tamil; only the story text, the headings and the front matter change.

## Files

- `GLOSSARY.md`: the one Tamil form for every name and term. **Edit this.**
- `glossary-report.md`: generated by `python3 -I -X utf8 scripts/glossary_check.py`; do not edit.
- `pdf/narratives-ta/<slug>.txt`: the Tamil story texts, same slugs and same paragraphs as the English (54 of 142 at the end of 2026-11-23: Murugan, Volume IV but two, and the pilot's Gajendra and Kama).
- `PILOT-NOTES.md`: the choices applied in the pilot and what was found, for the owner's review.
- The book build (`make_pdf.py`, `book.typ`) gets a language switch later; that change will be proposed before it is made.

## Order of work

1. **Glossary** (544 rows on 2026-11-23; owner reviews the *check* rows and the choices below).
2. **Pilot**: five stories across the volumes (`m-valli-disguise`, `avatar-gajendra`, a Sambandar story, a Tamil-lore
   story, a Shiva story). The owner and a Tamil reader the owner trusts judge tone and register.
3. **Volumes**, Murugan first (the most-verified English), then Vol IV, Shiva, Vishnu. A story is translated only
   after its English is settled (owner decisions and the expert's song corrections in `HANDOFF.md` section 0).

## Style: simple modern prose

- Short sentences, everyday modern Tamil words, the register of a good Tamil newspaper feature or a children's
  encyclopedia for adults: not the commentary style of the `pathavurai`, not colloquial.
- Same content as the English, paragraph for paragraph: no added praise, no added detail, nothing dropped. If the
  English says the text does not have something, the Tamil says so too.
- Verse numbers in brackets exactly as in the English: (பாடல் 1851), (Utpatti 1259-1265 → உற்பத்திக் காண்டம்
  1259-1265). Thiruppugazh songs: "பாடல் 928 (கருவூர்)" with the app's place for that song.
- Where the English renders a Tamil source (Kanda Puranam, Periya Puranam, Tevaram, Tiruvasagam, Kamban,
  Purananuru, Divya Prabandham), quote or closely follow the Tamil original from the cached text; do not
  back-translate the English. Sanskrit sources (Valmiki, Bhagavata, Mahabharata, the Puranas, Devi Mahatmya) are
  translated from our English, which was written from the verses.
- Names: the glossary, always. A name not in it gets a row before it is used.
- Numbers in digits; dates and spans as in the English.

## To settle in the pilot

- **Honorific verb forms.** Gods and saints with the respectful plural (முருகன் வந்தார், சம்பந்தர் பாடினார்), villains and
  animals with the plain form (சூரன் போரிட்டான்)? This is the usual modern practice and the draft assumption.
- **Shiva** as சிவபெருமான் throughout, or சிவன் in running narration.
- **Rama and Krishna**: the plain names as the songs have them (ராமன், கண்ணன்) with respectful verbs (ராமன்
  வந்தார்), or the respectful names (ராமர், கிருஷ்ணர்), which would change the glossary row for Krishna.
- Whether each story ends with the English edition's closing note on sources in Tamil too (proposed: yes).
