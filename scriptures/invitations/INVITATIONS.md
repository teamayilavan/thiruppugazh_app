> *thOgai mEl kondu mun ...... varavENum*  

**Gloss:** Come before me on your peacock, that I may see your pure feet and their anklets.

**Commentary:** Before that event, (You must appear) with Your Trident, Sword, Rooster Staff and Bow adorning Your shoulders, with Your broad-hallowed chest, and pure feet with victorious anklets attached to them;
**Commentary:** and You must come in front of me on Your peacock that is so pleasing to behold.
