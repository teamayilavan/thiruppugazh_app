# Divya Prabandham references for the Ramayana episodes

Written 2026-10-26 by hand from `divyaprabandham.koyil.org` pages (one page per pasuram, with commentary). Machine-readable copy: `dp/ramayana.json`.

How to read it:
- Refs are `prabandham decad.pasuram` as numbered on that site (e.g. Periyazhwar Thirumozhi 3.10.6). No 1-4000 running number was stated on the pages, so none is given.
- "Opened" means I opened that pasuram's page. The pages were read through a summarising fetch tool, so first words and gists should be spot-checked against a printed edition.
- "Commentary only" means the page's commentary mentions the episode, not necessarily the verse itself.
- Only opened pages count as confirmed. Everything else is under "Candidates, not confirmed".
- Index page: `https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/index.php/category/mudhalayiram/` (and `/category/irandam-ayiram/periya-thirumozhi/`) lists posts by prabandham. The best single places are Perumal Thirumozhi decad 10 (a Rama life story, 10.1 to 10.11) and Periyazhwar Thirumozhi 4.9.1 (a Srirangam verse retelling the story).

## Episodes with no confirmed reference

`garland` (the gaja-creeper for Sugriva), `army-march`, `crown-rama` (Rama shatters Ravana's crown), `kumbhakarna`, `kumbha`, `atikaya`, `indrajit`, `sita-agni`.

## Candidates, not confirmed

- ahalya: Periyazhwar Thirumozhi 4.4.6 *bUdham aindhodu* (a search snippet says the commentary mentions Rama's feet freeing Ahalya; I did not open the page).
- vibhishana-refuge: Periya Thirumozhi 7.1.5 *villEr nudhal* (commentary cites Yuddha 18.33 on the vow to protect those who surrender, but describes no episode); the Thirupullani decad of Periya Thirumozhi is also associated with the Vibhishana refuge and the setu, but I did not find a verse on the site.
- kumbhakarna: Thirumalai 6 (a search snippet says he appears only as an adviser to Ravana).
- Ravana's death and the Vibhishana crown also appear in other Periyazhwar and Periya Thirumozhi verses that came up in search (for example Periya Thirumozhi 9.1.7, 4.5.5; Periyazhwar Thirumozhi 4.3.8, 4.4.8, 4.7.1) but I did not open them.
- Perumal Thirumozhi 10.9 (Shambuka, Lavanasura, the curse on Lakshmana) and 10.10 (Rama returning to Paramapadam) are Uttara Kanda episodes, not in the Thiruppugazh song list.
