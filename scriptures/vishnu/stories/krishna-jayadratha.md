# Jayadratha killed; the sun hidden

`krishna-jayadratha` | Krishna / Mahabharata | 3 songs | primary text + Divya Prabandham

On the fourteenth day Krishna hides the sun (tradition: with the Sudarshana discus), Jayadratha steps out thinking the day is done, and Arjuna kills him.
