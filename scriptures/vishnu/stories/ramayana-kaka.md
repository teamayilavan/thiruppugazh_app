# The crow loses an eye

`ramayana-kaka` | Ramayana | 1 songs | primary text + Divya Prabandham

A crow (Indra's son) pecks at Sita; Rama takes a blade of grass, empowers it as a missile and blinds the crow's eye, sparing its life. Sita later tells it to Hanuman as a token of Rama's compassion.
