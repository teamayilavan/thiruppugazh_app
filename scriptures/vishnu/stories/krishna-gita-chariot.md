# Krishna drives Arjuna's chariot; the Gita opens

`krishna-gita-chariot` | Krishna / Mahabharata | 11 songs | primary text + Divya Prabandham

Krishna drives Arjuna's chariot through the Kurukshetra war and speaks the Gita at its start.
