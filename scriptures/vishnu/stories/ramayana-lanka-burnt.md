# Hanuman burns Lanka

`ramayana-lanka-burnt` | Ramayana | 7 songs | primary text + Divya Prabandham

Hanuman leaps the sea, finds Sita, gives her Rama's ring and takes her jewel back. Captured by Indrajit, his tail is set alight and he burns Lanka.
