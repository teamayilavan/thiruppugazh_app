# Sita's dropped jewels

`ramayana-sita-jewels` | Ramayana | 1 songs | primary text + no confirmed DP

As Ravana carries her off Sita drops her ornaments and upper cloth among the monkeys; Sugriva later shows them to Rama.

## References

| Source | Reference | What it says / quote |
|---|---|---|
| Valmiki Ramayana | Kishkindha 6.11–20 | Sugriva shows Rama the ornaments and cloth Sita dropped |
