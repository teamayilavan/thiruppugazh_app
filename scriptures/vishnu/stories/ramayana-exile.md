# Exile at the mother's word

`ramayana-exile` | Ramayana | 3 songs | primary text + Divya Prabandham

On the eve of his coronation Kaikeyi claims her two boons and Rama goes to the forest at his father's word with Sita and Lakshmana. Several verses name this as the Lord 'going to the forest at his mother's word'.
