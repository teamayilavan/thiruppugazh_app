# Maricha as the golden deer

`ramayana-maricha` | Ramayana | 1 songs | primary text + Divya Prabandham

Ravana asks Maricha to lure Rama away; Maricha becomes a golden deer, Sita asks for it, and Rama follows and kills it. Maricha's cry draws Lakshmana off, and Ravana carries Sita away.
