# Kuvalayapida elephant killed

`krishna-kuvalayapida` | Krishna / Mahabharata | 2 songs | primary text + Divya Prabandham

At Mathura's gate Krishna meets Kamsa's elephant Kuvalayapida, breaks its tusks and kills it.
