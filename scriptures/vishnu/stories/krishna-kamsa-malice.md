# Kamsa's cruelty to his sister's child

`krishna-kamsa-malice` | Krishna / Mahabharata | 2 songs | primary text + no confirmed DP

Krishna and Balarama go to Mathura, defeat the wrestlers and kill their maternal uncle Kamsa.

## References

| Source | Reference | What it says / quote |
|---|---|---|
| Bhagavata Purana | SB 10.4.1-5 | Kamsa persecutes the newborn; he is Krishna's maternal uncle |
