# Khara, Dushana and Trishira killed

`ramayana-khara` | Ramayana | 2 songs | primary text + Divya Prabandham

After Surpanakha is mutilated, her brother Khara marches with fourteen thousand rakshasas. Rama alone destroys them and their generals Dushana and Trishira.
