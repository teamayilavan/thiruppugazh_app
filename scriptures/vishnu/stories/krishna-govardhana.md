# Lifting Govardhana

`krishna-govardhana` | Krishna / Mahabharata | 5 songs | primary text + Divya Prabandham

When Indra's rain threatens Vrindavan, Krishna lifts Govardhana hill on one hand as an umbrella for seven days.
