# Ahalya freed

`ramayana-ahalya` | Ramayana | 2 songs | primary text + Divya Prabandham

On the way to Mithila the dust of Rama's feet frees Ahalya, Gautama's wife, who had stood as stone/unseen for years after Indra's deceit. Murugan's hymn picks 'the foot that touched the stone'.
