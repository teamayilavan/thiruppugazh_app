# The twin arjuna trees

`krishna-yamalarjuna` | Krishna / Mahabharata | 9 songs | primary text + Divya Prabandham

Dragging the mortar, Krishna passes between two arjuna (maruda) trees; they break and release Kubera's sons Nalakuvara and Manigriva from Narada's curse.
