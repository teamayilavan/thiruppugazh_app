# Vibhishana crowned

`ramayana-vibhishana-crown` | Ramayana | 5 songs | primary text + Divya Prabandham

Ravana's brother Vibhishana, rejected for urging the return of Sita, joins Rama and is given refuge. After Ravana's death Rama installs him king of Lanka.
