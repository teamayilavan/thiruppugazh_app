# The gopis' clothes on the kadamba tree

`krishna-clothes` | Krishna / Mahabharata | 4 songs | primary text + Divya Prabandham

Gopis bathing in the Yamuna to win Krishna as husband find their clothes taken to the kurunda (kadamba) tree.
