# Gajendra and the crocodile

`avatar-gajendra` | Avatars and Vishnu images | 21 songs | primary text + Divya Prabandham

The elephant king Gajendra is seized by a crocodile in a pond; after long struggle he calls out and Vishnu rides Garuda to kill the crocodile with his discus. One of the most common images (about 20 songs).

Note: The Thiruppugazh 'Mulame!' cry is the Tamil devotional form of the elephant's call; the Sanskrit prayer is addressed to Vasudeva/Narayana. The discus killing the crocodile is a Tamil/Alvar detail more explicit than in the Bhagavata passage cited.
