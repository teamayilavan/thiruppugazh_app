# Sita brought to Rama

`ramayana-sita-freed` | Ramayana | 6 songs | primary text + Divya Prabandham

After the victory Hanuman tells Sita and Vibhishana brings her to Rama. The Thiruppugazh lines treat the war as freeing Sita from prison.
