# The iron pestle thrown into the sea

`krishna-mausala` | Krishna / Mahabharata | 1 songs | primary text + no confirmed DP

A curse leads to an iron pestle; its powder grows into reeds with which the Yadavas destroy each other; the pestle's last piece is thrown into the sea.
