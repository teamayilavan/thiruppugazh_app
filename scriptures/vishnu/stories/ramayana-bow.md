# Shiva's bow broken

`ramayana-bow` | Ramayana | 3 songs | primary text + Divya Prabandham

At Mithila King Janaka offers Sita to whoever strings Shiva's bow. Rama lifts and breaks it; the weddings follow. Parashurama then challenges Rama and is humbled.
