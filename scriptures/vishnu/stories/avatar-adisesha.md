# Vishnu lying on Adisesha in the milk ocean

`avatar-adisesha` | Avatars and Vishnu images | 26 songs | primary text + Divya Prabandham

A repeating image rather than a story: Vishnu asleep on the serpent bed (Ananta/Adisesha) in the milk ocean.
