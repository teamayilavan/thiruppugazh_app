# Vishwamitra's sacrifice protected

`ramayana-yajna` | Ramayana | 1 songs | primary text + Divya Prabandham

At Siddhashrama Rama and Lakshmana guard Vishwamitra's sacrifice; Rama kills the demon Subahu and drives Maricha away, flung into the sea (Valmiki Bala 30; Kamba, Velvi Padalam 39, 41).
