# Vamana and Mahabali; Trivikrama

`avatar-vamana` | Avatars and Vishnu images | 18 songs | primary text + Divya Prabandham

The dwarf Vamana asks King Bali for three paces of land, then grows to measure the worlds with two steps and sends Bali to Sutala with the third.
