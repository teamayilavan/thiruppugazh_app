# Divya Prabandham references for the Krishna episodes

Session 1, 2026-10-26. Source: the per-pasuram pages of divyaprabandham.koyil.org (read through its site API: transliteration,
word-for-word meaning, simple translation and commentary). Machine-readable copy: `dp/krishna.json`.

How to read: refs are `decad.pasuram` inside each prabandham (Periyazhwar Thirumozhi 3.1.3 = Pattu 3, decad 1, verse 3), as the site numbers them.
The continuous 1-4000 numbers are not given on those pages and are not stated here. Tamil first words are my transcription of the site's
transliteration; check them against a printed text. "Confirmed" = I read the page text and it mentions the episode. A note marks anything taken only from a
search-result description of a page.

Thiruppugazh song ids per episode come from `scripts/krishna_episodes.py`.

## Putana (songs 626, 1001)
- Periyazhwar Thirumozhi 2.10.6 *thaLLith thaLar*: Krishna looks steadily at Putana knowing she came to harm him. https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/index.php/2026/03/periyazhwar-thirumozhi-2-10-6/
- Periyazhwar 3.5.9 *vanpEy mulai*: drank the poisoned breast's milk, obeys his herd-woman mother. https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/index.php/2026/04/periyazhwar-thirumozhi-3-5-9/
- Periyazhwar 4.1.6 *pollA vadivu*: mouth on Putana's breast, she is killed. https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/index.php/2026/07/periyazhwar-thirumozhi-4-1-6/
- Tiruppavai 6 *puLLum silambina kAN* (note: from search description). https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/index.php/2014/12/thiruppavai-06-pullum-silambina-kan/

## Jayadratha and the hidden sun (songs 568, 574)
- Periyazhwar Thirumozhi 4.1.8 *nAzhigai kURittuk kAththu ninRa*: the kings guard Jayadratha counting the day, Krishna hides the sun with the discus, Arjuna's arrow rolls Jayadratha's head into the pit. https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/index.php/2026/07/periyazhwar-thirumozhi-4-1-8/
  This is the Tamil tradition's discus-hides-sun version that appears in the Mahabharata Critical Edition only in interpolated lines (see `krishna-episodes.md`).
- Not confirmed: a search result says Poigai Alvar's Mudhal Thiruvandhadhi, Thirumazhisai's Nanmugan Thiruvandhadhi and Tirumangai's Periya Thirumozhi also mention the covered sun; verse numbers not found. Source: https://granthams.koyil.org/?p=7977
