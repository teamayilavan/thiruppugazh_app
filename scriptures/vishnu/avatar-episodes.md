## Gajendra and the crocodile  (`gajendra`, 21 songs)

**Bhagavata Purana**

- SB 8.2.27-28: A crocodile seizes the elephant king's leg in the lake; the herd cannot help
- SB 8.3.2-2: Gajendra's prayer begins by bowing to Vasudeva
- SB 8.3.30-33: Hari comes on Garuda with the discus; Gajendra offers a lotus; the Lord pulls elephant and crocodile out
- SB 8.4.3-4: The crocodile was the gandharva Huhu, freed from a curse
- SB 8.4.13-13: Gajendra is given liberation

Note: The Thiruppugazh 'Mulame!' cry is the Tamil devotional form of the elephant's call; the Sanskrit prayer is addressed to Vasudeva/Narayana. The discus killing the crocodile is a Tamil/Alvar detail more explicit than in the Bhagavata passage cited.

Songs: 25, 70, 82, 173, 175, 403, 484, 651, 704, 710, 875, 935, 963, 1001, 1048, 1059, 1084, 1188, 1204, 1272, 1336
