# The Mountain King's Daughter (Parvati, Daughter of Himavan)

`d-himavan` | Devi | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: serene, tender, hopeful

## One-line story

The Mountain-King Himavan does penance for a child, and the Goddess is born to him as a boon: Parvati, who will one day be the mother of Murugan.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Parvati (Imayavalli, Umadevi) is the daughter of Himavan, the Mountain-King | Thiruppugazh songs (19 songs, e.g. 32, 60, 63, 107, 116) |
| She is born as a boon after his penance | same |
| The songs use this as the Devi's identity before saying she gave birth to Murugan | same |

**Honesty note:** the research keeps this as the songs' identity of the Goddess; the details of Himavan's penance and Parvati's childhood are tradition and are not read verse by verse here.
**Added for the screen:** the visuals, the narration.

## Characters

- **Himavan:** a majestic, white-bearded mountain king; his form is half-person, half-mountain.
- **Menaka:** his queen, gentle.
- **Parvati:** a child, then a young woman, calm.
- **The sage Narada** (tradition): omitted.

## The storyline

### 1. The mountain (20 sec)
The great snow mountain, a king asleep in white.
**Narration:** "Himavan, the king of the mountains, longed for a child."

### 2. The penance (25 sec)
He sits in stillness; snow, wind, fire, seasons turn around him.
**Narration:** "He did penance for a daughter."

### 3. The boon (25 sec)
A radiant light appears; a voice grants his wish.
**Narration:** "And the Goddess granted it."

### 4. The birth (25 sec)
In the palace, a baby girl is born; the mountain shines.
**Narration:** "A daughter was born to him."

### 5. A child of the hills (25 sec)
The girl grows, playing among flowers; her eyes are serene.
**Narration:** "She grew up among the snows and the flowers."

### 6. The name (20 sec)
She is called Imayavalli, Umadevi, daughter of the mountain.
**Narration:** "They called her Parvati: the daughter of the mountain."

### 7. A promise (20 sec)
In the sky, a faint golden child with a spear, smiling.
**Narration:** "And one day she would give a child to the world."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

19 songs name the Goddess as the daughter of Himavan before saying that she gave birth to Murugan.
