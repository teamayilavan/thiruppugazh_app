# The Dance on the Battlefield (Durga and Bhadrakali)

`d-battlefield` | Devi | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: wild, festive, thunderous

## One-line story

During Murugan's war with the demons, Durga and Bhadrakali and the Goddess's attendants dance on the battlefield, shouting the "kulavai" cry, as an army of fiends feasts.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| When Murugan fought the demons, the Goddess's attendants and Bhadrakali danced on the field, shouting the "kulavai" cry, as the army of fiends feasted | Thiruppugazh songs 138, 900, 903, 1079, 1127 |

**Honesty note:** the research gives this as the songs' image; it is not a narrative in the Kanda Puranam read here. The film is a spectacle, not a plot, and it follows the songs.
**Added for the screen:** all visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Bhadrakali:** a fierce, bright goddess, dancing.
- **Durga:** radiant, on a lion.
- **The Goddess's attendants.**
- **The army of fiends (bhootas):** grotesque and comic, shown feasting.
- **Murugan** (in the distance, calm).

## The storyline

### 1. The war (20 sec)
Murugan's battle, seen from a hill: banners, light.
**Narration:** "Murugan fought the demons."

### 2. The goddesses arrive (25 sec)
Durga and Bhadrakali appear at the edge of the field with their attendants.
**Narration:** "The Goddess came, with Bhadrakali and her attendants."

### 3. The cry (20 sec)
They raise a shrill, rolling cry: the kulavai.
**Narration:** "They raised the high cry of joy: the kulavai."

### 4. The dance (30 sec)
They dance, stamping and whirling, with drums.
**Narration:** "And they danced on the field of battle."

### 5. The feast (25 sec)
The bhootas hold a huge, messy banquet; the feast is comic.
**Narration:** "And the army of fiends feasted."

### 6. The calm (20 sec)
Murugan, serene, watches over the dance.
**Narration:** "Murugan watched."

### 7. The dawn (20 sec)
The battlefield turns to sunrise.
**Narration:** "And victory came with the dawn."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 138, 900, 903, 1079 and 1127.

## Production notes

- **Make the feast comedy,** not horror: no body parts, no blood.
- **A film of sound.** The "kulavai" cry and the drums are its core.
