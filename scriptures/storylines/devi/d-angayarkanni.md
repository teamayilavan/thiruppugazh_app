# The Lady of Madurai (Angayarkanni)

`d-angayarkanni` | Devi | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: devotional, quiet, an invocation

## One-line story

A short, reverent portrait of Madurai, the city where Shiva is the Lord and Angayarkanni (Meenakshi) is his consort, as the poet sees it in one song.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Song 708 sets Shiva as Madurai's lord with his consort Angayarkanni (Meenakshi) | Thiruppugazh song 708 |

**Honesty note:** the research has only this one reference and no verse-level text for her story. This film is therefore an invocation rather than a plot: it shows the city and the pair, and points to `d-fisher` for her marriage to Shiva as told in the Tiruvilaiyadal Puranam.
**Added for the screen:** the whole visual treatment; the narration.

## Characters

- **Meenakshi (Angayarkanni):** the goddess, calm, with fish-shaped eyes.
- **Shiva** (Sundareswarar): beside her.
- **Pilgrims, priests, flower-sellers.**

## The storyline

### 1. Madurai at dawn (25 sec)
The temple towers, mist, the sound of conch.
**Narration:** "Madurai, the city of the temple of Meenakshi."

### 2. The streets (20 sec)
Flower-sellers, pilgrims, drummers, children.
**Narration:** "The streets are full of devotion."

### 3. The shrine (25 sec)
A lamplit sanctum: the Goddess, radiant.
**Narration:** "There she sits, Angayarkanni, whose eyes are like fish."

### 4. The consort (20 sec)
Shiva beside her, quiet.
**Narration:** "And beside her, the Lord of Madurai."

### 5. The poet (20 sec)
A song of the Thiruppugazh floats up as light over the temple.
**Narration:** "The poet saw them both, and sang."

### 6. The people (20 sec)
Pilgrims offering flowers; a child lifted to see.
**Narration:** "Generations have come to see her."

### 7. The evening (20 sec)
Lamps; the temple glows.
**Narration:** "And the lamps are lit."
