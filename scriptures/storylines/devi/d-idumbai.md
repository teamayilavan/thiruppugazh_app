# The Forest of Idumbai (Durga and the Pandavas)

`d-idumbai` | Devi | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: mysterious, protective, quiet

## One-line story

During the Pandavas' exile, the song remembers the forest of Idumbai, where the Goddess Durga dwelt.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Song 574 mentions the forest of Idumbai where the Goddess Durga dwelt, in the passage about the Pandavas' exile | Thiruppugazh song 574 |

**Honesty note:** the research has only this one reference. The Mahabharata's account of the Pandavas in the forest (Hidimba episode) is the wider background, but it is not read in this research and the film does not narrate it. This is a short atmospheric film, not a story.
**Added for the screen:** the whole visual treatment; the narration.

## Characters

- **The Pandavas:** five brothers and their mother Kunti, weary, in forest clothes (shown at a distance).
- **Draupadi:** their wife.
- **Durga:** an unseen presence, a glow in the forest.

## The storyline

### 1. Exile (25 sec)
Five brothers and their wife walk into a deep forest.
**Narration:** "The Pandavas walked into the forest, in exile."

### 2. The forest of Idumbai (25 sec)
Ancient trees, mist, strange sounds.
**Narration:** "They came to the forest of Idumbai."

### 3. A presence (25 sec)
Among the trees, a faint golden glow; a lioness's eyes.
**Narration:** "The song says the Goddess Durga dwelt there."

### 4. The night (20 sec)
They rest; the glow stays at the edge of the clearing.
**Narration:** "She watched over them."

### 5. The shrine (20 sec)
A small stone shrine among roots, lit by a lamp.
**Narration:** "A place of worship."

### 6. The poet (20 sec)
A Thiruppugazh line drifts over.
**Narration:** "The poet remembered it."

### 7. Dawn (15 sec)
The brothers walk on.
**Narration:** "And they went on."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Song 574.
