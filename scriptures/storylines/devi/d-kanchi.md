# The Sand Lingam and the Flood (Parvati at Kanchi)

`d-kanchi` | Devi | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: devout, then urgent, then tender

## One-line story

Parvati makes a lingam of sand under the mango tree beside the river and worships it; when the river floods she clasps the lingam to save it, and Shiva takes the marks of her embrace and marries her.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Parvati's worship is broken off when the river Kampa rises; she clasps the lingam; the Lord of the one mango tree takes the mark of her breasts | Kanchi Puranam (Sivagnana Munivar), invocation vv. 4-6 |
| Temple legend: she made a sand lingam under the mango tree beside the Vegavati; when the river flooded she embraced it, and her bangles left their marks; Shiva then wedded her | Temple tradition |
| The songs add the scar on his chest left by her embrace | Thiruppugazh songs 338, 347, 352, 438 |

**Honesty note:** the "Vegavati" and the bangle marks are not in the parts of the Puranam that could be read: they are temple tradition. The film says so.
**Added for the screen:** the visuals, the narration.

## Characters

- **Parvati:** a young woman at prayer, devoted, in simple clothes.
- **Shiva:** present as a light, then in person.
- **The river:** a character in itself.

## The storyline

### 1. The mango tree (20 sec)
A single great mango tree on a riverbank at Kanchi.
**Narration:** "At Kanchi stands a mango tree, beside a river."

### 2. The sand lingam (25 sec)
Parvati shapes a lingam of sand and begins to worship.
**Narration:** "Parvati made a lingam of sand, and worshipped it."

### 3. The penance (20 sec)
Days pass; she sits, lost in prayer.
**Narration:** "She worshipped day after day."

### 4. The flood (25 sec)
The river rises, brown and loud, rushing at the lingam.
**Narration:** "Then the river rose."

### 5. The embrace (25 sec)
She throws her arms around the lingam to shield it from the water; the flood covers her.
**Narration:** "She clasped the lingam, to save it."

### 6. The mark (20 sec)
The waters recede; on the lingam, the mark of her bangles and her embrace.
**Narration:** "Her bangles left their marks."

### 7. The Lord (20 sec)
Shiva appears and takes her hand.
**Narration:** "And the Lord appeared, and wed her."

### 8. The scar (10 sec)
On Shiva's chest, a faint mark.
**Narration:** "The songs speak of the mark on his chest."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 338, 347, 352 and 438.

## Production notes

- **Keep the embrace chaste.** It is a gesture of devotion.
- **Mark the Vegavati and the bangle marks** as temple tradition.
