# Abirami at Thiruvathigai (Where the Cities Burn)

`d-thiruvathigai` | Devi | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: grand, bright, a single arrow

## One-line story

At Thiruvathigai, Shiva burns the three flying cities with a single arrow, Meru as his bow, and the Goddess Abirami stands by him.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|

| Sundarar's hymn at Thiruvathigai Veerattanam (Tevaram 7.38, v. 9) praises the lord who bent the bow and burned the three cities with one arrow | Tevaram 7.38 |

## Characters

- **Shiva:** a tall archer, calm, with Meru as his bow.
- **Abirami:** the Goddess, beside him, serene.
- **The three cities:** golden, silver and iron, floating.
- **The gods:** watching.

## The storyline

### 1. The three cities (25 sec)
Three great cities float in the sky: gold, silver, iron.
**Narration:** "Three cities flew across the sky."

### 2. The gods' plea (20 sec)
The gods ask Shiva for help.
**Narration:** "The gods asked Shiva for help."

### 3. The bow (25 sec)
Shiva takes up Mount Meru as a bow.
**Narration:** "He took the great mountain Meru as his bow."

### 4. The Goddess (20 sec)
Abirami stands beside him, calm.
**Narration:** "And the Goddess stood by him."

### 5. The arrow (25 sec)
He draws; a single arrow leaves.
**Narration:** "He fired a single arrow."

### 6. The fire (25 sec)
The three cities burn in a vast, slow blaze.
**Narration:** "And the three cities burned."

### 7. The quiet (20 sec)
The sky clears; the Goddess and the Lord stand together at the shrine.
**Narration:** "At Thiruvathigai, the place is remembered."
