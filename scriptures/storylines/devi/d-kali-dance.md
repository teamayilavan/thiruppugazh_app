# The Dance Contest at Thiruvalangadu (Kali and Shiva)

`d-kali-dance` | Devi | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: fierce, then playful, then reverent

## One-line story

A furious Kali threatens Shiva's town; Shiva proposes a dance contest, and when she matches every pose he lifts his foot to his ear, a pose she will not match.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Kali, furious after killing demons, threatens Shiva's town at Thiruvalangadu | Temple legend (accounts differ in the details) |
| Shiva proposes a dance contest | same |
| She matches every pose; he lifts his foot to his ear (Urdhva Tandava), a pose she will not match, and she yields | same |
| Songs 94 and 117 recall it | Thiruppugazh songs |

**Honesty note:** this is temple legend and the songs; Sambandar's hymn at Thiruvalangadu (Tevaram 1.45) does not describe the dance.
**Added for the screen:** the poses and the music, the narration.

## Characters

- **Kali:** a fierce, dark goddess in red, wild-haired, with an unbroken energy.
- **Shiva:** a graceful, laughing dancer, calm.
- **The gods and attendants** watching from the sidelines.
- **Mount at the forest of Alangadu.**

## The storyline

### 1. The fury (20 sec)
After killing demons, Kali stands among the ruins of the war, still furious, and turns toward the forest of Alangadu.
**Narration:** "Kali, after destroying the demons, was still full of anger."

### 2. The threat (20 sec)
She sees Shiva's town, and strides toward it, shaking the ground.
**Narration:** "And she threatened Shiva's town."

### 3. The challenge (20 sec)
Shiva stands before her, smiling: "Let us dance, and see who is the master."
**Narration:** "Shiva did not fight. He offered a dance."

### 4. The first steps (25 sec)
They begin: pose after pose, each more intricate. She matches each.
**Narration:** "She matched every step."

### 5. The pace (20 sec)
Faster, wilder; the earth shakes with their rhythm.
**Narration:** "They danced faster and faster."

### 6. The foot (25 sec)
Shiva lifts his foot high, to his ear, in a pose of perfect balance. Kali stops, her foot stuck on the floor.
**Narration:** "Then he raised his foot to his ear."

### 7. The yielding (20 sec)
Kali looks at the pose, smiles, bows, and her rage falls away.
**Narration:** "She would not do it, and she yielded."

### 8. The peace (15 sec)
Both stand, side by side, in the evening light.
**Narration:** "And the temple has told it ever since."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 94 and 117.
