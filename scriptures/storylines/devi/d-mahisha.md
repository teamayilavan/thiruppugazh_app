# The Goddess and the Buffalo Demon (Durga Slays Mahishasura)

`d-mahisha` | Devi | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: crisis, gathering power, triumph, hymn

## One-line story

When the buffalo demon Mahishasura defeats the gods and makes himself Indra, their combined energies join into a Goddess who destroys his armies and then the demon himself.

## Characters

- **Mahishasura:** a buffalo-headed demon in armour; vast, arrogant.
- **The gods:** Indra, Shiva, Vishnu, Brahma and others, weary and driven out.
- **The Goddess:** radiant, many-armed, on a lion, formed of light.

- **The demon's armies.**

## The storyline

### 1. The usurper (20 sec)
A heaven in ruins; the demon on Indra's throne, laughing; the gods driven from the golden city.
**Narration:** "The buffalo demon Mahishasura defeated the gods and made himself Indra."

### 2. The gods go to the Trinity (20 sec)
The gods, haggard, appeal to Vishnu and Shiva, who listen, and their faces darken with anger.
**Narration:** "The gods went to Shiva and Vishnu, and told them."

### 3. The gathering light (30 sec)
From the gods' mouths, and from the anger of Shiva, Vishnu and Brahma, streams of light flow together into one blazing shape: a woman, radiant. Each god gives her a weapon.
**Narration:** "Their energies joined, and out of them rose a Goddess."

### 4. The lion (15 sec)
She mounts a lion; a roar shakes heaven.
**Narration:** "She came on a lion, with every weapon of the gods."

### 5. The armies (25 sec)
The demon's armies pour toward her; she sweeps through them in a whirl of light and silent power.
**Narration:** "She destroyed his armies."

### 6. The shapes (20 sec)
Mahishasura changes shape: buffalo, lion, elephant, buffalo again.
**Narration:** "The demon changed his shape again and again."

### 7. The end (25 sec)
In the last combat she presses his neck with her foot, strikes with her spear, and as he half steps out of the buffalo's body, she ends him. Light flashes; silence.
**Narration:** "She pressed him down with her foot, and struck, and he fell."

### 8. The hymn (10 sec)
The gods bow and sing her praise as dawn breaks over heaven.
**Narration:** "And the gods sang a hymn to the Goddess."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 575 and 1126 mention the deed in passing.

## Production notes

- **Stylised combat.** The demon's fall is a flash of light, no blood.
- **The Goddess is formed from all the gods:** make the gathering light the film's centre.
