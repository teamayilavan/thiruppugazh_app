# The Goddess and the Buffalo Demon (Durga Slays Mahishasura)

`d-mahisha` | Devi | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: crisis, gathering power, triumph, hymn | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

When the buffalo demon Mahishasura defeats the gods and makes himself Indra, their combined energies join into a Goddess who destroys his armies and then the demon himself.

## Characters

- **Mahishasura:** a buffalo-headed demon in armour; vast, arrogant.
- **The gods:** Indra, Shiva, Vishnu, Brahma and others, weary and driven out.
- **The Goddess:** radiant, many-armed, on a lion, formed of light.
- **Her lion.**
- **The demon's armies.**

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; grave at the start, rising with the gathering light, fast in the battle, and full and grateful in the hymn. The combat is shown as light and silhouette, never blood.

### 1. The usurper (20 sec)
**Visual:** The golden city of heaven, its gardens trampled. On Indra's throne lounges Mahishasura, a vast buffalo-headed demon in armour, laughing. The gods, weary and ashamed, are driven out through the gates.
**Narration:** "Long ago, the buffalo demon Mahishasura made war on the gods for a hundred years, and won. He drove them out of heaven and made himself Indra, the king of the gods. The gods wandered on the earth like ordinary men."
**Sound:** a heavy, mocking laugh; gates slamming; a broken drum.

### 2. The gods go to the Trinity (20 sec)
**Visual:** The gods, haggard, follow Brahma to Shiva and Vishnu. They tell their story. As Shiva and Vishnu listen, their faces darken; their brows draw together in anger.
**Narration:** "The beaten gods went with Brahma to Shiva and Vishnu, and told them everything. As they listened, Shiva and Vishnu grew angry, and their faces burned."
**Sound:** murmuring voices; a low, building drone.

### 3. The gathering light (30 sec)
**Visual:** From the face of Vishnu, from Shiva, from Brahma, and then from every god, streams of light pour out and flow together, like rivers meeting, into one blazing mountain of light. Slowly it takes the shape of a woman, radiant, with many arms. Each god steps forward and gives her a weapon: Shiva a trident drawn from his own, Vishnu a discus from his, and the rest their gifts.
**Narration:** "Out of their anger came a great light. Then light came from the bodies of all the gods, and it all flowed together into one blaze, like a mountain on fire. And from that light the Goddess took form. Each god gave her a weapon. Shiva drew a trident from his own, and Vishnu a discus from his."
**Sound:** a swelling choir; chimes as each weapon is given.

### 4. The lion (15 sec)
**Visual:** A great lion bounds to her side; she mounts it. She laughs aloud, and the sound shakes the sky and the mountains.
**Narration:** "Riding a lion, she laughed aloud, and her laughter filled the sky. The earth shook, and the mountains trembled."
**Sound:** a lion's roar; a ringing laugh; rumbling earth.

### 5. The armies (25 sec)
**Visual:** The demon's armies pour toward her in dark waves, generals at their head. She moves through them in a whirl of light; the dark waves break and scatter like smoke. The lion leaps beside her.
**Narration:** "Mahishasura sent his armies against her, with all his great generals. She went through them like a storm through dry grass. One after another, the generals fell, and the armies of the demon melted away."
**Sound:** drums and conches; a rushing wind; then a sudden quiet.

### 6. The shapes (20 sec)
**Visual:** Mahishasura himself charges, in buffalo form, tossing mountains with his horns. Then he changes: a lion, a man with a sword, a huge elephant, and the buffalo again, each shape breaking against her light.
**Narration:** "Then the demon came himself. He changed his shape again and again: a buffalo, a lion, a warrior, an elephant, and then a buffalo once more. But no shape could stand against her."
**Sound:** a bellow, a roar, a trumpet, a bellow, each cut short.

### 7. The end (25 sec)
**Visual:** In silhouette against a blazing sky, the Goddess leaps; her foot presses the buffalo's neck. Her spear flashes. A figure begins to rise out of the buffalo's mouth; a great flash of white light fills the screen. Silence.
**Narration:** "The Goddess called out to him: roar for a moment, fool, for soon the gods will be roaring here. She leapt on him and pressed his neck with her foot, and struck him with her spear. And as the demon came half out of the buffalo's body, she ended him."
**Sound:** her cry; the rush of the leap; a white flash of sound; silence.

### 8. The hymn (10 sec)
**Visual:** Dawn over heaven. The gods, led by Indra, bow before the Goddess and sing.
**Narration:** "Then the gods, led by Indra, sang her praise. Arunagiri remembers her as the destroyer of the buffalo demon."
**Sound:** a hymn rising with the dawn.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri names the Goddess as the destroyer of Mahishasura among her many deeds. Songs to show: 575 (Viralimalai) and 1126 (general songs).

## Production notes

- **Stylised combat.** The demon's fall is a flash of light, no blood.
- **The Goddess is formed from all the gods:** make the gathering light the film's centre.
