# The Fishermen's Daughter (Parvati and the Shark)

`d-fisher` | Devi | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: gentle, comic, romantic

## One-line story

The Goddess, cursed to be born a fisherman's daughter, is found under a punnai tree; Shiva comes as a young fisherman, catches the shark that has wrecked the nets, and marries her.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Parvati hears Shiva's Vedic teaching inattentively and is cursed to be born a fisherman's daughter | Tiruvilaiyadal Puranam, padalam 57 |
| She is found as a baby under a punnai tree and raised by the chief of the Pandya coast's fishermen | same |
| Nandi, also cursed, becomes a shark that wrecks the nets | same |
| The chief promises his daughter (Meenakshi) to whoever catches the shark | same |
| Shiva comes as a young fisherman, catches the shark and marries her | same |
| Song 1165 adds that Murugan came to end the curse | Thiruppugazh song 1165 |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals, the narration.

## Characters

- **Parvati:** first a baby, then a young woman with fisherfolk jewelry, radiant.
- **The fishermen's chief:** a broad-shouldered, kind man.
- **Shiva:** a young fisherman with a net, laughing eyes.
- **Nandi:** a great shark.
- **Murugan** (a golden figure).

## The storyline

### 1. The teaching (20 sec)
On Kailasa, Shiva teaches the Vedas; Parvati's mind drifts.
**Narration:** "Parvati did not listen as she should, and a curse was spoken."

### 2. The baby (25 sec)
On the Pandya coast, fishermen find a baby under a punnai tree.
**Narration:** "A baby was found under a punnai tree."

### 3. The chief's daughter (20 sec)
The chief raises her as his own: she grows into a graceful young woman, mending nets.
**Narration:** "The chief of the fishermen raised her as his own."

### 4. The shark (25 sec)
A vast shark, Nandi in a curse, tears the nets; fishermen are frightened.
**Narration:** "Nandi, also cursed, became a shark, and wrecked the nets."

### 5. The promise (20 sec)
The chief vows: whoever catches the shark will marry his daughter.
**Narration:** "The chief promised his daughter to the one who could catch it."

### 6. The young fisherman (25 sec)
A young man arrives with a net; he laughs, casts, and draws the shark in.
**Narration:** "A young fisherman came, and caught the shark."

### 7. The wedding (20 sec)
The two are married on the shore, with the fishermen dancing.
**Narration:** "And they were married."

### 8. The curse ends (10 sec)
A golden child with a spear lifts his hand; light flows.
**Narration:** "The songs say Murugan ended the curse."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 291 and 1165.

## Production notes

- **Do not make the fishermen comic.** They are the heroes.
- **Pair with `d-angayarkanni`** (Meenakshi at Madurai).
