# The Penance under the Pathiri Tree

`d-pathiri` | Devi | running time 2:35 (7 scenes) | tone: still, devout, patient

## One-line story

At Thiruppathirippuliyur, the Goddess is said to have done penance under the pathiri tree to be one with Shiva; the shrine marks the spot with a bare pedestal.
