# The Penance under the Pathiri Tree

`d-pathiri` | Devi | running time 2:35 (7 scenes) | tone: still, devout, patient | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

At Thiruppathirippuliyur, the Goddess is said to have done penance under the pathiri tree to be one with Shiva; the shrine marks the spot with a bare pedestal.

## Characters

- **The Goddess:** a young woman in penance, radiant.
- **Shiva** (a light at the end).

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; slow and still, with long pauses. There is no dialogue. The narration says plainly that the penance is temple legend.

### 1. The town (20 sec)
**Visual:** A small town near the sea in the Tamil south, at dawn: a river, coconut palms, a temple tower rising over tiled roofs. A sign of the old name: Thiruppathirippuliyur, today part of Cuddalore.
**Narration:** "Near the sea, in the town now called Cuddalore, stands an old temple of Shiva. Its ancient name is Thiruppathirippuliyur, the holy town of the pathiri tree. Its story, the temple says, is a story of patience."
**Sound:** the distant sea; temple drums at dawn.

### 2. The tree (20 sec)
**Visual:** In the temple courtyard, a pathiri tree, old and gnarled, its trumpet-shaped flowers pale pink and fragrant, falling slowly in the breeze.
**Narration:** "The pathiri is a tree with pale, fragrant flowers. Temple tradition says that long ago the Goddess came to this place, and sat down in the shade of a pathiri tree to do penance."
**Sound:** a soft breeze; flowers dropping; a single bird.

### 3. The penance (30 sec)
**Visual:** The Goddess, young and radiant, sits beneath the tree, eyes closed, hands folded, utterly still. Petals fall on her shoulders. Sun and moon pass over her; rain falls; she does not move.
**Narration:** "She wished to be one with Shiva, never apart from him. So she sat in stillness, her mind fixed on him alone. Days passed, and seasons, and she did not move. The songs of Arunagiri call it a penance like no other."
**Sound:** long silences; rain; wind; the drone of a single note.

### 4. The wait (20 sec)
**Visual:** Close on her face: calm, patient, unchanging. Around her the world changes; she alone is still. A lamp beside her keeps burning.
**Narration:** "Temple tradition says she had already worshipped Shiva in a thousand and seven holy places before she came here. Here, under this tree, she waited."
**Sound:** a slow heartbeat of a drum; the lamp's flame.

### 5. The pedestal (25 sec)
**Visual:** The present-day temple. Beneath the pathiri tree, a bare stone pedestal, with no image on it, only flowers and a small lamp. Devotees bow before it.
**Narration:** "Today the temple marks the place with a bare pedestal. There is no image on it. Tradition says she worshipped here in a form beyond form, and so the place is left empty, and full of her."
**Sound:** temple bells; whispered prayers.

### 6. The hymn (20 sec)
**Visual:** The poet-saint Appar, old and gentle, stands before the shrine singing. Behind him, faint images: Shiva with the mountain's daughter at his side, and three cities burning far away.
**Narration:** "The saint Appar sang at this shrine, of the Lord who dwells here in love with the mountain's daughter, and who burned the three cities of the demons. He does not tell of her penance."
**Sound:** Appar's voice, soft, over a tambura.

### 7. The Lord (20 sec)
**Visual:** Under the pathiri tree, a light rises before the Goddess. She opens her eyes and smiles. The light becomes Shiva, who takes her hand. Petals fall over them both.
**Narration:** "At last the penance was answered. Shiva came to her in the shade of the tree, and she was one with him. Arunagiri sings that the Goddess once did a matchless penance here, and he calls her the one who did penance in the shade of the pathiri tree."
**Sound:** a warm chord; temple bells; birdsong.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri sings of the Goddess's penance under the pathiri tree at Thiruppathirippuliyur. Songs to show: 745 (Thiruppathirippuliyur) and 1162 (general songs).
