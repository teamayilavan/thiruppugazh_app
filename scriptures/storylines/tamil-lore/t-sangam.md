# The Mute Boy Who Judged the Poets (Murugan as Rudrasarman)

`t-sangam` | Tamil lore | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: comic, then reverent, then bright

## One-line story

When the Sangam poets of Madurai quarrel over whose poems are best, Shiva sends a five-year-old mute boy to judge; the boy shows delight only at true poetry, and the songs say that boy was Murugan.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| The Sangam poets of Madurai quarrel over whose poems are best | Tiruvilaiyadal Puranam |
| Shiva sends the five-year-old mute boy Rudrasarman, son of the merchant Dhanapati, to judge | TVP |
| The boy shows his delight only at true poetry and ranks Kapilar, Paranar and Nakkeerar highest | TVP padalam 55 |
| The plank (a magic seat that fits only the worthy) is in padalam 51 | TVP 51 |
| Songs 126, 568 and 987 say Murugan was that boy | Thiruppugazh songs |
| **Legend:** 49 poets disputed the meaning of Shiva's Akapporul, which the boy settled | Tradition (some songs) |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals, the dialogue, the narration.

## Characters

- **Rudrasarman:** a five-year-old boy, mute, bright-eyed.
- **The poets:** a dozen scholars in white.
- **Kapilar, Paranar, Nakkeerar:** three poets, dignified.
- **Dhanapati:** his father, a merchant.

## The storyline

### 1. The quarrel (20 sec)
In Madurai, forty-nine poets argue loudly in a hall, each insisting his is best.
**Narration:** "In Madurai, the poets quarrelled over whose verses were best."

### 2. The decision (20 sec)
A voice from the temple: "Let a boy judge."
**Narration:** "A voice said a boy would judge."

### 3. The boy (25 sec)
A mute child of five, the merchant's son, is led into the hall.
**Narration:** "He was five years old, and could not speak."

### 4. The recitation (25 sec)
One poet after another recites; the boy listens, expressionless.
**Narration:** "They recited, one after another."

### 5. A smile (25 sec)
At a true verse, his face lights; he laughs aloud.
**Narration:** "Only at true poetry did he show delight."

### 6. The three (25 sec)
He points to three poets: Kapilar, Paranar and Nakkeerar.
**Narration:** "He chose three: Kapilar, Paranar and Nakkeerar."

### 7. The settling (15 sec)
The poets bow; the quarrel is over.
**Narration:** "The quarrel ended."

### 8. The song (10 sec)
A glow of gold behind the boy.
**Narration:** "The songs say that boy was Murugan."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 126, 451, 568, 987 and 992.

## Production notes

- **No mockery of the poets.** The boy is wise, not a prankster.
- **The 49 poets** are tradition: say so.
