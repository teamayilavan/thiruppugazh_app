# A Handful of Beaten Rice (Sudama)

`krishna-sudama` | Vishnu | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: poor, tender, overflowing

## One-line story

The poor Brahmin friend Sudama (Kuchela) visits Krishna at Dwaraka.

## Characters

- **Sudama:** a thin, humble Brahmin in rags.
- **His wife:** gentle, practical.
- **Krishna:** a king, dazzling, warm.
- **Rukmini.**

## The storyline

### 1. The poor home (25 sec)
A bare hut; Sudama's wife tells him to ask his old friend Krishna for help.
**Narration:** "Sudama was very poor."

### 2. The gift (20 sec)
She gives him a handful of beaten rice, tied in a rag.
**Narration:** "He took a little rice for a gift."

### 3. The journey (20 sec)
He walks to Dwaraka.
**Narration:** "He walked to Dwaraka."

### 4. The gate (20 sec)
The golden city; the guards look at his rags.
**Narration:** "He was ashamed of his rags."

### 5. The welcome (25 sec)
Krishna runs to him, embraces him, washes his feet.
**Narration:** "But Krishna ran to meet him."

### 6. The rice (25 sec)
Krishna takes the rice and eats it with delight.
**Narration:** "Krishna ate the rice with joy."

### 7. The silence (15 sec)
Sudama asks for nothing.
**Narration:** "Sudama asked for nothing."

### 8. The home (15 sec)
Back at his hut, a palace stands.
**Narration:** "And when he went home, his hut was a palace."
