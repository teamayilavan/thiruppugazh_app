# Kumbha Falls to Sugriva

`ramayana-kumbha` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: fierce, brisk, decisive

## One-line story

Kumbha, son of Kumbhakarna, is felled by a blow from Sugriva.

### 3. The king (20 sec)
Sugriva steps forward.
