# The Army of Monkeys Sets Out

`ramayana-army-march` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: eager, rhythmic, a parade

## One-line story

Sugriva's vanara army sets out south for Lanka, eager to free Sita.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Sugriva's vanara army sets out south for Lanka | Valmiki Yuddha Kanda 4.62-70 |
| They are eager to free Sita | same |
| Songs 941 and 1231 recall it | Thiruppugazh songs |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Sugriva, Hanuman, Angada, Jambavan.**
- **Rama and Lakshmana.**
- **The monkeys and bears.**

## The storyline

### 1. The gathering (25 sec)
Monkeys and bears pour out of forests and hills.
**Narration:** "Monkeys and bears gathered from every hill."

### 2. The order (20 sec)
Sugriva gives the order.
**Narration:** "Sugriva ordered them south."

### 3. The march (25 sec)
A great army, banners of leaves and branches.
**Narration:** "They marched."

### 4. The songs (20 sec)
They sing as they go, leaping.
**Narration:** "They sang as they went."

### 5. The mountains (20 sec)
Mountains, rivers, forests pass.
**Narration:** "They crossed hills and rivers."

### 6. The sea (25 sec)
At last, the sea.
**Narration:** "And they came to the sea."

### 7. The pause (15 sec)
The army halts, silent.
**Narration:** "And they stopped, and looked."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 941 and 1231.
