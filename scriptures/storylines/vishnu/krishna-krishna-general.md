# Krishna, the Beloved (A Portrait)

`krishna-krishna-general` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: loving, an overview, a prayer | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

The poet names Krishna without an episode; this film is a short portrait of the Lord who is a child, a cowherd, a friend and a charioteer.

## Characters

- **Krishna:** a baby, a boy, a friend, a charioteer.

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; loving and unhurried, like a prayer. The images are drawn from the other Krishna films of this set. Where a scene has no voice, the music carries it.

### 1. The baby (20 sec)
**Visual:** Gokula at dawn: cows, smoke from the roofs, a cradle in Nanda's house. A dark baby kicks his feet and laughs; Yashoda bends over him. Brief glimpses: a cart tipping over, a vast shadow fading in the pasture.
**Narration:** "He was born in a prison in Mathura, and carried by night to a village of cowherds. There, in Yashoda's arms, the Lord of all the worlds was simply a baby, kicking his little feet."
**Sound:** cowbells, a lullaby hummed by a woman's voice.

### 2. The butter (20 sec)
**Visual:** A toddler climbs to a hanging pot of butter; monkeys wait below. Yashoda chases him with a stick, then ties him to a mortar with a rope that is always two fingers short, until he lets it fit.
**Narration:** "He stole butter and fed it to the monkeys. His mother chased him and tried to tie him up, and every rope was too short, until he let himself be bound. He could be held only by love."
**Sound:** comic flute, the thump of a mortar, laughter.

### 3. The flute (25 sec)
**Visual:** A boy of the forest, a peacock feather in his hair, leads the calves to the river. He sits under a tree and plays the flute; the cows stop grazing, the river seems to pause, the birds fall silent. Rain clouds gather; he lifts a hill on one hand like an umbrella over the village.
**Narration:** "He grew up with the calves and the cows, among the forests by the Yamuna. When he played his flute, the cows stopped grazing and the birds fell silent to listen. When the storms came, he held up a hill and kept his village safe beneath it."
**Sound:** the flute, clear and long; rain on stone.

### 4. The friend (20 sec)
**Visual:** Dwaraka by the sea. A thin brahmin in a worn cloth stands at the palace door; Krishna runs to embrace him and washes his feet. Another glimpse: Krishna seated beside the Pandava brothers, laughing as an equal.
**Narration:** "He became a king in Dwaraka by the sea. But when a poor old school friend came to his door, he ran to embrace him. Kings were his companions; he was a friend first."
**Sound:** soft palace music, a warm string melody.

### 5. The charioteer (25 sec)
**Visual:** Kurukshetra at dawn. Two great armies. Between them, a white chariot; Krishna holds the reins and turns to Arjuna, who has dropped his bow. The golden light of the Gita surrounds them.
**Narration:** "When the great war came, he would not take up a weapon. He chose instead to drive his friend Arjuna's chariot. And there, between the armies, he taught him the Gita: do your work, and give it all to me."
**Sound:** conches far off, then a hush and one sustained tone.

### 6. The Lord (25 sec)
**Visual:** The images begin to dissolve into one another: the baby, the butter thief, the flute player, the king, the charioteer. Behind them a vast, calm form of Vishnu rises, dark as a rain cloud, holding conch and discus. All the faces are one face.
**Narration:** "Baby, thief of butter, cowherd, king, friend and charioteer. The songs call him Mukunda, the giver of freedom; Gopala, keeper of the cows; Damodara, bound by a rope; Mayan, the wonder-worker. In every form he was the same Lord."
**Sound:** the flute melody returns, joined by temple bells.

### 7. The song (15 sec)
**Visual:** The hill temple of Thiruchengode in evening light. A lamp is lit before Murugan. The flute tune fades into the sound of devotees singing.
**Narration:** "And Arunagiri, singing to Murugan, called him the nephew of this Lord, Mukunda of the cows and calves."
**Sound:** the singing of devotees, a bell, silence.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

In this song Arunagiri names Krishna without any one story, calling Murugan the nephew of Mukunda, the cowherd friend of the calves. Song to show: 586 (Thiruchengode).
