# The Golden Deer (Maricha)

`ramayana-maricha` | Vishnu | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: beautiful, ominous, tragic

## One-line story

Ravana asks Maricha to lure Rama away; Maricha becomes a golden deer, Sita asks for it, and Rama follows and kills it; Maricha's cry draws Lakshmana off, and Ravana carries Sita away.

## The storyline

### 1. The plan (25 sec)
Ravana asks Maricha to help.
**Narration:** "Ravana asked Maricha for help."

### 2. The deer (25 sec)
A golden deer with jewelled spots appears near the hut.
**Narration:** "A golden deer appeared."

### 3. The wish (20 sec)
Sita sees it and asks Rama for it.
**Narration:** "Sita wished for it."

### 4. The chase (25 sec)
Rama follows the deer deep into the forest.
**Narration:** "Rama followed it."

### 5. The arrow (20 sec)
His arrow strikes; the deer cries in Rama's voice.
**Narration:** "It cried out in Rama's own voice."

### 6. The fear (25 sec)
Sita hears; she sends Lakshmana to help.
**Narration:** "Sita sent Lakshmana to help."

### 7. The empty hut (25 sec)
Ravana enters the empty hut.
**Narration:** "And Ravana came to the empty hut."
