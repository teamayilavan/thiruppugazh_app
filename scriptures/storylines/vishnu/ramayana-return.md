# The Return to Ayodhya

`ramayana-return` | Vishnu | running time 2:35 (7 scenes) | tone: joyful, golden, a homecoming | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

Rama, Sita and Lakshmana return by the Pushpaka chariot; Rama is crowned king.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Rama, Sita and Lakshmana return by the Pushpaka chariot | Valmiki Yuddha Kanda; Kamba, Mitchip Padalam, Thirumudi Soottu Padalam (traditional source; not read verse by verse here) |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Rama, Sita, Lakshmana, Hanuman, Vibhishana, Sugriva.**
- **Bharata and Shatrughna.**
- **The people of Ayodhya.**

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it. This is the last film of the Ramayana series: let it be golden and glad.

### 1. The chariot (25 sec)
**Visual:** Morning on the shore of Lanka. The Pushpaka, a flying chariot shaped like a palace of gold and jewels, settles gently on the sand. Vibhishana bows and offers it. Rama, Sita and Lakshmana climb aboard; Sugriva, Hanuman and the monkey chiefs crowd in after them, delighted. Flowers fall from the sky as it rises.
**Narration:** "The war was over, and the fourteen years of exile were almost done. Vibhishana, the new king of Lanka, brought Rama the Pushpaka, a chariot that flew through the air. Kamban says Rama climbed into it with a glad heart, thinking, our work is finished. The gods showered flowers and blessings."
**Sound:** a bright wind, bells, a rising melody.

### 2. The journey (25 sec)
**Visual:** The Pushpaka glides north over the sea and the great bridge, over Kishkindha's hills, over the forests of the exile. Rama points down; Sita looks, remembering. The places of the story pass below like a map.
**Narration:** "As they flew north, Rama showed Sita the places they had passed: the bridge across the sea, where the battle had been, the hills of Kishkindha, the forests where they had lived and where she had been taken. Now the long road ran backwards, toward home."
**Sound:** soft strings; the wind of flight.

### 3. Bharata (20 sec)
**Visual:** Nandigrama, a small village near Ayodhya. Bharata, thin, with matted hair, in bark clothes, sits beside a throne on which rest Rama's wooden sandals. Hanuman lands before him, bows and speaks. Bharata rises, his face breaking into tears of joy.
**Narration:** "Hanuman flew ahead with the news. In a village outside Ayodhya, Bharata had lived all those years like a hermit, ruling only in his brother's name, with Rama's sandals on the throne."
**Dialogue:** Hanuman: "Rama is coming home."
**Sound:** a hush, then a swell of strings.

### 4. The meeting (25 sec)
**Visual:** The Pushpaka descends. Bharata runs forward, barefoot, carrying the sandals on his head. Rama steps down and lifts him up before he can fall at his feet. Bharata kneels and places the sandals on Rama's feet. The two brothers embrace; Lakshmana and Shatrughna join them; the three mothers come forward weeping.
**Narration:** "Bharata ran to meet him, carrying the sandals on his head. He knelt and put them back on Rama's feet, and gave him back the kingdom he had only kept for him. The brothers held one another, and the mothers wept for joy."
**Dialogue:** Bharata: "Your kingdom was only kept in trust. Today I give it back to you."
**Sound:** warm full music, voices of the crowd.

### 5. The city (20 sec)
**Visual:** Ayodhya decked for a festival: banners on every tower, flowers on every street, music in the squares. Children run along the roofs. At dusk, lamps glow in every window as the procession enters.
**Narration:** "Ayodhya had waited fourteen years. Now every street was hung with banners and strewn with flowers, and the whole city came out to see him. Tradition says that lamps were lit in every home that night."
**Sound:** conches, drums, the cheering of a great crowd, then evening bells.

### 6. The coronation (25 sec)
**Visual:** The royal hall. The sage Vasishtha, white-bearded, stands with the priests. Rama sits on the throne with Sita beside him. Water is poured over them from golden pots. Vasishtha raises the crown and sets it on Rama's head. Hanuman kneels close by, hands joined.
**Narration:** "On the day and hour the sage Vasishtha had chosen, Rama was crowned king, with Sita beside him. Then Rama gave Sita a string of pearls, and at a sign from him she placed it round Hanuman's neck, the friend who had found her."
**Sound:** chanting, a great conch, then a single clear bell.

### 7. The peace (15 sec)
**Visual:** Wide over Ayodhya at sunset: fields green, cattle grazing, children playing by the river. Final image: the throne room, golden and calm.
**Narration:** "And so Rama ruled, and the old poets say the land knew peace, and no one wept for want."
**Sound:** the opening melody returns, slow and glad; one bell.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri names the homecoming once. In song 959 (Madurai) he calls Murugan the beloved nephew of Hari, who cut down the demon who had wronged Janaki and came back to victorious Ayodhya.

## Production notes

- **Pure joy.** Close the Ramayana series.
