# The Return to Ayodhya

`ramayana-return` | Vishnu | running time 2:35 (7 scenes) | tone: joyful, golden, a homecoming

## One-line story

Rama, Sita and Lakshmana return by the Pushpaka chariot; Rama is crowned king.

## Characters

- **Rama, Sita, Lakshmana, Hanuman, Vibhishana, Sugriva.**
- **Bharata and Shatrughna.**
- **The people of Ayodhya.**

## The storyline

### 1. The chariot (25 sec)
The flying chariot rises from Lanka.
**Narration:** "They flew home in the Pushpaka chariot."

### 2. The journey (25 sec)
Rama points out the places of exile.
**Narration:** "Rama pointed out each place they had known."

### 3. Bharata (20 sec)
Bharata, in hermit's clothes, waits at the city gate, with Rama's sandals on his head.
**Narration:** "Bharata waited, with Rama's sandals."

### 4. The meeting (25 sec)
The brothers embrace.
**Narration:** "The brothers met."

### 5. The lamps (20 sec)
Ayodhya lights a million lamps.
**Narration:** "The city lit lamps."

### 6. The coronation (25 sec)
Rama is anointed and crowned.
**Narration:** "Rama was crowned."

### 7. The peace (15 sec)
A golden dawn over Ayodhya.
**Narration:** "And the world was at peace."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Song 959.
