# Tied to the Mortar (Damodara)

`krishna-mortar-bound` | Vishnu | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: comic, then tender, then moving

## One-line story

Caught stealing, Krishna sits on an upturned mortar giving butter to monkeys and runs when Yashoda comes with a stick; every rope she brings is two fingers too short until he lets her bind him, and so he is called Damodara.

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Krishna:** a small boy.
- **Yashoda:** his mother, tired and loving.
- **The monkeys.**

## The storyline

### 1. The mischief (20 sec)
Krishna sits on an upturned mortar, handing butter to monkeys.
**Narration:** "Krishna sat on an upturned mortar, feeding the monkeys."

### 2. The mother (20 sec)
Yashoda comes in, stick in hand.
**Narration:** "His mother came with a stick."

### 3. The chase (25 sec)
He runs; she follows, out of breath.
**Narration:** "He ran, and she ran after him."

### 4. The catch (20 sec)
She catches him; his eyes fill with tears.
**Narration:** "She caught him."

### 5. The rope (25 sec)
She ties a rope round his waist; it is too short.
**Narration:** "But the rope was two fingers too short."

### 6. The ropes (25 sec)
She adds another rope, another, and still two fingers short.
**Narration:** "And so was the next, and the next."

### 7. The surrender (20 sec)
She is tired, sweating; he sees it, and lets her bind him.
**Narration:** "Then he let her tie him."

### 8. The name (10 sec)
A small knot at his belly; he is Damodara.
**Narration:** "So he is called Damodara: the one with a rope round his belly."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 156, 178 and 629.

## Production notes

- **The point is love.** He is bound by his mother's love.
- **Comic first, moving last.**
