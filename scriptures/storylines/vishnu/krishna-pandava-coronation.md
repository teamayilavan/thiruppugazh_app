# The Crowning of Yudhishthira

`krishna-pandava-coronation` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: quiet, sombre, a new beginning

## One-line story

After the war Yudhishthira is anointed king of the earth.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| After the war Yudhishthira is anointed king of the earth | Mahabharata (Critical Edition) 12,40.15 |
| Songs 155 and 573 recall it | Thiruppugazh songs |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Yudhishthira:** a grave, righteous king, weary.
- **Krishna and the brothers.**
- **Draupadi, Kunti.**

## The storyline

### 1. The war's end (25 sec)
The battlefield at dusk, silent.
**Narration:** "The war was over."

### 2. The grief (25 sec)
Yudhishthira looks over the field, weeping.
**Narration:** "Yudhishthira grieved."

### 3. The counsel (20 sec)
Krishna and the sages persuade him to take up his duty.
**Narration:** "Krishna and the sages told him to rule."

### 4. The city (20 sec)
Hastinapura, decorated.
**Narration:** "The city was prepared."

### 5. The anointing (25 sec)
Water and flowers; the crown is lifted.
**Narration:** "He was anointed."

### 6. The brothers (20 sec)
His brothers bow.
**Narration:** "His brothers stood by him."

### 7. The dawn (15 sec)
A new day over the city.
**Narration:** "And a new age began."

Songs 155 and 573.
