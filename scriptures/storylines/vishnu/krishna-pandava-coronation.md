# The Crowning of Yudhishthira

`krishna-pandava-coronation` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: quiet, sombre, a new beginning | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

After the war Yudhishthira is anointed king of the earth.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| After the war Yudhishthira is anointed king of the earth | Mahabharata (Critical Edition) 12,40.15 |
| Songs 155 and 573 recall it | Thiruppugazh songs |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Yudhishthira:** a grave, righteous king, weary.
- **Krishna and the brothers.**
- **Draupadi, Kunti.**

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; quiet and sombre, opening slowly toward a new morning. Where a scene has no voice, the music carries it.

### 1. The war's end (25 sec)
**Visual:** Kurukshetra at dusk after the eighteenth day. A vast, silent plain under drifting smoke; broken chariot wheels, fallen banners, far-off funeral fires. Five brothers stand at the edge of it, with Krishna a little apart. No bodies are shown, only the empty field.
**Narration:** "After eighteen days, the great war at Kurukshetra was over. The Pandavas had won. But the field was silent, and nearly every king and warrior of the land was gone, friends and kinsmen and teachers alike."
**Sound:** wind, the crackle of distant fires, a single low drone.

### 2. The grief (25 sec)
**Visual:** Yudhishthira sits alone by the Ganga, his crown untouched beside him. His brothers, Draupadi and Kunti watch from a distance. He stares at the water.
**Narration:** "Yudhishthira, the eldest, could feel no joy. He thought of all who had died so that he might rule, and he wanted to give up the kingdom and go away into the forest."
**Dialogue:** Yudhishthira: "What is a kingdom worth, bought at such a price?"
**Sound:** the river, a slow lament on the veena.

### 3. The counsel (20 sec)
**Visual:** One by one they come to him: his brothers, Draupadi, the sage Vyasa, and Krishna, who sits beside him and speaks gently. Yudhishthira slowly lifts his head.
**Narration:** "His brothers pleaded with him, and Draupadi, and the sages. Krishna spoke to him too. A king's duty, they told him, is not to run from his grief, but to protect the people who are left."
**Sound:** quiet voices under the music; a gentle rising phrase.

### 4. The city (20 sec)
**Visual:** Hastinapura. Yudhishthira enters the city in a white chariot, his brothers beside him. Streets hung with flowers, but the crowd is quiet, many in mourning white. Old Dhritarashtra and Gandhari wait in the hall.
**Narration:** "So he came at last to Hastinapura. The city had been decorated, but the people were quiet, for every house had lost someone. In the great hall waited the old blind king Dhritarashtra, whose sons had all fallen."
**Sound:** soft conches, muted drums, footsteps on stone.

### 5. The anointing (25 sec)
**Visual:** Yudhishthira sits on a golden throne facing east; facing him sit Krishna and Satyaki, with Bhima and Arjuna on either side. He touches white flowers, grain, earth, gold and gems. The priest Dhaumya lights the sacred fire with Draupadi seated beside the king. Krishna rises with his conch, and water pours over Yudhishthira's head.
**Narration:** "He sat on a golden throne, facing the east, and Krishna sat before him. The family priest Dhaumya lit the sacred fire. Then they anointed the son of Kunti as lord of the earth, and the old king Dhritarashtra and all the people anointed him too. Many tellings say Krishna himself poured the water from his conch."
**Sound:** chanting, the crackle of the fire, one clear conch.

### 6. The brothers (20 sec)
**Visual:** The brahmins raise their hands in blessing. Bhima, Arjuna, Nakula and Sahadeva bow to their brother. Yudhishthira bows in return. Krishna watches, still and gentle.
**Narration:** "The brahmins blessed him and wished him victory. His brothers bowed to their king. He took the kingdom, the text says, as his duty, and honoured all who had come."
**Dialogue:** A brahmin: "By good fortune you have won, O king. Now rule with justice."
**Sound:** kettledrums, softened; hands joining in blessing.

### 7. The dawn (15 sec)
**Visual:** Morning over Hastinapura. The first plough moves in a field outside the walls; a child leads a cow along the river. On the palace terrace Yudhishthira watches the sun rise, Krishna beside him.
**Narration:** "A war had ended, and a just king sat on the throne. The land could begin, slowly, to heal."
**Sound:** birdsong, the river, a quiet hopeful melody.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri credits the Pandavas' kingdom to Krishna, praising Murugan as the nephew of the Lord who set Kunti's sons on the throne to rule with justice. Songs to show: 573 (Viralimalai) and 155 (Palani).

## Production notes

- **Quiet, not festive.** A king crowned after a war.
