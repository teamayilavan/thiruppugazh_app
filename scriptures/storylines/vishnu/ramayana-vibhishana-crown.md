# The Crown of Lanka (Vibhishana Installed)

`ramayana-vibhishana-crown` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: solemn, just, merciful

## One-line story

After Ravana's death Rama installs Vibhishana as king of Lanka.

## The storyline

### 1. The funeral (25 sec)
Ravana's funeral; Vibhishana grieves.
**Narration:** "Vibhishana mourned his brother."

### 2. The permission (20 sec)
Rama asks that the rites be done with honour.
**Narration:** "Rama asked that Ravana be honoured."

### 3. The city (20 sec)
Lanka's people gather, uncertain.
**Narration:** "Lanka waited."

### 4. The preparation (20 sec)
Lakshmana brings waters for the anointing.
**Narration:** "The ceremony was prepared."

### 5. The crown (25 sec)
Vibhishana is crowned.
**Narration:** "Vibhishana was crowned."

### 6. The people (20 sec)
The crowd cheers.
**Narration:** "And the people rejoiced."

### 7. The promise (20 sec)
Rama's promise of refuge is kept.
**Narration:** "Rama had kept his word."
