# The Crown of Lanka (Vibhishana Installed)

`ramayana-vibhishana-crown` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: solemn, just, merciful | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

After Ravana's death Rama installs Vibhishana as king of Lanka.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Ravana's brother Vibhishana, rejected for urging the return of Sita, joins Rama and is given refuge | Research set `ramayana-vibhishana-crown` (see `ramayana-vibhishana-refuge`) |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Vibhishana, Rama, Lakshmana, Hanuman.**
- **The people of Lanka.**

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it. Give Ravana's funeral full dignity: the enemy is mourned, not mocked.

### 1. The funeral (25 sec)
**Visual:** Evening on the battlefield, after the war. Ravana's body lies covered with a royal cloth on a bier; only the shape is seen. The women of Lanka mourn at a distance. Vibhishana stands apart, torn, his face full of grief and doubt.
**Narration:** "Ravana had fallen, and the war was over. But Vibhishana stood apart, torn in two. Ravana had been cruel and unjust; yet he was his elder brother. Should he perform the funeral rites for such a man?"
**Dialogue:** Vibhishana: "He did great wrong. How can I honour him?"
**Sound:** a slow drum, the far sound of mourning.

### 2. The permission (20 sec)
**Visual:** Rama comes to Vibhishana and lays a hand on his shoulder. He speaks gently, looking toward the covered bier with respect.
**Narration:** "Rama answered him kindly. Enmity ends with death, he said. Our quarrel is over. Give him the rites that are his due; he is as much mine now as he is yours."
**Dialogue:** Rama: "Enmity ends with death. Honour your brother as a king."
**Sound:** a soft, solemn chord.

### 3. The city (20 sec)
**Visual:** Night. A great pyre burns by the sea; Vibhishana, in white, performs the rites with the priests of Lanka. The smoke rises over the silent golden city. The people watch from the walls.
**Narration:** "So Vibhishana gave his brother a king's funeral, with all the rites of their family. The great fire burned by the sea, and Lanka, the golden city, watched in silence. An age had ended."
**Sound:** chanting, the crackle of the pyre, waves.

### 4. The preparation (20 sec)
**Visual:** Morning. Rama sits outside the city with Lakshmana. He gives Lakshmana a golden pot. Monkeys bring water from the sea, and the leaders of Lanka come out and bow. Rama does not go in.
**Narration:** "In the morning Rama asked Lakshmana to crown Vibhishana king of Lanka. Rama himself would not enter the city, for his years of exile were not yet over. So the monkeys brought water from the sea in golden pots, and the people of Lanka came out to see."
**Sound:** waves, gentle bells, a growing murmur.

### 5. The crown (25 sec)
**Visual:** Vibhishana sits on a jewelled throne under a white umbrella. The sacred water is poured over him. Lakshmana lifts the crown and sets it on his head. For a moment, Vibhishana, dark-skinned in the morning sun, looks like a dark hill crowned with sunrise.
**Narration:** "The sacred water was poured over him, as the scriptures direct. Then, at Rama's word, Lakshmana set the crown on his head. Kamban says he shone on his jewelled throne like a dark hill wearing the rising sun. And the people of Lanka cheered their new king."
**Sound:** conches, drums, cheering.

### 6. The people (20 sec)
**Visual:** Vibhishana, crowned, goes among the people of Lanka; the frightened demon citizens bow, then slowly smile. Children peep out of doorways. Gifts are brought to Rama outside the city; he receives them and gives them away to his army.
**Narration:** "The people of Lanka had been afraid. Now they had a king who loved what was right. Vibhishana went among them gently. They brought gifts for Rama, and Rama gave them all away to the army that had fought for him."
**Sound:** a lighter, peaceful melody.

### 7. The promise (20 sec)
**Visual:** Vibhishana kneels before Rama. Rama raises him. Behind them, the sea is calm and blue; the bridge stretches back toward the mainland. Vibhishana turns toward the Ashoka grove, where Sita waits.
**Narration:** "Long before, on the shore, Rama had promised Vibhishana that he would be king of Lanka. Now that promise was kept. And Rama's next thought was for Sita."
**Dialogue:** Rama: "Go now, my friend, and bring Sita to me."
**Sound:** the melody resolves; one long conch.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri returns to Vibhishana's crowning in five songs. Songs to show: 161 (Palani), Vibhishana stands to wear the golden crown while Ravana and his kin go into the fire; 840 (Vedaranyam), the Lord who blocked the sea and gave Lanka to Vibhishana; and 423 (Thiruvannamalai), the kingdom given to him together with release.
