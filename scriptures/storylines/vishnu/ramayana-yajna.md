# Guarding the Sacrifice (Siddhashrama)

`ramayana-yajna` | Vishnu | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: watchful, then swift | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

At Siddhashrama Rama and Lakshmana guard Vishwamitra's sacrifice; Rama kills the demon Subahu and drives Maricha away, flung into the sea.

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Rama and Lakshmana:** two young princes, calm and alert.
- **Vishwamitra:** an intense sage in a sacrifice.
- **Subahu and Maricha:** two demons.

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it. The fight is a stylised duel of light and wind; no gore.

### 1. The hermitage (25 sec)
**Visual:** Siddhashrama at evening: a clearing of huts among old trees, a fire altar of bricks, sages in white with matted hair. Vishwamitra leads Rama and Lakshmana in; the sages come out to greet them with folded hands. Deer graze unafraid at the edge of the clearing.
**Narration:** "After Tataka fell, Vishwamitra brought the two princes to his hermitage, Siddhashrama, the place of perfection. Here he meant to perform a great sacrifice. But every time he began, demons came out of the sky and spoiled it, and a sage at his sacrifice may not fight."
**Dialogue:** Vishwamitra: "From tomorrow I keep silence. Guard the sacrifice for six days and nights."
**Sound:** evening birds, a soft bell, the crackle of the altar fire.

### 2. The watch (25 sec)
**Visual:** Night. The sacred fire burns on the altar; the sages chant, Vishwamitra seated in silence among them. At the edge of the firelight Rama and Lakshmana stand with bows ready, one on each side, watching the sky. Stars wheel overhead.
**Narration:** "The fire was lit, and the sage sat in silence. Rama and Lakshmana took their places at the edge of the firelight, bows in hand. They did not sleep. All night they stood and watched the dark sky."
**Sound:** low chanting, the fire, crickets.

### 3. Six days (20 sec)
**Visual:** A time-lapse: sunrise, noon, dusk, night, again and again. The brothers keep their posts in rain and heat, eyes on the sky. The chanting never stops. On the sixth morning Lakshmana glances at Rama; Rama nods.
**Narration:** "For six days and six nights the brothers kept watch, never leaving their places. On the sixth day, as the sacrifice came near its end, the altar fire suddenly blazed up high."
**Sound:** the chant continues; a sudden rush as the flames leap.

### 4. The storm (25 sec)
**Visual:** The sky darkens. Out of the clouds come two great demons, Maricha and Subahu, the sons of Tataka, with a horde behind them, seen as huge shadows and glowing eyes. A dark, foul rain falls toward the altar. The sages look up in terror and cry out to Rama.
**Narration:** "Then the sky went dark. With a roar, the demons came: Maricha and Subahu, the sons of Tataka, and their followers behind them. They meant to pour foul things onto the sacred fire. Kamban says the sages trembled, like the gods who once ran to Shiva when the poison rose from the sea."
**Dialogue:** The sages: "O dark-hued one, you are our refuge!"
**Sound:** thunder, howling wind, the roar of the demons.

### 5. The arrow (25 sec)
**Visual:** Rama steps forward, calm. He fits a shining arrow and looses it at Maricha. A blast of cold white light strikes Maricha full on; he is lifted and hurled away across the sky, tumbling, far over the hills and out to the distant sea, where he drops with a splash, stunned but alive.
**Narration:** "Rama did not hurry. He fitted a shining arrow and loosed it at Maricha. It did not kill him. It struck him like a great wind and flung him far away, across the land, a hundred leagues, into the sea. There he lay stunned, but alive."
**Sound:** a hum, then a great gust; a distant splash.

### 6. The wind (25 sec)
**Visual:** Rama turns to Subahu and looses an arrow of fire; a flash, and the shadow of Subahu is gone. Then he looses a third arrow that becomes a whirlwind, sweeping the rest of the demons away like dry leaves. The clouds tear open and sunlight floods the clearing.
**Narration:** "Then he loosed an arrow of fire at Subahu, and Subahu fell. With one more arrow, a mighty wind, he swept the rest away. Kamban says the Lord's arrow sent one brother into the sea, and the other to the city of death."
**Sound:** a crack of fire, a rising whirlwind, then sudden calm.

### 7. The completion (15 sec)
**Visual:** Sunlight on the altar; the last offering goes into the fire. Vishwamitra opens his eyes and smiles at the two boys. The sages bless them; flowers fall.
**Narration:** "The sacrifice was complete. Vishwamitra blessed the princes. And Maricha, far away in the sea, would one day return."
**Sound:** a bright conch, the opening melody, warm and glad.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri remembers the sacrifice in one song. In song 366 (Thiruvanaikka) Murugan is the nephew of the ocean of grace who, to protect the sacrifice hall of the sages in their forest hermitage, killed Tataka when she came against it.

## Production notes

- **A stylised duel.** No gore.
- **Show Maricha's survival,** since he returns in `ramayana-maricha`.
