# Guarding the Sacrifice (Siddhashrama)

`ramayana-yajna` | Vishnu | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: watchful, then swift

## One-line story

At Siddhashrama Rama and Lakshmana guard Vishwamitra's sacrifice; Rama kills the demon Subahu and drives Maricha away, flung into the sea.

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Rama and Lakshmana:** two young princes, calm and alert.
- **Vishwamitra:** an intense sage in a sacrifice.
- **Subahu and Maricha:** two demons.

## The storyline

### 1. The hermitage (25 sec)
A peaceful hermitage; the sage begins a sacrifice.
**Narration:** "The sage Vishwamitra began a great sacrifice."

### 2. The watch (25 sec)
Two young princes stand guard, bows ready.
**Narration:** "Rama and Lakshmana kept watch."

### 3. Six days (20 sec)
Days and nights pass.
**Narration:** "They did not sleep for six days."

### 4. The storm (25 sec)
On the last day, a dark cloud brings two demons.
**Narration:** "On the last day, the demons came."

### 5. The arrow (25 sec)
Rama looses a flame-arrow and Subahu falls.
**Narration:** "Rama's arrow ended Subahu."

### 6. The wind (25 sec)
Another arrow hurls Maricha far over the sea.
**Narration:** "Another arrow flung Maricha into the sea."

### 7. The completion (15 sec)
The sacrifice is complete; the sage blesses them.
**Narration:** "The sacrifice was finished."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Song 366.

## Production notes

- **A stylised duel.** No gore.
- **Show Maricha's survival,** since he returns in `ramayana-maricha`.
