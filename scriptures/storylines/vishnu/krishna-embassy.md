# The Envoy of Peace (Krishna at the Kuru Court)

`krishna-embassy` | Vishnu | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: tense, dignified, then overwhelming

## One-line story

Before the war Krishna goes to the Kuru court as envoy, stays with Vidura, asks for five villages, and when Duryodhana tries to seize him shows his divine form.

## Characters

- **Krishna:** calm and dignified.
- **Duryodhana:** proud and hard.
- **Dhritarashtra, Bhishma, Drona, Vidura:** the elders of the court.
- **Karna** (a presence).

## The storyline

### 1. The road (20 sec)
Krishna travels to Hastinapura, humble in dress.
**Narration:** "Krishna went to Hastinapura as the Pandavas' envoy."

### 2. Vidura's house (20 sec)
He stays with Vidura, not in the palace, and eats simple food.
**Narration:** "He stayed in Vidura's humble house."

### 3. The court (25 sec)
He enters the great court and greets the elders.
**Narration:** "He spoke in the court of the Kurus."

### 4. The request (20 sec)
"Give the Pandavas five villages, and there will be peace."
**Narration:** "He asked for five villages."

### 5. The refusal (20 sec)
Duryodhana rejects it: "Not even a needle's point of land."
**Narration:** "Duryodhana refused."

### 6. The plot (25 sec)
Duryodhana orders his guards to seize Krishna.
**Narration:** "He tried to seize him."

### 7. The form (25 sec)
Krishna rises; light fills the hall; the gods and universe appear in him.
**Narration:** "And Krishna showed his divine form."

### 8. The bowing (10 sec)
The elders cover their eyes and bow.
**Narration:** "The elders bowed."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 289, 541 and 574 among eight.

## Production notes

- **A film of dignity.** Krishna never raises his voice.
- **The divine form** must be shown as light, not a monster.
