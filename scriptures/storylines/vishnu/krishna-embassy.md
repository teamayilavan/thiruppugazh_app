# The Envoy of Peace (Krishna at the Kuru Court)

`krishna-embassy` | Vishnu | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: tense, dignified, then overwhelming | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

Before the war Krishna goes to the Kuru court as envoy, stays with Vidura, asks for five villages, and when Duryodhana tries to seize him shows his divine form.

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Krishna:** calm and dignified.
- **Duryodhana:** proud and hard.
- **Dhritarashtra, Bhishma, Drona, Vidura:** the elders of the court.
- **Karna** (a presence).

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it.

### 1. The road (20 sec)
**Visual:** A single chariot on a long road through autumn fields toward the walls of Hastinapura. Krishna stands in it, calm, in yellow silk, a garland at his chest. Villagers line the road and bow as he passes.
**Narration:** "The Pandavas had kept their word. Twelve years in the forest, and one year in hiding. Now they asked for their kingdom back, and their cousins refused. Before the war could begin, Krishna went alone to the Kuru capital, to ask for peace."
**Sound:** chariot wheels, birdsong, a single flute line.

### 2. Vidura's house (20 sec)
**Visual:** Duryodhana's palace is hung with banners and set with a great feast; Krishna's chariot passes it by. It stops at a simple house. Vidura, the wise and honest minister, comes out with folded hands, overjoyed. Inside, Krishna sits on a plain mat.
**Narration:** "Duryodhana had made his palace ready with a royal feast. But Krishna did not go there. He went to the house of Vidura, the wise and honest uncle of both families, and he stayed there, as a guest in a simple home."
**Dialogue:** Vidura: "You honour my house, Lord."
**Sound:** quiet household sounds; a lamp being lit.

### 3. The court (25 sec)
**Visual:** The great assembly hall. On the throne, old blind King Dhritarashtra. Around him the elders: Bhishma, white-haired; Drona, the teacher; Vidura. Duryodhana sits proud, Karna beside him. Krishna enters; the whole hall rises. He takes his seat, unhurried.
**Narration:** "The next morning Krishna entered the court of the Kurus. The blind king sat on his throne, with the elders around him: Bhishma the grandsire, Drona the teacher, and Vidura. Duryodhana sat proud and silent. When Krishna came in, every king in the hall stood up."
**Sound:** a hush, then the rustle of a great crowd rising.

### 4. The request (20 sec)
**Visual:** Krishna speaks to the whole hall, his voice even and gentle. He spreads his hands. The elders nod; Bhishma and Drona look at Duryodhana, pleading.
**Narration:** "Krishna spoke of peace. War would destroy both families, he said, and the whole land with them. Give the Pandavas their share. In the telling best loved in Tamil, he asked for no more than five villages. The elders begged Duryodhana to listen."
**Dialogue:** Krishna: "Let there be peace between brothers."
**Sound:** one low note held under his words.

### 5. The refusal (20 sec)
**Visual:** Duryodhana rises, face hard. He holds up a finger and thumb, pinched close together, as if holding a needle. The elders look away in sorrow.
**Narration:** "But Duryodhana would not give. Not a kingdom, not a village. He would not give them, he said, even as much land as the point of a needle could cover."
**Dialogue:** Duryodhana: "Not even the point of a needle."
**Sound:** the note breaks off; silence in the hall.

### 6. The plot (25 sec)
**Visual:** Duryodhana whispers with Karna and his brothers in a corner, glancing at Krishna. Guards with ropes move along the walls. Krishna sees, and smiles, as if at children.
**Narration:** "Then Duryodhana planned something worse. He would seize the envoy and bind him, here in the hall. Krishna saw it all. He was not angry. He laughed aloud, and spoke to Duryodhana."
**Dialogue:** Krishna: "Suyodhana, you think that I am alone. I am not alone."
**Sound:** whispers, the clink of chains, then a clear laugh.

### 7. The form (25 sec)
**Visual:** Light pours out of Krishna. His form grows vast and blazing: on his forehead sits Brahma, on his chest Shiva, and the gods and heavenly beings shine all through him, like stars in a dark sky. Draw it as light and calm, never as a monster. The kings in the hall cover their eyes. Only Bhishma, Drona, Vidura and the sages look on, their hands folded.
**Narration:** "And then they saw who he was. Light blazed from his body. Brahma shone on his forehead and Shiva on his chest, and all the gods shone within him. The kings shut their eyes; they could not bear to look. Only Bhishma, Drona, Vidura and the sages saw it and bowed."
**Sound:** a vast chord, conches, a rushing wind.

### 8. The bowing (10 sec)
**Visual:** The light folds back into Krishna, calm in yellow silk again. He walks out of the hall alone. Behind him the kings sit in silence. Outside, the road, and far away, the dust of armies.
**Narration:** "Then the light was gone. Krishna walked out of the hall alone. Peace had been offered, and refused."
**Sound:** the flute line from the road, quiet; one drum, far off.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri calls Murugan the nephew of the Pandavas' envoy in about eight songs. Songs 289 (Thiruthanigai), 574 (Viralimalai), 747 (Thiruvetkalam) and 1055 are the ones to show: the messenger to the Kuru king, Dharma's envoy, and the one who set Vidura and Duryodhana apart.

## Production notes

- **A film of dignity.** Krishna never raises his voice.
- **The divine form** must be shown as light, not a monster.
