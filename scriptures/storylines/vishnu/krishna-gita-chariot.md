# The Charioteer (Krishna and the Gita)

`krishna-gita-chariot` | Vishnu | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: stillness before storm, then deep calm

## One-line story

Krishna drives Arjuna's chariot through the Kurukshetra war and speaks the Gita at its start.

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration. The Gita's teaching is not quoted; the film shows the moment.

## Characters

- **Krishna:** the charioteer, calm.
- **Arjuna:** a great archer, shaken.
- **The two armies.**

## The storyline

### 1. The field (25 sec)
Kurukshetra: two vast armies, banners, conches.
**Narration:** "Two great armies stood at Kurukshetra."

### 2. The chariot (20 sec)
Arjuna's chariot drives between them, Krishna holding the reins.
**Narration:** "Krishna was Arjuna's charioteer."

### 3. The fear (25 sec)
Arjuna sees his teachers and kin on the other side; the bow slips from his hand.
**Narration:** "Arjuna saw his own kin on the other side."

### 4. The collapse (20 sec)
He sinks down in the chariot.
**Narration:** "He put down his bow."

### 5. The silence (20 sec)
Time seems to stop.
**Narration:** "And the world stood still."

### 6. The teaching (25 sec)
Krishna speaks, gently; light fills the chariot.
**Narration:** "And Krishna spoke."

### 7. The rising (20 sec)
Arjuna lifts his bow.
**Narration:** "Arjuna stood up."

### 8. The charge (10 sec)
The chariot moves into the war.
**Narration:** "And the chariot moved on."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 155, 339 and 527 among eleven.
