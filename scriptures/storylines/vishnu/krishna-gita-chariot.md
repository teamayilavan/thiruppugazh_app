# The Charioteer (Krishna and the Gita)

`krishna-gita-chariot` | Vishnu | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: stillness before storm, then deep calm | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

Krishna drives Arjuna's chariot through the Kurukshetra war and speaks the Gita at its start.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration. The Gita's teaching is not quoted; the film shows the moment.

## Characters

- **Krishna:** the charioteer, calm.
- **Arjuna:** a great archer, shaken.
- **The two armies.**

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it.

### 1. The field (25 sec)
**Visual:** Dawn over the plain of Kurukshetra. Two vast armies face each other: elephants, horses, chariots, banners in their thousands, dust in the low sun. On one side the Kauravas under old Bhishma; on the other the Pandavas. In the Kaurava camp, old Bhishma lifts his conch and blows.
**Narration:** "On the field of Kurukshetra, the field of dharma, two great armies stood face to face. They were one family, divided. Cousins, uncles, teachers and friends stood on both sides. Then old Bhishma blew his conch, and the war was about to begin."
**Sound:** wind, the stamp of horses, then one great conch.

### 2. The chariot (20 sec)
**Visual:** In the Pandava line, a white chariot drawn by white horses, with a monkey on its banner. Krishna holds the reins, a whip in his hand; Arjuna stands behind with his great bow. Krishna raises his conch, Panchajanya; Arjuna raises his, Devadatta. They blow together, and the sound rolls across the field.
**Narration:** "In the Pandava army stood a white chariot, drawn by white horses. Its charioteer was Krishna himself, and the archer behind him was Arjuna. Krishna blew his conch, Panchajanya, and Arjuna blew Devadatta, and the earth and sky rang."
**Sound:** two conches, then the whole Pandava army answering.

### 3. The fear (25 sec)
**Visual:** Arjuna lifts his bow, then pauses. He leans forward and speaks to Krishna. Krishna drives the chariot out alone into the empty ground between the two armies and stops it, facing Bhishma and Drona.
**Narration:** "Arjuna lifted his bow. Then he paused, and spoke to his charioteer. Krishna drove the chariot out into the open ground between the two armies and stopped there, right in front of Bhishma and Drona."
**Dialogue:** Arjuna: "Achyuta, stop my chariot between the two armies. Let me see whom I must fight."
**Dialogue:** Krishna: "Partha, look at all these Kurus gathered here."
**Sound:** the noise of the armies falls away to wind.

### 4. The collapse (20 sec)
**Visual:** Arjuna looks across the field and sees, one by one, the faces of grandfathers, teachers, uncles, brothers, sons and friends. His hands shake. The great bow Gandiva slips from his fingers. He sinks down onto the seat of the chariot, his head in his hands.
**Narration:** "Arjuna looked, and he saw them all: grandfathers, teachers, uncles, brothers, sons and friends. His hands began to shake. His great bow slipped from his fingers, and he sat down in the chariot, overcome with sorrow."
**Dialogue:** Arjuna: "I will not fight."
**Sound:** the bow falls with a hollow sound; then a long, low note.

### 5. The silence (20 sec)
**Visual:** Everything stops. The two armies are frozen, as if time itself is waiting. Dust hangs in the air. In the middle of it all, the white chariot, the drooping horses, the weeping archer, and the calm charioteer, who turns to look at him with a smile.
**Narration:** "And everything stood still. Two armies waited. In the middle of the field sat a great warrior who could not lift his bow. And his charioteer, the Lord himself, turned around to him, and smiled."
**Sound:** silence; one sustained tone.

### 6. The teaching (25 sec)
**Visual:** Krishna speaks. We do not hear the words. The camera circles the chariot slowly; light grows around the two figures until the armies fade into gold. Arjuna's face changes as he listens: grief, then questions, then wonder.
**Narration:** "Then Krishna spoke to him. What he said there, between the armies, is the Bhagavad Gita, seven hundred verses long. We will not put it into our own words. It is best heard as it was spoken."
**Sound:** a deep, quiet drone, like a temple at night.

### 7. The rising (20 sec)
**Visual:** Arjuna lifts his head. His eyes are clear. He stands, picks up Gandiva, and folds his hands to Krishna. Krishna gathers the reins.
**Narration:** "When Krishna had finished, Arjuna rose. His doubts were gone. He picked up his bow, and folded his hands to his charioteer."
**Dialogue:** Arjuna: "My delusion is gone. I stand firm. I will do as you say."
**Sound:** the drone opens into a bright chord.

### 8. The charge (10 sec)
**Visual:** The white horses leap forward. The chariot races back to the Pandava line. Conches sound on both sides; the armies move. Hold on Krishna's hands on the reins.
**Narration:** "Krishna shook the reins, and the white horses ran. He would drive that chariot to the end of the war."
**Sound:** conches, hooves, drums, rising.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri calls Murugan the nephew of Arjuna's charioteer in about eleven songs. Songs 339 (Kanchipuram), 527 (Thiruvenkatam), 574 (Viralimalai), 928 (Karuvur) and 1053 are the ones to show: the Achyuta who drove the chariot, Vijaya's great charioteer, and the conch blown from the chariot.

## Production notes

- **Do not paraphrase the Gita.** Leave it to the original.
- **The stillness** is the film's core.
