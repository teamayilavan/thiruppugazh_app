# The Butter Thief (Krishna and the Hanging Pots)

`krishna-butter-pot` | Vishnu | running time 2:35 (7 scenes) | tone: mischievous, playful, affectionate

## One-line story

Krishna steals curd and butter from the swinging pots in the gopis' houses, piling planks to reach them.

He dips his hand in and scoops butter.

**Narration:** "He shared it with all."
