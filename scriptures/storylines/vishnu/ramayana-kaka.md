# The Crow and the Blade of Grass

`ramayana-kaka` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: gentle, tense, merciful

## One-line story

A crow (Indra's son) pecks at Sita; Rama takes a blade of grass, empowers it as a missile and blinds the crow's eye, sparing its life.

## Characters

- **Rama and Sita.**
- **The crow:** a bold black crow.

## The storyline

### 1. The rest (25 sec)
Rama sleeps with his head in Sita's lap, in the forest.
**Narration:** "Rama slept in Sita's lap."

### 2. The crow (20 sec)
A crow swoops and pecks at Sita.
**Narration:** "A crow pecked at her."

### 3. The wound (20 sec)
Sita endures, not to wake him.
**Narration:** "She bore it, so as not to wake him."

### 4. The waking (20 sec)
Rama wakes; sees the blood.
**Narration:** "Rama woke and saw."

### 5. The grass (25 sec)
He takes a blade of grass; it glows.
**Narration:** "He took a blade of grass."

### 6. The flash (25 sec)
The crow flees; the blade follows; it loses one eye.
**Narration:** "It struck the crow's eye."

### 7. The mercy (15 sec)
The crow bows; Rama spares it.
**Narration:** "But he spared its life."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Song 228.

- **A film of mercy.** The crow is Indra's son and survives.
