# The Ogress and the Baby (Putana)

`krishna-putana` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: ominous, comic-eerie, then triumphant

## One-line story

The ogress Putana, sent by Kamsa, tries to nurse the infant Krishna with poisoned milk, and he drinks out her life.

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration. The death is shown through shadow and light.

## Characters

- **Putana:** a demoness who looks like a lovely woman, then a vast shadow.
- **Baby Krishna:** a baby, dark and smiling.
- **Yashoda:** his mother, warm.

## The storyline

### 1. The village (20 sec)
Gokula at dawn; cows, milk pots.
**Narration:** "In Gokula, a baby was born."

### 2. The visitor (25 sec)
A beautiful woman enters, smiling, and asks to hold the baby.
**Narration:** "A beautiful woman asked to hold the child."

### 3. The cradle (20 sec)
She lifts Krishna, gentle, with a secret.
**Narration:** "She was Kamsa's servant, and her milk was poison."

### 4. The nursing (25 sec)
The baby nurses; her face changes.
**Narration:** "He drank."

### 5. The scream (25 sec)
She grows monstrous, screaming, struggling.
**Narration:** "He drank her life out."

### 6. The fall (20 sec)
She falls, a vast shadow over the hill.
**Narration:** "And she fell."

### 7. The baby (15 sec)
The baby sits on her body, laughing.
**Narration:** "And the baby laughed."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 626 and 1001.

## Production notes

- **Keep the monster stylised.** Avoid anything frightening to young viewers.
- **The baby is never in danger on screen.**
