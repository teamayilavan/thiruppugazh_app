# Sita Comes to Rama

`ramayana-sita-freed` | Vishnu | running time 2:35 (7 scenes) | tone: tender, hushed, relieved

## One-line story

After the victory Hanuman tells Sita and Vibhishana brings her to Rama; the Thiruppugazh lines treat the war as freeing Sita from prison.

## The storyline

### 1. The grove (25 sec)
Sita sits under a tree, waiting.
**Narration:** "Sita sat under the tree."

### 2. The news (25 sec)
Hanuman comes: "Rama has won."

### 3. The joy (20 sec)
She weeps with joy.
**Narration:** "She wept."

### 4. The summons (20 sec)
Vibhishana invites her to Rama.
**Narration:** "Vibhishana came to take her to Rama."

### 5. The bath (20 sec)
She is dressed in fine clothes.
**Narration:** "She was dressed with honour."

### 6. The road (20 sec)
She is carried through the crowd.
**Narration:** "The army parted for her."

### 7. The meeting (25 sec)
She stands before Rama, silent.
**Narration:** "And she stood before him."
