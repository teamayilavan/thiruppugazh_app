# Sita Comes to Rama

`ramayana-sita-freed` | Vishnu | running time 2:35 (7 scenes) | tone: tender, hushed, relieved | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

After the victory Hanuman tells Sita and Vibhishana brings her to Rama; the Thiruppugazh lines treat the war as freeing Sita from prison.

## Characters

- **Sita, Rama, Hanuman, Vibhishana.**

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it. Keep it quiet and tender; the next film (`ramayana-sita-agni`) is harder, so do not rush toward it.

### 1. The grove (25 sec)
**Visual:** The Ashoka grove in Lanka, at morning after the war. Tall ashoka trees with red flowers, a still pool. Under one tree sits Sita, thin, in a single faded yellow cloth, her hair in one long plait. The guard-women sit at a distance, uneasy. Far off, smoke rises from the battlefield; the city is strangely quiet.
**Narration:** "For many months Sita had lived as a prisoner in the Ashoka grove of Lanka. She had refused Ravana every day. Now the noise of the war had stopped, and the city was silent. She did not yet know what the silence meant."
**Sound:** birdsong, the soft drip of water, a far-off conch.

### 2. The news (25 sec)
**Visual:** Hanuman enters the grove, small and humble, and bows low before Sita with joined hands. He speaks. Sita looks at him, and slowly her face changes; she cannot speak.
**Narration:** "Then Hanuman came into the grove, the same messenger who had once brought her Rama's ring. He bowed before her and told her the news: Ravana had fallen, and Rama had won. For a long moment Sita could not say a word."
**Dialogue:** Hanuman: "Mother, Rama has won. Ravana is no more. Rama asks after you."
**Sound:** the music lifts gently, then holds still.

### 3. The joy (20 sec)
**Visual:** Sita's eyes fill. She smiles for the first time in the film. Hanuman glances at the guard-women who had threatened her and speaks; Sita lifts her hand gently to stop him, shaking her head.
**Narration:** "Her joy was too great for words. Hanuman offered to punish the guards who had frightened her for so long. But Sita would not allow it. They had only obeyed their king, she said. Who in this world has never done wrong?"
**Dialogue:** Sita: "Let them be, my son. Be kind, even to those who have done wrong."
**Sound:** a warm, simple melody.

### 4. The summons (20 sec)
**Visual:** Vibhishana, the new king of Lanka, enters the grove with attendants and bows deeply before Sita. Behind him, women of the palace carry fresh clothes, flowers and ornaments. He speaks Rama's message respectfully.
**Narration:** "Then Vibhishana, Ravana's brother and Rama's friend, came to bring her to Rama. Rama had asked that she come bathed and adorned. Kamban says Vibhishana bowed his crowned head at her feet, tender as young leaves."
**Dialogue:** Sita: "I would go to him just as I am. But if he wishes it, so be it."
**Sound:** soft bells.

### 5. The bath (20 sec)
**Visual:** Behind a screen of flowering trees, the palace women help Sita. We see only water poured from a golden pot, a fresh silk cloth unfolded, flowers woven into her hair. When she steps out she is radiant, calm and very still.
**Narration:** "So she bathed, and put on fresh silk, and the women set flowers in her hair. The months of sorrow fell from her like dust. She came out quiet and shining, ready to see her husband."
**Sound:** flowing water, a gentle flute.

### 6. The road (20 sec)
**Visual:** Sita rides in a covered palanquin through the streets toward Rama's camp, like a deer in the circle of the moon. Monkeys and demons crowd the way to catch a glimpse of her. At the camp's edge the palanquin stops; Sita steps down and walks the last steps on foot.
**Narration:** "They carried her in a palanquin through the city. Kamban says she sat in it like a deer within the circle of the moon. Monkeys and demons alike crowded the road to see her. At the last, she stepped down and walked."
**Sound:** the murmur of a great crowd, falling silent as she walks.

### 7. The meeting (25 sec)
**Visual:** Rama stands at the centre of the camp, bow in hand, Lakshmana and Sugriva beside him. Sita stops a little way off and looks at him. Close on her face: tears, and a deep stillness. Close on Rama's face: grave, unreadable. The crowd holds its breath. Hold on the two of them, a space still between them.
**Narration:** "There he stood, dark as a rain cloud, his bow in his hand. Sita looked upon his face, and all her months of waiting were in that one look. She had kept faith through everything. Now she stood before him, free. What would happen next, no one yet knew."
**Sound:** everything falls silent; a single sustained note.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri names Sita's release in six songs. Songs to show: 166 (Palani), the Lord who dammed the sea, cut down Ravana's crowns and took back Janaki, his very life; 252 (Thiruthani), the one who released Sita from prison; 435 (Thiruvannamalai); and 833 (Ettikudi), the discus-bearer who brought her out of captivity.
