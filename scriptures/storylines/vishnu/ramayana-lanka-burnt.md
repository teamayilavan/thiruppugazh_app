# The Tail of Fire (Hanuman Burns Lanka)

`ramayana-lanka-burnt` | Vishnu | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: bold, comic, blazing | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

Hanuman leaps the sea, finds Sita, gives her Rama's ring and takes her jewel back; captured by Indrajit, his tail is set alight and he burns Lanka.

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration. The destruction is shown as light and smoke only.

## Characters

- **Hanuman:** a monkey hero, strong, humble, quick.
- **Sita:** grieving under a tree.
- **Ravana, Indrajit.**

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it.

### 1. The leap (25 sec)
**Visual:** On the top of Mount Mahendra by the southern sea, Hanuman grows huge, presses down so hard the mountain trembles, and springs into the sky. He flies across the ocean like a comet, clouds parting, his shadow racing over the waves.
**Narration:** "No one knew where Sita was. Hanuman, the son of the wind, climbed a mountain by the sea. He grew bigger and bigger, pressed down with his feet, and leapt into the sky. Across the whole ocean he flew, toward the island of Lanka."
**Sound:** a deep rush of wind, a soaring melody.

### 2. The search (20 sec)
**Visual:** Night in Lanka: golden palaces, towers, lamps. Hanuman, now as small as a cat, slips over walls and along rooftops, peeping into halls and gardens. Every face he sees is not Sita's.
**Narration:** "He landed quietly and made himself small. By moonlight he searched the golden city, palace after palace, garden after garden. But Sita was nowhere to be found."
**Sound:** soft night sounds, a quick tiptoeing tune.

### 3. Sita (25 sec)
**Visual:** At last, in the Ashoka grove, Hanuman sees a woman under a tree, thin and pale, in a faded garment, surrounded by sleeping rakshasa guards. She is praying. Hanuman, hidden in the branches above, folds his hands, his eyes full of tears.
**Narration:** "At last he came to a quiet grove of Ashoka trees. Under one tree sat a woman, thin and pale, guarded by rakshasa women. She was praying, and she spoke Rama's name. Hanuman knew at once that he had found Sita."
**Sound:** the music slows; a gentle, sorrowful flute.

### 4. The ring (20 sec)
**Visual:** Hanuman softly sings Rama's story from the branches. Sita looks up, startled. He comes down, bows low and holds out a gold ring. She takes it, recognises it, and presses it to her heart.
**Narration:** "Softly, Hanuman began to tell the story of Rama. Then he came down and bowed, and gave her Rama's own ring. Sita knew it at once, and for the first time in many months she smiled."
**Dialogue:** Hanuman: "Mother, Rama will come for you. Do not lose hope."
**Sound:** a warm, bright chord.

### 5. The jewel (15 sec)
**Visual:** Sita takes a jewel from her hair and places it in Hanuman's hands. He wraps it carefully and bows.
**Narration:** "In return she gave him a jewel from her hair, to take back to Rama, so that he would know Hanuman had truly found her."
**Sound:** a single chime.

### 6. The capture (20 sec)
**Visual:** Hanuman, wanting to test the strength of Lanka, playfully tears up trees in the grove. Warriors rush in; Indrajit, Ravana's son, looses a glowing weapon that wraps Hanuman in ropes of light. Hanuman lets himself be taken, and is brought before Ravana on his throne.
**Narration:** "Before leaving, Hanuman wanted to see Lanka's strength. He broke down the grove, and the rakshasas came. At last Ravana's son Indrajit bound him with a mighty weapon. Hanuman let himself be taken to Ravana."
**Sound:** clashes, then a tense drum.

### 7. The fire (25 sec)
**Visual:** Ravana, angry, points. Rakshasas wrap Hanuman's tail in cloth, soak it in oil and set it alight. They parade him through the streets. In the grove, Sita hears, closes her eyes and prays to the god of fire. The flames on Hanuman's tail glow, but do not hurt him; he looks surprised, then grins.
**Narration:** "Ravana ordered that the monkey's tail be set on fire. They wrapped it in cloth and oil and lit it, and dragged him through the streets. But Sita prayed to the god of fire to be cool to him. And the fire did not burn him at all."
**Sound:** crackling flames, jeering crowds, then a soft, cool chime.

### 8. The burning (15 sec)
**Visual:** Hanuman slips his bonds, grows big, and leaps from roof to roof, his blazing tail touching each one. Flames and smoke rise over the golden towers; no person is shown harmed. At last he dips his tail in the sea and looks back, then sees Sita safe in her grove.
**Narration:** "Then Hanuman broke free. He leapt from roof to roof, and Lanka went up in flames. He put out his tail in the sea, saw that Sita was safe, and flew home."
**Sound:** a blazing, joyful rush of drums, then the waves.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Seven songs recall the burning of Lanka, crediting it to the Lord acting through Hanuman. Show song 1265 (a general song, Pothu Padalgal), song 835 (Enkan) and song 700 (Perungudi).

## Production notes

- **Comic and heroic.** Hanuman is lively.
- **No one is harmed on screen.**
