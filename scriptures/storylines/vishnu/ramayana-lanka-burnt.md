# The Tail of Fire (Hanuman Burns Lanka)

`ramayana-lanka-burnt` | Vishnu | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: bold, comic, blazing

## One-line story

Hanuman leaps the sea, finds Sita, gives her Rama's ring and takes her jewel back; captured by Indrajit, his tail is set alight and he burns Lanka.

## Characters

- **Hanuman:** a monkey hero, strong, humble, quick.
- **Sita:** grieving under a tree.
- **Ravana, Indrajit.**

## The storyline

### 1. The leap (25 sec)
Hanuman grows and leaps across the sea.
**Narration:** "Hanuman leapt across the sea."

### 2. The search (20 sec)
He searches Lanka by night.
**Narration:** "He searched the city of Lanka."

### 3. Sita (25 sec)
In a grove, a sad woman under a tree.
**Narration:** "He found Sita."

### 4. The ring (20 sec)
He drops Rama's ring in her lap.
**Narration:** "He gave her Rama's ring."

### 5. The jewel (15 sec)
She gives him her crest-jewel.
**Narration:** "She gave him her jewel."

### 6. The capture (20 sec)
Indrajit binds him; he is taken to Ravana.
**Narration:** "He allowed himself to be caught."

### 7. The fire (25 sec)
His tail is wrapped in cloth and set alight.
**Narration:** "They set his tail on fire."

### 8. The burning (15 sec)
He leaps across the rooftops; Lanka burns.
**Narration:** "And he burned Lanka."
