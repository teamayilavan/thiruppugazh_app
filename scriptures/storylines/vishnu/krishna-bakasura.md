# The Crane Demon (Bakasura)

`krishna-bakasura` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: eerie, then quick, then relieved

## One-line story

The demon Baka, in the form of a huge crane, swallows Krishna, who burns in its throat until it spits him out; Krishna then splits its beak.

## Characters

## The storyline

### 1. The riverbank (20 sec)
Cowherd boys play by a pond.
**Narration:** "The boys were playing by a pond."

### 2. The hill that moves (25 sec)
A white hill, which is a giant crane, opens its beak.
**Narration:** "A great crane stood among them."

### 3. The swallow (20 sec)
It swallows Krishna.
**Narration:** "And it swallowed Krishna."

### 4. The fear (20 sec)
The boys gasp.
**Narration:** "The boys cried out."

### 5. The burning (25 sec)
Inside, a golden glow; the crane shakes.
**Narration:** "He burned in its throat."

### 6. The spit (25 sec)
It spits him out.
**Narration:** "The crane spat him out."

### 7. The beak (15 sec)
Krishna takes the beak in his hands and splits it.
**Narration:** "And Krishna split its beak."
