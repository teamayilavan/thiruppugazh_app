# The Parijata Tree for Satyabhama

`krishna-parijata` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: loving, comic, bright | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

Krishna brings the heavenly parijata tree from Indra's garden for Satyabhama.

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Krishna and Satyabhama.**
- **Indra:** protective of his garden.
- **Garuda.**

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; bright and loving, a little comic in the chase. Where a scene has no voice, the music carries it.

### 1. The flower (25 sec)
**Visual:** Garuda glides up through golden clouds to Amaravati, the city of the gods. Krishna and Satyabhama step down. Krishna places a pair of shining earrings in the hands of Aditi, the mother of the gods. Indra and his queen bow and honour the two guests.
**Narration:** "After Naraka was defeated, Krishna flew on Garuda to heaven with his queen Satyabhama, to return the earrings that Naraka had stolen from Aditi, the mother of the gods. Indra and his queen welcomed them and honoured them both."
**Sound:** soft celestial chimes, wings, a warm welcome theme.

### 2. The wish (20 sec)
**Visual:** In Indra's garden stands the parijata tree, glowing, its blossoms pale gold and coral, bees circling it. Satyabhama stops, enchanted, and breathes the fragrance. She turns to Krishna with a look that says everything.
**Narration:** "In Indra's garden grew the parijata, the wishing tree of heaven, whose flowers never fade and whose fragrance fills the sky. Satyabhama saw it, and could not look away."
**Dialogue:** Satyabhama: "My lord, how lovely it would look in my garden at Dwaraka."
**Sound:** a shimmering harp; humming bees.

### 3. The flight (20 sec)
**Visual:** Krishna smiles. With one hand he lifts the whole tree, roots and all, as easily as a flower. He sets it on Garuda's broad back. Satyabhama laughs and climbs up beside it, holding a branch.
**Narration:** "Krishna only smiled. At his queen's wish he lifted the whole tree out of the ground, roots and all, and set it on Garuda's back. Then they rose into the sky, the tree swaying behind them."
**Sound:** a rush of wind, playful flute, the creak of branches.

### 4. The tree (20 sec)
**Visual:** Garuda flies low over the heavenly city. Gardeners run out shouting. Petals stream from the tree like a comet's tail. A cloud of bees follows, determined not to lose their tree.
**Narration:** "The gardeners of heaven came running, and petals streamed behind them like a trail of light. And a cloud of bees came flying after them all the way, unwilling to lose that fragrance."
**Sound:** shouts, buzzing, comic drums.

### 5. The challenge (25 sec)
**Visual:** Indra appears on his white elephant, with the gods behind him, and raises his thunderbolt to bar the way. Light and colour only: a flash of the thunderbolt, the turn of the discus, and the gods' weapons fall away like leaves. No one is hurt.
**Narration:** "Then Indra came after them with all the gods, to take back his tree. He had bowed at Krishna's feet when he needed help; now that his earrings were safe, he chose to fight. The Bhagavata sighs at the forgetfulness of the gods. But no one could stand against the Lord."
**Sound:** thunder, a ringing clash, then calm.

### 6. The yielding (20 sec)
**Visual:** Indra lowers the thunderbolt and bows his head. The gods step back. Satyabhama looks down from Garuda, half proud and half sorry. Krishna raises his hand in peace.
**Narration:** "Indra lowered his thunderbolt and stepped aside. Tradition tells many versions of this quarrel, and in all of them heaven gives way, and the tree goes down to earth."
**Sound:** a gentle resolving chord.

### 7. The planting (20 sec)
**Visual:** Dwaraka by the sea. In Satyabhama's palace garden, Krishna sets the tree into the earth. It glows; blossoms drift onto the paths; the bees settle into its branches. Satyabhama stands beneath it, radiant, and Krishna beside her.
**Narration:** "So the parijata was planted in Satyabhama's garden at Dwaraka. Its flowers made the whole palace beautiful, and the bees of heaven made their home in it. A tree of heaven, brought to earth for love."
**Sound:** the sea, the bees, the welcome theme returning softly.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri recalls the parijata in one line, praising Murugan as the nephew of the conch-bearing Lord who brought Indra's tree down to earth. Song to show: 885 (Thanjavur).
