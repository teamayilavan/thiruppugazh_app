# The Parijata Tree for Satyabhama

`krishna-parijata` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: loving, comic, bright

## One-line story

Krishna brings the heavenly parijata tree from Indra's garden for Satyabhama.

## The storyline

### 1. The flower (25 sec)
A heavenly flower, brought by Narada, is given to Rukmini; Satyabhama sulks.
**Narration:** "A heavenly flower came from the parijata tree."

### 2. The wish (20 sec)
Satyabhama asks for the whole tree.
**Narration:** "Satyabhama asked for the tree itself."

### 3. The flight (20 sec)
Krishna and Satyabhama fly to Indra's garden on Garuda.
**Narration:** "They flew to Indra's garden."

### 4. The tree (20 sec)
They uproot the tree, fragrant and shining.
**Narration:** "They took the tree."

### 5. The challenge (25 sec)
Indra comes in anger; a brief contest.
**Narration:** "Indra came to stop them."

### 6. The yielding (20 sec)
Indra smiles and gives in.
**Narration:** "And at last, Indra smiled."

### 7. The planting (20 sec)
The tree is planted in Dwaraka.
**Narration:** "The tree was planted at Dwaraka."
