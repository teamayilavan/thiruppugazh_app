# Dancing on the Serpent (Kaliya)

`krishna-kaliya` | Vishnu | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: dread, a dance, release

## One-line story

Krishna jumps into the poisoned Yamuna, dances on the hoods of the five-headed serpent Kaliya, and sends him away.

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Krishna:** a boy, fearless.
- **Kaliya:** a five-headed serpent, proud, then humbled.
- **Kaliya's wives:** who plead.
- **The cowherd boys and cattle.**

## The storyline

### 1. The poisoned river (25 sec)
The Yamuna smokes; birds fall silent; the boys cannot drink.
**Narration:** "A serpent had poisoned the river."

### 2. The leap (20 sec)
Krishna climbs a kadamba tree and dives in.
**Narration:** "Krishna dived into the water."

### 3. The coils (25 sec)
Kaliya rises and coils around him.
**Narration:** "The serpent coiled around him."

### 4. The villagers' fear (20 sec)
On the bank, the villagers cry.
**Narration:** "On the bank, they feared the worst."

### 5. The rise (20 sec)
Krishna grows vast and bursts free.
**Narration:** "He grew, and the coils fell away."

### 6. The dance (30 sec)
He leaps onto the five hoods and dances.
**Narration:** "He danced on the serpent's heads."

### 7. The prayer (15 sec)
The serpent's wives plead.
**Narration:** "The serpent's wives begged for mercy."

### 8. The sending (10 sec)
Krishna sends Kaliya away to the sea.
**Narration:** "He sent the serpent away."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 163, 620 and 881.

## Production notes

- **A great dance sequence.** Make the rhythm the heart.
- **Mercy.** The serpent leaves alive.
