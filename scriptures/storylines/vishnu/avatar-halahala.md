# The Poison from the Sea (Halahala)

`avatar-halahala` | Vishnu | running time 2:35 (7 scenes) | tone: alarm, then a lone act of courage

## One-line story

When the churned ocean throws up the kalakuta poison, all beings go to Shiva, who drinks it from the hollow of his palm.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| The churned ocean throws up the kalakuta poison | Bhagavata Purana 8.7.18-19 |
| All beings go to Shiva, who drinks it from the hollow of his palm | SB 8.7.18-19, 8.7.42; Valmiki Bala 45.19-25 |
| Songs 27, 46, 70 and others recall it | Thiruppugazh songs |

**Note:** the same event is told from Shiva's side in `deeds-poison`. This film is the Vishnu-set view: it ends with the churning going on.
**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **The gods and demons, all beings.**
- **Shiva:** calm.
- **Vishnu:** nearby, watchful.

## The storyline

### 1. The churning (20 sec)
The sea churns; the mountain turns.
**Narration:** "The churning went on."

### 2. The poison (25 sec)
A black cloud billows from the sea.
**Narration:** "And a poison rose."

### 3. The flight (25 sec)
All beings, gods, demons, creatures, run in terror.
**Narration:** "All beings fled."

### 4. The shelter (20 sec)
They arrive at Kailasa and fall at Shiva's feet.
**Narration:** "They went to Shiva."

### 5. The palm (25 sec)
Shiva cups his hand; the poison flows in.
**Narration:** "He took the poison in the hollow of his palm."

### 6. The drinking (25 sec)
He drinks it.
**Narration:** "And he drank it."

### 7. The churning goes on (20 sec)
The sea is calm; the churning begins again.
**Narration:** "And the churning went on."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 27, 46 and 70.
