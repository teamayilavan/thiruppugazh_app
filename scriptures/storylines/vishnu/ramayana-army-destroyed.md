# Rama and the Army of Lanka

`ramayana-army-destroyed` | Vishnu | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: vast, swift, relentless | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

Rama destroys the rakshasa troops of Ravana's army.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Rama destroys the rakshasa troops of Ravana's army | Valmiki Ramayana, Yuddha Kanda 93.7-34 |
| Songs 9, 27, 66 and others (19 songs) recall it | Thiruppugazh songs |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration. No bodies are shown.

## Characters

- **Rama and Lakshmana.**
- **The rakshasa army.**
- **Ravana** (a distant figure).

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it.

### 1. The host (25 sec)
**Visual:** Dawn over Lanka. The city gates open and the rakshasa army pours out: chariots, elephants, horses and foot soldiers, shown as a vast moving sea of dark banners and glinting spears. On a high tower, far away, Ravana watches, a small proud silhouette.
**Narration:** "After many days of war, Ravana sent out his army once more. They came through the gates of Lanka in a great flood: chariots and elephants, horsemen and foot soldiers, more than anyone could count. From his tower, Ravana watched them go."
**Sound:** deep drums, conch blasts, the roar of a crowd far off.

### 2. The line (20 sec)
**Visual:** On the plain, the monkey army waits, nervous; some monkeys look back over their shoulders. In front of them stands Rama, alone, calm, his bow in his hand. Lakshmana stands nearby.
**Narration:** "The monkeys saw that great host and their hearts shook. But Rama stood in front of them, calm and unafraid. He did not raise his voice. He only lifted his bow."
**Sound:** the drums stop; the wind across the plain.

### 3. The bow (20 sec)
**Visual:** Close on Rama's hands as he draws. His bow is golden. As he draws again and again, the bow begins to blur into a circle of golden light, like a firebrand whirled in the dark.
**Narration:** "Then he began to shoot. Valmiki says the rakshasas could not see Rama at all. They saw only his golden bow, turning like a wheel of fire, and the arrows coming from it."
**Sound:** the twang of the string, faster and faster, becoming one ringing tone.

### 4. The rain (30 sec)
**Visual:** Fully stylised. From the circle of light, streaks of gold fly out like rain. Where they pass, the dark banners of the host turn to drifting smoke and vanish. Confused rakshasa silhouettes turn this way and that, seeing many Ramas shimmering around them, each a figure of light. No bodies; only smoke and fading shapes.
**Narration:** "The arrows fell like rain. The rakshasas were confused. Some saw a thousand Ramas all around them. Some saw only one. Chariots, elephants and horses melted away like mist in the morning sun, and the great host grew thinner and thinner."
**Sound:** a rushing wind, a choir rising, the rumble of falling banners.

### 5. The silence (25 sec)
**Visual:** The light fades. The wide plain is empty under a pale sky, with only drifting smoke and fallen banners. Rama lowers his bow. He stands still, his face sorrowful, not proud.
**Narration:** "Then it was over. The great field was empty and quiet. Rama lowered his bow. He did not rejoice. A whole army had come out, and none of it remained. Even for the victor, it was a sorrowful sight."
**Sound:** silence, then one low note.

### 6. The wonder (20 sec)
**Visual:** Above the field, the gods and sages appear on clouds, folding their hands. The monkeys come slowly forward, amazed, and Lakshmana looks at his brother with wonder.
**Narration:** "The gods and sages in the sky praised him. The monkeys came forward, hardly believing what they saw. Kamban says that Rama and Lakshmana together had swept away an army so great that it was counted in floods."
**Sound:** soft bells, a gentle rising melody.

### 7. The pause (20 sec)
**Visual:** Evening. Far off on his tower, Ravana stands alone, looking at the empty plain. The camera returns to Rama, standing calm in the last light.
**Narration:** "In his tower, Ravana understood that his army was gone. Now only he was left to face Rama. And Arunagiri sings of Murugan as the nephew of the Lord who won this war, so that even the army of Lanka perished."
**Sound:** the opening drums return, slow and quiet; one conch far away.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Nineteen songs recall the destruction of Ravana's army, naming Murugan the nephew of the Lord who won so that even the army of Lanka perished. Show song 200 (Palani), song 524 (Thiruvenkatam) and song 750 (Vriddhachalam).
