# Across the Sky (The Abduction of Sita)

`ramayana-sita-abduction` | Vishnu | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: anguished, swift, ominous

## One-line story

After Lakshmana cuts off Surpanakha's nose and ears, Ravana lures Rama away with the golden deer and carries Sita through the sky.

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration. The mutilation is mentioned in narration only and not shown.

## Characters

- **Surpanakha:** a demoness, jealous.
- **Ravana:** ten-headed, disguised as a holy man at first.
- **Sita:** brave, in tears.
- **Jatayu** (a flash).

## The storyline

### 1. The demoness (25 sec)
Surpanakha comes to the hut and pursues Rama; Lakshmana drives her off, wounding her.
**Narration:** "A demoness was wounded by Lakshmana."

### 2. The brother's revenge (20 sec)
She runs to Ravana in Lanka.
**Narration:** "She ran to her brother Ravana."

### 3. The plan (20 sec)
Ravana's face hardens; he calls his chariot.
**Narration:** "He resolved to take Sita."

### 4. The beggar (25 sec)
At the empty hut, he comes in the guise of a holy man.
**Narration:** "He came as a holy man, begging."

### 5. The seizure (25 sec)
He shows his true form and seizes her.
**Narration:** "Then he showed his true form."

### 6. The sky (25 sec)
His chariot rises over the forest with Sita.
**Narration:** "He carried her across the sky."

### 7. The cry (20 sec)
Sita's cry echoes over the trees.
**Narration:** "And her cry echoed in the forest."

Song 272.
