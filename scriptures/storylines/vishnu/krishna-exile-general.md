# The Years in the Forest (The Pandavas' Exile)

`krishna-exile-general` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: weary, resolute, forest-green | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

The songs refer in general to the Bharata war, the Kauravas' fall and its aftermath; this film gives a short overview of the Pandavas' exile that led to it.

## Characters

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it.

### 1. The loss (25 sec)
**Visual:** The lamplit hall of Hastinapura. Dice roll across a board; a hand sweeps the winnings away. Yudhishthira, the eldest Pandava, sits with bowed head; his four brothers behind him, and Queen Draupadi, standing tall in her grief. Duryodhana and his uncle Shakuni smile across the board.
**Narration:** "It began with a game of dice. Yudhishthira, the eldest of the five Pandava brothers, was challenged to play, and he could not refuse. One throw after another, he lost his wealth, his kingdom, his brothers, himself, and even Queen Draupadi."
**Sound:** the rattle of dice; a crowd's murmur growing louder.

### 2. The leaving (20 sec)
**Visual:** A city gate at dawn. The five brothers in bark garments, bows on their backs, walk out with Draupadi. Their old mother Kunti stays behind in Vidura's care, weeping at the gate. People of the city follow a little way, then stop.
**Narration:** "The terms were hard. The Pandavas would live twelve years in the forest, and a thirteenth year in hiding. If they were found in that year, it would all begin again. So the five brothers and Draupadi left the city in bark clothes, and the people wept."
**Sound:** a slow drum, a mother's cry, footsteps fading.

### 3. The forest (25 sec)
**Visual:** Seasons turn over the great forests. The brothers build huts of leaves, gather fruit, hunt; sages come and sit with them under the trees and tell old stories. Bhima carries firewood; Arjuna practises with his bow; Yudhishthira listens to the sages. Draupadi tends the fire. Once, a chariot arrives through the trees: Krishna, come to visit his friends.
**Narration:** "For twelve years they lived in the forest. They slept on the ground and ate what the forest gave. Sages came to sit with them and tell them the old stories of patience and dharma. And Krishna, their friend and kinsman, came to see them in the forest, and comforted them."
**Sound:** the forest through the seasons: rain, cicadas, wind in the dry leaves.

### 4. The hidden year (25 sec)
**Visual:** A king's court in another country. Among the servants, if we look closely, are the brothers in disguise: a man who teaches the king dice, a cook in the kitchen, a dance teacher, a horse-keeper, a cowherd, and Draupadi as a maid of the queen. A calendar of moons crosses the screen.
**Narration:** "In the thirteenth year they had to vanish. They went in disguise into the court of King Virata, as servants. A teacher of dice, a cook, a horse-keeper, a cowherd, a teacher of dance, and a lady's maid. They counted every day, and nobody found them."
**Sound:** court music, muffled, as if heard from behind a wall; a slow clock-like drum.

### 5. The return (20 sec)
**Visual:** The disguises fall away. The five brothers and Draupadi stand in royal dress again, thin and strong. They send a messenger toward Hastinapura.
**Narration:** "At last the thirteen years were over. The Pandavas had kept every word of the bargain. Now they asked for their kingdom back."
**Sound:** a conch, clear and hopeful.

### 6. The refusal (20 sec)
**Visual:** In Hastinapura, Duryodhana turns his back. Krishna goes to the court as envoy, and comes back alone along a long road. The sky darkens.
**Narration:** "But Duryodhana would not give it. Even when Krishna himself went to ask for peace, he refused. Thirteen years of patience had come to nothing."
**Sound:** the conch fades; wind.

### 7. The road to war (15 sec)
**Visual:** Two great armies gather on the plain of Kurukshetra, banners rising like a forest. Krishna takes the reins of Arjuna's chariot. The camera rises above the field.
**Narration:** "And so the two families gathered on the field of Kurukshetra, and the great war of the Bharatas began, with Krishna as Arjuna's charioteer."
**Sound:** drums and conches from both sides, rising.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Song 785 (Thirukkadavur) compresses the whole sequence into a single breath: the Kuru princes went to the forest as fate decreed, lived in disguise counting the days, and the great hero Mayan brought about the Bharata war. Murugan is his nephew.
