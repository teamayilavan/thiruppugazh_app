# The Uncle Who Feared a Prophecy (Kamsa's Malice)

`krishna-kamsa-malice` | Vishnu | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: ominous, tragic, then hopeful

## One-line story

Kamsa, Krishna's maternal uncle, persecutes his sister Devaki's newborn children.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Kamsa persecutes his sister Devaki's newborn children | Bhagavata Purana 10.4.1-5 |
| Songs 181 and 1274 recall it | Thiruppugazh songs |
| The prophecy that the eighth child will end him, and Krishna's birth in the prison, are the widely told frame | Bhagavata Purana, canto 10 (traditional source; not read verse by verse here) |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration. The persecution is shown by shadow and sound; no child is seen harmed.

## Characters

- **Kamsa:** a proud king, afraid.
- **Devaki:** his sister, gentle, steadfast.
- **Vasudeva:** her husband, quiet strength.

## The storyline

### 1. The wedding (25 sec)
Kamsa drives his sister Devaki's chariot at her wedding, proud and loving.
**Narration:** "Kamsa drove his sister's wedding chariot himself."

### 2. The voice (20 sec)
A voice from the sky: "The eighth child of Devaki will end you."
**Narration:** "Then a voice warned him."

### 3. The prison (25 sec)
He imprisons Devaki and Vasudeva.
**Narration:** "He shut his sister in prison."

### 4. The first child (25 sec)
A cry in the dark; Kamsa appears at the door.
**Narration:** "Each child that was born, he took away."

### 5. The grief (20 sec)
Devaki and Vasudeva hold each other.
**Narration:** "They bore it, and prayed."

### 6. The waiting (25 sec)
Years pass, seen in the passing of lamps.
**Narration:** "And they waited for the eighth."

### 7. The hope (20 sec)
A small light grows in the prison.
**Narration:** "And hope came."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 181 and 1274.

## Production notes

- **No harm to a child on screen.** Use sound and shadow only.
- **Kamsa's fear** is what drives him; give him weight, not caricature.
