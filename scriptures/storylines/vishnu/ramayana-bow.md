# Shiva's Bow Is Broken (Mithila)

`ramayana-bow` | Vishnu | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: expectant, mighty, then comic-humble

## One-line story

At Mithila King Janaka offers Sita to whoever strings Shiva's bow; Rama lifts and breaks it, the weddings follow, and Parashurama then challenges Rama and is humbled.

## Characters

- **Rama and Lakshmana.**
- **Janaka:** a wise king.
- **Sita:** a young princess, grave and calm.
- **Parashurama:** an axe-wielding sage.

## The storyline

### 1. The bow (25 sec)
A huge bow, on a wheeled cart, is drawn into Janaka's hall by hundreds of men.
**Narration:** "In Mithila lay the great bow of Shiva."

### 2. The condition (20 sec)
Janaka: "Whoever strings it shall marry my daughter."
**Narration:** "The king offered his daughter to anyone who could string it."

### 3. The kings fail (20 sec)
Princes try and fail.
**Narration:** "Kings tried, and failed."

### 4. Rama steps up (25 sec)
Rama rises, lifts the bow, and bends it.
**Narration:** "Rama lifted it."

### 5. The crack (20 sec)
The bow snaps with a thunderclap.
**Narration:** "And it broke."

### 6. The weddings (20 sec)
Sita garlands Rama.
**Narration:** "Sita chose him."

### 7. Parashurama (20 sec)
A fierce sage storms in with an axe and a second bow.
**Narration:** "Then Parashurama came, and challenged him."

### 8. The humbling (15 sec)
Rama strings the second bow with ease; the sage bows.
**Narration:** "And Parashurama was humbled."

Songs 649, 799 and 968.
