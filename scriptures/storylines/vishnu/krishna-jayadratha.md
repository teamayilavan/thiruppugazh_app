# The Sun Hidden (Jayadratha)

`krishna-jayadratha` | Vishnu | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: desperate, then a trick, then a strike

## One-line story

On the fourteenth day Krishna hides the sun (tradition: with the Sudarshana discus), Jayadratha steps out thinking the day is done, and Arjuna kills him.

## The storyline

### 1. The vow (25 sec)
Arjuna swears to kill Jayadratha before sunset, or end his own life.
**Narration:** "Arjuna swore to kill Jayadratha before sunset."

### 2. The hiding (20 sec)
Jayadratha is hidden deep in the army.
**Narration:** "But Jayadratha was hidden behind his guards."

### 3. The day passes (25 sec)
The sun sinks; Arjuna's face tightens.
**Narration:** "The day was running out."

### 4. The darkness (25 sec)
Krishna hides the sun; it seems to set.
**Narration:** "Krishna made the sun seem to set."

### 5. The mistake (20 sec)
Jayadratha steps out, relieved.
**Narration:** "Jayadratha stepped out."

### 6. The light (25 sec)
The sun reappears; Krishna says: "Now."
**Narration:** "And the sun came back."

### 7. The arrow (20 sec)
Arjuna's arrow flies.
**Narration:** "And Arjuna shot."
