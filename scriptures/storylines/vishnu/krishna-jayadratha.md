# The Sun Hidden (Jayadratha)

`krishna-jayadratha` | Vishnu | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: desperate, then a trick, then a strike | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

On the fourteenth day Krishna hides the sun (tradition: with the Sudarshana discus), Jayadratha steps out thinking the day is done, and Arjuna kills him.

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Arjuna:** sworn to a vow.
- **Krishna.**
- **Jayadratha:** a king hidden behind guards.

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it.

### 1. The vow (25 sec)
**Visual:** Night in the Pandava camp, the thirteenth day of the war. Torches; quiet grief. Arjuna returns from battle to find his young son Abhimanyu gone. He stands in the torchlight, then lifts his bow above his head and speaks his vow before everyone. Krishna stands beside him, grave.
**Narration:** "On the thirteenth day of the war, Arjuna's young son Abhimanyu fell, fighting alone. King Jayadratha had held the gate of the battle formation shut, so that no one could reach the boy in time. That night Arjuna made a terrible vow."
**Dialogue:** Arjuna: "Before the sun sets tomorrow, Jayadratha will fall. If he does not, I will enter the fire myself."
**Sound:** torches crackling; a hush; one heavy drum.

### 2. The hiding (20 sec)
**Visual:** Dawn in the Kaurava camp. Drona arranges a vast formation, many miles deep. At its very back, behind rings of chariots, elephants and the greatest warriors, sits Jayadratha, small and afraid.
**Narration:** "The Kauravas heard of the vow. If they could keep Jayadratha safe for just one day, Arjuna would die by his own word. So they hid him at the very back of their army, behind ring after ring of chariots, elephants and great warriors."
**Sound:** the clank of armour, marching feet.

### 3. The day passes (25 sec)
**Visual:** A sun-dial shadow sweeps across the field as Krishna drives Arjuna's chariot deeper and deeper into the Kaurava ranks. The horses tire; Krishna waters them in the middle of the battle. The sun slides lower; the sky turns orange. Jayadratha is still far away.
**Narration:** "All day long Krishna drove Arjuna's chariot through the Kaurava army, deeper and deeper. Arjuna fought without rest. But the army was vast, and the hours ran out. The sun sank lower and lower, and Jayadratha was still far away."
**Sound:** battle noise; a ticking, quickening drum.

### 4. The darkness (25 sec)
**Visual:** Krishna looks at the sun, then raises his hand. In the Tamil tradition, his shining discus rises into the sky and covers the sun. Darkness falls over the field like dusk. Draw it as a soft eclipse, the discus a ring of light.
**Narration:** "Then, the old songs say, Krishna did something wonderful. He raised his discus, Sudarshana, and set it before the sun. A shadow fell across the field, as if the sun had already set. This is how the Tamil tradition tells it, and the songs of Arunagiri too."
**Sound:** the battle noise drops away; a low, hollow tone.

### 5. The mistake (20 sec)
**Visual:** In the Kaurava ranks, warriors lower their weapons and cheer. Jayadratha, relieved, steps out from behind his guards and looks up at the dark sky, laughing. The rings of chariots loosen.
**Narration:** "The Kauravas saw the darkness and shouted with joy. The day was over, they thought, and Arjuna had failed. Jayadratha came out from behind his guards and looked up at the sky, smiling."
**Sound:** a roar of cheering, then laughter.

### 6. The light (25 sec)
**Visual:** Krishna lowers his hand; the discus slides away, and the sun is still there, low and golden above the horizon. The cheering stops. Krishna turns to Arjuna and points.
**Narration:** "Then Krishna drew back the darkness, and there was the sun, still shining, low in the sky. The day was not over yet. Krishna turned to Arjuna and spoke quickly."
**Dialogue:** Krishna: "Dhananjaya, now! There he is. Do not wait."
**Sound:** a bright swell of music, the cheering cut off.

### 7. The arrow (20 sec)
**Visual:** Arjuna draws his bow. One arrow flies, a streak of light across the golden field. A flash; Jayadratha's figure is gone from the screen. The sun sets. Arjuna lowers his bow; Krishna puts a hand on his shoulder.
**Narration:** "Arjuna drew his bow and let fly a single arrow, swift as light. Jayadratha fell. And then, at last, the sun went down. The vow was kept."
**Sound:** one arrow's whistle, a flash of sound, then silence and evening birds.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri tells the Tamil form of the story, in which Krishna hides the sun with his discus. Songs 568 and 574 (Viralimalai) and 902 (Vayalur) are the ones to show. Murugan is the nephew of the Lord who covered the sun to keep his devotee's vow.
