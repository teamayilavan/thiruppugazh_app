# Three Steps (Vamana and Mahabali)

`avatar-vamana` | Vishnu | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: humble, humorous, then vast | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

The dwarf Vamana asks King Bali for three paces of land, then grows to measure the worlds with two steps and sends Bali to Sutala with the third.

**Left out of the film:** Garuda binding Bali with Varuna's ropes (8.21.26-27); the book narrative mentions it.
**Added for the screen:** the visuals, the spoken lines (plain paraphrases of 8.18.32, 8.19.16-19, 8.19.30-33, 8.21.29 and 8.22.2) and the wording of the narration.

## Characters

- **Vamana:** a small Brahmin boy with an umbrella, calm.
- **Bali:** a generous demon king, majestic and generous.
- **Shukracharya:** Bali's teacher (a warning voice).

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it.

### 1. The great king (20 sec)
**Visual:** A great sacrifice on the north bank of the Narmada. Fires blaze, priests chant. King Bali, tall and generous-faced, presides, the ruler of all three worlds. Beggars and sages leave his hall with full hands.
**Narration:** "Bali, king of the demons and grandson of Prahlada, had won all three worlds. He was mighty, but he was also generous, and no one who came to him asking ever went away empty-handed. Now he was holding a great sacrifice by the river Narmada."
**Sound:** chanting, crackling fire, a stately theme for Bali.

### 2. The dwarf (20 sec)
**Visual:** Through the gate walks a tiny boy-priest with a sacred thread, a deerskin, a little umbrella, a staff and a water pot, so bright that the priests shade their eyes. Bali hurries to welcome him, seats him, and washes his small feet.
**Narration:** "Into the hall walked a little boy, a young student priest, carrying an umbrella and a water pot. He shone so brightly that the priests wondered whether the sun himself had come. Bali welcomed him with joy, and washed his feet."
**Dialogue:** Bali: "Welcome, young one! Ask for anything you like: cows, gold, a house, even land."
**Sound:** a light, skipping melody for the dwarf.

### 3. The request (20 sec)
**Visual:** The dwarf looks up, perfectly serious, and holds up three small fingers. Bali laughs kindly; the court smiles.
**Dialogue:** Vamana: "Only three paces of land, measured by my own steps."
**Dialogue:** Bali: "Only three steps? You are a clever boy, but not a wise one. Ask for more!"
**Narration:** "But the boy said that one who is not content with a little will never be content with the whole world."
**Sound:** gentle laughter, a playful pluck of strings.

### 4. The warning (20 sec)
**Visual:** Bali's teacher Shukracharya steps forward, grim, and speaks into the king's ear. Bali listens, is silent for a while, then shakes his head. His queen brings a golden water pot.
**Dialogue:** Shukracharya: "This is no boy. This is Vishnu himself, and with his steps he will take everything you have."
**Narration:** "Bali was silent for a moment. Then he said: I am the grandson of Prahlada. I have given my word, and I will not take it back."
**Sound:** the music darkens, then steadies on Bali's theme.

### 5. The growth (25 sec)
**Visual:** Bali pours the water to seal the gift. The dwarf begins to grow: past the priests, past the fires, past the treetops, past the clouds, until the hall is a speck at his feet. Bali, looking up, sees whole worlds within the Lord's vast body.
**Narration:** "Bali poured the water and sealed the gift, and the gods in heaven sang his praise. Then the little boy began to grow. He grew above the hall, above the hills, above the clouds, until his body filled the universe, and Bali could see all the worlds within him."
**Sound:** a vast, swelling crescendo, deep drums.

### 6. The two steps (25 sec)
**Visual:** One giant foot comes down and covers the whole earth. The second sweeps up through the heavens, beyond the stars, to the highest world. There Brahma washes the foot with water from his pot, and the water flows down through the sky as a shining river.
**Narration:** "With one step he covered the earth. With the second he covered the heavens, and his foot rose beyond the highest worlds. There Brahma washed that foot, and the water, the Bhagavata says, flowed down as the holy river Ganga. Now there was no place left for a third step."
**Sound:** two enormous footfalls; then a shimmering, flowing sound for the river.

### 7. The third (25 sec)
**Visual:** The Lord, vast and gentle, looks down at Bali. Bali kneels and bows his head. The Lord's foot rests lightly upon it.
**Dialogue:** Vamana: "You promised me three steps. I have taken all with two. Where shall I put the third?"
**Dialogue:** Bali: "My word must not be false. Place your third step on my head."
**Narration:** "Bali feared nothing, not the loss of his kingdom, but only the breaking of his word."
**Sound:** silence, then a single deep, tender chord.

### 8. Sutala (10 sec)
**Visual:** Bali and his family descend to a beautiful, peaceful realm, Sutala. At its gate stands the Lord, as its guardian.
**Narration:** "The Lord sent Bali to live in Sutala, a world even the gods desire, and promised to guard its gate himself."
**Sound:** Bali's theme, now calm and full.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri recalls this story in eighteen songs, almost always as "the one who measured the worlds". Songs to show: 134 (Palani), the Lord who was glad to measure the world; 970 (Sri Purushamangai), who measured the great earth; and 367 (Thiruvannamalai), the small one who measured all the worlds, whom no one can measure.

## Production notes

- **Bali is noble.** Treat him with respect.
- **Humour in the dwarf's first scenes;** awe in the last.
