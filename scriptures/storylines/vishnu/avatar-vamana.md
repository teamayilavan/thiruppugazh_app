# Three Steps (Vamana and Mahabali)

`avatar-vamana` | Vishnu | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: humble, humorous, then vast

## One-line story

The dwarf Vamana asks King Bali for three paces of land, then grows to measure the worlds with two steps and sends Bali to Sutala with the third.

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Vamana:** a small Brahmin boy with an umbrella, calm.
- **Bali:** a generous demon king, majestic and generous.
- **Shukracharya:** Bali's teacher (a warning voice).

## The storyline

### 1. The great king (20 sec)
King Bali, in a feast of giving, offers any gift to any asker.
**Narration:** "Bali was a great king, and he gave whatever was asked."

### 2. The dwarf (20 sec)
A small boy with an umbrella walks into the hall.
**Narration:** "A dwarf came to ask a gift."

### 3. The request (20 sec)
"Three paces of land, measured with my own feet."
**Narration:** "He asked for three paces of land."

### 4. The warning (20 sec)
The teacher warns Bali; Bali smiles and pours the water of gift.
**Narration:** "Bali's teacher warned him, but he gave his word."

### 5. The growth (25 sec)
The dwarf begins to grow, to the size of the sky.
**Narration:** "And the dwarf grew."

### 6. The two steps (25 sec)
One foot covers the earth; the other the heavens.
**Narration:** "Two steps measured the earth and the sky."

### 7. The third (25 sec)
"Where shall I put the third?" Bali bows his head.
**Narration:** "For the third step, Bali offered his own head."

### 8. Sutala (10 sec)
The foot rests; Bali goes down to the world of Sutala, smiling.
**Narration:** "And Bali was sent to Sutala, in peace."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 134, 248 and 289 among eighteen.

## Production notes

- **Bali is noble.** Treat him with respect.
- **Humour in the dwarf's first scenes;** awe in the last.
