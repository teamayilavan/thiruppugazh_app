# The Eighteen Days (The Bharata War)

`krishna-war-general` | Vishnu | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: grave, vast, sombre

## One-line story

The songs refer in general to the Bharata war, the Kauravas' fall and its aftermath; this film gives a short overview of the eighteen days at Kurukshetra.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| General references to the Bharata war, the Kauravas' fall and its aftermath | Thiruppugazh songs 339, 541, 573 and others |
| The war at Kurukshetra | Mahabharata (traditional source; not read verse by verse here) |

**Honesty note:** the research marks this entry "General references". The film is an overview; its episodes are told in full in the neighbouring films (`krishna-gita-chariot`, `krishna-bhishma-shikhandi`, `krishna-jayadratha`, `krishna-duryodhana-fall`).
**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Krishna, Arjuna, the Pandavas, the Kauravas, the armies.**

## The storyline

### 1. The armies (25 sec)
Two vast armies on the field.
**Narration:** "Two great armies met at Kurukshetra."

### 2. The Gita (20 sec)
Krishna and Arjuna at the chariot, still.
**Narration:** "Krishna spoke the Gita."

### 3. The eighteen days (25 sec)
Days pass, shown as a sun crossing the sky eighteen times.
**Narration:** "The war lasted eighteen days."

### 4. The fall of Bhishma (20 sec)
A bed of arrows.
**Narration:** "Bhishma fell."

### 5. The sun hidden (20 sec)
The sun darkens.
**Narration:** "The sun was hidden for Jayadratha."

### 6. The mace (20 sec)
Two maces meet.
**Narration:** "And Duryodhana fell."

### 7. The silence (30 sec)
The empty field.
**Narration:** "Of the great armies, almost no one remained."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 339, 541 and 573.

## Production notes

- **An overview and a lament,** not a glory.
- **No scenes of combat.** Use light and silence.
