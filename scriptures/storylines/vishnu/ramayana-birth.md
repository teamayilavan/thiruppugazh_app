# "Come Here, My King" (The Child Rama)

`ramayana-birth` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: tender, domestic, glowing

## One-line story

Kausalya calls the infant Rama to her ("come here, king"); the Thiruppugazh image is of the child Vishnu as a mother's baby.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Kausalya calls the infant Rama to her ("come here, king") | Thiruppugazh song 68; Kamba, Thiru Avatara Padalam (traditional source; not read verse by verse here) |
| The Thiruppugazh image is of the child Vishnu as a mother's baby | research set `ramayana-birth` |

**Honesty note:** the research gives the image only. The film follows it and keeps the birth of Rama (the sacrifice, the divine pudding) out, to avoid adding what was not read.
**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Baby Rama:** dark, bright-eyed.
- **Kausalya:** a loving mother.
- **Dasaratha** (a glimpse).

## The storyline

### 1. Ayodhya (25 sec)
A bright city at dawn; flags and drums.
**Narration:** "In Ayodhya, a prince was born."

### 2. The nursery (20 sec)
A sunlit room; a mother with a baby.
**Narration:** "His mother was Kausalya."

### 3. The first steps (25 sec)
The baby toddles across the floor.
**Narration:** "He took his first steps."

### 4. The call (25 sec)
Kausalya opens her arms: "Come here, my king."
**Narration:** "And she called him: 'Come here, my king.'"

### 5. The embrace (20 sec)
He runs to her; she lifts him.
**Narration:** "He ran into her arms."

### 6. The glimpse (25 sec)
Behind the baby, a faint blue Vishnu with the discus.
**Narration:** "The Lord of all became a mother's baby."

### 7. The song (10 sec)
A Thiruppugazh line floats over.
**Narration:** "And the poet remembered it."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Song 68.
