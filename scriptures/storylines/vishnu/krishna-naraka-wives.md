# Naraka and the Sixteen Thousand

`krishna-naraka-wives` | Vishnu | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: grim, then liberating

## One-line story

Krishna kills Narakasura and marries the princesses he had imprisoned.

**Honesty note:** the research gives the story in one line. The film treats the "marriage" as the tradition does: Krishna accepts the women who, once held by Naraka, would have been outcast, and gives them honour and a home.
**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Narakasura:** a violent demon king in a fortified city.
- **Krishna and Satyabhama.**
- **The princesses:** frightened, then free.

## The storyline

### 1. The fortress (25 sec)
A walled city of iron; Naraka laughs.
**Narration:** "Narakasura ruled a fortress, and terrorised the worlds."

### 2. The captives (20 sec)
Princesses sit behind bars in a long hall.
**Narration:** "He had imprisoned many princesses."

### 3. The siege (25 sec)
Krishna, with Satyabhama, flies on Garuda to the city.
**Narration:** "Krishna came with Satyabhama."

### 4. The fight (25 sec)
A flash of the discus, and the demon falls.
**Narration:** "He ended Naraka."

### 5. The freedom (25 sec)
Doors open; the princesses step out.
**Narration:** "The princesses were free."

### 6. The shame (20 sec)
They fear to return, outcast by the world.
**Narration:** "But they feared to go home."

### 7. The honour (25 sec)
Krishna takes their hands and offers them his home.
**Narration:** "Krishna gave them his name, and a home."
