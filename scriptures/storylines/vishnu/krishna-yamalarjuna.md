# The Twin Trees (Yamalarjuna)

`krishna-yamalarjuna` | Vishnu | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: quiet, then a crash, then release

## One-line story

Dragging the mortar, Krishna passes between two arjuna trees; they break and release Kubera's sons Nalakuvara and Manigriva from Narada's curse.

## The storyline

### 1. The mortar (20 sec)
Krishna, tied to a mortar, drags it across the yard.
**Narration:** "Krishna dragged the mortar behind him."

### 2. The trees (20 sec)
Two tall arjuna trees stand side by side.
**Narration:** "There were two great trees."

### 3. The squeeze (25 sec)
He crawls between them; the mortar catches across them.
**Narration:** "He passed between them, and the mortar stuck."

### 4. The pull (25 sec)
He pulls; the trees groan.
**Narration:** "He pulled."

### 5. The crash (20 sec)
The trees fall with a great crack.
**Narration:** "And the trees broke."

### 6. The youths (30 sec)
From the fallen trunks, two radiant young men step out, bowing.
**Narration:** "Two sons of Kubera stepped out, free of an old curse."

### 7. The thanks (20 sec)
They bow and rise into the sky.
**Narration:** "They thanked him, and went home."
