# The Enchantress (Mohini and the Nectar)

`avatar-mohini` | Vishnu | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: graceful, witty, then decisive

## One-line story

The demons snatch the jug of nectar, and Vishnu takes the form of the beautiful woman Mohini; they are charmed, and she smiles and speaks to them.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| The demons snatch the jug of nectar | Bhagavata Purana 8.8 |
| Vishnu takes the form of Mohini; the demons are charmed by her beauty; she smiles and speaks to them | SB 8.9.2, 8.9.8-9 |
| Songs 46, 169 and 367 recall it | Thiruppugazh songs |

**Honesty note:** the research gives the charm and the speech; the continuation (the nectar given to the gods) is the widely known tradition and is not read verse by verse in this research. The film's last scene follows it and is marked in the narration.
**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Mohini:** a graceful, radiant woman, calm in the eyes.
- **The demons:** quarrelling over the jug.
- **The gods.**

## The storyline

### 1. The quarrel (25 sec)
The demons argue over the jug.
**Narration:** "The demons had the nectar, and could not agree who should drink first."

### 2. The vision (25 sec)
A woman of dazzling beauty walks through the crowd.
**Narration:** "Then a woman of wonderful beauty appeared."

### 3. The charm (20 sec)
The demons stop; their anger melts.
**Narration:** "They stopped and stared."

### 4. The smile (20 sec)
She smiles and speaks to them softly.
**Narration:** "She smiled, and spoke to them."

### 5. The jug (25 sec)
They hand her the jug.
**Narration:** "And they gave her the jug."

### 6. The serving (25 sec)
She walks to the gods and serves them.
**Narration:** "The tradition says she served the gods."

### 7. The form (20 sec)
She smiles; for a moment, Vishnu's face glimmers.
**Narration:** "It was Vishnu, all along."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 46, 169 and 367.

## Production notes

- **Tasteful.** Mohini is graceful, not sensual.
- **No humiliation of the demons.** They are charmed, not mocked.
