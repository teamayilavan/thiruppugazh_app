# The Lion from the Pillar (Narasimha and Prahlada)

`avatar-narasimha` | Vishnu | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: dread, then the roar, then peace

## One-line story

The demon king Hiranyakashipu has a boon that he cannot be killed by day or night, indoors or outdoors, by man or beast; Vishnu appears from a pillar as a man-lion at dusk and tears him open, saving his devoted son Prahlada.

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration. The killing is shown only as a shadow and a flash.

## Characters

- **Hiranyakashipu:** a proud king with a golden mace.
- **Prahlada:** a small, serene boy.
- **Narasimha:** man-lion, golden, mane blazing.
- **The gods:** watching from the sky.

## The storyline

### 1. The king's pride (20 sec)
A great hall; the king on a golden throne claims to be the only god.
**Narration:** "The demon king Hiranyakashipu claimed to be a god."

### 2. The boy (20 sec)
His small son Prahlada refuses and sings the name of Vishnu.
**Narration:** "But his own son sang the name of Vishnu."

### 3. The tests (20 sec)
The king tries to harm the boy: the boy remains unharmed.
**Narration:** "He could not harm the boy."

### 4. The question (20 sec)
The king, enraged: "Where is your Lord? In this pillar?" He strikes a pillar.
**Narration:** "He asked: 'Is your Lord in this pillar?' And struck it."

### 5. The pillar splits (25 sec)
The pillar cracks; a roar; a golden light.
**Narration:** "And the pillar split."

### 6. The man-lion (25 sec)
Narasimha steps out, half man, half lion.
**Narration:** "Out came the man-lion."

### 7. The dusk (25 sec)
At the threshold, in the dusk, a flash of claws; the king falls.
**Narration:** "At dusk, on the threshold, he ended the king."

### 8. The calm (10 sec)
Prahlada bows; the lion's gaze softens.
**Narration:** "And the child was safe."

## Production notes

- **Do not frighten small children.** The killing is a shadow.
- **Prahlada's calm is the film's centre.**
- **The Sarabha film `deeds-sarabha` follows;** keep the lion's design consistent.
