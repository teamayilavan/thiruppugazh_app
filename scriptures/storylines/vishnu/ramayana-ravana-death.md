# The Brahma Weapon (The End of Ravana)

`ramayana-ravana-death` | Vishnu | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: solemn, final, still

## One-line story

Rama fits the Brahma weapon to his bow; it tears Ravana's heart and he falls from his chariot.

## The storyline

### 1. The last attempt (25 sec)
Rama and Ravana, exhausted, face each other.
**Narration:** "The battle had gone on a long time."

### 2. The weapon (25 sec)
Rama takes up the Brahma weapon, blazing.
**Narration:** "Rama took up the Brahma weapon."

### 3. The blessing (20 sec)
The gods bless it from the sky.
**Narration:** "The gods blessed it."

### 4. The loosing (25 sec)
He looses it; it flies, a line of light.
**Narration:** "He loosed it."

### 5. The strike (25 sec)
It strikes Ravana; light pours through him.
**Narration:** "It pierced Ravana's heart."

### 6. The fall (20 sec)
He falls from his chariot.
**Narration:** "He fell from his chariot."

### 7. The stillness (20 sec)
The sky is silent; the gods scatter flowers.
**Narration:** "And the war was over."
