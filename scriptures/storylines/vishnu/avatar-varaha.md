# The Boar Who Lifted the Earth (Varaha)

`avatar-varaha` | Vishnu | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: urgent, mighty, then gentle

## One-line story

The demon Hiranyaksha drags the earth to the bottom of the cosmic ocean; Vishnu becomes a boar, kills the demon, and lifts the earth on his tusks.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Vishnu lifts the earth from the cosmic ocean on his tusks and kills the demon Hiranyaksha | Research set `avatar-varaha`; Bhagavata Purana 3.13-3.19 (traditional source, not read verse by verse here) |
| Songs 233 and 545 recall it | Thiruppugazh songs |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Hiranyaksha:** a gold-eyed demon, huge.
- **Varaha:** a colossal boar, blue-black, with golden tusks.
- **Bhumi (Earth):** a goddess, serene.
- **Brahma and the gods.**

## The storyline

### 1. The dark ocean (20 sec)
The earth is gone; the sky is an ocean of darkness.
**Narration:** "A demon dragged the earth to the bottom of the ocean."

### 2. The prayer (20 sec)
Brahma sneezes, and a tiny boar leaps from his nose.
**Narration:** "Brahma prayed, and a tiny boar appeared."

### 3. The growth (25 sec)
The boar swells until it is as large as a mountain.
**Narration:** "It grew until it filled the sky."

### 4. The dive (20 sec)
He plunges into the ocean, following the scent of the earth.
**Narration:** "He dived."

### 5. The demon (25 sec)
Hiranyaksha rises from the deep, roaring; the two clash.
**Narration:** "The demon rose to stop him."

### 6. The end (20 sec)
The demon falls in a flash of light.
**Narration:** "And the demon fell."

### 7. The lifting (25 sec)
Vishnu rises, the earth balanced on his tusks, calm.
**Narration:** "He lifted the earth on his tusks."

### 8. The setting (10 sec)
He sets her on the waters; she shines.
**Narration:** "And set her in her place."

Songs 233 and 545.
