# The Boar Who Lifted the Earth (Varaha)

`avatar-varaha` | Vishnu | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: urgent, mighty, then gentle | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

The demon Hiranyaksha drags the earth to the bottom of the cosmic ocean; Vishnu becomes a boar, kills the demon, and lifts the earth on his tusks.

## Characters

- **Hiranyaksha:** a gold-eyed demon, huge.
- **Varaha:** a colossal boar, blue-black, with golden tusks.
- **Bhumi (Earth):** a goddess, serene.
- **Brahma and the gods.**

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it.

### 1. The dark ocean (20 sec)
**Visual:** A dark, endless ocean under a grey sky. Far below the surface, faintly visible, a round shape lies at the bottom: the earth, with her mountains and forests, sunk in the deep.
**Narration:** "At the beginning of an age, the earth had sunk into the great ocean. Her mountains, her forests and her rivers lay deep beneath the dark water, and there was no ground anywhere for any creature to live on."
**Sound:** low, slow waves; a muffled, sunken drone.

### 2. The prayer (20 sec)
**Visual:** Brahma on his lotus, four faces troubled. Before him stands Manu, the first man, with folded hands. Brahma closes his eyes in thought.
**Dialogue:** Manu: "Lord Brahma, lift the earth out of the water, so that living beings may have a home."
**Narration:** "Brahma thought for a long time. The task was beyond him. So he left it to the Lord."
**Sound:** a quiet, questioning flute.

### 3. The growth (25 sec)
**Visual:** As Brahma sits thinking, a tiny boar, no bigger than a thumb tip, pops out of his nostril. The sages blink. In a moment it is as big as an elephant, then as big as a mountain, hanging in the sky. It roars, and the sound rolls through all the worlds.
**Narration:** "Suddenly, out of Brahma's nostril came a tiny boar, no bigger than the tip of a thumb. While they watched, it grew as big as an elephant, and then bigger still, like a mountain in the sky. Then it roared, and the sages, knowing it was the Lord, sang his praise."
**Sound:** a small, comic squeak, then a swelling of music, then a thunderous roar; chanting voices.

### 4. The dive (20 sec)
**Visual:** The great boar, dark and bristling, flies through the sky, scattering clouds with his hooves, then dives. The ocean parts in two huge waves. Underwater he sniffs, searching through the dark, hooves like arrows cutting down.
**Narration:** "He leapt into the air, scattering the clouds, and dived into the ocean like a falling mountain. The sea split in two before him. Down in the dark he searched for the earth by her scent, as a boar does."
**Sound:** rushing wind, a huge splash, then deep, muffled underwater music.

### 5. The demon (25 sec)
**Visual:** At the bottom he finds the earth. He lifts her gently on his white tusks. A huge figure looms in the murk, a demon with golden hair and fearsome tusks, mace in hand, sneering.
**Narration:** "At the bottom he found the earth, and gently lifted her on his tusks. But there stood the demon Hiranyaksha, the golden-eyed, who mocked him and challenged him to fight. The Lord bore the insults patiently, for he saw that the earth on his tusks was afraid."
**Dialogue:** Hiranyaksha: "You beast of the water! The earth belongs to us. You will not take her."
**Sound:** a menacing brass phrase; the demon's laugh echoing.

### 6. The end (20 sec)
**Visual:** The boar rises to the surface and sets the earth floating safely on the water. Then he turns to face the demon. Light and shadow only: the mace swings, the discus flashes, and one great blow. The demon's shadow falls. Stillness.
**Narration:** "First he set the earth safely on the surface of the water. Then he turned to the demon. They fought long and hard, until the Lord ended it with a single blow, and the demon fell."
**Sound:** clashes muffled as if far off, one great stroke, then silence.

### 7. The lifting (25 sec)
**Visual:** The boar stands in the ocean, blue-black like a tamala tree, the earth resting on the curve of his gleaming tusks like a lotus held by an elephant rising from a lake. Sages on the clouds bow and sing; drops shaken from his bristles fall on them like blessing.
**Narration:** "There he stood, dark as a rain cloud, with the earth resting on his white tusks like a lotus lifted from a lake. The sages sang to him, and the drops of water shaken from his shoulders fell on them, and they felt themselves made pure."
**Sound:** chanting voices, a broad and peaceful theme.

### 8. The setting (10 sec)
**Visual:** With his hooves he gently sets the earth upon the water. Green spreads across her; rivers shine; the first light of morning.
**Narration:** "Then he set the earth upon the waters, steady and safe, a home for every living thing."
**Sound:** birdsong, the first melody returning softly.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri recalls the boar in two songs. Songs to show: 545 (Perainagar), Vishnu who rose in a beautiful form and dug up the earth; and 233 (Swamimalai), which also names the boar. In both, Murugan is praised as this Lord's nephew.

## Production notes

- **Stylised fight.** Show only light and shadow.
- **Bhumi is serene,** not a damsel in distress.
