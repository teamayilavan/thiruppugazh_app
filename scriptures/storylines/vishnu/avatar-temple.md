# The Reclining Lord of Srirangam (Ranganatha)

`avatar-temple` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: devotional, still, a pilgrimage

## One-line story

Ranganatha reclines on Adisesha at Srirangam, the great temple of Vishnu that the poet also links with Kanchi and Tirupati.

## The storyline

### 1. The river (25 sec)
Two rivers embrace an island at dawn.
**Narration:** "Between two rivers stands the temple of Srirangam."

### 2. The towers (20 sec)
Towering gopurams, concentric walls, a crowd entering.
**Narration:** "Seven walls surround it."

### 3. The sanctum (25 sec)
Within, a dark chamber lit by lamps: the Lord, reclining.
**Narration:** "And inside, the Lord rests."

### 4. The serpent (20 sec)
Adisesha's coils under him.
**Narration:** "On the serpent Adisesha."

### 5. The pilgrims (20 sec)
People bow in a long line.
**Narration:** "Pilgrims come from every land."

### 6. Kanchi and Tirupati (20 sec)
Two more shrines flash: Vishnu standing, Vishnu on a hill.
**Narration:** "The poet sings of other shrines as well."

### 7. The lamp (20 sec)
A single lamp flickers.
**Narration:** "And a lamp is lit."
