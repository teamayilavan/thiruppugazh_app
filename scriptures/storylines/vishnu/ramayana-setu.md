# The Bridge Across the Sea (Setu)

`ramayana-setu` | Vishnu | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: industrious, joyful, triumphant | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

The monkeys build a causeway across the sea with Nala as architect, and the whole army crosses to Lanka.

## Characters

- **Nala:** the architect monkey.
- **Hanuman, Angada, the monkey army.**
- **Rama.**
- **A small squirrel** (tradition: not in the research; included only as a light touch, marked in the narration).

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it. This is a film of teamwork and joy in the work.

### 1. The plan (20 sec)
**Visual:** The southern shore at dawn. The ocean, calm now after Rama's anger, rises as a tall shining figure, the Sea-king, and bows to Rama. He points to a young monkey in the crowd: Nala. Nala steps forward, surprised, then stands tall.
**Narration:** "The ocean lay between Rama and Lanka. Then the Lord of the Sea rose from the waves and said: there is one among you who can build a bridge, and I will hold it up. His name is Nala."
**Dialogue:** Nala: "Give me stones and trees, and I will make a road across the sea."
**Sound:** waves, then a deep, gentle voice of water.

### 2. The quarry (20 sec)
**Visual:** The monkey army pours into the hills and forests. They tear up great trees, sala, palms, bamboo, and break loose rocks as big as houses. Long chains of monkeys pass stones hand to hand down to the shore. Some carry whole hilltops on their shoulders.
**Narration:** "The whole army went to work. They pulled up great trees, palms and sala and bamboo. They broke off rocks and whole hilltops, and carried them, laughing and shouting, down to the shore."
**Sound:** a working rhythm on drums, cheerful shouts, crashing stone.

### 3. The rolling (20 sec)
**Visual:** Rocks splash into the sea; spray rises high. Nala stands at the water's edge, directing, holding a measuring line stretched out across the waves. The first stones settle in a straight row.
**Narration:** "They hurled the rocks into the sea, and the water leapt to the sky. Nala stood at the edge and set each stone in its place, straight as a measuring line. The poets say the sea itself was shaken."
**Sound:** great splashes, the drum rhythm continuing.

### 4. The stones (25 sec)
**Visual:** Close on Nala: a huge mountain that a crowd of monkeys could barely lift, he takes in one hand and sets it in place. He breaks rocks, fits them together, smooths the top with sand. The road grows out from the shore, wide and level.
**Narration:** "Kamban says that a mountain which millions together could hardly lift, Nala took up in one hand and set in its place. He broke the rocks, packed them close, laid mountains side by side and smoothed them with sand. The road grew out over the water."
**Sound:** a satisfying thud for each stone; the melody brightens.

### 5. The squirrel (20 sec)
**Visual:** A small grey squirrel runs into the sea, rolls in the sand, runs up onto the stones and shakes the sand off, again and again. Rama notices. He lifts it gently in his palm and strokes its back with three fingers; three pale stripes appear on its fur.
**Narration:** "Tradition tells of a little squirrel who wanted to help. Again and again it wet itself in the sea, rolled in the sand, and shook the sand onto the bridge. Rama picked it up and stroked its back, and the squirrel still carries the marks of his fingers."
**Sound:** a light pizzicato tune for the squirrel, a warm chord at Rama's touch.

### 6. The line grows (25 sec)
**Visual:** Time-lapse from high above: day after day the bridge stretches across the blue sea toward a far green island. Clouds of gods and singers gather in the sky to watch. By the end it reaches the far shore.
**Narration:** "Day after day the bridge reached further out across the sea. Valmiki counts the days, and says it took five. Kamban says three. The gods came to watch from the sky, and Kamban says the bridge lay on the sea like the great serpent Adishesha, offering its back to the Lord."
**Sound:** the music builds, layer on layer.

### 7. The crossing (20 sec)
**Visual:** The army streams over the bridge, banners flying, a river of monkeys and bears. Rama walks at the front with Lakshmana, Sugriva, Hanuman and Angada close by. Waves break harmlessly on either side.
**Narration:** "Then the whole army crossed the sea, so many that they could not be counted. They marched with joy, and the noise of their cheering, Kamban says, split the sky."
**Sound:** marching drums, conches, the roar of a great crowd.

### 8. Lanka (15 sec)
**Visual:** The far shore. Rama steps off the bridge onto the sand of Lanka. Ahead, on its mountain, the golden city glows in evening light. Rama looks up at it, calm.
**Narration:** "And Rama set foot on the shore of Lanka. Somewhere in that golden city, Sita was waiting."
**Sound:** the drums stop; one long conch; the wind.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

The bridge appears in fourteen of Arunagiri's songs, usually in a single phrase: Murugan is the nephew of the Lord who blocked the sea and crossed to Lanka. Songs to show: 166 (Palani), the one who dammed the ocean and cut down Ravana's crowns; 840 (Vedaranyam), the one who blocked the sea at the Sea-king's word and gave Lanka to Vibhishana; 19 (Thiruparankundram); and 750 (Virudhachalam).
