# The Bridge Across the Sea (Setu)

`ramayana-setu` | Vishnu | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: industrious, joyful, triumphant

## One-line story

The monkeys build a causeway across the sea with Nala as architect, and the whole army crosses to Lanka.

## Characters

- **Nala:** the architect monkey.
- **Hanuman, Angada, the monkey army.**
- **Rama.**
- **A small squirrel** (tradition: not in the research; included only as a light touch, marked in the narration).

## The storyline

### 1. The plan (20 sec)
Nala draws the line of the bridge on the sand.
**Narration:** "Nala the architect drew the plan."

### 2. The quarry (20 sec)
Monkeys uproot hills and trees.
**Narration:** "The monkeys tore up hills and trees."

### 3. The rolling (20 sec)
Boulders are rolled down to the shore.
**Narration:** "They rolled them to the sea."

### 4. The stones (25 sec)
Each stone floats and fits.
**Narration:** "And the stones floated."

### 5. The squirrel (20 sec)
A tiny squirrel rolls in the sand and shakes it off onto the bridge.
**Narration:** "Even a little squirrel helped, so the tradition says."

### 6. The line grows (25 sec)
The bridge extends toward the horizon.
**Narration:** "The bridge grew mile by mile."

### 7. The crossing (20 sec)
The army marches over.
**Narration:** "The whole army crossed."

### 8. Lanka (15 sec)
They arrive on the far shore.
**Narration:** "And they reached Lanka."
