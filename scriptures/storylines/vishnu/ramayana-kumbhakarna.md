# The Sleeper of Lanka (Kumbhakarna)

`ramayana-kumbhakarna` | Vishnu | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: huge, comic, then noble

## One-line story

Rama kills Ravana's brother Kumbhakarna, cutting off his head; the body falls on the wall of Lanka.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Rama kills Ravana's brother Kumbhakarna, cutting off his head | Valmiki Yuddha Kanda 67.165-172; Kamba, Kumbakarnan Vadhai Padalam 337-359 |
| The body falls on the wall of Lanka | same |
| Song 961 recalls it | Thiruppugazh song 961 |
| His long sleep and waking are the widely told frame | Valmiki and Kamba (traditional; not read verse by verse here) |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Kumbhakarna:** a giant, sleepy, honest.
- **Ravana.**
- **Rama and the monkeys.**

## The storyline

### 1. The sleeper (25 sec)
A giant asleep in a vast cave.
**Narration:** "Kumbhakarna slept for months at a time."

### 2. The waking (25 sec)
Drums, elephants and food wake him.
**Narration:** "They woke him for war."

### 3. The counsel (20 sec)
He tells Ravana: "Give Sita back."
**Narration:** "He told Ravana he was wrong."

### 4. The loyalty (20 sec)
But he will fight for his brother.
**Narration:** "But he would fight for his brother."

### 5. The rampage (20 sec)
He wades into the monkey army.
**Narration:** "He fought like a mountain."

### 6. Rama (20 sec)
Rama faces him and draws his bow.
**Narration:** "Rama came to meet him."

### 7. The end (25 sec)
An arrow of light; he falls.
**Narration:** "And Rama ended him."

### 8. The wall (10 sec)
The body falls on the wall.
**Narration:** "He fell across the wall of Lanka."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Song 961.

## Production notes

- **Treat him as noble.** His honesty is the film's tone.
- **No head shown.** The fall is a shadow on a wall.
