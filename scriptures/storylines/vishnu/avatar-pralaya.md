# The Child on the Banyan Leaf (Pralaya)

`avatar-pralaya` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: hushed, vast, tender

## One-line story

At the end of a cycle the world dissolves; Vishnu holds all the worlds in his belly and lies as a child on a banyan leaf, floating on the waters.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| At the end of a cycle the world is held in Vishnu's belly | Research set `avatar-pralaya` |
| He lies as a child on a banyan leaf | same |
| Songs 276, 402, 727 and others recall it | Thiruppugazh songs |

**Honesty note:** the research gives this in two lines; the film is a meditation. The sage Markandeya's vision of the child is the traditional frame (Bhagavata 12.8-12.9) but is not read here, so it is not used.
**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **The child:** a baby, dark blue, lying on a leaf, sucking his toe.
- **The leaf:** a banyan leaf as big as a boat.

## The storyline

### 1. The end (25 sec)
The world's last fire fades; the stars go out.
**Narration:** "At the end of the age, the worlds dissolve."

### 2. The flood (25 sec)
Water covers everything.
**Narration:** "Everything is water."

### 3. The leaf (20 sec)
A single banyan leaf floats on the water.
**Narration:** "One leaf floats."

### 4. The child (25 sec)
On it lies a baby, blue, sucking his toe.
**Narration:** "And on it, a child."

### 5. The belly (25 sec)
Inside the child, tiny worlds turn like fireflies.
**Narration:** "He holds all the worlds inside him."

### 6. The drift (15 sec)
The leaf drifts on the dark waters.
**Narration:** "He waits."

### 7. The dawn (15 sec)
A faint light on the horizon.
**Narration:** "And a new age will begin."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 276, 402 and 727.

- **A lullaby film.** Use a soft score.
