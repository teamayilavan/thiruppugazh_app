# The Heads That Grew Back

`ramayana-heads-cut` | Vishnu | running time 2:35 (7 scenes) | tone: frustrating, strange, mounting

## One-line story

Rama cuts off Ravana's heads one after another, but each grows back; a hundred are cut in vain.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Rama cuts off Ravana's heads one after another, but each grows back | Valmiki Yuddha Kanda 107.54-58; Kamba, Ravana Vadhai Padalam 148-162 |
| A hundred are cut in vain | same |
| Songs 29, 44, 66 and others (50 songs) recall it | Thiruppugazh songs |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration. The heads are shown as masks of light.

## Characters

- **Ravana:** a ten-headed king, battered.
- **Rama.**
- **Matali** (the charioteer; a presence).

## The storyline

### 1. The duel (25 sec)
Rama and Ravana face each other in the sky.
**Narration:** "Rama and Ravana fought in the sky."

### 2. The first head (20 sec)
Rama's arrow cuts off a head; a new one grows.
**Narration:** "Rama cut off a head. Another grew."

### 3. The second (20 sec)
The same again.
**Narration:** "He cut off another. It grew back."

### 4. The count (25 sec)
Heads fall like leaves and grow again.
**Narration:** "A hundred heads were cut."

### 5. The wonder (20 sec)
The gods stare, uneasy.
**Narration:** "And none could end him."

### 6. The thought (20 sec)
Rama pauses and thinks.
**Narration:** "Rama paused, and thought."

### 7. The weapon (25 sec)
He reaches for another weapon.
**Narration:** "And he reached for another weapon."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 29, 44 and 66 among fifty. This is the most-mentioned episode of the Ramayana in the collection.

## Production notes

- **A film of repetition.** Make the regrowth look like water closing.
- **No blood.**
