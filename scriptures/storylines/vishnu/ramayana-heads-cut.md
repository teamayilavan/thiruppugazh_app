# The Heads That Grew Back

`ramayana-heads-cut` | Vishnu | running time 2:35 (7 scenes) | tone: frustrating, strange, mounting | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

Rama cuts off Ravana's heads one after another, but each grows back; a hundred are cut in vain.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Rama cuts off Ravana's heads one after another, but each grows back | Valmiki Yuddha Kanda 107.54-58; Kamba, Ravana Vadhai Padalam 148-162 |
| A hundred are cut in vain | same |
| Songs 29, 44, 66 and others (50 songs) recall it | Thiruppugazh songs |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration. The heads are shown as masks of light.

## Characters

- **Ravana:** a ten-headed king, battered.
- **Rama.**
- **Matali** (the charioteer; a presence).

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it.

### 1. The duel (25 sec)
**Visual:** The last battle at Lanka. Two chariots face each other on a darkened field: Ravana's, black and gold, and Rama's, the chariot of Indra driven by the charioteer Matali. Ravana, ten-headed and twenty-armed, battered but fierce, shoots storms of arrows. Rama answers calmly. The armies stand back and watch.
**Narration:** "The war had come to its last day. All of Ravana's army was gone, and his brothers and sons had fallen. Now Ravana himself faced Rama, chariot against chariot. The gods had sent Rama their own chariot, driven by Matali. Both armies stood still and watched."
**Sound:** thunder, the clash of arrows, a deep war drum.

### 2. The first head (20 sec)
**Visual:** Rama sees an opening. He draws a crescent-headed arrow. It flies like a streak of moonlight. One of Ravana's heads, shown as a mask of light, is struck away and falls far off into the sea like a mountain peak.
**Narration:** "Rama saw his moment. He loosed one arrow with a head like the half moon. It struck, and one of Ravana's heads fell away. Kamban says it fell into the sea like a peak of the great mountain Meru, broken in a storm."
**Sound:** a single ringing note; a far splash.

### 3. The second (20 sec)
**Visual:** The armies gasp. But on Ravana's shoulders, light flows like water closing over a stone, and a new head rises in the empty place, glaring. Rama looks, steady, and fits another arrow.
**Narration:** "But as everyone watched, the head rose again in its place, just as before. Rama struck again, and again a new head rose. Kamban says it was the power of Ravana's long penance. What can stand against penance?"
**Sound:** a low, rippling sound like water closing; then silence.

### 4. The count (25 sec)
**Visual:** A rhythmic sequence: arrow, flash, a mask of light falling, water-like regrowth. Again and again, faster and faster. The fallen masks drift away like sparks over the waves, over hills, over the plain. A faint tally of lights builds in the sky.
**Narration:** "Again and again Rama's arrows flew, and again and again the heads grew back. Valmiki says that a hundred heads were cut off, all alike, and still Ravana stood. Kamban says the heads fell everywhere, on every wave and every hill, in every direction, like thunderbolts falling."
**Sound:** a repeating pattern, growing faster: twang, flash, ripple.

### 5. The wonder (20 sec)
**Visual:** Rama lowers his bow a little. He looks at his quiver, then at Ravana, thoughtful rather than afraid. Ravana laughs, all ten mouths at once.
**Narration:** "Rama paused and wondered. These were the arrows that had brought down Maricha and Khara, and Vali. Why did they fail now? Ravana laughed, and his ten mouths laughed with him."
**Sound:** Ravana's laughter echoing; the music drops to one note.

### 6. The thought (20 sec)
**Visual:** Matali turns from the reins and speaks quietly to Rama. Rama listens, and his face becomes still and clear.
**Narration:** "Then Matali, the charioteer of the gods, turned and spoke softly. Why fight on as if you did not know, he asked. There is a weapon made for this very hour."
**Dialogue:** Matali: "My lord, use the weapon of Brahma."
**Sound:** wind; a soft, rising chord.

### 7. The weapon (25 sec)
**Visual:** Rama draws a single great arrow, glowing like the sun, and fits it to the string. The sky darkens, then brightens. He holds it, calm, as the whole world seems to wait. The image freezes on the drawn bow and the shining arrow.
**Narration:** "Rama took out one shining arrow, the weapon of Brahma, and set it to his bow. The whole world seemed to hold its breath. The heads could grow back no more. And Arunagiri sings, in fifty songs, of the Lord whose single arrow brought down the ten heads of Lanka's king."
**Sound:** a great rising hum, a conch, then silence.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

About fifty songs recall the fall of Ravana's heads; Arunagiri's usual picture is the ten crowns falling to the Lord's arrows, often to a single arrow. Show song 29 (Thiruchendur), song 166 (Palani) and song 1007 (a general song, Pothu Padalgal).

## Production notes

- **A film of repetition.** Make the regrowth look like water closing.
- **No blood.**
