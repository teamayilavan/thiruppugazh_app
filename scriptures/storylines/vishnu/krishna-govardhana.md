# The Hill as an Umbrella (Govardhana)

`krishna-govardhana` | Vishnu | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: storm, wonder, gentle triumph | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

When Indra's rain threatens Vrindavan, Krishna lifts Govardhana hill on one hand as an umbrella for seven days.

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Krishna:** a boy, calm, a smile.
- **The villagers and cattle.**
- **Indra:** proud, then humbled.
- **Nanda.**

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it.

### 1. The offering (20 sec)
**Visual:** Vraja in harvest time. The cowherds pile up sweets, rice and milk for the yearly sacrifice to Indra, king of the gods and lord of rain. Krishna, a small boy, tugs at his father Nanda's sleeve and asks questions. Nanda listens, laughs, and nods. The whole village turns and carries the offerings to the green hill of Govardhana instead, and the cows are garlanded.
**Narration:** "Every year the cowherds of Vraja made a great offering to Indra, the king of the gods, who sends the rain. But that year little Krishna asked his father: why Indra? It is this hill, Govardhana, that feeds our cows. So that year they worshipped the hill and the cows instead."
**Sound:** festive drums, bells, cattle lowing happily.

### 2. Indra's anger (20 sec)
**Visual:** High in the clouds, Indra on his white elephant, thunderbolt in hand, watching. His face darkens. He calls up great black storm clouds, the clouds of the end of the world, and points them down at Vraja.
**Narration:** "High above, Indra saw it, and his pride was wounded. These cowherds have forgotten me, he thought, all because of a boy. In his anger he called up the clouds that come at the end of the world, and sent them down on Vraja."
**Dialogue:** Indra: "Go. Drown their pastures, and their cows."
**Sound:** a rumble of thunder rolling closer.

### 3. The storm (25 sec)
**Visual:** Black clouds roll over the village. Lightning, thunder, wind, hail. Rain falls in thick ropes until the land is a single sheet of water and high ground and low ground look the same. Cows shiver and try to shield their calves with their own bodies.
**Narration:** "The storm broke. Wind howled, lightning flashed, and hail came down with the rain. The water rose until hill and hollow looked the same. The cows trembled and tried to shelter their calves with their own bodies. The people did not know where to run."
**Sound:** a roaring storm; hail on roofs; frightened cattle.

### 4. The call (20 sec)
**Visual:** Through the rain, the whole village, people and cattle, struggles toward Krishna. They kneel before him, soaked and shivering. He looks up at the sky, calm, and understands who has sent this.
**Narration:** "They all came to Krishna, the people and the cows together, soaked and shivering."
**Dialogue:** The cowherds: "Krishna, save the cows, and save us from Indra's anger!"
**Narration (continued):** "Krishna looked up at the clouds, and he knew who had sent them."
**Sound:** the storm, slightly muffled around Krishna.

### 5. The lifting (25 sec)
**Visual:** Krishna walks to the foot of Govardhana. He puts one small hand under the edge of the hill and lifts. The whole mountain rises from the earth, trees, rocks, waterfalls and all, and he holds it up high, balanced on one hand, easily, as a child holds a mushroom. Tradition shows it on the tip of his little finger.
**Narration:** "He went to the foot of Govardhana and, with one hand, he lifted the whole hill out of the ground. He held it up as easily as a child holds a mushroom. Tradition says he held it on his little finger."
**Sound:** a deep rumble of earth; then one clear, bright note.

### 6. The shelter (25 sec)
**Visual:** Under the hill it is dry and warm. Families, cows, calves, carts and old people crowd in. Rain thunders on top of the hill but cannot reach them. Day and night pass; Krishna stands without moving, smiling, while the people gaze at him.
**Narration:** "He called to them: come under, with your cows and your carts; do not be afraid, the hill will not fall. And they all came in. For seven days and seven nights he stood there, without food or sleep, holding up the hill, and the people looked at him and forgot the storm."
**Sound:** rain overhead, muffled; inside, quiet voices and a lullaby.

### 7. The end (20 sec)
**Visual:** Above the clouds, Indra stares, astonished; his anger drains away. He lowers his thunderbolt and sends the clouds away. The rain stops, the wind drops, the sun comes out.
**Narration:** "Indra watched, astonished, and his pride melted away. He called back his clouds. The wind fell, the rain stopped, and the sun came out over Vraja."
**Sound:** the storm fades; birdsong returns.

### 8. The return (10 sec)
**Visual:** Krishna calls everyone out. They come into the sunlight with their cows and carts. Then he sets the hill gently back exactly where it stood.
**Narration:** "Then Krishna called everyone out into the sunshine, and set the hill back down where it had always stood."
**Sound:** a soft thud of earth; joyful drums.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri recalls the lifting of Govardhana in about five songs. Songs 116 (Palani), 674 (Thiruvalangadu), 770 (Sirkazhi) and 1183 are the ones to show: the hill held as an umbrella, and the herds sheltered from the rain.

## Production notes

- **A gentle triumph.** Indra's humility is welcome, not a punishment.
- **Show the hill lifted by a little finger,** as tradition has it.
