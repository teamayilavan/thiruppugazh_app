# The Hill as an Umbrella (Govardhana)

`krishna-govardhana` | Vishnu | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: storm, wonder, gentle triumph

## One-line story

When Indra's rain threatens Vrindavan, Krishna lifts Govardhana hill on one hand as an umbrella for seven days.

## The storyline

### 1. The offering (20 sec)
The villagers prepare the yearly worship of Indra; Krishna suggests they honour their hill instead.
**Narration:** "Krishna told the villagers to honour their own hill, Govardhana."

### 2. Indra's anger (20 sec)
In the sky, Indra frowns; storm clouds gather.
**Narration:** "Indra grew angry, and sent a storm."

### 3. The storm (25 sec)
Rain, thunder, floods; villagers and cows crowd together.
**Narration:** "Rain poured on Vrindavan."

### 4. The call (20 sec)
They cry out to Krishna.
**Narration:** "The villagers ran to Krishna."

### 5. The lifting (25 sec)
He lifts the hill on his little finger, as an umbrella.
**Narration:** "He lifted Govardhana on one hand."

### 6. The shelter (25 sec)
Everyone crowds beneath; days pass; the rain falls.
**Narration:** "For seven days they stood beneath it."

### 7. The end (20 sec)
The rain stops; Indra, humbled, descends.
**Narration:** "At last Indra gave up."

### 8. The return (10 sec)
Krishna sets the hill down; people cheer.
**Narration:** "And he set the hill back in its place."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 116, 186 and 674 among five.

## Production notes

- **A gentle triumph.** Indra's humility is welcome, not a punishment.
- **Show the hill lifted by a little finger,** as tradition has it.
