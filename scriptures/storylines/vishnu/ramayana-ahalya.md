# The Stone That Woke (Ahalya)

`ramayana-ahalya` | Vishnu | running time 2:35 (7 scenes) | tone: hushed, sorrowful, redemptive

## One-line story

On the way to Mithila the dust of Rama's feet frees Ahalya, Gautama's wife, who had stood as stone, or unseen, for years after Indra's deceit.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration. The film follows Kamba's tradition of a stone; Valmiki has her unseen, in penance.

## Characters

- **Ahalya:** a graceful woman, still as stone.
- **Gautama:** a sage.
- **Rama, Lakshmana, Vishwamitra.**

## The storyline

### 1. The hermitage (25 sec)
An abandoned hermitage in a wild place.
**Narration:** "There was a hermitage, empty."

### 2. The stone (25 sec)
In a clearing, a stone shaped like a woman.
**Narration:** "A woman stood there, as still as stone."

### 3. The reason (20 sec)
A flash of a past: a deceit, a curse.
**Narration:** "A curse had made her so, after a deceit."

### 4. The princes (20 sec)
Rama walks through the clearing with Vishwamitra.
**Narration:** "Rama came by."

### 5. The dust (25 sec)
The dust from his foot settles on the stone.
**Narration:** "The dust of his foot touched her."

### 6. The waking (25 sec)
The stone becomes a woman, who bows.
**Narration:** "She woke."

### 7. The song (15 sec)
A Thiruppugazh line over her bowing.
**Narration:** "The poet remembers the foot that touched the stone."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 483 and 669.

## Production notes

- **Treat Ahalya with respect.** Her story is of waking, not blame.
- **Say that the stone is Kamba's telling.**
