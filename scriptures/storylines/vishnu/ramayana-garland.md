# The Creeper Garland (Telling the Brothers Apart)

`ramayana-garland` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: practical, tense, a small clever act

## One-line story

Before the second fight between Vali and Sugriva, Rama has a gaja creeper hung round Sugriva's neck so that he can tell the brothers apart.

## The storyline

### 1. The first fight (25 sec)
Two identical monkey kings fight; Rama, behind a tree, cannot tell them apart.
**Narration:** "The two brothers looked exactly alike."

### 2. The hold (20 sec)
Rama lowers his bow.
**Narration:** "Rama could not shoot."

### 3. Sugriva's return (20 sec)
Sugriva comes back, beaten.
**Narration:** "Sugriva came back, bruised."

### 4. The remedy (25 sec)
Rama asks Lakshmana to pick a creeper.
**Narration:** "Rama had a creeper picked."

### 5. The garland (20 sec)
Lakshmana hangs it round Sugriva's neck.
**Narration:** "It was hung round Sugriva's neck."

### 6. The sign (20 sec)
Sugriva stands tall with it.
**Narration:** "So he would be known."

### 7. The second fight (20 sec)
Sugriva goes out again; Rama waits, bow in hand.
**Narration:** "And the second fight began."
