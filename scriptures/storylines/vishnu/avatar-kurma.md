# The Tortoise Under the Mountain (Kurma)

`avatar-kurma` | Vishnu | running time 2:35 (7 scenes) | tone: heavy, patient, steady

## One-line story

As the gods and demons churn the Ocean of Milk, the mountain begins to sink; Vishnu becomes a tortoise and holds it on his back.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|

**Honesty note:** the research gives the avatar in a single line. For the full churning, see `avatar-samudra-manthana`. This film is the tortoise's quiet part of it.

## The storyline

### 1. The churning begins (25 sec)
Gods and demons pull the serpent; the mountain turns in the sea.
**Narration:** "The gods and demons began to churn the sea."

### 2. The sinking (25 sec)
The mountain, too heavy, begins to sink into the water.
**Narration:** "But the mountain sank."

### 3. The fear (20 sec)
The gods look at each other, dismayed.
**Narration:** "They did not know what to do."

### 4. The Lord's call (20 sec)
Vishnu hears and smiles.
**Narration:** "Vishnu heard their need."

### 5. The tortoise (30 sec)
A giant tortoise rises from the deep and slides beneath the mountain.
**Narration:** "He became a tortoise, and rose beneath it."

### 6. The steadiness (25 sec)
The mountain turns on his back; the churning goes on.
**Narration:** "And the mountain turned on his back."

### 7. The sea (10 sec)
The sea churns, the tortoise steady.
**Narration:** "He did not move."
