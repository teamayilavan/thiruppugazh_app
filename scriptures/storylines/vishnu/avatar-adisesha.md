# The Lord on the Serpent Bed (Vishnu and Adisesha)

`avatar-adisesha` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: serene, vast, a lullaby of an image

## One-line story

Vishnu lies asleep on the great serpent Adisesha (Ananta) in the Ocean of Milk, the image the poet returns to again and again.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|

**Honesty note:** this is an image, not an episode. The film is a meditation, and adds nothing that the research does not say except what is needed to make the image visible.
**Added for the screen:** every visual; the narration.

## Characters

- **Vishnu:** blue, reclining, calm, eyes half-closed.
- **Adisesha:** a vast, many-hooded serpent, silver-white, coiled as a bed and canopy.
- **Lakshmi:** at his feet (traditional in the image).

## The storyline

### 1. The milk ocean (25 sec)
A calm white sea, mist, no horizon.
**Narration:** "In the Ocean of Milk, there is no shore."

### 2. The coils (25 sec)
Silver coils rise from the water, forming a great bed.
**Narration:** "The serpent Adisesha makes a bed."

### 3. The hoods (20 sec)
Many hoods lift and open like a canopy.
**Narration:** "And his hoods are a canopy."

### 4. The Lord (25 sec)
Vishnu lies on the coils, blue and quiet, one hand under his head.
**Narration:** "On it lies the Lord, at rest."

### 5. The breath (20 sec)
Each breath of his sends a ripple to the horizon.
**Narration:** "The worlds rise and fall with his breath."

### 6. The poet (20 sec)
A line of the Thiruppugazh floats over the scene.
**Narration:** "The poet sings of him again and again."

### 7. The stillness (15 sec)
The camera rises and the sea is still.
**Narration:** "And all is still."
