# The Elephant at the Gate (Kuvalayapida)

`krishna-kuvalayapida` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: thrilling, quick, triumphant

## One-line story

At Mathura's gate Krishna meets Kamsa's elephant Kuvalayapida, breaks its tusks and kills it.

## Characters

- **Krishna and Balarama.**
- **Kuvalayapida:** a huge, maddened elephant.

## The storyline

### 1. The gate (20 sec)
The city gate of Mathura; the brothers approach.
**Narration:** "Krishna and Balarama came to Mathura's gate."

### 2. The elephant (25 sec)
A huge elephant blocks the road; the mahout goads it.
**Narration:** "Kamsa had set an elephant to stop them."

### 3. The charge (25 sec)
It charges; the boy dodges between its legs.
**Narration:** "It charged."

### 4. The dodge (20 sec)
Krishna slips between its legs, tugs its tail.
**Narration:** "Krishna slipped under it."

### 5. The tusk (25 sec)
He seizes a tusk and breaks it.
**Narration:** "He broke off its tusk."

### 6. The end (20 sec)
The elephant falls.
**Narration:** "The elephant fell."

### 7. The brothers (15 sec)
The brothers walk on, tusks in hand.
**Narration:** "And they went on into the city."
