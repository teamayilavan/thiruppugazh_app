# The Iron Pestle (The End of the Yadavas)

`krishna-mausala` | Vishnu | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: foreboding, tragic, resigned

## One-line story

A curse leads to an iron pestle; its powder grows into reeds with which the Yadavas destroy each other, and the pestle's last piece is thrown into the sea.

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration. The Yadavas' fight is shown in silhouette.

## Characters

- **The Yadava princes:** young, boastful.
- **The sages:** who pronounce the curse.
- **Krishna:** grave, accepting.

## The storyline

### 1. The prank (25 sec)
Young Yadavas dress one of them as a pregnant woman and mock the sages.
**Narration:** "Some young princes mocked a group of sages."

### 2. The curse (20 sec)
The sages speak: the pestle will destroy the clan.
**Narration:** "The sages cursed them."

### 3. The pestle (25 sec)
An iron pestle is born; they grind it to powder.
**Narration:** "An iron pestle came, and they ground it down."

### 4. The sea (20 sec)
The powder is cast into the sea, and washes ashore.
**Narration:** "They threw the powder into the sea."

### 5. The reeds (20 sec)
Tall reeds grow on the shore.
**Narration:** "It grew into reeds."

### 6. The fight (25 sec)
At a feast, the Yadavas quarrel and snatch reeds as weapons.
**Narration:** "They fought each other with the reeds."

### 7. The end (20 sec)
Silhouettes fall.
**Narration:** "And the clan fell."

### 8. The last piece (10 sec)
A last iron piece is thrown into the sea.
**Narration:** "The last piece went into the sea."
