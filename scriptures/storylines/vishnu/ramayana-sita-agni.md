# The Fire Ordeal (Sita and Agni)

`ramayana-sita-agni` | Vishnu | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: grave, painful, then luminous

## One-line story

Rama asks Sita to prove her purity; she enters the fire, and Agni returns her unharmed.

## The storyline

### 1. The request (25 sec)
Rama, in front of the army, says Sita must prove her purity.
**Narration:** "Rama asked Sita to prove her purity."

### 2. Sita's silence (20 sec)
She stands, calm, hurt.
**Narration:** "She did not answer in anger."

### 3. The pyre (25 sec)
She asks Lakshmana to build a fire.
**Narration:** "She asked for a fire."

### 4. The walk (20 sec)
She walks around Rama and enters the flames.
**Narration:** "She walked into the fire."

### 5. The wait (20 sec)
The army is silent.
**Narration:** "Everyone waited."

### 6. Agni (25 sec)
Agni rises from the fire, holding Sita, unharmed.
**Narration:** "Agni, the fire-god, brought her out."

### 7. The truth (25 sec)
He says she is pure; Rama bows.
**Narration:** "And all knew the truth."
