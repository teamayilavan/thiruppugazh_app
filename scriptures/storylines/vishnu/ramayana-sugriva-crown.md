# The Monkey King Crowned (Sugriva)

`ramayana-sugriva-crown` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: ceremonial, relieved, hopeful | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

After Vali's death Sugriva is crowned king of Kishkindha.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| After Vali's death Sugriva is crowned king of Kishkindha | Valmiki Kishkindha Kanda 26; Kamba, Arasiyal Padalam 5 |
| Song 128 recalls it | Thiruppugazh song 128 |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Sugriva, Hanuman, Angada, Tara.**
- **Rama and Lakshmana.**

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it. This is quiet, not triumphant: Vali's death still hangs over it.

### 1. The mourning (25 sec)
**Visual:** Dawn on a riverbank below the hills of Kishkindha. A funeral pyre of fragrant wood. The young prince Angada, head bowed, lights it. Tara stands veiled beside him; the monkeys stand in long silent rows. Smoke rises straight into a pale sky.
**Narration:** "Vali was gone. His people carried him to the river on a bier, with music and with tears, as befits a king. His son, the young prince Angada, lit the fire. All Kishkindha stood in silence and watched the smoke rise."
**Sound:** a slow drum, a low conch, the crackle of the fire.

### 2. The sorrow (20 sec)
**Visual:** Sugriva sits apart on a rock, his head in his hands. The crown he had longed for means nothing to him now. Hanuman stands near, waiting.
**Narration:** "Sugriva had won back his kingdom, but he could not rejoice. He had fought his brother, and his brother was dead. He sat alone, and grieved for the one he had once loved."
**Dialogue:** Sugriva: "I wanted the kingdom. I did not want this."
**Sound:** a single flute.

### 3. The comfort (20 sec)
**Visual:** Rama comes and sits beside Sugriva. He speaks gently. Lakshmana stands behind him. Slowly Sugriva lifts his head.
**Narration:** "Rama came and sat beside him. He spoke to him kindly, as one brother to another. What was done was done. Now Sugriva had a people to care for, and a son of Vali to raise."
**Dialogue:** Rama: "Rise, Sugriva. Your people need their king."
**Sound:** the flute joined by soft strings.

### 4. The preparation (20 sec)
**Visual:** Hanuman bows before Rama and invites him into the city. Rama shakes his head gently and gestures at his bark clothes: he will not enter any town. The monkeys bring golden pots of water, white umbrellas and fans, and the crown, to the open ground outside the city.
**Narration:** "Hanuman asked Rama to come into the city for the crowning. But Rama had promised to live fourteen years in the forest, and would not enter any town. So the monkeys made ready at the city's edge, bringing water in golden pots, a white umbrella and a crown."
**Sound:** busy footsteps, gentle bells.

### 5. The anointing (25 sec)
**Visual:** Sugriva sits on a seat of honour. The elders pour water over his head from golden pots. Then Lakshmana steps forward, at a nod from Rama, lifts the crown and places it on Sugriva's head. Angada stands at his side and is named the heir.
**Narration:** "The elders poured the sacred water over Sugriva, and he was made king of the monkeys. Kamban says that at Rama's word, Lakshmana himself placed the crown on Sugriva's head, as the rules of the learned require. And Vali's son, Angada, was named the prince who would rule after him."
**Sound:** chanting, a soft conch, the cheering of the monkeys, restrained.

### 6. The vow (20 sec)
**Visual:** The new king, crowned, kneels before Rama and joins his hands. Rama raises him. Sugriva looks south, toward where Sita must be.
**Narration:** "Then Sugriva knelt before Rama and renewed his promise. As Rama had helped him, so he would help Rama find Sita, with every monkey in his kingdom."
**Dialogue:** Sugriva: "When the time comes, my whole army is yours. We will find her."
**Sound:** a firm, quiet theme.

### 7. The rain (15 sec)
**Visual:** Dark clouds roll over the hills. The first heavy drops fall. Rama and Lakshmana climb to a cave on a mountainside and sit at its mouth, looking out at the rain.
**Narration:** "But the rains had come, and no army could march. So Rama waited in a mountain cave, and watched the rain, and thought of Sita."
**Sound:** thunder, then the steady sound of rain; the flute again.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri touches this moment in one line, and it can be read two ways. In song 128 (Palani) Murugan is the nephew of Raghava, who graciously gave the long crown and the kingdom to the younger brother; that brother may be Sugriva or Vibhishana.

- **Quiet, not triumphant.** Vali's death hangs over it.
