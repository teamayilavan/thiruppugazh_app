# Rama's Anger at the Ocean

`ramayana-ocean-arrow` | Vishnu | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: stern, mighty, then resolved | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

When the sea-god does not appear Rama takes his bow against the ocean; frightened, the sea-god advises him how to build the bridge.

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Rama, Lakshmana, Sugriva.**
- **The sea-god:** a majestic figure rising from waves.

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it.

### 1. The shore (25 sec)
**Visual:** The southern shore at sunset. The monkey army fills the beach as far as the eye can see. Before them stretches the endless ocean, wave after wave. Rama stands at the water's edge with Lakshmana and Sugriva, looking south toward Lanka, which cannot be seen.
**Narration:** "The army had reached the end of the land. Somewhere beyond the waves lay Lanka, and Sita. But how could an army cross the ocean? Vibhishana, Ravana's own brother, now Rama's friend, gave counsel. Ask the god of the sea to show you the way."
**Sound:** great waves; seabirds; a quiet, waiting melody.

### 2. The prayer (25 sec)
**Visual:** Rama spreads a bed of kusha grass on the sand facing the sea. He lies down on it, hands folded, eyes on the waves. Night falls; the stars wheel; dawn comes. Again night, again dawn. Three times.
**Narration:** "So Rama spread a bed of sacred grass on the shore. He lay down facing the sea, his hands folded, and he waited for the god of the ocean to come. One night passed. Then a second. Then a third."
**Sound:** the waves, steady and patient; soft temple bells at each dawn.

### 3. The silence (20 sec)
**Visual:** The third dawn. Rama rises. The sea rolls on, careless and empty. His calm face slowly changes; his eyes grow bright, like the sun.
**Narration:** "But the sea gave no answer. Kamban says Rama thought: we have not even had a reply, a yes or a no. And for the first time, his patience came to an end. His face shone like the sun at the end of an age."
**Sound:** the waves only; a low note begins to rise.

### 4. The bow (20 sec)
**Visual:** Rama turns to Lakshmana and holds out his hand. Lakshmana, his own face flushed, gives him the great bow and the quiver. Rama ties on the quiver, fits an arrow, and draws the string to his ear. The twang of the string rolls like thunder over the sea.
**Narration:** "Rama turned to his brother."
**Dialogue:** Rama: "Lakshmana, bring my bow. If the sea will not listen, I will dry it up, and the army will walk to Lanka."
**Narration (continued):** "He drew the string, and its sound rolled across the waters like thunder."
**Sound:** the deep twang of the string, echoing like thunder.

### 5. The fire (25 sec)
**Visual:** Arrows like streaks of flame fly into the ocean. The water begins to churn; waves rise and fall back; a ripple of steam spreads over the surface. Fish, turtles and sea serpents swim in fear. The whole ocean trembles, but the image stays restrained: steam and light, not destruction.
**Narration:** "His arrows flew into the ocean like fire. The waters churned and began to steam. The fish and the sea serpents fled in fear. Kamban says the whole deep sea seemed like a sacrificial fire, with its waters for the offering. The ocean trembled from end to end."
**Sound:** hissing steam, rising waves, a fierce drum.

### 6. The god (25 sec)
**Visual:** The sea parts at its centre. Out of the waves rises the god of the ocean, majestic, crowned with pearls and coral, his robes flowing like water, attended by rivers in the form of women. He folds his hands and bows low to Rama.
**Narration:** "Then the ocean itself rose up. Out of the waves came the god of the sea, crowned with pearls and coral, and he bowed low before Rama, with folded hands."
**Dialogue:** The sea-god: "Forgive me, Lord. I cannot change my nature. I am deep and wide, and no one can make me shallow. But I will show you the way across."
**Sound:** the waves fall quiet; a deep, reverent chord.

### 7. The advice (25 sec)
**Visual:** The sea-god points to Nala, a monkey builder in the army, who steps forward shyly. Stones on the shore glint. Rama lowers his bow; the anger leaves his face. The sea grows calm and blue again.
**Narration:** "There is one in your army, said the sea-god, named Nala. Let him build a bridge across my waters, and I will hold it up. Rama's anger left him, and the sea was calm again. The way to Lanka was open."
**Sound:** gentle waves; the opening melody returns, hopeful.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Six songs recall Rama's arrow against the ocean, naming Murugan the nephew of the Lord whose single arrow made the sea cry out. Show song 1125 (a general song, Pothu Padalgal), song 738 (Thiruvathigai) and song 820 (Thiruvarur).

## Production notes

- **Do not boil the sea in full scale.** A ripple of steam is enough.
