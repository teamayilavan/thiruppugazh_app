# Rama's Anger at the Ocean

`ramayana-ocean-arrow` | Vishnu | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: stern, mighty, then resolved

## One-line story

When the sea-god does not appear Rama takes his bow against the ocean; frightened, the sea-god advises him how to build the bridge.

## The storyline

### 1. The shore (25 sec)

### 2. The prayer (25 sec)

### 3. The silence (20 sec)

**Narration:** "The sea did not answer."

### 4. The bow (20 sec)

### 5. The fire (25 sec)
Arrows of fire; the sea boils.
**Narration:** "The sea began to boil."

### 6. The god (25 sec)
The sea-god rises, hands folded.
**Narration:** "The sea-god came."

### 7. The advice (25 sec)
He tells how to build a bridge.
**Narration:** "And he told them how to build the bridge."
