# The Dropped Ornaments (Sita's Jewels)

`ramayana-sita-jewels` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: hopeful, sad, a clue

## One-line story

As Ravana carries her off Sita drops her ornaments and upper cloth among the monkeys; Sugriva later shows them to Rama.

## Characters

- **Sita:** in the chariot.

- **Rama.**

## The storyline

### 1. The sky (20 sec)
The chariot flies over a range of hills.
**Narration:** "Ravana carried Sita over the hills."

### 2. The thought (20 sec)
Sita sees five monkeys on a peak and has an idea.
**Narration:** "She saw five monkeys on a mountain."

### 3. The bundle (25 sec)
She ties her ornaments in her cloth and drops it.
**Narration:** "She dropped her jewels."

### 4. The fall (20 sec)
The bundle falls through the air onto the peak.
**Narration:** "They fell among the monkeys."

### 5. The keeping (20 sec)
The monkeys keep it safely.
**Narration:** "The monkeys kept them."

### 6. The meeting (25 sec)
Months later, Rama meets Sugriva; he is shown the bundle.
**Narration:** "Later, Sugriva showed them to Rama."

### 7. The recognising (20 sec)
Rama holds them, tears running.
**Narration:** "And Rama knew they were hers."
