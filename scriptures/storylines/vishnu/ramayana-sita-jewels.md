# The Dropped Ornaments (Sita's Jewels)

`ramayana-sita-jewels` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: hopeful, sad, a clue | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

As Ravana carries her off Sita drops her ornaments and upper cloth among the monkeys; Sugriva later shows them to Rama.

## Characters

- **Sita:** in the chariot.
- **Five monkeys on a hill** (Sugriva and his friends).
- **Rama.**

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it. This is a film of hope: the jewels are a trail.

### 1. The sky (20 sec)
**Visual:** High above the southern forests, Ravana's chariot races through the clouds. Sita sits in it, held fast, her face wet with tears but her eyes open and searching the land below. Hills, rivers and forests rush past.
**Narration:** "Ravana was carrying Sita away through the sky, south toward Lanka. She had called out for Rama and for Lakshmana, but no one answered. Yet Sita did not give up. She looked down at the land below, searching."
**Sound:** rushing wind, a high anxious string line.

### 2. The thought (20 sec)
**Visual:** Below, on a mountain top, Sita sees five monkeys sitting together, looking up. Her face changes: an idea. She glances at Ravana, who is looking ahead, and quietly begins to slip off her bangles and anklets.
**Narration:** "On a mountain top she saw five monkeys sitting together, looking up at the sky. And a thought came to her. If Rama ever came this way searching for her, perhaps they could tell him which way she had gone."
**Sound:** the strings soften; a single hopeful note.

### 3. The bundle (25 sec)
**Visual:** Close on Sita's hands, hidden from Ravana. She takes off her golden ornaments one by one, the bangles, the necklace, the anklets, and ties them into a bundle with her yellow silk upper cloth. Her hands are quick and steady.
**Narration:** "Without Ravana seeing, she took off her golden ornaments, her bangles and her anklets and her necklace. She wrapped them in her silken upper cloth, and tied it into a little bundle. Her hands did not shake."
**Sound:** the faint chime of gold, under the wind.

### 4. The fall (20 sec)
**Visual:** Sita leans out and lets the bundle fall. The yellow silk tumbles down through the clouds, catching the sunlight, and lands among the rocks right in front of the five monkeys. They stare at it, then up at the vanishing chariot.
**Narration:** "Then she let it fall. Down through the clouds it tumbled, a flash of yellow and gold, and landed among the monkeys on the mountain. They looked up, but the chariot was already gone."
**Sound:** a falling whistle, a soft thud, then silence.

### 5. The keeping (20 sec)
**Visual:** One of the five, Sugriva, picks up the bundle carefully and looks at it. Hanuman stands beside him. Sugriva carries it into a cave and puts it away safely in a niche in the rock.
**Narration:** "The leader of the five was Sugriva, and beside him was Hanuman. Sugriva did not know whose jewels they were. But he kept them safe in his cave, as if he knew someone would come for them."
**Sound:** a gentle, waiting melody.

### 6. The meeting (25 sec)
**Visual:** Months later. Rama and Lakshmana, weary and travel-stained, sit with Sugriva and Hanuman on the same hill. Sugriva speaks, then goes into the cave and comes back with the yellow bundle. He lays it in Rama's hands.
**Narration:** "A long time later, Rama and Lakshmana came to those hills, searching for Sita, and made friends with Sugriva. Then Sugriva remembered. He went into his cave and brought out the bundle, and laid it in Rama's hands."
**Dialogue:** Sugriva: "We saw a lady carried through the sky, crying your name. She dropped these."
**Sound:** the waiting melody returns, fuller.

### 7. The recognising (20 sec)
**Visual:** Rama opens the cloth. He sees the ornaments and presses them to his heart, weeping. He shows them to Lakshmana. Lakshmana looks at the bangles and earrings and shakes his head; then at the anklets, and nods, his eyes filling.
**Narration:** "Rama knew them at once, and held them to his heart and wept. Lakshmana looked and said he did not know the bangles or the earrings. But the anklets he knew, for every day he had bowed at Sita's feet."
**Dialogue:** Lakshmana: "These anklets I know. I saw them every day, when I bowed at her feet."
**Sound:** the melody resolves, sad and hopeful at once.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

The link here is uncertain. Song 1325 (Punavayil) has been connected with this episode; it calls Murugan the beloved nephew of Narayana, but its images may belong to Vishnu's measuring of the worlds rather than to Sita's jewels.
