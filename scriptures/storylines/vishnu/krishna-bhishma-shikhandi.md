# The Fall of Bhishma

`krishna-bhishma-shikhandi` | Vishnu | running time 2:35 (7 scenes) | tone: solemn, tragic, noble | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

In the Bharata war Arjuna shoots Bhishma, with Shikhandi placed in front of him.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| In the Bharata war Arjuna shoots Bhishma, with Shikhandi placed in front of him | Mahabharata (Critical Edition) 6,114.53 |
| Song 155 recalls it | Thiruppugazh song 155 |
| Bhishma's vow not to fight Shikhandi and his bed of arrows are the widely told details | Mahabharata, Bhishma Parva (traditional source; not read verse by verse here) |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration. No arrow-wounds are shown.

## Characters

- **Bhishma:** an old, majestic warrior, white-haired.
- **Arjuna:** grave, reluctant.
- **Shikhandi:** a warrior, strong and grim.
- **Krishna.**

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it.

### 1. The invincible (25 sec)
**Visual:** Kurukshetra, the tenth day of the war. Dust, banners, the sound of conches. In the middle of the Kaurava army stands a tall chariot with a palm-tree banner; on it an old warrior with white hair and a white beard, calm as a mountain. Arrows fly from his bow like a steady rain, and the Pandava lines bend back before him.
**Narration:** "For nine days of the great war, one old man held back the whole Pandava army. He was Bhishma, the grandsire of both families, the eldest of the Kurus. He had vowed long ago never to marry and never to take the throne, and he fought now for the side he did not love, because it was his duty."
**Sound:** conches, a low drum, the hiss of arrows.

### 2. The vow (20 sec)
**Visual:** Night. In Bhishma's tent, a lamp. The five Pandavas and Krishna come quietly and bow to him. The old man blesses them, then speaks gently. Behind him, in the shadow, the faint shape of a woman: Amba, from an old story.
**Narration:** "Tradition tells that the Pandavas went to him by night and asked him how he could be overcome. He told them himself. He would never raise his bow against Shikhandi, who had been born a woman. When Shikhandi stood before him, he would lay down his weapons."
**Dialogue:** Bhishma: "Put Shikhandi in front of you. I will not fight him."
**Sound:** a lamp's flicker; the old man's quiet voice.

### 3. The plan (20 sec)
**Visual:** Dawn. Arjuna's chariot, with Krishna holding the reins. Shikhandi, grim and strong, takes his place in front. Arjuna's face is heavy; he looks at the old man across the field the way a boy looks at his grandfather.
**Narration:** "So it was done. On the tenth day Shikhandi rode in front, and Arjuna came behind him, with Krishna driving the chariot. Arjuna's heart was heavy. As a child he had climbed into this old man's lap."
**Sound:** a single drum, slow.

### 4. The silence (20 sec)
**Visual:** Bhishma sees Shikhandi before him. He lowers his bow. Around him the battle roars, but on his chariot everything is still. He does not raise his weapon again.
**Narration:** "Bhishma saw Shikhandi before him, and he lowered his bow. The battle roared on every side, but the grandsire stood still and would not fight. He kept his word, even now."
**Sound:** the battle noise falls away to a single sustained note.

### 5. The arrows (25 sec)
**Visual:** From behind Shikhandi, Arjuna's arrows fly as rays of light. They come in a steady stream, and they settle into the old warrior's armour like sunlight. Bhishma does not flinch; he looks across at Arjuna with something like pride. No wounds are shown; only light.
**Narration:** "Then, with Shikhandi in front of him, Arjuna loosed his arrows at Bhishma. The old man knew whose arrows they were. The Mahabharata tells us he said they were Arjuna's, not Shikhandi's, for no other archer could strike like that."
**Sound:** a rushing like wind; the music swells and breaks.

### 6. The bed (25 sec)
**Visual:** Slowly, as the sun moves toward evening, Bhishma falls from his chariot. He does not touch the ground: he lies on a bed of shining arrows, held up between earth and sky. Both armies stop. Warriors from both sides lay down their weapons and come to stand around him.
**Narration:** "At last, as the sun went down, the grandsire fell from his chariot. But he did not touch the earth. He lay on a bed of arrows, held up between earth and sky. And the war stopped. Warriors from both sides laid down their weapons and gathered around him."
**Sound:** silence, then a soft lament on a single flute.

### 7. The salute (20 sec)
**Visual:** Arjuna kneels by the old man's head. At Bhishma's request he shoots three arrows into the ground to make a pillow for his head. Krishna stands a little apart, hands folded. Bhishma looks up at the sky, waiting.
**Narration:** "Arjuna knelt by his grandfather and made him a pillow of arrows, as he asked. Bhishma had been granted the power to choose the hour of his death. He would wait there, the tradition says, until the sun turned north."
**Dialogue:** Bhishma: "You have done well, my child."
**Sound:** a single bell; evening birds.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Song 155 (Palani) recalls, in a single line whose reading is uncertain, the Lord who drove the chariot, Shikhandi, and the Kuru land given to the Pandavas. Murugan is the nephew of Vishnu, the Pandavas' friend.

## Production notes

- **Treat Bhishma with reverence.**
- **No blood.** The arrows are rays of light.
