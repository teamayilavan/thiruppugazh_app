# The Fall of Bhishma

`krishna-bhishma-shikhandi` | Vishnu | running time 2:35 (7 scenes) | tone: solemn, tragic, noble

## One-line story

In the Bharata war Arjuna shoots Bhishma, with Shikhandi placed in front of him.

## Characters

- **Arjuna:** grave, reluctant.

## The storyline

### 1. The invincible (25 sec)
Bhishma on his chariot, scattering armies.
**Narration:** "No one could stand before Bhishma."

### 2. The vow (20 sec)
He will not fight Shikhandi.
**Narration:** "But he would never fight Shikhandi."

### 3. The plan (20 sec)
Krishna and Arjuna look at the field, quiet.
**Narration:** "So Shikhandi was placed in front."

### 4. The silence (20 sec)
Bhishma lowers his bow.
**Narration:** "Bhishma lowered his bow."

### 5. The arrows (25 sec)
Arjuna's arrows fly, in light.
**Narration:** "And Arjuna shot, from behind him."

### 6. The bed (25 sec)
Bhishma falls; the arrows hold him above the ground.
**Narration:** "Bhishma fell upon a bed of arrows."

### 7. The salute (20 sec)
The warriors lay down their weapons and bow.
**Narration:** "And the field fell silent."
