# The Mace Duel (The Fall of Duryodhana)

`krishna-duryodhana-fall` | Vishnu | running time 2:35 (7 scenes) | tone: bitter, fierce, a vow fulfilled

## One-line story

In the mace duel Bhima smashes Duryodhana's thighs, fulfilling the vow he made at the dice game.

## The storyline

### 1. The dice game (25 sec)
A flashback: Duryodhana mocks Draupadi; Bhima swears to break his thighs.
**Narration:** "At the dice game, Bhima had made a vow."

### 2. The lake (20 sec)
Duryodhana, alone, hides in a lake after the war.
**Narration:** "At the end of the war Duryodhana hid in a lake."

### 3. The challenge (20 sec)
The Pandavas call him out; he comes up with his mace.
**Narration:** "He came out to fight."

### 4. The duel (30 sec)
Bhima and Duryodhana circle, swinging maces.
**Narration:** "The two maces met."

### 5. The blow (25 sec)
Bhima's mace falls on Duryodhana's thighs.
**Narration:** "Bhima's mace struck him down."

### 6. The fall (15 sec)
Duryodhana crashes.
**Narration:** "And Duryodhana fell."

### 7. The silence (20 sec)
The field is hushed.
**Narration:** "And the vow was fulfilled."
