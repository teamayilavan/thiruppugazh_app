# The Mace Duel (The Fall of Duryodhana)

`krishna-duryodhana-fall` | Vishnu | running time 2:35 (7 scenes) | tone: bitter, fierce, a vow fulfilled | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

In the mace duel Bhima smashes Duryodhana's thighs, fulfilling the vow he made at the dice game.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|

| It fulfils the vow he made at the dice game | same |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration. The blow is shown in silhouette.

## Characters

- **Bhima:** huge, grim.
- **Duryodhana:** proud, wounded.
- **Krishna, Balarama.**

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it.

### 1. The dice game (25 sec)
**Visual:** Years earlier. The great hall of Hastinapura, lamplit and crowded. The dice have fallen; the Pandavas sit with bowed heads, having lost everything. Draupadi stands in the hall, grief and anger in her eyes. Duryodhana, proud and mocking, slaps his own thigh in an insulting gesture toward her (shown from behind, in shadow). Bhima rises, enormous, trembling with rage.
**Narration:** "This story begins years before the war, in the hall of the dice game. The Pandavas had lost everything, even their honour. And Duryodhana, mocking, showed his thigh to Queen Draupadi. Then Bhima stood up, and made a terrible vow."
**Dialogue:** Bhima: "With my mace I will break that thigh in battle."
**Sound:** a hush in the hall; one heavy drumbeat.

### 2. The lake (20 sec)
**Visual:** Thirteen years of exile and then the war have passed. The battlefield of Kurukshetra at evening, silent, smoking. Duryodhana, alone, his armies gone, walks into a still lake and hides beneath the water. Reeds close over the place.
**Narration:** "Many years later, at the end of the great war, Duryodhana's armies were gone. His brothers were dead. Alone, he went down into a still lake and hid himself beneath the water."
**Sound:** wind over an empty field; water closing.

### 3. The challenge (20 sec)
**Visual:** The Pandavas and Krishna stand at the lakeside. Yudhishthira calls out. The water stirs; Duryodhana rises, mace in hand, still proud. Balarama, Krishna's elder brother, arrives from his pilgrimage to watch, for both men were his students in the mace.
**Narration:** "The Pandavas found him. Yudhishthira called to him to come out and fight one of them. Duryodhana rose from the water with his mace. He chose to fight Bhima. And Balarama, who had taught them both, came to watch."
**Dialogue:** Duryodhana: "I am alone, but I am not afraid."
**Sound:** water streaming; a conch, low.

### 4. The duel (30 sec)
**Visual:** The two giants circle each other on open ground. Maces flash in silhouette against a red sky. Sparks fly when they clash. Duryodhana is quicker and more skilful; again and again he slips past Bhima's blows. Bhima staggers. Arjuna, worried, turns to Krishna.
**Narration:** "They fought like two bulls, and the ground shook. Bhima was the stronger, but Duryodhana was the more skilful, and he would not be beaten. Then Arjuna asked Krishna which of them would win. Krishna answered that if Bhima fought fairly, he could not. Let him remember his vow."
**Sound:** the clash of iron, grunts, a racing drum.

### 5. The blow (25 sec)
**Visual:** Arjuna catches Bhima's eye and strikes his own left thigh with his hand. Bhima understands. Duryodhana leaps high; Bhima swings low. The blow is shown only as two silhouettes and a flash of light against the sunset. The sound cuts out.
**Narration:** "So Arjuna struck his own thigh, where Bhima could see. Bhima understood. When Duryodhana leapt into the air, Bhima swung his mace low, against the rules of the mace fight, and broke his thighs, as he had sworn in the hall long ago."
**Sound:** a single crack, then silence.

### 6. The fall (15 sec)
**Visual:** Duryodhana falls to the earth, a dark shape against the red sky. Balarama raises his plough in anger; Krishna holds his arm and speaks to him quietly.
**Narration:** "Duryodhana fell. Balarama cried out in anger that the rules had been broken. Krishna calmed his brother: a vow had been kept, and the war was over."
**Sound:** a low drone; the wind returns.

### 7. The silence (20 sec)
**Visual:** Dusk on the empty field. The Pandavas turn away; nobody cheers. The camera stays on the fallen king, his crown in the dust, then rises slowly to the first stars.
**Narration:** "There was no joy in it. The Pandavas walked away in silence. A vow made in anger had been fulfilled at last, and the long war of the Bharatas was over."
**Sound:** a single bell; the last light fades.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Song 1135 recalls, in a single line, Hari who brought about the Bharata victory in which Suyodhana fell. Murugan is that Hari's nephew.

## Production notes

- **A dark film.** Keep it grave, never celebratory.
- **No close-ups of the blow.**
