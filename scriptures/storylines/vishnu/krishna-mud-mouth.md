# The Universe in His Mouth

`krishna-mud-mouth` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: comic, then awestruck, then tender | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

Accused of eating mud, Krishna opens his mouth and Yashoda sees the whole universe inside.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Accused of eating mud, Krishna opens his mouth and Yashoda sees the whole universe inside | Bhagavata Purana 10.8.32-45 |
| Songs 674 and 1170 recall it | Thiruppugazh songs |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Krishna:** a small boy.
- **Yashoda.**
- **His friends** who tell on him.

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; playful at first, hushed in the vision, tender at the end. Where a scene has no voice, the music carries it.

### 1. The accusation (20 sec)
**Visual:** A sunny lane in Gokula. Little boys and Balarama play among the cows. In a quiet corner, toddler Krishna squats by a patch of earth, cheeks bulging, eyes innocent. The boys spot him and race off to his house in a noisy crowd.
**Narration:** "One day little Krishna was playing with Balarama and the other cowherd boys. Then all his friends came running to his mother together, with something important to tell her."
**Dialogue:** The boys: "Mother! Mother! Krishna has been eating mud!"
**Sound:** children's laughter, cowbells, running feet.

### 2. The question (20 sec)
**Visual:** Yashoda drops her work and scoops Krishna up by the hand. Her face is half cross and half frightened. She bends down to look at him; he looks away, very busy with his own toes.
**Narration:** "Yashoda, always anxious for her son, caught him by the hand. She meant to scold him, and to look into his mouth, but her eyes were full of worry."
**Dialogue:** Yashoda: "Why are you so naughty? Why do you eat mud in a corner? Your friends say so, and so does your own brother."
**Sound:** a comic woodwind phrase; the boys giggling behind her.

### 3. The denial (20 sec)
**Visual:** Krishna puts on his most honest face and shakes his head. He points at the boys, who protest. He folds his little arms, then opens his mouth wide.
**Dialogue:** Krishna: "Mother, I never ate any mud. They are all telling lies. If you think they are telling the truth, look in my mouth and see for yourself."
**Narration:** "So Yashoda said, open your mouth, then. And the little boy opened his mouth wide."
**Sound:** a playful pause, a single plucked note.

### 4. The mouth (30 sec)
**Visual:** The camera moves past his small teeth into darkness, and the darkness opens: stars, the turning sky, the moon, wind and fire, oceans and islands, mountains, rivers and forests, the whole earth. Then, deep inside, Gokula itself; the lane; and Yashoda herself, bending over her child, looking in.
**Narration:** "And in that little mouth she saw everything. The sky and all its directions. The mountains, the islands and the oceans. The wind, the fire, the moon and the stars. Everything that moves and everything that is still. And there, in the middle of it all, she saw her own village, and herself, looking in."
**Sound:** all sound falls away; a vast slow chord, like breath.

### 5. The mother (25 sec)
**Visual:** Yashoda stands frozen, her hand still on his chin. Her face fills with wonder, then fear, then a kind of prayer. The child stands before her, very still, with a light around him she has never noticed.
**Narration:** "Yashoda trembled. Was this a dream? Was it some magic? Or was it the power of her own little child? She understood that this was the Lord from whom all things come, and that she and everything she called her own belonged to him. She bowed her head."
**Sound:** a single bell, held long.

### 6. The forgetting (20 sec)
**Visual:** Krishna smiles. A soft golden mist drifts down over Yashoda like a veil. Her face relaxes; the wonder fades from her eyes; she blinks, and sees only her small, mud-smudged son.
**Narration:** "Then the Lord drew a veil of his own gentle power over her. She forgot what she had seen. And her heart filled again with a mother's love, only stronger than before."
**Sound:** the chord softens into the lullaby melody.

### 7. The cuddle (15 sec)
**Visual:** Yashoda lifts him onto her lap and wipes his face with the end of her sari, laughing. The friends drift back to their play. Krishna leans against her, content.
**Narration:** "She took him on her lap as before. The sages seek him in the Vedas. She held him as her little boy."
**Sound:** the lullaby; a cow lows; birdsong.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri speaks in two songs of Murugan's uncle as the Lord who ate the earth, a phrase tradition links with this story of the child's open mouth. Songs to show: 674 (Thiruvalangadu) and 1170 (a general song).

## Production notes

- **A wonder, then a hush.** The universe shot is the film.
- **Keep Yashoda's love** central.
