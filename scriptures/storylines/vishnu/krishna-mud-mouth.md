# The Universe in His Mouth

`krishna-mud-mouth` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: comic, then awestruck, then tender

## One-line story

Accused of eating mud, Krishna opens his mouth and Yashoda sees the whole universe inside.

## Characters

- **Krishna:** a small boy.
- **Yashoda.**
- **His friends** who tell on him.

## The storyline

### 1. The accusation (20 sec)
His friends run to Yashoda: "Krishna ate mud!"
**Narration:** "His friends told on him."

### 2. The question (20 sec)
She asks him sternly.
**Narration:** "His mother asked him: 'Did you eat mud?'"

### 3. The denial (20 sec)
"No, mother. Look in my mouth."
**Narration:** "He said: 'Look in my mouth.'"

### 4. The mouth (30 sec)
He opens it; inside, stars, planets, oceans, all the worlds.
**Narration:** "And she saw the whole universe."

### 5. The mother (25 sec)
She stares, dazed.
**Narration:** "She saw the earth, the sky, the stars... and herself."

### 6. The forgetting (20 sec)
A soft mist, and she forgets.
**Narration:** "Then her love made her forget."

### 7. The cuddle (15 sec)
She holds her little son.
**Narration:** "And she held her little boy."
