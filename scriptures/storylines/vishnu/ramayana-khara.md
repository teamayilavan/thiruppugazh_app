# Fourteen Thousand Against One (Khara and Dushana)

`ramayana-khara` | Vishnu | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: ominous, calm, overwhelming skill | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

After Surpanakha is mutilated, her brother Khara marches with fourteen thousand rakshasas; Rama alone destroys them and their generals Dushana and Trishira.

## Characters

- **Rama and Lakshmana.**
- **Sita.**
- **Khara, Dushana, Trishira:** demon generals.

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it.

### 1. The complaint (25 sec)
**Visual:** The rakshasa stronghold of Janasthana, dark towers among dry forests. Surpanakha, Ravana's sister, comes before her brother Khara, her face covered with her hands, crying out in rage and pain. Khara rises from his seat, furious. Around him stand his generals, Dushana and three-headed Trishira.
**Narration:** "In the forest of Dandaka, Ravana's sister Surpanakha had tried to harm Sita, and Lakshmana had driven her away, wounded. She ran to her brother Khara, who ruled that part of the forest with a great army of rakshasas."
**Dialogue:** Surpanakha: "Two princes of men have shamed me. Avenge me!"
**Sound:** a low growl of drums; harsh wind.

### 2. The march (25 sec)
**Visual:** The gates of Janasthana open. A dark host pours out: chariots, banners, spears, shown as a vast shadow moving across the land, raising dust that darkens the sky. Khara rides at the head in a great chariot, with Dushana and Trishira beside him. Birds scatter; omens flash in the sky.
**Narration:** "Khara gathered fourteen thousand rakshasas, with his generals Dushana and Trishira, and marched against the hermitage. The dust of that army darkened the sky. Even the birds and beasts fled before it."
**Sound:** pounding drums, the rumble of chariots.

### 3. The cave (20 sec)
**Visual:** At the hermitage of Panchavati, Rama hears the distant thunder of the army. He calmly ties on his armour and quiver. He speaks to Lakshmana, who leads Sita to a sheltered cave in the hills.
**Narration:** "Rama heard them coming. He did not hurry and he did not fear. He asked Lakshmana to take Sita to a cave in the hills and guard her there. He would meet them alone."
**Dialogue:** Rama: "Take Sita to the cave, brother. I will face them here."
**Sound:** the drums continue far off; a quiet, steady note near Rama.

### 4. The wait (20 sec)
**Visual:** Rama stands alone in a clearing, bow in hand, perfectly still. Behind him, unseen to the rakshasas, gods and sages gather on the clouds to watch. The dark tide appears on the horizon.
**Narration:** "Rama stood alone before the hermitage, his bow in his hand. The gods and sages gathered in the sky to watch. One man against fourteen thousand. They held their breath."
**Sound:** wind; a long held breath of silence.

### 5. The storm (30 sec)
**Visual:** Fully stylised. The dark host surges forward like a wave. Rama draws, and draws again; streams of golden light leave his bow like rain falling from a dark cloud. Where they meet the dark tide, it breaks apart into drifting smoke and scattered banners. Rama hardly moves; only his hands and the light are fast.
**Narration:** "The rakshasas rushed at him from every side, and their weapons fell on him like rain. But Rama's arrows answered them, faster still. Kamban says he poured out arrows like a cloud pouring down rain. And the great dark army began to melt away."
**Sound:** a rushing storm of strings and drums, a choir rising.

### 6. The generals (20 sec)
**Visual:** Silhouettes only. Three-headed Trishira charges in his chariot; a flash of light, and his shadow falls. Dushana charges; a flash, and he falls. Last comes Khara, alone now, raging. A single arrow of light flies; Khara's shadow falls.
**Narration:** "Then the generals came. Trishira of the three heads fell, and Dushana fell. Last of all came Khara himself, and Valmiki says Rama's arrow struck him in the chest, and he fell like a great tree."
**Sound:** three short, deep drum strokes, then silence.

### 7. The calm (20 sec)
**Visual:** The smoke clears over an empty field. Flowers fall from the sky. Rama lowers his bow. Lakshmana and Sita come down from the cave; Sita runs to Rama, overjoyed that he is safe, and embraces him.
**Narration:** "The field was quiet. Flowers fell from the sky, and the sages blessed him. Sita came running from the cave, full of joy to see him safe. And Arunagiri sings of the Lord who, with his bow, destroyed Khara and his host."
**Sound:** soft bells, a gentle rising melody.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Two songs recall the battle in the forest. Song 961 (Madurai) praises Mukunda who with his bow destroyed Khara and Trishira; song 1295 (a general song, Pothu Padalgal) praises the Lord with the conch whose arrows destroyed the rakshasa clan. Murugan is his nephew.

## Production notes

- **A film of calm power.** Do not show the host in detail.
- **Rama's speed** is shown as light, not bodies.
