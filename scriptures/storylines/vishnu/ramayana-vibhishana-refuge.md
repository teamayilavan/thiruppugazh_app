# The Brother Who Left Lanka (Vibhishana Seeks Refuge)

`ramayana-vibhishana-refuge` | Vishnu | running time 2:35 (7 scenes) | tone: tense, suspicious, generous | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

Ravana's brother Vibhishana, rejected for urging the return of Sita, joins Rama and is given refuge.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |

| He joins Rama and is given refuge | same |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Vibhishana:** a righteous demon, calm.
- **Ravana.**
- **Rama, Sugriva, Hanuman.**

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it. Rama's generosity is the centre of the film; show Hanuman's trust.

### 1. The counsel (25 sec)
**Visual:** Ravana's council hall in Lanka, golden and dim. Ministers boast of war. One figure rises, calm and dignified: Vibhishana, Ravana's younger brother, dark-skinned, in simple white. He bows to the throne and speaks earnestly, hands open.
**Narration:** "Rama's army was gathering on the far shore. In Lanka, Ravana's ministers boasted that they would crush it. Only one spoke otherwise: Vibhishana, Ravana's own younger brother. Again and again he begged the king to give Sita back and make peace with Rama."
**Dialogue:** Vibhishana: "Brother, return Sita to Rama. Save yourself, and save Lanka."
**Sound:** the murmur of the hall, a tense drone.

### 2. The rejection (20 sec)
**Visual:** Ravana rises in fury, his ten faces dark. He points at Vibhishana and shouts. The ministers look away. Vibhishana stands still, sorrowful, and folds his hands once more.
**Narration:** "But Ravana would not listen. He called his brother a traitor and an enemy in his own house, and told him to be gone. Vibhishana did not answer in anger. Kamban says he said only: I have told you what is good in every way I could; forgive me, and he left."
**Dialogue:** Vibhishana: "I have told you what is good. You will not hear it. Forgive me, brother."
**Sound:** Ravana's roar, then a heavy silence.

### 3. The flight (20 sec)
**Visual:** Vibhishana rises into the night sky above Lanka with four loyal companions. They fly across the dark sea toward the far shore, where the campfires of Rama's army glitter in long rows.
**Narration:** "So Vibhishana left Lanka, and all he had there. Four faithful companions went with him. They flew across the sea in the night, toward the camp of the man his brother called an enemy."
**Sound:** wind over the waves, a lonely melody.

### 4. The doubt (25 sec)
**Visual:** Vibhishana hovers in the sky above the camp, hands folded, calling down. The monkeys leap up, grabbing trees and rocks. Sugriva hurries to Rama, alarmed, and points upward.
**Narration:** "He stayed in the sky above the camp and called out that he had come to Rama for refuge. The monkeys seized trees and rocks. Sugriva ran to Rama in alarm. This is Ravana's brother, he said. He has come to spy on us, or to kill us. He must not be trusted."
**Dialogue:** Sugriva: "Rama, he is Ravana's brother. Do not let him in."
**Sound:** a rising clamour, alarm drums.

### 5. The question (20 sec)
**Visual:** Rama sits calmly and asks each of the leaders in turn. Some shake their heads. Then Hanuman steps forward, bows, and speaks, his face open and sure.
**Narration:** "Rama asked each of his counsellors what they thought, and most of them were wary. Then Hanuman spoke. He had seen Vibhishana in Lanka. This was a good man, he said; his words were honest, and he had come in good faith."
**Dialogue:** Hanuman: "I have seen this man. His heart is good. He has come to you in truth."
**Sound:** the drums fall away; a warm theme for Hanuman.

### 6. The refuge (25 sec)
**Visual:** Rama rises. He looks up at Vibhishana in the sky and raises his hand in welcome. His face is open and kind. Sugriva lowers his eyes, then nods.
**Narration:** "Then Rama gave his answer, and it has been remembered ever since. Whoever comes to me even once, he said, and says I am yours, I give him safety from every being. That is my vow. Even if Ravana himself came, I would not turn him away."
**Dialogue:** Rama: "Bring him to me. I will never turn away one who comes for refuge."
**Sound:** a single clear bell, then a broad, warm melody.

### 7. The welcome (20 sec)
**Visual:** Vibhishana comes down and falls at Rama's feet. Rama raises him and embraces him. Lakshmana, Sugriva and Hanuman gather round. Rama places a hand on Vibhishana's shoulder and speaks.
**Narration:** "Vibhishana came down and fell at Rama's feet, and Rama raised him up. Kamban gives Rama these words: we were five brothers with Guha, six with Sugriva, and now, with you, who came in love, we are seven."
**Dialogue:** Rama: "Now, with you, we are seven brothers."
**Sound:** the melody swells and resolves; gentle conches.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri names the refuge in song 623 (Kunrakkudi): the noble younger brother of Kumbhakarna came before Rama and bowed, and gladly told him the secrets of Lanka. Murugan is the nephew of the cloud-hued Lord who won.
