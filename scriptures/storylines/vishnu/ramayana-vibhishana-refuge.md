# The Brother Who Left Lanka (Vibhishana Seeks Refuge)

`ramayana-vibhishana-refuge` | Vishnu | running time 2:35 (7 scenes) | tone: tense, suspicious, generous

## One-line story

Ravana's brother Vibhishana, rejected for urging the return of Sita, joins Rama and is given refuge.

## The storyline

### 1. The counsel (25 sec)
In Ravana's court Vibhishana urges: "Return Sita."
**Narration:** "Vibhishana advised his brother to return Sita."

### 2. The rejection (20 sec)
Ravana kicks him from the court.
**Narration:** "Ravana would not listen."

### 3. The flight (20 sec)
Vibhishana flies over the sea with four companions.
**Narration:** "He crossed the sea."

### 4. The doubt (25 sec)
The monkey chiefs mistrust a demon.
**Narration:** "The monkeys doubted him."

### 5. The question (20 sec)
Rama asks: "Shall we receive him?"
**Narration:** "Rama asked what they thought."

### 6. The refuge (25 sec)
Rama says: "I give refuge to all who seek it."
**Narration:** "Rama said: 'I never refuse one who comes for refuge.'"

### 7. The welcome (20 sec)
Vibhishana bows at Rama's feet.
**Narration:** "And Vibhishana was welcomed."
