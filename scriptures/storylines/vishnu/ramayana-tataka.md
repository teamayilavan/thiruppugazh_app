# The Demoness of the Forest (Tataka)

`ramayana-tataka` | Vishnu | running time 2:35 (7 scenes) | tone: tense, quick, a first test

## One-line story

On the road to Siddhashrama the sage Vishwamitra has Rama kill the demoness Tataka; Rama's arrow strikes her in the chest and she falls dead.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| On the road to Siddhashrama the sage Vishwamitra has Rama kill the demoness Tataka | Valmiki Ramayana, Bala Kanda 26.25; Kamba, Tataka Vadhai Padalam 45, 49 |
| Rama's arrow strikes her in the chest and she falls dead | same |
| Songs 366 and 649 recall it | Thiruppugazh songs |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration. Rama's hesitation to strike a woman is the traditional frame; it is not read here.

## Characters

- **Rama and Lakshmana.**
- **Vishwamitra:** a stern sage.
- **Tataka:** a huge demoness who lays the forest waste.

## The storyline

### 1. The forest (25 sec)
A once-green forest, now barren and silent.
**Narration:** "A fine forest had been laid waste."

### 2. The sage's word (20 sec)
Vishwamitra points ahead: "She lives there."
**Narration:** "The sage told Rama of a demoness."

### 3. The bow (20 sec)
Rama twangs his bow; the sound rings through the forest.
**Narration:** "Rama twanged his bow."

### 4. The roar (25 sec)
Tataka comes, hurling rocks and dust.
**Narration:** "She came roaring."

### 5. The arrow (25 sec)
Rama looses an arrow.
**Narration:** "Rama's arrow struck her in the chest."

### 6. The fall (20 sec)
She falls; the forest is quiet.
**Narration:** "And she fell."

### 7. The green (20 sec)
Life returns to the forest.
**Narration:** "And the forest grew green again."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 366 and 649.

## Production notes

- **The kill is a flash of light.** No body shown.
- **Show Rama's reluctance in his face.**
