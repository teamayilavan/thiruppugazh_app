# The Demoness of the Forest (Tataka)

`ramayana-tataka` | Vishnu | running time 2:35 (7 scenes) | tone: tense, quick, a first test | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

On the road to Siddhashrama the sage Vishwamitra has Rama kill the demoness Tataka; Rama's arrow strikes her in the chest and she falls dead.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| On the road to Siddhashrama the sage Vishwamitra has Rama kill the demoness Tataka | Valmiki Ramayana, Bala Kanda 26.25; Kamba, Tataka Vadhai Padalam 45, 49 |
| Rama's arrow strikes her in the chest and she falls dead | same |
| Songs 366 and 649 recall it | Thiruppugazh songs |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration. Rama's hesitation to strike a woman is the traditional frame; it is not read here.

## Characters

- **Rama and Lakshmana.**
- **Vishwamitra:** a stern sage.
- **Tataka:** a huge demoness who lays the forest waste.

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it. This is young Rama's first test; keep it quick and clean, with the kill as a flash of light.

### 1. The forest (25 sec)
**Visual:** A dark, ruined forest: dead trees, cracked earth, no birds. The sage Vishwamitra walks ahead, staff in hand; behind him come two boys, Rama and Lakshmana, bows on their shoulders. Rama looks around at the silence, troubled.
**Narration:** "The sage Vishwamitra had asked King Dasaratha for his son Rama, to guard a sacrifice from the demons. On the way, the sage led the two young princes into a dark forest where nothing grew and no bird sang. Once this had been a rich and happy land."
**Sound:** a dry wind, a low uneasy drone, no birdsong.

### 2. The sage's word (20 sec)
**Visual:** Vishwamitra stops and turns to the boys. He points into the gloom. His face is grave.
**Narration:** "Vishwamitra told them who had ruined it: Tataka, a demoness as strong as a thousand elephants, who killed every traveller who passed. He told Rama that he must end her, for the sake of all the people of the land."
**Dialogue:** Vishwamitra: "Rama, do not hesitate. To protect the people is a prince's duty."
**Sound:** the drone deepens.

### 3. The bow (20 sec)
**Visual:** Close on Rama's young face. He hesitates; tradition says he did not wish to strike a woman. Then he looks at the ruined land, bows to the sage, and strings his bow. He plucks the string; the sound rings out through the forest.
**Narration:** "Tradition says Rama was unwilling, for she was a woman. But he remembered his father's command to obey the sage, and the people who suffered. He bowed to Vishwamitra, strung his bow, and plucked the string. Its sound rang through the forest."
**Dialogue:** Rama: "At my father's word, and at yours, I will do it."
**Sound:** the deep twang of the bowstring, echoing.

### 4. The roar (25 sec)
**Visual:** The ground shakes. Out of the darkness rushes Tataka, huge, shown mostly as shadow and burning eyes, dust swirling around her. She raises a trident blazing like fire and hurls it at Rama.
**Narration:** "The sound woke her. Tataka came rushing out of the dark, raising clouds of dust, roaring with rage. Kamban says she hurled at Rama a trident that blazed like fire, with fire in her own eyes."
**Sound:** a roar, crashing trees, the whistle of the trident.

### 5. The arrow (25 sec)
**Visual:** Rama stands firm. He looses an arrow; it meets the trident in the air and breaks it. Then, steady, he fits a second arrow and draws. Tataka charges. He looses it: a streak of light flies into the darkness.
**Narration:** "Rama did not move. His arrow broke her weapon in the air. Then he fitted another arrow and let it fly. Kamban says it went as swift as a sage's curse, straight through her heart, hard as stone, and out beyond."
**Sound:** a crack as the trident shatters; the rush of the arrow.

### 6. The fall (20 sec)
**Visual:** A white flash where the arrow meets the dark shape. The shadow of Tataka sways and falls; the dust settles. We see no body, only the clearing growing still. Vishwamitra raises his hand in blessing. Flowers fall from the sky.
**Narration:** "The arrow struck her in the chest, and the demoness fell. Valmiki says the gods rejoiced. And Vishwamitra, glad at heart, blessed the young prince."
**Sound:** a single deep drum, then quiet; distant bells.

### 7. The green (20 sec)
**Visual:** Dawn. The same forest, but the air has cleared; shoots of green push up through the earth, a bird lands on a branch and sings. The sage and the two boys walk on toward the hermitage, the morning light ahead of them.
**Narration:** "Morning came to the forest, and the land began to breathe again. Rama's first battle was won. And the three walked on toward the hermitage where the sacrifice was waiting."
**Sound:** birdsong returns, a gentle melody.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri names Tataka in two songs. Song 366 (Thiruvanaikka): Murugan is the nephew of the ocean of grace who killed Tataka when she came against the sages' sacrifice. Song 649 (Kathirgamam): the Lord who loosed one arrow at Tataka, saw the sacrifice through and broke the great bow.

## Production notes

- **The kill is a flash of light.** No body shown.
- **Show Rama's reluctance in his face.**
