# The Gopis' Clothes on the Kadamba Tree

`krishna-clothes` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: playful, modest, then gentle

## One-line story

Gopis bathing in the Yamuna to win Krishna as husband find their clothes taken to the kurunda (kadamba) tree.

**Honesty note:** this is a delicate story. In the Bhagavata it is a vow-ritual (the gopis' observance of Katyayani) and a lesson in surrender; the film is drawn modestly, with the bathing and the clothes only suggested.
**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Krishna:** a cheerful boy in a tree.
- **The gopis:** devout young women on the riverbank.

## The storyline

### 1. The vow (25 sec)
At dawn the gopis go to the river for a month-long vow.
**Narration:** "The gopis made a vow, to win Krishna."

### 2. The bank (20 sec)
They leave their clothes on the bank and enter the river.
**Narration:** "They bathed in the Yamuna."

### 3. The tree (20 sec)
In a kadamba tree, Krishna sits with their clothes in his lap.
**Narration:** "Krishna took their clothes up the tree."

### 4. The plea (25 sec)
They call out to him, laughing and scolding.
**Narration:** "They called him to give them back."

### 5. The lesson (25 sec)
He says to come as they are, with folded hands; they bow, and he returns the clothes.
**Narration:** "He taught them that the Lord asks for surrender."

### 6. The dressing (20 sec)
They dress and thank him.
**Narration:** "And they were content."

### 7. The tree (15 sec)
The empty branches sway.
**Narration:** "And the tree remembers."
