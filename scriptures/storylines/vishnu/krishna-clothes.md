# The Gopis' Clothes on the Kadamba Tree

`krishna-clothes` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: playful, modest, then gentle | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

Gopis bathing in the Yamuna to win Krishna as husband find their clothes taken to the kurunda (kadamba) tree.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |

**Honesty note:** this is a delicate story. In the Bhagavata it is a vow-ritual (the gopis' observance of Katyayani) and a lesson in surrender; the film is drawn modestly, with the bathing and the clothes only suggested.
**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Krishna:** a cheerful boy in a tree.
- **The gopis:** devout young women on the riverbank.

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it.

### 1. The vow (25 sec)
**Visual:** Early winter in Vraja, before sunrise. Mist on the Yamuna. A line of young cowherd girls, fully dressed, walks hand in hand to the river with lamps. On the bank they shape a small clay image of the goddess and worship her with flowers and incense, eyes closed.
**Narration:** "In the cold month of early winter, the young girls of Vraja kept a vow to the goddess Katyayani. For a whole month they ate simple food, and every dawn they went to the Yamuna to bathe and pray. And every one of them prayed for the same thing: that Krishna would be her husband."
**Dialogue:** The girls: "Goddess, make the son of Nanda my husband."
**Sound:** soft singing, a small bell, the river.

### 2. The bank (20 sec)
**Visual:** The girls fold their clothes neatly on the bank and go down into the water. We see only the bank, the folded clothes, and the mist; then the girls far off, small figures shoulder-deep in the river, singing, their backs to the shore.
**Narration:** "One morning they left their clothes on the bank, as always, and went down into the river, singing his name. They did not see who was watching from among the trees."
**Sound:** splashing, distant singing.

### 3. The tree (20 sec)
**Visual:** From behind a bush, Krishna, a laughing boy, with his friends hiding their smiles. He gathers the folded clothes and climbs up a tall tree by the water. He drapes them on the branches like garlands and sits among the leaves, swinging his feet.
**Narration:** "It was Krishna. Quietly he gathered up their clothes and climbed a tall tree on the bank. The Bhagavata calls it a kadamba tree; the Tamil poets call it kurundham. There he sat, and waited, and laughed."
**Sound:** a playful flute phrase; rustling leaves.

### 4. The plea (25 sec)
**Visual:** The girls turn and see the empty bank, then Krishna in the tree. Only their heads and shoulders above the water, they cry out, half laughing, half shy, shivering in the cold. Keep the camera on faces and the tree.
**Narration:** "The girls turned and saw the empty bank, and Krishna in the tree. They stood in the cold water, shivering, laughing in their embarrassment, and begged him."
**Dialogue:** The girls: "Son of Nanda, we are your servants, and we are cold. Give us back our clothes!"
**Dialogue:** Krishna: "I have never told a lie. Come and take them."
**Sound:** chattering teeth, laughter, the flute teasing.

### 5. The lesson (25 sec)
**Visual:** Krishna's face becomes gentle and serious. He speaks to them kindly. The girls lower their eyes and raise their joined hands above their heads in a bow. Their shyness turns to calm.
**Narration:** "Then Krishna spoke gently. To bathe without clothes in the river, he said, was a fault against the gods. Let them bow, with joined hands, and the fault would be gone. The girls bowed to him, with nothing held back."
**Sound:** the flute turns warm and slow.

### 6. The dressing (20 sec)
**Visual:** The clothes float down from the tree one by one, each landing softly on the bank. A curtain of mist and leaves fills the screen; when it clears, the girls stand dressed on the bank, glancing shyly at him, not wanting to leave.
**Narration:** "Pleased with them, Krishna gave back their clothes. They were not angry at all. They only stood there shyly, looking at him, not wanting to go home."
**Sound:** soft rustle of cloth, birdsong.

### 7. The tree (15 sec)
**Visual:** Krishna climbs down and speaks to them. The sun rises over the Yamuna. The girls walk home in a line, smiling. The empty tree stands by the river, its branches swaying.
**Narration:** "He told them their vow was fulfilled, and their prayer would come true. Then he sent them home, happy, in the morning sun."
**Sound:** the first song of the girls returns; one bell.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri gives only the image: the cloud-coloured Lord who climbed the kurundham tree with the girls' garments. Songs 614 (Thenserigiri), 1072 and 1244 are the ones to show.

## Production notes

- **Handle with modesty.** Show silhouettes, never skin. The gopis are devotees.
