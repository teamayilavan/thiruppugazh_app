# The Lotus from the Navel (The Birth of Brahma)

`avatar-brahma-origin` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: wondering, quiet, new beginnings

## One-line story

A lotus grows from Vishnu's navel as he lies on the serpent Ananta over the waters, and Brahma is born in it.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| A lotus grows from Vishnu's navel as he lies on Ananta over the waters | Bhagavata Purana 3.8.14-16 |
| Brahma is born in it | same |
| Many songs name Brahma as "the one on the lotus" | Thiruppugazh songs 150, 175, 612 and others |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Vishnu**, reclining.
- **Brahma:** newly born, four-faced, looking in all directions.
- **The lotus.**

## The storyline

### 1. The waters (25 sec)
A shoreless sea in darkness.
**Narration:** "Before the worlds, there were only waters."

### 2. The Lord (20 sec)
Vishnu lies on the serpent over the waters, at rest.
**Narration:** "And on them lay the Lord."

### 3. The stem (25 sec)
From his navel a stem rises, long and slender.
**Narration:** "From his navel a lotus began to grow."

### 4. The bud (20 sec)
The stem climbs; the bud swells.
**Narration:** "It rose, and swelled."

### 5. The opening (25 sec)
The lotus opens, light spills; in its heart, a small figure.
**Narration:** "It opened, and Brahma was born."

### 6. The looking (20 sec)
The figure turns to look: four faces appear in turn.
**Narration:** "He looked in all four directions."

### 7. The beginning (15 sec)
The lotus floats; the first light of the world.
**Narration:** "And so the creation began."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 150, 175 and 612 among others name Brahma as "the one on the lotus".

## Production notes

- **A gentle birth.** Brahma is a newborn, not yet the proud creator.
- **Pair with `avatar-adisesha`.**
