# The Lotus from the Navel (The Birth of Brahma)

`avatar-brahma-origin` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: wondering, quiet, new beginnings | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

A lotus grows from Vishnu's navel as he lies on the serpent Ananta over the waters, and Brahma is born in it.

## What the story rests on

|---|---|

| The seed of creation pierces his navel; a lotus bud shines like the sun and dries the waters | 3.8.13-14 |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals (the heartbeat of the bud, the faces appearing one by one), Brahma's spoken question (a plain paraphrase of 3.8.18), and the wording of the narration.

## Characters

- **Vishnu**, reclining.
- **Brahma:** newly born, four-faced, looking in all directions.
- **The lotus.**

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it.

### 1. The waters (25 sec)
**Visual:** Dark water of dissolution in every direction, no light but a faint grey shimmer. Mist moves slowly. Nothing stirs.
**Narration:** "When the worlds had ended, only the waters remained. There was no earth, no sky, and no creature that could see. Everything that would ever live lay hidden, waiting, like seeds asleep under the ground. Time itself was waiting."
**Sound:** a deep, quiet drone; distant water.

### 2. The Lord (20 sec)
**Visual:** A soft glow on the water: the serpent bed, and Vishnu resting on it, blue and still, eyes half closed. Slowly his gaze turns, as if toward something far away.
**Narration:** "On the serpent Ananta lay Vishnu, alone upon the water. For ages he rested. Then, the Bhagavata says, the time came for the worlds to begin again, and his attention turned toward them."
**Sound:** a single low flute note, held.

### 3. The stem (25 sec)
**Visual:** From his navel a tiny green shoot appears and grows into a slender stem, climbing up through the dark water toward the surface. At its tip a bud forms and begins to shine; light floods outward and the black water pales and recedes.
**Narration:** "From his navel something stirred. A slender stem rose up through the water, climbing higher and higher. At its tip was a bud, and the bud began to shine like the sun, until its light reached every corner of the dark and dried the endless waters."
**Sound:** a rising string line; the glow brings a warm chord.

### 4. The bud (20 sec)
**Visual:** The bud, huge now and golden, petals still closed, pulsing gently like a heartbeat. A thread of light passes from the Lord into the flower.
**Narration:** "Into that lotus the Lord himself entered, as the life within it. And in the heart of the flower a child was born, born of no mother, born of the lotus alone."
**Sound:** a soft heartbeat rhythm on a hand drum.

### 5. The opening (25 sec)
**Visual:** The petals open one by one. In the centre sits Brahma, newborn and golden, with one face. He looks around: nothing but sky and water. He turns east, south, west and north, and with each turn a new face appears, until he has four.
**Narration:** "The petals opened, and there sat Brahma. He looked around, but there was no world to see. So he turned to look in every direction, east and south and west and north, and as he looked he grew four faces, one for each direction."
**Sound:** four gentle bell tones, one with each turn.

### 6. The looking (20 sec)
**Visual:** Brahma climbs down inside the hollow stem into the dark, searching; the stem goes on and on and he cannot reach its root. He climbs back to the flower, sits cross-legged and closes all his eyes.
**Dialogue:** Brahma: "Who am I, sitting on this lotus? Where has it come from?"
**Narration:** "He went down inside the stem to look for its root, searching for ages, but he never found it. So he came back to the flower, sat still, and turned his mind within."
**Sound:** echoing, searching music; then silence.

### 7. The beginning (15 sec)
**Visual:** Brahma opens his eyes. Below him he sees the Lord on the serpent, the lotus rising from the navel, the waters, the air and the sky, all clear in a golden light. He folds his hands. Behind him the first stars appear.
**Narration:** "Then at last he saw: the Lord on the serpent, the lotus from his navel, the waters and the sky. Brahma bowed, and began to create the world."
**Sound:** the opening drone returns, now warm and bright; a conch.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri does not tell this story; he uses it as a title, calling Vishnu the father of four-faced Brahma. Songs to show: 150 (Palani), the dark cloud who sleeps on the sea and gave the world Brahma; 175 (Palani), Sridhara, good father of the four-faced one; 612 (Oothimalai); and 743 (Thirunavalur), Narayana, from whom the four-faced Brahma was born.

## Production notes

- **A gentle birth.** Brahma is a newborn, not yet the proud creator.
- **Pair with `avatar-adisesha`.**
