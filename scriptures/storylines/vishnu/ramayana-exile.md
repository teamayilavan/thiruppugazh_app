# At His Mother's Word (The Exile)

`ramayana-exile` | Vishnu | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: sorrowful, noble, calm

## One-line story

On the eve of his coronation Kaikeyi claims her two boons and Rama goes to the forest at his father's word with Sita and Lakshmana.

## The storyline

### 1. The joy (25 sec)
Ayodhya prepares for Rama's coronation: flowers and drums.
**Narration:** "Ayodhya was ready to crown Rama."

### 2. The boons (20 sec)
Kaikeyi, in the palace, remembers two boons.
**Narration:** "The queen Kaikeyi remembered two boons."

### 3. The demand (25 sec)
She tells the king: "Send Rama to the forest, and crown my son."
**Narration:** "She asked for Rama's exile."

### 4. The king's grief (20 sec)
Dasaratha falls to the floor.
**Narration:** "The king was broken."

### 5. Rama hears (20 sec)
Rama enters; he hears it calmly.
**Narration:** "Rama heard it, and smiled."

### 6. The departure (25 sec)
Rama, Sita and Lakshmana leave in bark clothes; the city weeps.
**Narration:** "He went to the forest at his father's word."

### 7. The road (20 sec)
They walk out of the city.
**Narration:** "The people followed him, weeping."

### 8. The forest (10 sec)
The three disappear into trees.
**Narration:** "And he went to the forest."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 597, 968 and 1265.

## Production notes

- **Do not demonise Kaikeyi.** Show her in her own fear.
- **Rama's calm is the film's centre.**
