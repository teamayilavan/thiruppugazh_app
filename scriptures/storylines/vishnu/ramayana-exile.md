# At His Mother's Word (The Exile)

`ramayana-exile` | Vishnu | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: sorrowful, noble, calm | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

On the eve of his coronation Kaikeyi claims her two boons and Rama goes to the forest at his father's word with Sita and Lakshmana.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| On the eve of his coronation Kaikeyi claims her two boons | Research set `ramayana-exile`; Valmiki, Ayodhya Kanda; Kamba, Kaikeyi Suzhchip Padalam (traditional source; not read verse by verse here) |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Rama, Sita, Lakshmana.**
- **Dasaratha:** an old king, broken.
- **Kaikeyi:** a queen, hard-eyed.
- **The citizens.**

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; where a scene has no voice, the music carries it.

### 1. The joy (25 sec)
**Visual:** Ayodhya on the eve of a festival. Streets washed and sprinkled, flags on every roof, lamps being lit, people stringing flowers. Children run with garlands. In the palace, priests prepare the vessels for a coronation.
**Narration:** "Ayodhya was full of joy. King Dasaratha had grown old, and tomorrow his eldest son Rama was to be crowned as the young king. The streets were washed, the flags were raised, and everyone was waiting for the morning."
**Sound:** bright festive music, bells, happy voices.

### 2. The boons (20 sec)
**Visual:** Inside a dim chamber, Queen Kaikeyi listens as her old maid Manthara whispers to her. Kaikeyi's face turns from joy to doubt to fear. A flashback, in soft colours: long ago, on a battlefield, young Kaikeyi saves the wounded Dasaratha, and he gratefully promises her two boons.
**Narration:** "But in the palace, Queen Kaikeyi listened to a whisper from her old maid, and fear took hold of her. She remembered that long ago she had saved the king's life in battle, and he had promised her two boons, whatever she wished."
**Sound:** the festive music fades to a low, uneasy note.

### 3. The demand (25 sec)
**Visual:** Night. Kaikeyi sits on the floor of her chamber, her jewels thrown aside. Dasaratha enters, alarmed, and kneels by her. She speaks; he recoils as if struck.
**Narration:** "When the king came to her, she reminded him of his promise, and she asked for her two boons."
**Dialogue:** Kaikeyi: "Let my son Bharata be crowned. And let Rama go to the forest for fourteen years."
**Narration (continued):** "The king could not believe what he heard. He begged her to ask for anything else."
**Sound:** silence, then a low drum.

### 4. The king's grief (20 sec)
**Visual:** Dasaratha, broken, lies on the floor through the night as the lamps burn down. Kaikeyi turns her face away, her hands trembling. Dawn light creeps in.
**Narration:** "All night the old king wept. He had given his word, and a king of his line could not break it. By morning he could not even speak Rama's name."
**Sound:** a single sorrowful flute.

### 5. Rama hears (20 sec)
**Visual:** Rama is called to the chamber. Kaikeyi tells him. The camera stays on his face: it does not cloud. He bows to his father, and to Kaikeyi.
**Narration:** "Rama was called. It was Kaikeyi who told him. Kamban says that when he heard it, he looked like a man relieved of an illness by a kind hand. He was not angry. He only said:"
**Dialogue:** Rama: "I will go to the forest today. I take my leave."
**Sound:** the flute holds one calm, steady note.

### 6. The departure (25 sec)
**Visual:** Rama, now in simple bark clothes, says farewell to his mother Kausalya, who sobs and falls. Sita, in bark clothes too, stands beside him, determined. Lakshmana, bow on his shoulder, refuses to be left behind. The three walk out of the palace.
**Narration:** "His mother Kausalya wept and fell to the ground. Rama comforted her. Sita would not stay behind, for her place was at his side. And his brother Lakshmana took up his bow and said he would follow wherever Rama went."
**Sound:** soft strings, weeping voices in the background.

### 7. The road (20 sec)
**Visual:** A chariot drives slowly through the city gate. The people of Ayodhya follow it, weeping, holding out their hands. Rama looks back once, with love, and then faces forward.
**Narration:** "The whole city followed the chariot, weeping. Their prince was leaving, and they did not want to let him go. Rama looked back at them with love, and then he turned his face to the road."
**Sound:** the crowd's sorrow; wheels on stone.

### 8. The forest (10 sec)
**Visual:** The three walk into the deep green forest, the light falling through the trees.
**Narration:** "So Rama went to the forest, to keep his father's word, and his mother's wish."
**Sound:** forest birds; a calm, gentle melody.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Three songs recall the exile, naming Murugan the nephew of the Lord who gave up his crown and went to the forest at his mother's word. Show song 968 (Sri Purushamangai), song 597 (Thiruchengode) and song 1265 (a general song, Pothu Padalgal).

## Production notes

- **Do not demonise Kaikeyi.** Show her in her own fear.
- **Rama's calm is the film's centre.**
