# The Cart That Broke (Sakata)

`krishna-sakata` | Vishnu | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: tender, then comic, then bright

## One-line story

Baby Krishna kicks a heavy cart haunted by a demon, and it shatters.

- **Yashoda:** busy, loving.

- **The villagers.**

## The storyline

### 1. The morning (20 sec)
Yashoda is busy; the baby lies under a cart, kicking.
**Narration:** "Little Krishna lay under a cart."

### 2. The hungry baby (20 sec)
He cries for milk and kicks his feet in the air.
**Narration:** "He was hungry, and he kicked."

### 3. The cart (25 sec)
The cart above him wobbles; something dark is inside.
**Narration:** "A demon hid in the cart."

### 4. The kick (25 sec)
His tiny foot touches the wheel; the cart flies apart.
**Narration:** "One kick, and the cart broke in pieces."

### 5. The scatter (20 sec)
Pots and pans fall; the cries of the villagers.
**Narration:** "The villagers ran to see."

### 6. The baby (20 sec)
He sits among the splinters, chuckling.
**Narration:** "And the baby was laughing."

### 7. The embrace (20 sec)
Yashoda sweeps him up.
**Narration:** "And his mother held him."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 116, 186 and 193 among seven.

## Production notes

- **Comedy of the huge and the tiny.**
- **No fear.** The demon is a shadow that dissolves.
