# The Mountain That Was a Trap (Agastya and the Krouncha Mountain)

`m-krouncha` | Murugan | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: curious, uncanny, then triumphant

## One-line story

A demon turns himself into a mountain to trap the sage Agastya; the sage sees through the trick and curses him to remain a mountain until Murugan's spear ends him.

**Added for the screen:** the visual details of the maze, Agastya's expressions, and the narration. Nothing in the text says how the small sage looks beyond "small".

## Characters

- **Agastya:** a short, radiant sage, grey-bearded, with a water pot and a staff. Calm, amused, never afraid for long.
- **Krouncha:** the asura; seen as a shadow in the mountain, then as a mountain with a face-like hollow.
- **Shiva:** calm and kind, at Kailasa.
- **Murugan** (last scene): the spear in his hand.

## Places

Kailasa with a great crowd; the long road south; the shifting mountain; the Podiya hill.

## The storyline

### 1. The tilted world (25 sec)
Kailasa, packed with gods, sages and beings from every world. The earth groans and tilts toward the north. Shiva looks calmly across the crowd, to a small sage near the back: Agastya.
**Narration:** "So many gathered at Kailasa that the earth tipped. And Shiva looked at the smallest sage and sent him south."

### 2. The question (15 sec)
Agastya, uneasy, bows. He is being sent far from Shiva's feet. He asks how he is to go.
**Narration:** "Agastya was afraid he had done wrong. 'Why must I go so far from you?'"

### 3. The road south (20 sec)
He walks on, over plains and rivers, staff in hand. As he walks, the earth levels behind him. Ahead, a mountain rises across the road.
**Narration:** "He walked, and the world grew level behind him. Then a mountain rose where there had been none."

### 4. Into the mountain (25 sec)
The road leads straight into the mountain. Agastya walks in. The way grows long, then strange, then lost. Fire, whirlwind and darkness gather around him.
**Narration:** "The road itself led into the mountain, and then it had no end. Fire and darkness gathered around him."

### 5. The sage laughs (20 sec)
Agastya stops. He looks around, and smiles. He laughs. He strikes the mountain with his staff, and holes open all over it.
**Narration:** "But Agastya saw through the trick. He laughed, and struck the mountain with his staff."

### 6. The curse (20 sec)
Krouncha's shape shows through the stone. Agastya speaks: "Remain a mountain, a home for the asuras. The holes my staff has made shall be the places of your illusions. And one day you will die by the spear that Murugan holds." He sprinkles water with a mantra, ends the illusion, and walks on.
**Narration:** "He cursed it to stay a mountain, until the day it should be destroyed by Murugan's spear."

### 7. The fortress (15 sec)
Time passes: seasons over the mountain, asuras moving in and out of the holes. It becomes a fortress, with a city of illusion beside it.
**Narration:** "So the mountain became the home of the asuras, and a trouble to all who passed by."

### 8. The word comes true (25 sec)
Murugan's spear flashes. It pierces the mountain and the asura beside it, with a sound as if the earth splits. The mountain cracks and falls apart. Far away, in the south, Agastya looks up and nods.
**Narration:** "And one day the spear came, as the sage had said, and the word came true."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri sings of Murugan piercing the Krouncha mountain in more than two hundred songs, sometimes using the name for the whole war. Songs to show: 20 (Thiruparankundram), 22, 24, 25, 37 and 38 (Thiruchendur).

## Production notes

- **Agastya is the hero of the first half.** Give him humour: his laugh is the turning point.
- **No gore.** The mountain's end is a crack and a fall.
- **Pair with `m-tarakasura`** and `m-agastya`; use the same mountain design.
