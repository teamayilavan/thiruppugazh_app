# The Mountain That Was a Trap (Agastya and the Krouncha Mountain)

`m-krouncha` | Murugan | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: curious, uncanny, then triumphant | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

A demon turns himself into a mountain to trap the sage Agastya; the sage sees through the trick and curses him to remain a mountain until Murugan's spear ends him.

**Added for the screen:** the visual details of the maze, Agastya's expressions, and the narration. Nothing in the text says how the small sage looks beyond "small".

## Characters

- **Agastya:** a short, radiant sage, grey-bearded, with a water pot and a staff. Calm, amused, never afraid for long.
- **Krouncha:** the asura; seen as a shadow in the mountain, then as a mountain with a face-like hollow.
- **Shiva:** calm and kind, at Kailasa.
- **Murugan** (last scene): the spear in his hand.

## Places

Kailasa with a great crowd; the long road south; the shifting mountain; the Podiya hill.

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice; quick and tense inside the mountain, triumphant at the end. About two words a second.

### 1. The tilted world (25 sec)
**Visual:** Kailasa, white and vast, crowded with gods, sages and beings from every world for the great gathering. The earth below groans and slowly tips toward the north. Shiva looks across the crowd to the smallest sage at the back: Agastya, short, radiant, with a water pot.
**Narration:** "Once, so many beings gathered at Kailasa that the whole earth tipped toward the north. Shiva looked over the crowd, to the smallest sage of all, Agastya, and told him to go south. When you are there, he said, the world will come level again."
**Sound:** a deep groan of the earth; a calm voice-like tone for Shiva.

### 2. The question (15 sec)
**Visual:** Agastya bows, troubled.
**Narration:** "Agastya was afraid he had done something wrong, to be sent so far from Shiva's feet."
**Dialogue:** Agastya: "Why must I go so far from you? And how am I to go?"
**Sound:** a single questioning note.

### 3. The road south (20 sec)
**Visual:** Agastya walks south with his staff. Behind him the land settles: hills sink, rivers flow straight. Ahead, a mountain rises suddenly across the road where there was none, and the road runs straight into it.
**Narration:** "He walked south, and behind him the world grew level. But on his road a mountain rose where no mountain had stood, and the road ran into it. The mountain was a demon, Krouncha, a master of illusions."
**Sound:** walking rhythm; a low rumble as the mountain rises.

### 4. Into the mountain (25 sec)
**Visual:** Inside, a maze of glowing passages. Agastya walks on; the way stretches, bends, doubles back. The passage behind him vanishes. Fire flares from the walls, a whirlwind of dust, then darkness. Only his small calm face is lit.
**Narration:** "The sage went in. The road stretched on and on, and had no end. Then it was lost altogether. Fire, whirlwind and darkness gathered around him on every side."
**Sound:** echoing steps, crackling fire, wind, then silence.

### 5. The sage laughs (20 sec)
**Visual:** Agastya closes his eyes, then opens them clear. He laughs. He strikes the mountain wall with his staff; holes burst open in it, letting in the daylight.
**Narration:** "But Agastya saw through the trick with the clear mind of the wise. He laughed, and struck the mountain with his staff, again and again, until it was full of holes."
**Sound:** a bright laugh; the knock of the staff echoing; daylight as a chord.

### 6. The curse (20 sec)
**Visual:** Agastya stands outside, staff raised, facing the mountain. He sprinkles water from his pot with a mantra; the illusion of the road dissolves.
**Narration:** "Then he cursed it."
**Dialogue:** Agastya: "Stay a mountain, and a home for demons. The holes I have made will be the places of your tricks. And in time you will die by the spear that Murugan holds."
**Narration (continued):** "He sprinkled holy water, ended the illusion, and went on his way."
**Sound:** the curse spoken low over a drone; water drops.

### 7. The fortress (15 sec)
**Visual:** Time passes over the mountain: demons make their home in its caves. Beside it rises a city of illusion, ruled by Tarakan, Surapadman's younger brother.
**Narration:** "So Krouncha stood as a mountain, a fortress of the demons. And beside it Tarakan built his city of illusions."
**Sound:** a dark, slow motif.

### 8. The word comes true (25 sec)
**Visual:** Ages later: Murugan's army arrives; the mountain casts its illusions over them and the warriors stagger. Murugan raises his spear and throws. A line of light passes through Tarakan and the mountain together; the mountain cracks open like the earth bursting; the illusions fall away like smoke. Final image: Agastya, far in the south on his hill, looking up and nodding.
**Narration:** "When Murugan came with his army, the mountain worked its tricks against his warriors. Then Murugan threw his spear. It passed through Tarakan and the mountain together, and every illusion in the world fell away. The word of the little sage had come true."
**Sound:** rising drums; a great crack; then a clear bell.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri sings of Murugan piercing the Krouncha mountain in more than two hundred songs, sometimes using the name for the whole war. Songs to show: 20 (Thiruparankundram), 22, 24, 25, 37 and 38 (Thiruchendur).

## Production notes

- **Agastya is the hero of the first half.** Give him humour: his laugh is the turning point.
- **No gore.** The mountain's end is a crack and a fall.
- **Pair with `m-tarakasura`** and `m-agastya`; use the same mountain design.
