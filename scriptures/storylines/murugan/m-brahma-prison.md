# The Child Who Asked the Creator a Question (Brahma in Prison)

`m-brahma-prison` | Murugan | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: playful at first, then grave, then warm | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

The child Murugan asks Brahma the meaning of the first letter of the Vedas; when the creator cannot answer, Murugan puts him in prison and takes over the work of creation, until Shiva comes to set things right.

## Characters

- **Murugan:** a child, serene, with a quick smile, in warm red-gold.
- **Brahma:** four faces, white beard, on a lotus; dignified, then flustered, then humbled.
- **Vishnu:** calm and thoughtful, blue.
- **Shiva:** kind, with a smile.
- **The gods and sages:** who plead on Brahma's behalf.

## Places

The Skanda mountain; Brahma's lotus; the prison; Murugan's hall with a jewelled seat.

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice with a little lightness in scenes 2 and 3; about two words a second.

### 1. The recital (15 sec)
**Visual:** A vast lotus floating in light. On it sits Brahma, four-faced, golden, with a palm-leaf book. His four mouths move together as he begins the sacred syllable. Letters of light rise from him.
**Narration:** "On his great lotus sat Brahma, the creator. He began to recite the sacred syllable, Om, the sound with which every Veda begins."
**Sound:** a deep, resonant chant tone.

### 2. A child's question (25 sec)
**Visual:** A small boy steps onto the edge of the lotus: Murugan, bright-eyed, three lines of ash on his brow, his spear taller than he is. He listens with his head on one side, then laughs. Brahma's four faces turn to him.
**Narration:** "A child had been listening. It was Murugan, the son of Shiva. He laughed, and asked a simple question."
**Dialogue:** Murugan: "You begin every Veda with that letter. Tell me, what does it mean?"
**Sound:** the chant stops; a single plucked note for the question.

### 3. The silence (15 sec)
**Visual:** Brahma's eyes widen. He opens his mouth, then closes it. The letters of light hang in the air and fade. Close on his hands, which tighten on his book.
**Narration:** "Brahma's eyes opened wide. He stammered. He stood in confusion. The creator of the world did not know. The old poet smiles: if even he did not know, what is our little learning worth?"
**Sound:** an awkward silence; a soft, comic pluck.

### 4. The prison (25 sec)
**Visual:** Murugan's face grows grave. At his sign, his attendants lead Brahma from the lotus. Brahma falls to his knees. The scene moves to a mountain stronghold, Kandagiri; a door closes on the creator and his attendants. No violence shown, only the closing of the door and a chain falling into place.
**Narration:** "A teacher of the Vedas who did not know the meaning of their first word could not go on teaching them. So the child had the creator chained, and shut him in a prison on his mountain, with all his attendants."
**Sound:** a heavy door; a chain; then quiet.

### 5. The creation goes on (25 sec)
**Visual:** Murugan sits where Brahma sat, one hand raised. From his hand, worlds take shape: rivers run, trees rise, birds, fish and animals spring into being, each glowing briefly as it forms. A time-lapse of stars wheeling overhead.
**Narration:** "Then Murugan himself took up the work of creation. Under his hand the worlds went on, and living beings were born, just as before. Age after age went by, and the creator stayed in prison."
**Sound:** a bright, rising melody, many small chimes for the new creatures.

### 6. The gods worry (25 sec)
**Visual:** Vishnu, dark blue, on his serpent couch, thoughtful. He rises and calls the gods and sages; they gather, anxious. Together they travel to Kailasa, where Shiva sits with Uma.
**Narration:** "At last Vishnu began to wonder how Brahma might be freed. He gathered the gods and the sages, and they went together to Shiva."
**Dialogue:** The gods: "Lord, your son put Brahma in prison because he could not tell the meaning of the first word of the Veda. And now he carries on the creation himself."
**Sound:** a low drone of concern, softening as they reach Kailasa.

### 7. The father comes (25 sec)
**Visual:** Shiva arrives at the mountain. Murugan runs to meet him, bows, and leads him to a great jewelled seat. The father sits; the son stands before him, hands folded, with the same bright eyes.
**Narration:** "So Shiva came to his son. And the child received him with honour, seated him on a great jewelled seat, and asked him, simply:"
**Dialogue:** Murugan: "You who are the soul of all souls, what has brought you here?"
**Sound:** a warm chord; the opening chant tone returns, softly.

### 8. The release (10 sec)
**Visual:** The prison door opens. Murugan himself takes Brahma by the hand and leads him out before his father. Brahma bows low to Shiva.
**Narration:** "Murugan took Brahma by the hand and set him free. And Brahma bowed to Shiva, with all his heart."
**Sound:** the full melody; one bell.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri says in many songs that Murugan "put Brahma in prison". Songs to show: 9 (Thiruparankundram), 96 (Thiruchendur), 164 and 197 (Palani), 211 (Swamimalai), 284 (Thiruthani). Swamimalai is the temple where the story of Murugan teaching his father the meaning of the Pranava is remembered (see `murugan-pranava`).

## Production notes

- **Keep Brahma dignified.** The humour is gentle; it is the poet's own point that even the creator is "confounded" by a child.
- **Murugan is a child, not a bully.** Avoid any cruelty in the imprisonment: show chains lightly and the prison as a quiet hall.
- **Open question for the owner:** whether to show the link to the Swamimalai story (Murugan teaching Shiva the Pranava) as a card at the end.
