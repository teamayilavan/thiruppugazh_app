# The Child Who Asked the Creator a Question (Brahma in Prison)

`m-brahma-prison` | Murugan | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: playful at first, then grave, then warm

## One-line story

The child Murugan asks Brahma the meaning of the first letter of the Vedas; when the creator cannot answer, Murugan puts him in prison and takes over the work of creation, until Shiva comes to set things right.

## Characters

- **Murugan:** a child, serene, with a quick smile, in warm red-gold.
- **Brahma:** four faces, white beard, on a lotus; dignified, then flustered, then humbled.
- **Vishnu:** calm and thoughtful, blue.
- **Shiva:** kind, with a smile.
- **The gods and sages:** who plead on Brahma's behalf.

## Places

The Skanda mountain; Brahma's lotus; the prison; Murugan's hall with a jewelled seat.

## The storyline

### 1. The recital (15 sec)
Brahma sits on his lotus, four faces chanting, light flowing from each. The sound of the Pranava fills the air.
**Narration:** "Brahma, seated on his lotus, began to recite the sacred syllable, the beginning of all the Vedas."

### 2. A child's question (25 sec)
A child appears: Murugan, laughing quietly. "What is the meaning of the first letter?" he asks. Brahma's four faces open their eyes wide.
**Narration:** "A child laughed, and asked him, 'What is the meaning of the first letter?'"

### 3. The silence (15 sec)
Brahma stammers. He looks at one face, then another. He does not know. He stands confused, his chant gone.
**Narration:** "Brahma was ashamed. He did not know."

### 4. The prison (25 sec)
Murugan makes the creator fall and has him chained, with his attendants, in a prison on the Skanda mountain. The lotus empties. The music turns grave.
**Narration:** "So the child put the creator in prison."

### 5. The creation goes on (25 sec)
Murugan, calm on a seat, begins to create: sparks and shapes become creatures, hills, rivers, birds. Ages pass in quick seasons.
**Narration:** "And with Brahma in prison, Murugan carried on the creation himself. Ages passed."

### 6. The gods worry (25 sec)
Vishnu, thoughtful, calls the gods and sages. They go together to Shiva and say: "Lord, your son, when Brahma could not give the meaning of the first word of the Veda, put him in prison, and carries on the creation himself."
**Narration:** "At last Vishnu wondered how Brahma might be freed, and the gods went to Shiva."

### 7. The father comes (25 sec)
Shiva comes to Murugan's hall. Murugan gets up, seats his father on a great jewelled seat, and asks with a smile, "You are the soul of all souls. What has brought you here?"
**Narration:** "Shiva came. And the child seated his father on a jewelled seat, and asked him why he had come."

### 8. The release (10 sec)
Murugan takes Brahma by the hand and sets him free before his father. Brahma bows low to Shiva, his four faces smiling at last.
**Narration:** "He took Brahma by the hand and set him free, and Brahma bowed to Shiva with all his heart."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri says in many songs that Murugan "put Brahma in prison". Songs to show: 9 (Thiruparankundram), 96 (Thiruchendur), 164 and 197 (Palani), 211 (Swamimalai), 284 (Thiruthani). Swamimalai is the temple where the story of Murugan teaching his father the meaning of the Pranava is remembered.

## Production notes

- **Keep Brahma dignified.** The humour is gentle; it is the poet's own point that even the creator is "confounded" by a child.
- **Murugan is a child, not a bully.** Avoid any cruelty in the imprisonment: show chains lightly and the prison as a quiet hall.
- **Open question for the owner:** whether to show the link to the Swamimalai story (Murugan teaching Shiva the Pranava) as a card at the end.
