# The Prince in the Prison (Jayanta Freed)

`m-jayanta` | Murugan | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: sorrow, hope, release | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

Indra's young son is held in the demon king's prison with the other gods; when the demon falls, Murugan sends his captain to set them free and give heaven back to Indra.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Jayantan, Indra's son, is shut in Surapadman's prison at Mahendrapuri with the other devas | Kanda Puranam, Mahendra Kandam 337 |
| Three padalams are named for him: his lament in the prison, his dream, and Veerabahu's words of comfort | Mahendra Kandam, padalams 9-11 |
| Veerabahu visits the prison | 450 |
| After the war, Murugan, on his peacock, tells Veerabahu to go to the fallen king's city and bring Jayantan and all the devas out of prison | Yuddha Kandam 2935-2943 |

**Added for the screen:** the content of Jayantan's dream (the text names the padalam, not what he dreamed), the look of the prison, and the narration. Where the song's commentary says Murugan freed him, the Puranam has Veerabahu do it at Murugan's command; the film follows the Puranam.

## Characters

- **Jayantan:** a young prince of the devas, fine-featured, thin from captivity, in torn silk.
- **The devas and goddesses:** in the prison, with tired eyes.
- **Veerabahu:** Murugan's chief captain, strong and kind.
- **Murugan:** calm on his peacock.
- **Indra and his queen** (end).

## Places

The prison of Mahendrapuri, the demon's city; the battlefield; Mount Meru; the golden city of heaven.

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice; low and slow in the prison, opening up at the end. About two words a second.

### 1. The prison (25 sec)
**Visual:** The demon city of Mahendrapuri by night, towers of black stone over a dark sea. Deep inside, a long hall of chains: gods sit in the dust, dimmed. Among them a young prince with a fallen crown, Jayantan, his face in his hands.
**Narration:** "Across the sea stood Mahendrapuri, the city of the demon king Surapadman. In its prison the gods of heaven sat in chains. Among them was a young prince, Jayantan, the son of Indra. He had not seen the sky for a very long time."
**Sound:** dripping water, a low drone, the faint sound of the sea.

### 2. A messenger and a dream (25 sec)
**Visual:** A shaft of light in the prison: Veerabahu, Murugan's captain, tall and shining, kneels beside the prince and speaks to him. Then night: Jayantan sleeps, and over him a dream of light, a peacock's wing, a spear rising like the sun.
**Narration:** "One day a visitor came secretly into the prison: Veerabahu, the captain of Murugan's army. He knelt beside the prince and spoke words of comfort. Help was coming. That night, Jayantan dreamed, and for the first time in years, the dream was full of light."
**Dialogue:** Veerabahu: "Do not grieve, prince. The son of Shiva is coming, and this prison will not stand."
**Sound:** a warm tone enters with Veerabahu; the dream is a soft shimmer.

### 3. The war (20 sec)
**Visual:** Silhouettes only: armies on the shore, the flash of a spear, the shadows of great towers falling. Then a long stillness: the battlefield at dawn, quiet.
**Narration:** "The war was long and terrible. But it ended. The demon king fell, and the battlefield grew still."
**Sound:** distant drums that fade into silence and a single bird.

### 4. The command (20 sec)
**Visual:** Murugan, seated on his peacock above the field, turns to Veerabahu and points across the water to the dark city.
**Narration:** "Then Murugan, seated on his peacock, called his captain."
**Dialogue:** Murugan: "Go to the city of the fallen king. Bring Jayantan and all the gods out of the prison."
**Sound:** a clear horn.

### 5. The door opens (20 sec)
**Visual:** Veerabahu strides through the empty streets of the demon city, through gates hanging open. He reaches the prison and throws the great doors wide. Light floods in on the faces of the gods. Chains fall.
**Narration:** "Veerabahu went into the silent city and came to the prison. He opened the doors, and the light of day fell on the faces of the gods."
**Sound:** a heavy door swinging; chains falling; a rising chord.

### 6. The walk (25 sec)
**Visual:** A long line of gods, thin but shining more with every step, walks out of the city and across the battlefield. Jayantan walks at their head, his crown back in his hands. They reach Murugan and fall at his feet.
**Narration:** "Jayantan and the gods came out together. They walked across the field where the war had been fought, and at the end of it they fell at Murugan's feet. The prisoners were free."
**Sound:** a slow processional melody, growing.

### 7. Heaven again (20 sec)
**Visual:** The golden city of Indra in the clouds, its banners raised again; Indra on his throne. On Mount Meru, a messenger bows to Indra's queen, and she weeps for joy.
**Narration:** "Indra ruled once more in his golden city. And a messenger carried the news to his queen on Mount Meru: the demon has fallen, and the gods are out of prison."
**Sound:** bells across the sky.

### 8. The prince's smile (10 sec)
**Visual:** Jayantan, crown on his head, stands at the edge of heaven and looks up into the open sky. He smiles.
**Narration:** "The long night was over. And the prince of heaven, who had waited so long in the dark, looked up at the open sky again, free."
**Sound:** the dream shimmer of scene 2, resolved; one bell.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Song 582 (Viralimalai) says Murugan protected Indra's son, freed him from the prison, and gave heaven back to Indra to rule. This is the only song assigned to this story. In the Kanda Puranam Veerabahu opens the prison at Murugan's command; the song gives the deed to Murugan.

## Production notes

- **A gentle story.** Give the prison dignity and the release warmth: no violence is shown beyond a single cut of the war.
- **Credit Veerabahu** as the one who opens the door, as the Puranam does.
- **Open question for the owner:** whether to name Jayantan's dream in a later pass, after reading padalam 10 line by line.
