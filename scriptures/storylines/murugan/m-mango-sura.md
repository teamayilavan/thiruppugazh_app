# The Mango Tree in the Sea (Surapadman's Last Day)

`m-mango-sura` | Murugan | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: terror, awe, release

## One-line story

On the last day of the war, the demon king Surapadman hides in the sea as a giant mango tree, but Murugan's spear finds him and splits him.

**Tradition note:** the songs say "the spear split the mango tree". The Puranam has the tree fall first, and the demon's body split afterward; the film follows the Puranam.
**Added for the screen:** the colours and details of the tree (the text gives its size and fruit), the narration.

## Characters

- **Surapadman:** a mighty demon king, once proud, now desperate. In the sea he is a mango tree with eyes that can be felt but not seen.
- **Murugan:** calm, six-faced, on his peacock.
- **The spear (Vel):** light.
- **The gods:** fleeing, small against the sky.
- **Brahma and Vishnu:** step back as the flame passes.

## Places

The sky; the open sea; Meru and Kailasa as refuges.

## The storyline

### 1. The demon rises (20 sec)
Surapadman rises into the sky, huge and dark. The gods scatter in terror, crying out to Murugan.
**Narration:** "On the last day of the war, the demon king rose into the sky, and the gods cried out to Murugan."

### 2. The command (15 sec)
Murugan looks at the spear in his hand: "Go, and split his chest in a moment."
**Narration:** "Murugan spoke to his spear."

### 3. The spear blazes (20 sec)
The spear leaves, shining like countless suns. The dark illusions around Surapadman drop away like torn cloth. He sees his tricks fail; he smiles anyway, trusting in his boon, and rage fills him.
**Narration:** "The spear flew, and all his tricks fell away. But he smiled, for he thought he could not die."

### 4. The mango tree (25 sec)
He dives into the middle of the sea. A tree rises from the waves: a mango tree larger than the sky, blossoms like gold, fruit like emerald and ruby, its shade over the seas and the earth. The moon and sun shrink back.
**Narration:** "He went into the sea and became a mango tree, as tall as the sky."

### 5. The world shakes (20 sec)
The tree shakes, the sea heaves, the tortoise and the great serpents that hold the earth reel. The gods flee to Meru and Kailasa.
**Narration:** "The earth shook with it. The gods ran for shelter."

### 6. The flame (25 sec)
The spear becomes a river of flame over the seas and the sky. Even Brahma and Vishnu step back as it passes.
**Narration:** "The spear followed him, a flame across all the seas."

### 7. The tree falls (30 sec)
The flame strikes the tree at its root. It groans and falls into the sea. But Surapadman does not die: he stands again in his own shape, sword in hand, and rushes at Murugan. The spear opens his chest, and he falls in two, into the sea.
**Narration:** "The tree fell. But he rose again, and ran at Murugan. And the spear split him, and he fell."

### 8. The turn (10 sec)
The sea settles. Two shadows move beneath the water.
**Narration:** "But the two halves did not die."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri says in about seventy songs that Murugan split the mango tree. Songs to show: 37, 48, 72, 82 and 101 (Thiruchendur), 215 (Swamimalai). The next film, `m-peacock-cock`, tells what happened to the two halves.

## Production notes

- **Not a gory scene.** The fall is shown through light, shadow and the sea.
- **Beauty first.** The tree is described in jewels: make it marvellous before it is menacing.
- **Link forward:** end on the two shadows so the next film begins at once.
