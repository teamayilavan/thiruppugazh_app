# The Mango Tree in the Sea (Surapadman's Last Day)

`m-mango-sura` | Murugan | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: terror, awe, release | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

On the last day of the war, the demon king Surapadman hides in the sea as a giant mango tree, but Murugan's spear finds him and splits him.

**Tradition note:** the songs say "the spear split the mango tree". The Puranam has the tree fall first, and the demon's body split afterward; the film follows the Puranam.
**Added for the screen:** the colours and details of the tree (the text gives its size and fruit), the narration.

## Characters

- **Surapadman:** a mighty demon king, once proud, now desperate. In the sea he is a mango tree with eyes that can be felt but not seen.
- **Murugan:** calm, six-faced, on his peacock.
- **The spear (Vel):** light.
- **The gods:** fleeing, small against the sky.
- **Brahma and Vishnu:** step back as the flame passes.

## Places

The sky; the open sea; Meru and Kailasa as refuges.

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; tense in the sky, full of wonder at the tree, hushed at the end.

### 1. The demon rises (20 sec)
**Visual:** The last morning of the war. Over a red horizon Surapadman rises into the sky, huge and dark, his shadow sliding over land and sea. The gods, small bright figures, scatter before him and turn their faces toward Murugan.
**Narration:** "The war had gone on for many days. Surapadman's brothers and sons had fallen, one by one. Now the demon king rose into the sky himself, and the gods ran in terror, crying out to Murugan to save them."
**Sound:** low war drums; a rush of wind; distant cries.

### 2. The command (15 sec)
**Visual:** Murugan, calm and six-faced on the field, looks down at the spear in his hand. The spear begins to glow.
**Narration:** "Murugan did not move. He looked at the spear in his hand, and spoke to it."
**Dialogue:** Murugan: "Go. Split his chest in a moment."
**Sound:** a single deep bell.

### 3. The spear blazes (20 sec)
**Visual:** The spear leaves his hand, shining like countless suns. The dark mist of illusion around Surapadman tears away like old cloth. He sees it, and yet he smiles; then rage fills his face.
**Narration:** "The spear flew out, as bright as countless suns, and all the demon's dark magic fell away. Surapadman saw it coming. Yet he smiled, for he had a boon, and he believed he could never be destroyed. Then fury filled him."
**Sound:** a rising shimmer; tearing cloth; a low laugh.

### 4. The mango tree (25 sec)
**Visual:** He dives into the middle of the sea. From the waves a tree rises and keeps rising: a mango tree taller than the clouds, its blossoms gold, its young fruit emerald green, the ripe fruit red as rubies. Its shade spreads over the seas, the sky and the earth. The moon and the sun draw back; the great elephants that guard the quarters lift their trunks in fear.
**Narration:** "He went down into the middle of the sea, and there he became a mango tree. It was taller than the sky could hold. Its blossoms were like gold, its fruit like emeralds and rubies, and its shade lay over the seas and the whole earth. Even the sun and the moon were afraid."
**Sound:** the sea closing; a slow, strange, beautiful chord.

### 5. The world shakes (20 sec)
**Visual:** The tree shakes its branches. The sea heaves; far below, the great tortoise and the serpents that bear the earth reel. The gods flee to the white peaks of Meru and Kailasa.
**Narration:** "Then the tree shook, and all the worlds shook with it. The tortoise and the serpents that hold up the earth reeled. The gods fled to the mountains for shelter."
**Sound:** a deep groan of wood and earth; rushing water.

### 6. The flame (25 sec)
**Visual:** The spear follows him into the sea. It becomes a river of flame spreading over the waters and up into the heavens. Brahma and Vishnu, watching from the sky, step back as it passes.
**Narration:** "But the spear followed him. It became a flame that spread over the seas and the heavens. Even Brahma and Vishnu stepped back as it passed. Nothing in all the worlds could stand in its way."
**Sound:** a roaring wind of fire; then a high, clear note.

### 7. The tree falls (30 sec)
**Visual:** The flame strikes the tree at its root. The great mango groans and falls into the sea in a slow wall of spray. From the foam Surapadman stands again in his own shape, sword raised, and rushes at Murugan. A flash of light across his chest; his silhouette parts in two and sinks into the waves. Nothing more is shown.
**Narration:** "The spear struck the tree at its root, and the mango tree fell with a groan. But because of his boon, the demon did not die. He took his own shape again, drew his sword, and rushed at Murugan. Then the spear opened his chest, split him in two, and he fell into the sea."
**Sound:** a long creak and crash; silence; one flash of sound; water closing.

### 8. The turn (10 sec)
**Visual:** The sea settles under a clearing sky. Deep beneath the water, two shadows stir.
**Narration:** "The songs say, in one line, that the spear split the mango tree. But the two halves did not die."
**Sound:** a soft, questioning note held over the waves.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri says in about seventy songs that Murugan's spear split the mango tree in which Surapadman hid. Songs to show: 37, 48, 72, 82 and 101 (Thiruchendur), 215 (Swamimalai). The next film, The Peacock and the Cock, tells what became of the two halves.

## Production notes

- **Not a gory scene.** The fall is shown through light, shadow and the sea.
- **Beauty first.** The tree is described in jewels: make it marvellous before it is menacing.
- **Link forward:** end on the two shadows so the next film begins at once.
