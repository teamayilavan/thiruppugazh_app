# Tarakan and the Mountain of Illusions

`m-tarakasura` | Murugan | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: tense, then bright, then still

## One-line story

Tarakan, brother of the demon king, hides in a mountain of illusions and fools Murugan's best warriors, until Murugan sends his spear and the illusions fall away.

**Added for the screen:** the look of the illusions (shifting paths, false enemies, echoing halls), the music, the narration. The text says the warriors were bewildered; it does not describe each illusion.

## Characters

- **Tarakan:** a tall warrior-asura in dark armour, wearing a discus on his arm as an ornament. Confident, then joyful, then startled.
- **Veerabahu:** Murugan's chief captain, fine and brave, shown lost in illusion.
- **Murugan:** seated, young and calm, with the spear in his hand.
- **The Vel (spear):** a spear of light; it is never seen thrown, only seen shining.
- **The warriors of Murugan's host.**

## Places

The Krouncha mountain, grey and strange, full of caves; the city of illusion at its side; Murugan's camp on the plain.

## The storyline

### 1. The mountain and the city (20 sec)
Dawn on a grey mountain full of holes. Beside it, in a city that seems to shimmer, a tall warrior walks the ramparts: Tarakan, the brother of Surapadman, a discus shining on his arm like an ornament.
**Narration:** "Beside the Krouncha mountain stood a city of illusion. Its master was Tarakan, the brother of the demon king."

### 2. The host arrives (15 sec)
Murugan's army marches over the plain: banners, drums, the spear in front. The mountain seems to watch them.
**Narration:** "Murugan's army marched toward the mountain, which harms all who come near it."

### 3. Into the maze (25 sec)
Veerabahu and the advance warriors go ahead. The paths bend, the walls move, false enemies come and go, and the warriors stagger, dazed, as though in a dream. Their weapons fall.
**Narration:** "Veerabahu and his men went in first. But the mountain was full of tricks, and they were lost."

### 4. Tarakan hides, then comes out (15 sec)
From a cave, Tarakan watches the warriors fall still. He believes them dead and strides out with a laugh, climbing the mountain.
**Narration:** "Tarakan watched from the cave. He thought they were dead, and he came out in joy."

### 5. Murugan looks to the spear (20 sec)
Cut to Murugan, calm on his seat. He lifts his eyes to the spear in his hand. A beat of silence.
**Narration:** "Then Murugan looked at his spear."

### 6. The light (25 sec)
The spear leaves his hand as a blaze of light. Wherever the light falls, the illusions melt: the false paths, the false enemies, the shimmer on the city. The warriors wake, standing clear in the open.
**Narration:** "The spear shone, and every illusion in all the worlds fell away."

### 7. The fall (30 sec)
The light strikes Tarakan and the mountain together. There is a roar as if the earth split and Meru were breaking. Tarakan leaps up with a cry and falls to the ground. The mountain cracks open. The sound fades to wind.
**Narration:** "It pierced Tarakan and the mountain together, and he fell, as Meru fell when the mountains lost their wings."

### 8. The clear day (15 sec)
Veerabahu rises and looks around. The grey fog is gone; the city is only stone. The army cheers softly. The spear returns, shining, to Murugan's hand.
**Narration:** "The illusions were gone. And the spear came home."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri names Tarakan among the asuras Murugan destroyed, often with the Krouncha mountain. Songs to show: 80 (Thiruchendur), 138 and 150 (Palani), 324 (Kanchipuram), 385 and 387 (Thiruvannamalai).

## Production notes

- **The illusions are the mood.** Make them beautiful and eerie, not scary for young viewers.
- **No on-screen death.** The fall is shown by sound, light and a cracking mountain.
- **Works as a pair with `m-krouncha`**, which tells why the mountain is cursed. Keep the two visually linked.
