# Tarakan and the Mountain of Illusions

`m-tarakasura` | Murugan | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: tense, then bright, then still | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

Tarakan, brother of the demon king, hides in a mountain of illusions and fools Murugan's best warriors, until Murugan sends his spear and the illusions fall away.

**Added for the screen:** the look of the illusions (shifting paths, false enemies, echoing halls), the music, the narration. The text says the warriors were bewildered; it does not describe each illusion.

## Characters

- **Tarakan:** a tall warrior-asura in dark armour, wearing a discus on his arm as an ornament. Confident, then joyful, then startled.
- **Veerabahu:** Murugan's chief captain, fine and brave, shown lost in illusion.
- **Murugan:** seated, young and calm, with the spear in his hand.
- **The Vel (spear):** a spear of light; it is never seen thrown, only seen shining.
- **The warriors of Murugan's host.**

## Places

The Krouncha mountain, grey and strange, full of caves; the city of illusion at its side; Murugan's camp on the plain.

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; hushed and uneasy in the maze, bright when the spear shines, still at the end.

### 1. The mountain and the city (20 sec)
**Visual:** Dawn on a grey mountain riddled with holes and caves. Beside it, a city that seems to shimmer and shift like heat haze. On its ramparts walks a tall warrior in dark armour, a discus shining on his arm like an ornament: Tarakan.
**Narration:** "Beside the Krouncha mountain stood a city of illusion. The mountain had once been a demon, turned to stone by a sage's curse. The master of the city was Tarakan, the brother of the demon king Surapadman. On his arm he wore the discus of Vishnu, as an ornament."
**Sound:** a low, uneasy drone; wind through caves.

### 2. The host arrives (15 sec)
**Visual:** Murugan's army marches across the plain: banners, drums, warriors in bright ranks. The mountain's dark hollows seem to watch them come.
**Narration:** "Murugan's army marched toward the mountain. It was a place that brought harm to everyone who came near it. Inside his fort, Tarakan waited with his demon host."
**Sound:** marching drums; a far-off conch.

### 3. Into the maze (25 sec)
**Visual:** Veerabahu, Murugan's chief captain, leads the first warriors into the mountain. The paths bend and double back; walls drift; enemies appear and melt into mist. The warriors slow, stagger, and stand dazed as if dreaming. Their weapons slip from their hands.
**Narration:** "Veerabahu and the bravest warriors went in first. But the mountain was full of tricks. The paths turned and turned again. Enemies came out of the mist and vanished. The warriors grew bewildered, and stood still, as if lost in a dream."
**Sound:** echoing steps; whispering voices; music that bends out of tune.

### 4. Tarakan comes out (15 sec)
**Visual:** Tarakan had hidden in a cave when the army came near. Now he peers out at the motionless warriors, grins, and strides out with a laugh, climbing the slope.
**Narration:** "Tarakan had hidden in a cave of the mountain. When he saw the warriors standing so still, he thought they were dead. He came out in joy, and climbed the mountain."
**Sound:** a deep laugh echoing off the rocks.

### 5. Murugan looks to the spear (20 sec)
**Visual:** Cut to Murugan in his camp, young and calm. He lifts his eyes to the spear in his hand. A beat of silence. The spear begins to glow.
**Narration:** "Far away, Murugan sat calm and still. He knew what had happened to his warriors. He did not call for an army. He only looked at the spear in his hand."
**Sound:** the drone fades; one clear, held note.

### 6. The light (25 sec)
**Visual:** The spear leaves his hand as a blaze of light. Wherever the light falls the illusions melt: false paths, false enemies, the shimmer on the city. The warriors blink and wake, standing clear in open daylight.
**Narration:** "The spear went out as a great light. Wherever the light fell, the tricks melted away. The false paths, the false enemies, the shimmering city: every illusion in all the worlds was gone. The warriors woke, and found themselves standing in the open sun."
**Sound:** a rising shimmer; a bright chord.

### 7. The fall (30 sec)
**Visual:** The light strikes Tarakan and the mountain together. A roar fills the screen as if the earth were splitting. Tarakan leaps up, a dark silhouette against the light, and falls. The mountain cracks open. The sound fades to wind.
**Narration:** "The spear pierced Tarakan and the mountain together. There was a roar, as though the earth were splitting and the great mountain Meru were breaking. Tarakan leaped up with a cry, and fell. The poet says he fell as Meru fell, long ago, when the mountains lost their wings."
**Sound:** a vast crack; a cry cut short; wind.

### 8. The clear day (15 sec)
**Visual:** Veerabahu rises and looks around. The grey fog is gone; the city is only stone. The army cheers softly. The spear returns, shining, to Murugan's hand.
**Narration:** "The fog was gone, and the city was only stone. It was the first great victory of the war. And the spear came home to Murugan's hand."
**Sound:** soft cheering; a returning chime.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri names Tarakan among the asuras Murugan destroyed, often together with the Krouncha mountain. Songs to show: 80 (Thiruchendur), 138 and 150 (Palani), 324 (Kanchipuram), 385 and 387 (Thiruvannamalai).

## Production notes

- **The illusions are the mood.** Make them beautiful and eerie, not scary for young viewers.
- **No on-screen death.** The fall is shown by sound, light and a cracking mountain.
- **Works as a pair with `m-krouncha`**, which tells why the mountain is cursed. Keep the two visually linked.
