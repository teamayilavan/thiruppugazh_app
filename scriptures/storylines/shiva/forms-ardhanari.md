# Half Shiva, Half Uma (Ardhanari)

`forms-ardhanari` | Shiva | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: serene, balanced, devotional

## One-line story

Shiva shares half his body with Parvati; at Kanchi, Kamakshi's penance wins her the left half of Ekambareshwara.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Shiva shares half his body with Parvati (Ardhanarishvara) | Shiva Purana, Satarudra-samhita ch.3 |
| At Kanchi, Kamakshi's penance wins her the left half of Ekambareshwara | research set `forms-ardhanari`; temple tradition |
| The Thiruppugazh often marks Murugan as son of the Lord with Uma at his left | Thiruppugazh songs (40 songs, e.g. 13, 94, 179, 226) |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Ardhanari:** one figure, right half Shiva (ash, matted hair, snake), left half Parvati (silk, jewels, flowers).
- **Murugan** (a child in the end).

## The storyline

### 1. The two (25 sec)
Shiva and Parvati sit side by side on Kailasa.
**Narration:** "Shiva and Parvati sat together on Kailasa."

### 2. The penance (25 sec)
At Kanchi, Parvati sits in penance under a mango tree.
**Narration:** "At Kanchi, the Goddess did penance."

### 3. The wish (20 sec)
A voice from the tree asks what she wants. She asks to be one with him.
**Narration:** "She asked to be one with the Lord."

### 4. The joining (30 sec)
The two figures merge into one: half Shiva, half Parvati.
**Narration:** "And the Lord gave her half of himself."

### 5. The form (25 sec)
The shared figure stands, calm and complete.
**Narration:** "So he is called Ardhanari: the Lord who is half woman."

### 6. The child (20 sec)
A golden child steps between them, smiling.
**Narration:** "And their son was born to them."

### 7. The song (15 sec)
A Thiruppugazh line floats: Uma at his left.
**Narration:** "The poet sings of Uma at the Lord's left."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

40 songs mark Murugan as the son of the Lord with Uma at his left, including 13, 94, 179 and 226.

## Production notes

- **Keep it perfectly balanced.** The figure should look unified, not split.
- **Pair with `d-kanchi`.**
