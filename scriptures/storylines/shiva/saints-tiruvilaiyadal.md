# The Sports of the Lord at Madurai (Tiruvilaiyadal)

`saints-tiruvilaiyadal` | Shiva | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: playful, wondrous, affectionate

## One-line story

Shiva as Sokkanathar, consort of Meenakshi, works sixty-four wonders at Madurai: turning jackals into horses, carrying earth for the Vaigai bank and being struck by the king's cane, and arbitrating the poet Nakkeerar's dispute.

## The storyline

### 1. The city (20 sec)
Madurai, the Lord's city: towers, pilgrims.
**Narration:** "At Madurai, the Lord played sixty-four sports."

### 2. The jackals (25 sec)
Foxes in the dark are turned to horses.
**Narration:** "He turned jackals into horses."

### 3. The river (20 sec)
The Vaigai in flood; the old woman Vandi's labourer.
**Narration:** "He carried earth for a poor woman's share of the bank."

### 4. The cane (25 sec)
The king strikes him with a cane; the blow is felt by all.
**Narration:** "The king struck him with a cane."

### 5. The poem (25 sec)
At the Sangam, the Lord reads a poem; Nakkeerar finds a fault.
**Narration:** "He wrote a poem, and the poet Nakkeerar found a fault."

### 6. The third eye (20 sec)
The Lord's eye opens in flame; Nakkeerar stands firm.
**Narration:** "The Lord's eye opened. The poet would not bow."

### 7. The grace (15 sec)
The Lord smiles and restores him.
**Narration:** "And the Lord was pleased."

### 8. The sixty-four (10 sec)
Sixty-four lamps glow.
**Narration:** "There were sixty-four such sports, and Madurai remembers each."
