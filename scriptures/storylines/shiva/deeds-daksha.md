# Daksha's Sacrifice

`deeds-daksha` | Shiva | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: grave, tragic, then merciful | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

Daksha insults Shiva and holds a sacrifice to which Shiva is not invited; his daughter Sati dies there, Virabhadra wrecks the sacrifice, and Daksha is later revived with a goat's head.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Daksha slights Shiva | Bhagavata Purana 4.2.1-19 |
| Sati dies | SB 4.2-4.5 |
| Shiva sends Virabhadra, who wrecks the sacrifice | SB 4.5.1-20 |
| Brahma propitiates Shiva; Daksha gets a goat's head | SB 4.6-4.7 |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration; the film is shown without any violence beyond a storm of wind and light.

## Characters

- **Daksha:** a proud king, with a crown.
- **Sati:** Daksha's daughter and Shiva's wife, gentle and dignified.
- **Shiva:** a calm figure, then angry.
- **Virabhadra:** a fierce form born of Shiva's anger.
- **Brahma and the gods.**

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; grave and sad through Sati's story, stormy in the middle, gentle and merciful at the end.

### 1. The great sacrifice (20 sec)
**Visual:** A vast sacrificial ground: rows of fire altars, banners, gods and sages arriving on chariots and clouds. At the centre, on a high seat, sits Daksha, proud and richly dressed.
**Narration:** "Daksha was a great lord among the creators, and the father of Sati, the wife of Shiva. Once he began a grand sacrifice, and gods and sages came from every world to share in it."
**Sound:** conch shells, chanting, a festive bustle.

### 2. The insult (20 sec)
**Visual:** Daksha's attendants set out shares of the offering for each god. One place is left empty. Daksha waves it away with a scornful hand.
**Narration:** "But Daksha did not invite Shiva. He thought little of his son-in-law, who smeared himself with ash and lived among the burning grounds. Before the gods he had spoken ill of him, and now he set no share aside for him."
**Sound:** the festive music falters on one sour note.

### 3. Sati comes (25 sec)
**Visual:** On Kailasa, Sati watches the bright procession of chariots going to her father's house. She turns to Shiva, pleading. He shakes his head gently; she goes anyway, alone.
**Narration:** "Sati saw the gods going to her father's house, and she longed to go too. Shiva warned her gently."
**Dialogue:** Shiva: "Where there is no welcome, a guest finds only pain. Your father will not honour you there."
**Dialogue:** Sati: "He is my father. I must go."
**Sound:** a tender, uneasy melody.

### 4. Sati's sorrow (20 sec)
**Visual:** Sati enters the sacrificial ground. No one rises; Daksha turns his face away. She sees the empty place where Shiva's share should be. She sits apart, closes her eyes, and becomes a column of soft white light that rises and is gone.
**Narration:** "At the sacrifice no one greeted her. Her father turned away. Sati could not bear to hear her husband scorned. She closed her eyes in meditation, and gave up her body."
**Sound:** the music stops; one long, sorrowful note.

### 5. The anger (20 sec)
**Visual:** On Kailasa, Shiva hears the news. His face is still, but the sky darkens. He pulls a single lock from his matted hair and casts it down; from it rises a towering dark warrior with many arms: Virabhadra.
**Narration:** "When Shiva heard, his grief became a terrible anger. He tore a lock from his matted hair and threw it down, and from it rose Virabhadra, a mighty and fearful warrior."
**Sound:** thunder; a deep war drum.

### 6. The storm (30 sec)
**Visual:** Virabhadra and Shiva's hosts sweep down on the sacrifice as a storm of wind and light. Banners tear, altars scatter, fires go out, the gods flee like leaves. Daksha falls, seen only as a shadow swept away in the gale. Nothing more is shown.
**Narration:** "Virabhadra and the hosts of Shiva came down on the sacrifice like a storm. The altars were scattered and the fires put out, and the gods fled in every direction. The proud Daksha fell. The sacrifice that had left out the Lord was broken."
**Sound:** a howling wind, drums, then sudden silence.

### 7. The prayer (20 sec)
**Visual:** Brahma leads the shaken gods up to Kailasa. They bow before Shiva, who sits calm again beneath a tree. He raises his hand in blessing.
**Narration:** "Then Brahma led the gods to Kailasa and begged Shiva for mercy. His anger cooled, for his heart is kind. He said that the sacrifice should be completed, and that Daksha should live."
**Sound:** a soft hymn; a single bell.

### 8. The goat's head (10 sec)
**Visual:** Daksha rises, humbled, with the head of a goat, and bows low toward Kailasa.
**Narration:** "Daksha rose again, with the head of a goat, and bowed at last to the Lord."
**Sound:** a quiet, settling chord.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri recalls the Lord who broke the proud Daksha's sacrifice. Songs to show: 309 (Kanchipuram), 458, 461 and 491 (Chidambaram), 904 (Vayalur).

## Production notes

- **Sati's death is shown as a light**, not as a body.
- **The goat's head is a symbol** of humility, not a joke.
