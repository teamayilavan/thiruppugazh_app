# Under the Kurundam Tree (Manikkavasagar Meets His Guru)

`saints-kurundam` | Shiva | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: stirring, quiet, transformative

## One-line story

The Pandya king's minister, sent to buy horses, meets Shiva as a guru under the kurundam tree at Tiruperunthurai, gives the money to the temple, and gives his life to Shiva.

**Honesty note:** this film is the inner story of the same events as `n-manikkavasagar` (which shows the horses and the foxes). It is the same story with a different focus, the transformation of the minister.
**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Manikkavasagar:** a young, learned minister, restless.
- **The guru:** Shiva, a teacher beneath the tree.

## The storyline

### 1. The minister (25 sec)
In the Pandya court, a brilliant minister, honoured but restless.
**Narration:** "A minister of the Pandya king, honoured and clever."

### 2. The mission (20 sec)
He takes the royal treasure and sets out to buy horses.
**Narration:** "He was sent with gold to buy horses."

### 3. The tree (25 sec)
At Perunthurai he hears chanting and sees a teacher beneath a kurundam tree.
**Narration:** "At Perunthurai he heard a voice."

### 4. The guru (25 sec)
He kneels before the teacher, and tears come.
**Narration:** "He had found his teacher."

### 5. The touch (20 sec)
The guru touches his head; his face changes.
**Narration:** "One touch changed him."

### 6. The offering (25 sec)
He gives the gold to build a shrine.
**Narration:** "He gave all the gold to the temple."

### 7. The gift of his life (25 sec)
He kneels, with folded hands: his life is the Lord's.
**Narration:** "And he gave his life to Shiva."

Songs 7, 844 and 1174.
