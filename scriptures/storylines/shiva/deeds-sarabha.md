# The Calming of Narasimha (Sarabha)

`deeds-sarabha` | Shiva | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: mighty, anxious, resolved

## One-line story

After Narasimha kills the demon Hiranyakashipu, his rage does not cool; Shiva takes the fierce form of Sarabha, part lion and part bird, and calms him.

**Honesty note:** the story is told in later Puranas and Shaiva tradition, not in the Bhagavata; the film says so. It is also a story about two gods that some devotees hold sacred in different ways, so it is handled with restraint.
**Added for the screen:** the visuals, the narration.

## Characters

- **Narasimha:** the man-lion, golden, mane blazing.
- **Sarabha:** Shiva's form, part lion, part bird, with wings.
- **The gods:** frightened.

## The storyline

### 1. The rage (25 sec)
The man-lion stands over the fallen demon; his rage blazes, and the worlds tremble.
**Narration:** "Narasimha had destroyed the demon. But his rage did not cool."

### 2. The gods' fear (20 sec)
The gods watch, terrified; they go to Shiva.
**Narration:** "The gods feared the whole world might burn."

### 3. The form (25 sec)
Shiva rises into a great winged lion-bird: Sarabha.
**Narration:** "Shiva took the form of Sarabha."

### 4. The meeting (25 sec)
Sarabha faces Narasimha across a burning sky.
**Narration:** "He stood before the man-lion."

### 5. The calming (25 sec)
Narasimha's roar softens; his mane subsides.
**Narration:** "And the rage faded."

### 6. The prayer (20 sec)
Narasimha bows to Shiva.
**Narration:** "Narasimha prayed to him."

### 7. The calm (10 sec)
The sky clears.
**Narration:** "And the world was at peace."

Songs 424 and 448.
