# The Meaning of Om Whispered in His Father's Ear

`murugan-pranava` | Shiva | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: warm, wondering, a reversal

## One-line story

When Shiva comes to ask for Brahma's release, Murugan tells him in his ear the meaning of Om, and the songs say he became his father's teacher.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Brahma could not explain the meaning of Om, so Murugan imprisoned him | Kanda Puranam, Utpatti Kandam 1213-1229 (see `m-brahma-prison`) |
| Shiva comes to Murugan and asks for Brahma's release | 1229-1256 |
| Murugan tells Shiva the meaning of Om in his ear | research set `murugan-pranava` (Thiruppugazh songs; 68 songs) |
| The Kanda Puranam sets this on Mount Skanda; later Tamil tradition places it at Swamimalai (Swaminatha) | research |

**Honesty note:** the Kanda Puranam lines read for this research give the release of Brahma; the whispering of the meaning to Shiva is the songs' and later tradition's telling, placed at Swamimalai.
**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Murugan:** a child, calm, six-faced or one-faced as the film prefers.
- **Shiva:** a loving father, curious and delighted.
- **Brahma:** in prison.

## The storyline

### 1. The prison (20 sec)
Brahma sits in prison on Mount Skanda; Murugan creates the world beside him.
**Narration:** "Brahma was in prison, because he did not know the meaning of Om."

### 2. The visitor (20 sec)
Shiva arrives, with the gods.
**Narration:** "Shiva came to ask for his release."

### 3. The seat (20 sec)
Murugan seats his father on a great jewelled seat.
**Narration:** "The child seated his father with honour."

### 4. The question (25 sec)
Shiva asks: "Tell me, then: what is the meaning of Om?"
**Narration:** "Shiva asked him the meaning."

### 5. The whisper (30 sec)
Murugan climbs onto his father's lap and whispers in his ear. Light flows between them.
**Narration:** "And Murugan told it to him in his ear."

### 6. The father's smile (20 sec)
Shiva laughs softly, with delight.
**Narration:** "The father listened, like a student."

### 7. The release (20 sec)
Brahma is freed and bows.
**Narration:** "And Brahma was freed."

### 8. The name (10 sec)
The title "Swaminatha" glows: the Lord who is lord of the Lord.
**Narration:** "At Swamimalai, they call him Swaminatha: the teacher of the Lord."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

68 songs repeat this story, including 6, 12, 33 and 50.

## Production notes

- **The whisper is silent.** Do not invent what the meaning is.
- **Respect Shiva.** The reversal is loving, not mocking.
