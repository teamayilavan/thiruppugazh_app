# The Dancer of the Golden Hall (Nataraja)

`forms-nataraja` | Shiva | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: rhythmic, ecstatic, cosmic

## One-line story

Shiva dances: the Ananda Tandava in the golden hall at Chidambaram, the dance contest with Kali at Thiruvalangadu, the dance in the burning ground, with his foot on the dwarf Muyalakan, while Vishnu, Brahma and the serpent Adisesha watch.

**Added for the screen:** the choreography, the music and the narration.

## Characters

- **Nataraja:** a four-armed dancer with a drum, a flame, and a raised foot, ringed by fire.
- **Muyalakan:** a small dark dwarf underfoot.
- **Vishnu, Brahma, Adisesha:** onlookers.
- **Kali:** in the contest.

## The storyline

### 1. The golden hall (25 sec)
The temple hall at Chidambaram, golden and quiet.
**Narration:** "At Chidambaram stands a golden hall."

### 2. The drum (20 sec)
A drum beats, a flame flickers. The dance begins.
**Narration:** "There the Lord dances."

### 3. The Ananda Tandava (30 sec)
Nataraja whirls within a ring of fire, one foot raised, drum and flame in hands.
**Narration:** "The dance of joy: the Ananda Tandava."

### 4. The dwarf (20 sec)
Beneath his foot, a small dark dwarf, Muyalakan, crouched.
**Narration:** "Under his foot is a dwarf, who stands for forgetfulness."

### 5. The watchers (20 sec)
Vishnu, Brahma and Adisesha look on, still.
**Narration:** "Vishnu, Brahma and the serpent watched."

### 6. The burning ground (20 sec)
He dances on the cremation ground with ghosts and goblins.
**Narration:** "He dances in the burning ground too."

### 7. The contest (20 sec)
A brief flash of Kali opposite him: the contest at Thiruvalangadu.
**Narration:** "He danced against Kali, and won."

### 8. The stillness (10 sec)
The dance stops; the hall is silent.
**Narration:** "And all the world is his dance."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

45 songs recall the dancing Lord, including 7, 23, 71 and 83.

## Production notes

- **A film made of music.** Use an authentic rhythm.
- **Do not make the dwarf a villain.** He is the symbol of forgetfulness.
