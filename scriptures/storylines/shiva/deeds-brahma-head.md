# Brahma's Fifth Head (Bhairava)

`deeds-brahma-head` | Shiva | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: grave, fierce, then a long wandering | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

Brahma's fifth head speaks with pride, and Shiva, as Bhairava, plucks it off with a fingernail; the skull sticks to his hand as a begging bowl and he wanders until it falls away.

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration. The head's removal is shown in light and shadow only.

## Characters

- **Brahma:** with four faces, and a fifth that shines.
- **Bhairava:** a dark, fierce form of Shiva.
- **Brahmahatya:** a shadow that follows him.

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; grave in the middle, slow and weary in the wandering, quiet at the end.

### 1. The five faces (20 sec)
**Visual:** Brahma on his lotus, with five faces: four looking to the four directions, and a fifth above them, shining with a hard, bright light of its own.
**Narration:** "In the beginning, Brahma the creator had not four heads, but five. Four looked out to the four directions. The fifth looked upward, and it shone with a fierce light of its own."
**Sound:** a bright, cold tone.

### 2. The boast (20 sec)
**Visual:** The fifth face grows brighter, its light pushing the others into shadow. It speaks, chin raised.
**Narration:** "The old texts tell this in more than one way. In one, Brahma spoke a lie before Shiva. In another, the fifth head blazed with pride and claimed to be equal to Shiva himself."
**Dialogue:** The fifth head: "I am the first of all. Who is greater than I?"
**Sound:** the cold tone swells, harsh.

### 3. The anger (20 sec)
**Visual:** On Kailasa, Shiva's brow darkens. From it springs a fierce dark figure, blazing at the edges, with wild hair and burning eyes: Bhairava.
**Narration:** "Then from Shiva's brow there sprang a fierce form, dark and blazing. This was Bhairava, the terrible one, before whom all fear and all pride must bow."
**Sound:** a deep drum, once, twice.

### 4. The fingernail (25 sec)
**Visual:** Bhairava stands before Brahma, seen only in silhouette against the fifth head's glare. He raises one hand; a single fingernail catches the light. A flash fills the screen. When it clears, only four faces remain, bowed and silent.
**Narration:** "Bhairava did not take up any weapon. With the nail of one finger, he plucked away the proud fifth head. There was a flash of light, and then there were only four faces, bowed and silent."
**Sound:** a sharp, ringing note; then silence.

### 5. The skull sticks (20 sec)
**Visual:** Bhairava opens his hand to let the skull fall, but it clings to his palm. He shakes his hand; it stays, pale and still.
**Narration:** "But the skull of that head would not leave his hand. He opened his fingers, and still it clung to his palm. Even the punisher of pride must bear the weight of what was done."
**Sound:** a low, hollow note.

### 6. The wandering (30 sec)
**Visual:** Bhairava walks as a beggar through forests, cities, the sky and the underworld, holding the skull as a bowl. Behind him glides a grey shadow-woman, Brahmahatya, always a few steps behind.
**Narration:** "So Bhairava became a wanderer, carrying the skull as his begging bowl. The Shiva Purana says he went through all three worlds in this vow, asking for alms. Behind him, like a shadow, followed Brahmahatya, the guilt of killing a brahmin, and she would not let him rest."
**Sound:** slow footsteps; a thin, following wind.

### 7. The release (20 sec)
**Visual:** He reaches a holy riverside place, bright with morning. The skull loosens and falls from his hand into the light; the shadow behind him fades like mist.
**Narration:** "At last he came to a holy place, and there the skull fell from his hand, and the shadow vanished. Different traditions name different places where this happened."
**Sound:** water; a clear bell.

### 8. The wisdom (10 sec)
**Visual:** Brahma, with four heads now, bows on his lotus toward the distant Bhairava.
**Narration:** "And Brahma, with four heads, bowed. His pride had been cut away."
**Sound:** a soft, settling chord.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri calls Shiva the Lord who plucked away Brahma's proud fifth head with his nail. Songs to show: 188 (Palani), 577 (Viralimalai), 750 (Vriddhachalam), 797 (Thiruvidaikkazhi).

## Production notes

- **No blood, no severed head shown.** Use light and shadow.
- **The place where the skull falls** varies in tradition; the film does not name it.
