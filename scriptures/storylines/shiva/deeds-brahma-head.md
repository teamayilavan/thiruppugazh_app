# Brahma's Fifth Head (Bhairava)

`deeds-brahma-head` | Shiva | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: grave, fierce, then a long wandering

## One-line story

Brahma's fifth head speaks with pride, and Shiva, as Bhairava, plucks it off with a fingernail; the skull sticks to his hand as a begging bowl and he wanders until it falls away.

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration. The head's removal is shown in light and shadow only.

## Characters

- **Brahma:** with four faces, and a fifth that shines.
- **Bhairava:** a dark, fierce form of Shiva.
- **Brahmahatya:** a shadow that follows him.

## The storyline

### 1. The five faces (20 sec)
Brahma with five heads; the fifth glows with pride.
**Narration:** "Brahma once had five heads."

### 2. The boast (20 sec)
The fifth head speaks proudly, claiming equality with Shiva.
**Narration:** "The fifth head spoke in pride."

### 3. The anger (20 sec)
From Shiva's brow, a fierce form leaps: Bhairava.
**Narration:** "And Shiva's anger took the form of Bhairava."

### 4. The fingernail (25 sec)
Bhairava plucks the head off with a nail; light and shadow flash.
**Narration:** "He plucked off the fifth head with a nail."

### 5. The skull sticks (20 sec)
The skull clings to his palm.
**Narration:** "The skull stuck to his hand."

### 6. The wandering (30 sec)
He wanders the three worlds, begging, with a shadow following him.
**Narration:** "He wandered the worlds with it as his bowl."

### 7. The release (20 sec)
At last the skull falls from his hand.
**Narration:** "At last it fell."

### 8. The wisdom (10 sec)
Brahma, with four heads, bows.
**Narration:** "And Brahma learned."

## Production notes

- **No blood, no severed head shown.** Use light and shadow.
- **The place where the skull falls** varies in tradition; the film does not name it.
