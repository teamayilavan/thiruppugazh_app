# The King Who Roared (Ravana Lifts Kailasa)

`deeds-ravana` | Shiva | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: arrogant, then crushed, then reconciled

## One-line story

Ravana tries to uproot Mount Kailasa; Shiva presses it down with his toe and crushes the demon's arms, and after a long cry and hymns Ravana is released and named "the one who roared".

**Added for the screen:** the visuals, the narration.

## Characters

- **Ravana:** a ten-headed king, massive, golden crown.
- **Nandi:** the bull-guardian, calm and unmoved.
- **Shiva:** serene on the mountain, with Parvati.
- **Parvati:** frightened by the shaking.

## The storyline

### 1. The flying chariot (20 sec)
Ravana's golden chariot flies over snowy peaks.
**Narration:** "Ravana flew in his chariot over the mountains."

### 2. The gate (25 sec)
At Kailasa, Nandi stops him: "No one passes here."
**Narration:** "At Kailasa, Nandi barred his way."

### 3. The scorn (20 sec)
Ravana mocks Nandi and strides toward the mountain.
**Narration:** "Ravana mocked him, and went to the mountain itself."

### 4. The lifting (25 sec)
He puts his twenty arms under Kailasa and heaves; the mountain shakes.
**Narration:** "He tried to lift the mountain."

### 5. The toe (25 sec)
Shiva presses with one toe; the mountain settles, Ravana's arms trapped.
**Narration:** "Shiva pressed it down with his toe."

### 6. The roar (25 sec)
Ravana cries out, a roar that shakes the worlds.
**Narration:** "He roared, and that is how he got his name."

### 7. The hymns (20 sec)
For a long time he sings hymns; at last Shiva releases him.
**Narration:** "He sang for the Lord's grace, and the Lord let him go."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 312, 485 and 522.

## Production notes

- **Pride, not cruelty.** Ravana is shown as magnificent and foolish.
- **Stylised pain.** No injury shown, only a roar and light.
