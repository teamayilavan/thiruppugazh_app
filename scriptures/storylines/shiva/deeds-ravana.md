# The King Who Roared (Ravana Lifts Kailasa)

`deeds-ravana` | Shiva | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: arrogant, then crushed, then reconciled | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

Ravana tries to uproot Mount Kailasa; Shiva presses it down with his toe and crushes the demon's arms, and after a long cry and hymns Ravana is released and named "the one who roared".

**Added for the screen:** the visuals, the narration.

## Characters

- **Ravana:** a ten-headed king, massive, golden crown.
- **Nandi:** the bull-guardian, calm and unmoved.
- **Shiva:** serene on the mountain, with Parvati.
- **Parvati:** frightened by the shaking.

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; grand and a little amused at Ravana's pride, sharp at the roar, warm and forgiving at the end.

### 1. The flying chariot (20 sec)
**Visual:** Ravana, the ten-headed king of Lanka, magnificent in gold, rides through the clouds on the shining flying chariot Pushpaka, his twenty arms spread wide in triumph.
**Narration:** "Ravana, the ten-headed king of Lanka, had won battle after battle. Proud of his strength, he rode through the sky in Pushpaka, the flying chariot he had taken from his brother Kubera. He feared no one in all the worlds."
**Sound:** a proud, brassy fanfare; rushing wind.

### 2. The gate (25 sec)
**Visual:** Over a white mountain, Kailasa, the chariot jolts and stops in mid-air. Ravana frowns. On a ledge stands Nandi, Shiva's attendant, calm, holding a staff, raising one hand.
**Narration:** "But over the mountain Kailasa, the chariot suddenly stopped, and would go no further. There stood Nandi, the attendant of Shiva, quiet and unafraid."
**Dialogue:** Nandi: "Turn back, king. The Lord is here with the Goddess. No one may pass this way."
**Sound:** the fanfare breaks off; a still, clear tone.

### 3. The scorn (20 sec)
**Visual:** Ravana throws back his heads and laughs; all ten faces sneer at Nandi. Nandi does not move, but his eyes are grave.
**Narration:** "Ravana laughed, and mocked Nandi's face. Who was this, to stop the king of Lanka? Nandi warned him that his pride would one day bring him down."
**Dialogue:** Ravana: "Then I will move the mountain out of my way."
**Sound:** a booming laugh, echoing off the peaks.

### 4. The lifting (25 sec)
**Visual:** Ravana leaps down and thrusts his twenty arms beneath the mountain. He heaves; Kailasa groans and shakes; rocks fall, the attendants stagger, and Parvati catches at Shiva's arm.
**Narration:** "In his anger Ravana set his twenty arms beneath the mountain and heaved with all his strength. Kailasa shook from its roots. The attendants of Shiva trembled, and Parvati held fast to the Lord."
**Sound:** a deep, grinding rumble.

### 5. The toe (25 sec)
**Visual:** On the summit, Shiva sits at ease. He smiles slightly and presses down with the big toe of one foot. The mountain settles back into place with a single soft thud, and Ravana is pinned beneath it, his arms caught.
**Narration:** "Shiva only smiled. Without rising, he pressed down gently with one toe. The great mountain settled back into its place, and the mighty Ravana was pinned beneath it, his arms caught fast."
**Sound:** the rumble stops; one soft, heavy note.

### 6. The roar (25 sec)
**Visual:** Light bursts out around the base of the mountain as Ravana cries out. The sound ripples through the sky; clouds scatter, seas shiver. No injury is shown, only light and sound.
**Narration:** "Ravana let out a cry so loud that it shook the three worlds. The Ramayana says that is how he got his name. Ravana means the one who roared."
**Sound:** a huge, echoing roar, then ringing silence.

### 7. The hymns (20 sec)
**Visual:** Still pinned, Ravana bows his ten heads and begins to sing, plucking strings stretched from his own arm like a veena. Seasons pass. At last Shiva lifts his toe, and Ravana kneels, freed and humbled.
**Narration:** "His ministers told him that only praise could free him now. So Ravana sang hymns to Shiva for a very long time. The Tamil hymns say he played on the strings of his own sinews. At last the Lord was moved by his song, and set him free."
**Sound:** a deep, pleading melody on strings; a warm closing chord.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri remembers Kailasa as the mountain that proud Ravana tried to lift. Songs to show: 312 (Kanchipuram), 485 (Chidambaram), 522 (Kailasa).

## Production notes

- **Pride, not cruelty.** Ravana is shown as magnificent and foolish.
- **Stylised pain.** No injury shown, only a roar and light.
