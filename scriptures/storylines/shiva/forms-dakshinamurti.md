# The Teacher Who Spoke in Silence (Dakshinamurti)

`forms-dakshinamurti` | Shiva | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: still, deep, luminous

## One-line story

Shiva sits facing south under a banyan tree and teaches the sages Sanaka and his brothers by silence alone.

**Honesty note:** the research gives the story in a single line; the film adds only what makes a silent teaching visible.
**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Dakshinamurti:** a young, radiant teacher, seated, with a hand in a gesture of knowledge.
- **The four sages (Sanaka and his brothers):** old in wisdom, young in form.

## The storyline

### 1. The banyan (25 sec)
A vast banyan tree on a quiet hill; the Lord sits beneath it, facing south.
**Narration:** "Under a banyan tree sat the Lord, facing south."

### 2. The students (20 sec)
Four sages arrive, bowing, full of questions.
**Narration:** "Four sages came to him with questions."

### 3. The silence (25 sec)
He says nothing; he lifts a hand.
**Narration:** "He did not speak."

### 4. The doubts fall (25 sec)
One by one, the sages' faces change, as their questions dissolve.
**Narration:** "And their doubts melted away."

### 5. The light (20 sec)
A soft light fills the circle.
**Narration:** "They understood."

### 6. The silence continues (20 sec)
The sages sit, content.
**Narration:** "The teacher was silent. The students were still."

### 7. The song (15 sec)
A line of the Thiruppugazh over the tree.
**Narration:** "The poet remembered it."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 110 and 823.
