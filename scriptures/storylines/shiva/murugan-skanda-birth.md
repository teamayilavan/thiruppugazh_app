# Fire, River and Reeds (The Birth of Skanda in the Epics)

`murugan-skanda-birth` | Shiva | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: mythic, luminous, tender

## One-line story

In the epic and Puranic telling, six sparks from Shiva's forehead eye are carried by Agni and the Ganga to the Saravana reed pool, where they become six babies nursed by the six Krittikas, and Uma joins them into one six-faced child.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Shiva's seed borne by Agni and Ganga; six Krittikas nurse the child; Kumara/Karttikeya | Valmiki Ramayana 1.36.5-18; 1.37.1-28 |
| Rudra's tejas goes to Agni and Ganga; the Krittikas find the child in the reed thicket; named Skanda/Guha | Mahabharata 9,43.7-14; 13,84-86 |
| Birth of Shiva's son: Shiva's seed, Agni, Parvati's curse | Shiva Purana, Rudra-samhita 4 (Kumara-khanda) ch.2 |
| Birth of Kumara Karttikeya | Skanda Purana, Kedara-khanda ch.27 |
| Uma joins the six babies into one six-faced child | Tamil telling (see `m-saravana`) |
| Songs 476, 929, 1005 and 1155 recall it | Thiruppugazh songs |

**Note:** this is the Sanskrit-epic telling. The Tamil telling in the Kanda Puranam is in `m-saravana`; the two differ in detail, and this film follows the epics.
**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Shiva, Agni, Ganga, the Krittikas, the child.**

## The storyline

### 1. A son needed (20 sec)
The gods ask Shiva for a commander: a son born of his power.
**Narration:** "The gods needed a son of Shiva."

### 2. The sparks (25 sec)
Sparks fall from Shiva's forehead eye.
**Narration:** "Sparks fell from his forehead eye."

### 3. Agni (20 sec)
Agni takes them, but cannot hold them.
**Narration:** "Agni carried them, but they were too strong."

### 4. The Ganga (20 sec)
The Ganga takes them, flows with them to the reeds.
**Narration:** "The Ganga bore them to the reeds."

### 5. The reed pool (25 sec)
A lake of reeds; six babies appear on lotuses.
**Narration:** "In the reed pool, six babies were born."

### 6. The Krittikas (25 sec)
Six women nurse the children; each child has a mother.
**Narration:** "Six women nursed them."

### 7. Uma (20 sec)
Uma embraces the six, and they become one, with six faces.
**Narration:** "Uma joined them into one."

### 8. Kumara (10 sec)
The child smiles.
**Narration:** "And Kumara was born."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 476, 929, 1005 and 1155.
