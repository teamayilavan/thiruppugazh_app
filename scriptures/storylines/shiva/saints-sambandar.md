# The Child Saint at Madurai

`saints-sambandar` | Shiva | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: wonder, tension, triumph | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

The child saint Sambandar of Sirkazhi, nursed with Uma's milk, goes to Madurai, heals the Pandya's fever with holy ash, and wins the contests against the Jains.

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice; tender for the child, tense in Madurai, bright at the end. About two words a second.

### 1. The milk of wisdom (25 sec)
**Visual:** Sirkazhi, morning. By a temple tank a small child of three cries for his father, who has gone into the water to bathe. On the temple hill above, Shiva looks down and turns to Uma. She comes down with a golden bowl of milk and feeds the child.
**Narration:** "At Sirkazhi, a small child sat crying by a temple tank. The Periya Puranam tells that the Lord saw him and bade Uma feed him. She gave him milk in a golden bowl: the milk of the wisdom of Shiva."
**Sound:** a child's cry; water; then a soft, sweet melody.

### 2. The first song (20 sec)
**Visual:** The father comes out of the water, sees milk on the child's lips, and raises a stick, angry: who fed you? The child points up at the sky, at the Lord and Uma, and begins to sing.
**Narration:** "His father asked angrily who had fed him. The child pointed to the sky and sang his first song, of the Lord with an earring in his ear. From then on he was called Sambandar, and he sang all his life."
**Sound:** the child's voice as a pure melody; bells.

### 3. To Madurai (20 sec)
**Visual:** Years pass. A young saint in a palanquin of pearl arrives at Madurai, with devotees singing. The Pandya queen Mangayarkkarasi and the minister Kulachchirai welcome him with joy. In the palace the king lies ill, surrounded by worried ascetics of the Jain faith.
**Narration:** "In Madurai, the Pandya king followed the Jain faith. But his queen and his minister were devoted to Shiva, and they sent for the young saint. Sambandar came, and comforted the queen."
**Sound:** a procession; then a hushed palace.

### 4. The fever (25 sec)
**Visual:** The king burns with fever; the Jain teachers' remedies fail. Sambandar sings softly, takes holy ash from the temple, and with his hand rubs it on the king. The heat leaves the king's face like mist.
**Narration:** "The king lay burning with a fever that no one could cure. Sambandar sang a hymn in praise of the holy ash. Then he took the ash and smeared it on the king with his own hand. The fever left him, and the king grew cool, as if he were bathing in a pond."
**Sound:** a low, burning drone; then a cool, clear chord.

### 5. The fire test (20 sec)
**Visual:** A great fire in the palace courtyard. The Jain teachers cast in a palm leaf with their writing; it curls to ash. Sambandar casts in a leaf with his hymn; it comes out fresh and green.
**Narration:** "The Jain teachers asked for a contest. Each side put a palm leaf with its sacred words into a fire. Theirs burned. Sambandar's leaf came out fresh and green."
**Sound:** roaring fire; a hush; a gasp from the crowd.

### 6. Against the flood (25 sec)
**Visual:** The river Vaigai in flood. Both sides cast their leaves into the water. The Jains' leaf is swept away downstream. Sambandar's leaf turns, and floats upstream against the current, until it stops by the bank, where the minister wades in and lifts it.
**Narration:** "Then they cast their leaves into the Vaigai in flood. The Jains' leaf was swept away. Sambandar's leaf turned, and floated upstream against the flood, until it stopped at a place called Edagam, and the minister lifted it out."
**Sound:** rushing water; one clear note rising against it.

### 7. The king is healed (15 sec)
**Visual:** The king, who had been bent with a hunched back, straightens and stands tall. He takes holy ash on his forehead and bows.
**Narration:** "The Periya Puranam says that in that moment the king's bent back was made straight. He turned to Shiva, and wore the holy ash."
**Sound:** a rising, joyful phrase.

### 8. Madurai sings (15 sec)
**Visual:** Madurai's streets fill with devotees wearing holy ash. Sambandar walks among them, singing; the crowd joins. Final image: the young saint at the temple of Meenakshi and the Lord.
**Narration:** "So the young saint won Madurai for Shiva, not with weapons, but with songs and holy ash. And Arunagiri sings of him again and again."
**Sound:** a full chorus; temple bells.
