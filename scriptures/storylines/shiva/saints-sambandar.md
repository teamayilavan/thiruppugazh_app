# The Child Saint at Madurai

`saints-sambandar` | Shiva | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: wonder, tension, triumph

## One-line story

The child saint Sambandar of Sirkazhi, nursed with Uma's milk, goes to Madurai, heals the Pandya's fever with holy ash, and wins the contests against the Jains.

## The storyline

### 1. The milk (20 sec)
A golden bowl, a tank, a crying child.
**Narration:** "Sambandar was nursed with Uma's milk."

### 2. The song (15 sec)
A boy of three sings.
**Narration:** "He sang from his earliest days."

### 3. Madurai (20 sec)
He arrives at Madurai; the queen welcomes him.
**Narration:** "He came to Madurai."

### 4. The fever (25 sec)
The king burns with fever; the saint gives him holy ash; the fever leaves.
**Narration:** "He healed the king with holy ash."

### 5. The fire (25 sec)
Two leaves in a fire; his stays green.
**Narration:** "His leaf stayed green in the fire."

### 6. The river (25 sec)
Two leaves in a flooded river; his floats upstream.
**Narration:** "It floated against the flood."

### 7. The king's turning (20 sec)
The king takes the holy ash.
**Narration:** "The king turned to Shiva."

### 8. The song (15 sec)
Sambandar sings, and the crowd joins.
**Narration:** "And Madurai sang with him."
