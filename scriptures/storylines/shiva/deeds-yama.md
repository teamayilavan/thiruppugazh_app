# The Boy Who Held the Lingam (Markandeya and Yama)

`deeds-yama` | Shiva | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: tender, then terrifying, then triumphant

## One-line story

The boy sage Markandeya, fated to die at sixteen, clings to the Shiva linga; Yama's noose falls around the linga as well, and Shiva comes out of it, kicks Yama down, and gives the boy lasting youth.

## Characters

- **Markandeya:** a boy of sixteen, devout, bright-eyed.
- **His parents:** gentle and anxious.
- **Yama:** the god of death, dark and dignified, on a buffalo.
- **Shiva:** bursting from the linga.

## The storyline

### 1. A short life (20 sec)
A boy plays at a small hermitage; his parents look on, sad. They know he is fated to die at sixteen.
**Narration:** "A boy, Markandeya, was fated to die at sixteen."

### 2. The devotion (25 sec)
He worships a lingam every day, with flowers and song.
**Narration:** "So he gave his days to Shiva."

### 3. The day arrives (20 sec)
Dusk; a dark cloud gathers; Yama's buffalo steps out of it.
**Narration:** "On the last day, Yama came."

### 4. The noose (25 sec)
The boy clings to the lingam. Yama throws his noose; it falls around both the boy and the lingam.
**Narration:** "Yama threw his noose, and it fell around the lingam as well."

### 5. The Lord comes (25 sec)
The lingam splits with light; Shiva steps out, angry.
**Narration:** "And the Lord came out of the lingam."

### 6. The kick (20 sec)
Shiva kicks Yama down.
**Narration:** "He kicked Yama down."

### 7. The gift (20 sec)
Shiva touches the boy: "Be sixteen for ever."
**Narration:** "And he gave the boy lasting youth."

### 8. The temple (10 sec)
The boy smiles at the Lord.
**Narration:** "So Markandeya never grew old."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Nine songs recall it, including 214, 491, 617 and 1063.

## Production notes

- **Yama is not evil.** He does his duty. The kick is a flash of light.
- **The boy's fear is real;** keep the noose symbolic.
