# Servants of the Lord (Songs of the Nayanmar)

`saints-nayanmar` | Shiva | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: affectionate, a gallery of devotion

## One-line story

Arunagiri's songs name the saints of the Tamil Shaiva tradition: Appar, Sundarar, Manikkavasagar and Kachchiyappar. This film is a short gallery of three of them and of the poet who wrote the Kanda Puranam.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Songs name Appar, Sundarar, Manikkavasagar and Kachchiyappar | Thiruppugazh songs 555, 862, 911, 960 (and others) |
| Shiva went as Sundarar's messenger to his wife Paravai | Periya Puranam; see `n-sundarar-messenger` |
| Shiva turned jackals into horses for Manikkavasagar | Tiruvilaiyadal Puranam; see `n-manikkavasagar` |
| Kachchiyappar is the poet of the Kanda Puranam | Research set |

**Honesty note:** songs 555, 862 and 911 use the word "Appar" for "father" (Shiva), not the saint. The film includes Appar only as a name in the gallery.
**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration.

## Characters

- **Sundarar, Manikkavasagar, Kachchiyappar, Appar** (a glimpse).
- **Shiva** (in different forms).

## The storyline

### 1. The servants (20 sec)
A line of glowing figures walks across a golden field.
**Narration:** "The Tamil country remembers the servants of the Lord."

### 2. Appar (20 sec)
An old saint, with a hoe and a broom, cleans a temple path.
**Narration:** "Appar, who swept the temple paths."

### 3. Sundarar (30 sec)
Sundarar at a door in the night; the Lord himself at Paravai's door.
**Narration:** "Sundarar, for whom the Lord went as messenger."

### 4. Manikkavasagar (30 sec)
A minister, a herd of horses, a flicker of foxes.
**Narration:** "Manikkavasagar, for whom jackals became horses."

### 5. Kachchiyappar (25 sec)
A poet writes by lamplight: the Kanda Puranam.
**Narration:** "Kachchiyappar, who sang the story of Murugan in a great Puranam."

### 6. The poet (15 sec)
Arunagiri bows to them.
**Narration:** "And the poet of the Thiruppugazh bowed to them all."

### 7. The song (15 sec)
A line of the Thiruppugazh over the scene.
**Narration:** "Their names live in his songs."

### 8. The field (10 sec)
The figures walk on.
**Narration:** "And the servants walk on."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 555, 862, 911 and 960 (7 songs in all).

## Production notes

- **A gallery.** Each saint has his own full film in the Saints set.
- **Do not claim Appar's story** beyond what the research gives; his sweeping the temple paths is widely told but not read in this research, so mark it as tradition.
