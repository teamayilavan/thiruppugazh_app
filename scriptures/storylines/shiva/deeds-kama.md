# The God of Love Burned (Kama)

`deeds-kama` | Shiva | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: tense, fiery, then compassionate

## One-line story

The gods send Kama, the god of love, to break Shiva's meditation so that he will marry Parvati and father a commander for their army; Shiva's forehead eye opens and burns Kama to ash, and Rati's grief wins him a bodiless life.

**Added for the screen:** the visuals, the narration. The film is modest.

## Characters

- **Kama:** a graceful young god with a sugarcane bow and flower arrows.
- **Rati:** his wife, tender and devoted.
- **Shiva:** in deep meditation, still as a mountain.
- **Parvati:** nearby, serving.
- **The gods:** watching anxiously.

## The storyline

### 1. The gods' need (20 sec)
The gods meet, worried: a demon threatens them and only Shiva's son can defeat him.
**Narration:** "The gods needed a commander, born of Shiva."

### 2. The meditation (25 sec)
Shiva sits in meditation on the Himalaya; Parvati serves him quietly.
**Narration:** "But Shiva was lost in meditation."

### 3. Kama is sent (20 sec)
The gods ask Kama to wake Shiva's heart.
**Narration:** "They sent Kama, the god of love."

### 4. The arrow (25 sec)
Kama, hidden in the trees, draws his flower bow; a fragrant breeze passes.
**Narration:** "He aimed his flower arrow."

### 5. The eye (25 sec)
Shiva opens his eyes; his third eye, in his forehead, flashes with fire.
**Narration:** "Shiva's third eye opened."

### 6. The ash (20 sec)
Kama is gone; only ash falls.
**Narration:** "And Kama was burned to ash."

### 7. Rati's grief (20 sec)
Rati weeps; Shiva, moved, grants Kama a life without a body.
**Narration:** "Rati wept, and Shiva granted him a life without a body."

### 8. The marriage (10 sec)
Shiva looks at Parvati, and smiles.
**Narration:** "And in time he married Parvati."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

27 songs recall Kama's burning, including 70, 77, 103 and 124.

## Production notes

- **The burning is symbolic.** Show light, not flesh.
- **Rati's grief and the Lord's compassion** are the emotional end.
