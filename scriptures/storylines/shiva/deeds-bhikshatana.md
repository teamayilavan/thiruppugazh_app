# The Wandering Beggar (Bhikshatana)

`deeds-bhikshatana` | Shiva | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: strange, playful, humbling

## One-line story

Carrying a skull-bowl, Shiva wanders naked through the Daruka forest asking alms, and the sages' wives are bewitched while their husbands' pride in their rites is broken.

**Added for the screen:** the visuals and the narration. The treatment is modest: the "naked" wanderer is shown in light, as ash and shadow, and the film makes no suggestion of a sensual scene.

## Characters

- **Shiva as Bhikshatana:** a dark, handsome, radiant wanderer with a skull-bowl, smeared in ash.
- **Vishnu as Mohini:** a graceful companion.
- **The sages of the Daruka forest:** proud and austere.
- **Their wives.**

## The storyline

### 1. The forest of the proud (25 sec)
In the Daruka forest, sages perform rites, certain of their wisdom.
**Narration:** "In the Daruka forest lived sages, proud of their rites."

### 2. The beggar (25 sec)
A radiant wanderer walks in with a skull-bowl.
**Narration:** "A beggar came, with a skull for a bowl."

### 3. The wives (20 sec)
The sages' wives come to the door and stop, spellbound.
**Narration:** "The women of the forest were drawn to him."

### 4. The sages' anger (20 sec)
The sages return, furious, and chase him with rites and curses.
**Narration:** "The sages were angry."

### 5. The failure of the rites (25 sec)
Their fires, charms and tigers all fall at his feet.
**Narration:** "But their rites could not touch him."

### 6. The bowing (25 sec)
The sages kneel in shame.
**Narration:** "And their pride was broken."

### 7. The Lord (20 sec)
The wanderer reveals himself in light.
**Narration:** "It was the Lord himself."
