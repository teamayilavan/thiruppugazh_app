# The Poet in the Cave (Nakkeerar and the Tirumurugatruppadai)

`n-nakkeerar` | Saints | running time 2:40 (8 scenes) | tone: dread, then a hymn that opens a cave

## One-line story

The poet Nakkeerar loses his concentration at Thiruparankundram and is seized by a demon and shut in a cave with many other prisoners; he sings the Tirumurugatruppadai, and Murugan opens the cave.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Nakkeerar loses his concentration at Thiruparankundram, is seized by a demon and shut in a cave with many other prisoners | Tradition (the songs; 9 songs) |
| He sings the Tirumurugatruppadai | Tradition |
| Murugan opens the cave and frees the prisoners | Tradition |
| The quarrel between Nakkeerar and Shiva over the Pandya's poem is told in the Tiruvilaiyadal Puranam padalams 52-54 | TVP 52-54 |

**Honesty note:** the cave story is **not** in the Tiruvilaiyadal Puranam padalams checked. It is tradition and the songs. The film says so in its narration.
**Added for the screen:** every visual; the narration.

## Characters

- **Nakkeerar:** a Sangam poet, proud and sharp, in white.
- **The demon:** a shadowy figure, not a monster, an echo in the cave.
- **The other prisoners:** weary captives.
- **Murugan:** a radiant young god with a spear.

## The storyline

### 1. The poet (20 sec)
Nakkeerar sits by a pool at Thiruparankundram, meditating on Shiva.
**Narration:** "The poet Nakkeerar sat at Thiruparankundram, at his prayers."

### 2. The lapse (20 sec)
A leaf falls from a tree into the water and turns into a fish-bird; he opens his eyes, his mind broken from his prayer.
**Narration:** "His mind wavered, and his concentration broke."

### 3. The seizure (25 sec)
A shadow rises behind him; he is seized and carried away.
**Narration:** "Then a demon seized him."

### 4. The cave (25 sec)
A cave blocked with stone; inside, many prisoners sit in the dark. The poet sits among them, afraid.
**Narration:** "He was shut in a cave with many other captives."

### 5. A vow (20 sec)
The poet closes his eyes; he begins a long hymn to Murugan.
**Narration:** "So he began a hymn to Murugan, line by line."

### 6. The hymn (25 sec)
Lines of the Tirumurugatruppadai drift into the cave as light: the six abodes appear in turn.
**Narration:** "He sang of the six abodes of the Lord, one by one."

### 7. The opening (20 sec)
Light strikes the cave; the stone rolls away. Murugan's spear gleams at the mouth.
**Narration:** "And Murugan opened the cave."

### 8. The prisoners go free (5 sec)
The captives and the poet walk out into the dawn.
**Narration:** "All were set free."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Nine songs recall the poet and his hymn: 92, 104, 431, 650, 681 among them.
