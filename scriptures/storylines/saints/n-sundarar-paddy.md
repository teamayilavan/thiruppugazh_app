# The Mountain of Paddy

`n-sundarar-paddy` | Saints | running time 2:35 (7 scenes) | tone: warm, comic, miraculous | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

A farmer who loves Sundarar has a mountain of paddy he cannot carry to him; at Sundarar's prayer, Shiva sends dwarf attendants who carry it overnight to Paravai's house.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Kundaiyur Kizhar, a farmer who sent paddy to Sundarar, is left short in a drought and grieves | Periya Puranam, Eyarkon Kalikkama Puranam, vv. 10-27 |

**Added for the screen:** the visual gags of the little attendants, the look of the paddy mountain, the narration.

## Characters

- **Kundaiyur Kizhar:** an old farmer, generous and humble.
- **Sundarar:** a gentle saint.
- **Paravai:** a household mistress, astonished.
- **Shiva's dwarf attendants (bhootas):** short, merry, strong.

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; warm and homely, worried in the drought, merry with the little attendants, glowing at the end.

### 1. The farmer (20 sec)
**Visual:** Kundaiyur: green paddy fields, a big farmhouse, an old farmer with a kind face. Year after year his carts roll toward Tiruvarur, loaded with paddy, lentils and sugarcane, and stop at the house of Paravai, Sundarar's wife.
**Narration:** "At Kundaiyur lived a farmer, Kundaiyur Kizhar, who loved the saint Sundarar. For many years he sent paddy, lentils and sugarcane to Sundarar's house in Tiruvarur, so that the saint's household never lacked anything."
**Sound:** bullock-cart bells; a happy village tune.

### 2. The drought (20 sec)
**Visual:** The sky turns white and hard. The fields crack. The farmer stands in an empty granary, a few grains in his palm. That night he will not eat; he lies awake, ashamed.
**Narration:** "Then the rains failed. The fields dried up, and the farmer could not gather enough paddy to send. He was so ashamed that he would not eat, and he lay awake in sorrow."
**Dialogue:** Kundaiyur Kizhar: "What shall I do? Sundarar's house will go without."
**Sound:** dry wind; a sad flute.

### 3. The mountain of paddy (25 sec)
**Visual:** In the night the Lord appears to the sleeping farmer. At dawn the farmer opens his door and staggers back: a golden mountain of paddy fills the whole village and climbs into the clouds. Villagers come out and stare.
**Narration:** "But the Lord came to him in his sleep, and said: I have given paddy to Sundarar through you. When the farmer woke at dawn, a mountain of paddy filled the whole town and rose into the sky."
**Sound:** a bright chord; astonished voices.

### 4. Too much to carry (20 sec)
**Visual:** The farmer runs to Sundarar. The saint comes to Kundaiyur and gazes up at the mountain, laughing in wonder. Then he looks at the few bullock carts. No human hands could carry it.
**Narration:** "The farmer ran to tell Sundarar. The saint came and looked up at the mountain, and praised the Lord. But no one on earth could carry so much paddy to Tiruvarur."
**Dialogue:** Sundarar: "Only the one who gave it can carry it."
**Sound:** the village tune, now full of wonder.

### 5. The prayer (20 sec)
**Visual:** Sundarar goes to the nearby temple of Thirukkolili and sings with folded hands. As he finishes, a voice comes from the sky, and the people around him look up.
**Narration:** "So Sundarar went to the nearby temple of Thirukkolili and sang a hymn, asking the Lord for people to carry it. And a voice came from the sky: our own attendants will carry it, and fill Tiruvarur."
**Sound:** his singing voice; then the deep, warm voice from above.

### 6. The carrying (25 sec)
**Visual:** Night at Kundaiyur. Hundreds of small, round, merry attendants of the Lord tumble out of the dark, laughing. They heave the paddy onto their shoulders and heads and run along the roads in a long golden stream under the moon, toward Tiruvarur.
**Narration:** "That night the Lord's attendants came, short and strong and merry. They took up the whole mountain of paddy and ran with it through the dark, all the way to Tiruvarur."
**Sound:** pattering feet; giggles; a comic, bouncing melody.

### 7. Tiruvarur in the morning (25 sec)
**Visual:** Dawn. Paravai opens her door: her house is filled to the roof with paddy. She steps out: every street of Tiruvarur is heaped with golden hills of grain. Townspeople climb them in amazement. Sundarar and the farmer meet in the street and laugh together.
**Narration:** "In the morning Paravai's house was full, and every street of Tiruvarur was heaped with paddy. The people asked where in the world such paddy had grown. It was a farmer's love, carried by the Lord's own attendants."
**Sound:** morning bells; the happy village tune, full and joyful.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Song 172 (Palani) recalls how the Lord, delighted with Sundarar, had paddy heaped like a mountain delivered to Paravai's house. Show song 172.

## Production notes

- **Keep it joyous.** The film is a comedy of kindness.
- **Pair with `n-sundarar-messenger`:** same saint and wife, different night.
