# The Mountain of Paddy

`n-sundarar-paddy` | Saints | running time 2:35 (7 scenes) | tone: warm, comic, miraculous

## One-line story

A farmer who loves Sundarar has a mountain of paddy he cannot carry to him; at Sundarar's prayer, Shiva sends dwarf attendants who carry it overnight to Paravai's house.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|

| At Sundarar's prayer Shiva sends dwarf attendants who carry it in the night to Paravai's house at Tiruvarur | same |

**Added for the screen:** the visual gags of the little attendants, the look of the paddy mountain, the narration.

## Characters

- **Kundaiyur Kizhar:** an old farmer, generous and humble.
- **Sundarar:** a gentle saint.
- **Paravai:** a household mistress, astonished.
- **Shiva's dwarf attendants (bhootas):** short, merry, strong.

## The storyline

### 1. The harvest (25 sec)
A great farm; sacks and sacks of paddy piled like a hill. The farmer looks proudly at it.
**Narration:** "A farmer had so much paddy that it stood like a mountain."

### 2. The gift he cannot give (20 sec)
He wants to send it to Sundarar, but there is no way to carry it.
**Narration:** "He wanted to give it to Sundarar. But how could he carry it?"

### 3. The prayer (20 sec)
Sundarar, hearing, prays at the temple: "Lord, help my friend."
**Narration:** "Sundarar prayed."

### 4. The attendants (25 sec)
In the night, a troop of small, laughing figures appears at the farm: Shiva's attendants.
**Narration:** "The Lord sent his little attendants."

### 5. The carrying (25 sec)
They lift the mountain of paddy sack by sack onto their shoulders, and run off, giggling.
**Narration:** "They carried the mountain of paddy in a single night."

### 6. The house in Tiruvarur (25 sec)
Paravai wakes to find her courtyard full of grain, up to the roof.
**Narration:** "In the morning, Paravai's house was full of grain."

### 7. The smile (15 sec)
Sundarar and the farmer meet, laughing.
**Narration:** "A farmer's love, carried by the Lord's own hands."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)
