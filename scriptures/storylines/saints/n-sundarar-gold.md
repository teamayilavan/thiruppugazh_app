# Gold for Paravai (Sundarar's Gold)

`n-sundarar-gold` | Saints | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: wry, tired, then relieved

## One-line story

Sundarar walks from shrine to shrine to earn gold for his wife Paravai and is worn out; in another telling, hunters who are Shiva's own hosts take away the gold he has been given.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Song 947's commentary: Sundarar went to many Shaivite shrines to earn gold for Paravai, and was exhausted by the walking | Thiruppugazh song 947 |
| The Puranam tells a different gold episode: Shiva's hosts, as hunters, take Sundarar's gold at Tirumurugan Poondi | Periya Puranam, vv. 164-172 |
| The song's version was not found in the text | research |

**Honesty note:** scenes 1 to 4 follow the song's commentary (not found in the Puranam). Scenes 5 to 8 follow the Puranam. The film says so in its narration.
**Added for the screen:** the walking montage and the comic touches, the narration.

## Characters

- **Sundarar:** a handsome saint, weary and wry.
- **Paravai:** his wife at Tiruvarur, waiting.
- **Shiva's hosts:** a band of hunters, jolly and fierce.
- **The Lord** (a voice).

## The storyline

### 1. A debt of love (20 sec)
Paravai at home in Tiruvarur, a household lamp lit; Sundarar needs gold to keep the house.
**Narration:** "Sundarar needed gold for his wife Paravai."

### 2. The road (25 sec)
He walks from one Shaiva shrine to another, singing at each and receiving a little gold.
**Narration:** "So he went from shrine to shrine."

### 3. The weariness (20 sec)
He sits by the road, feet sore, holding a small bag.
**Narration:** "He walked until he could walk no more."

### 4. The song says (15 sec)
A line of a Thiruppugazh song glows over the scene; he smiles.
**Narration:** "The poet remembered it, in one line."

### 5. The Puranam's tale (20 sec)
Another journey: at Tirumurugan Poondi, with a gift of gold from the Lord.
**Narration:** "The Puranam tells another time Sundarar had gold."

### 6. The hunters (25 sec)
A band of hunters leaps from the trees and takes the gold.
**Narration:** "Hunters came out of the forest and took it."

### 7. The cry (25 sec)
He cries out to the Lord: "Why?" The hunters laugh and vanish.
**Narration:** "They were the Lord's own hosts."

### 8. The Lord's play (15 sec)
The gold lies at the temple door; he is smiling.
**Narration:** "The Lord was only playing."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Song 947 names the walking for gold.
