# Kannappar, the Hunter Who Gave His Eyes

`n-kannappar` | Saints | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: rough, loving, then intense and tender

## One-line story

A hunter who knows no ritual worships the Shiva lingam in the forest his own way, with water carried in his mouth and meat he has tasted himself; when the lingam's eye bleeds, he gives his own eyes.

**Added for the screen:** the forest setting, the priest who comes to worship by the book (the research does not give this scene's details), the stopping of the hunter's hand at the end, and the narration. The verses read give the hunter's acts and the giving of the eyes; the Lord's words are not quoted here.

## Characters

- **Kannappar:** a young hunter, strong and simple, with bow and a bag of meat.
- **The priest** (tradition): a Brahmin who worships with ritual.
- **Shiva** (a voice and a light).

## Places

The forest hill of Kalahasti; a lingam under a tree.

## The storyline

### 1. The hill (20 sec)
A forest hill; a hunter climbs it with his bow. At the top, under a tree, a lingam. He stops: something in him is moved.
**Narration:** "A hunter found a lingam on a forest hill."

### 2. The love (25 sec)
He has no flowers or ritual. He brings water in his mouth and pours it over the lingam. He clears the old flowers with his foot, in his sandal.
**Narration:** "He had no rites to offer. He brought water in his mouth, and cleared the old flowers with his foot."

### 3. The offering (25 sec)
From his bag he takes cooked meat. He tastes it first, to be sure it is good, and offers it.
**Narration:** "He tasted the meat first, to be sure it was good enough for the Lord."

### 4. The priest (20 sec)
A priest arrives, shocked at the mess; he washes it clean and worships by the book, then leaves, grieved.
**Narration:** "The temple priest was horrified. But the hunter's love was true."

### 5. The test (25 sec)
Night. One eye of the lingam bleeds. The hunter sees it and weeps.
**Narration:** "Then the lingam's eye began to bleed."

### 6. The first eye (25 sec)
He seeks herbs, tries to staunch it, cannot. He decides to place his own eye in its place.
**Narration:** "He could not stop the bleeding. So he gave his own eye."

### 7. The second eye (15 sec)
When the other eye of the lingam bleeds, he lifts his foot to mark the place and prepares to give the second. A hand stops him.
**Narration:** "And then the second."

### 8. The Lord (10 sec)
Light fills the hill. The hunter is held and healed.
**Narration:** "He had given everything, and the Lord gave him back his sight."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 510, 664 and 1211 recall the hunter.

## Production notes

- **No gore.** The "eye" is shown as a light, the bleeding as red glow.
- **The hunter's love is the point.** Keep the priest respectful: he is the other kind of devotion.
- **Verify the last scene** (the hand that stops him) against the Puranam, which is beyond the verses checked.
