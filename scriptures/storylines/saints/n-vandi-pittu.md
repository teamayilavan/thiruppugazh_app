# Vandi's Pittu (The Blow Felt by All)

`n-vandi-pittu` | Saints | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: homely, then thrilling, then moving

## One-line story

When the Vaigai floods, every household owes a share of the embankment; old Vandi has no one, so Shiva comes as a labourer, eats her pittu as wages, sleeps on the job, and is struck with a cane by the king, and the blow is felt by every creature.

## Characters

- **Vandi:** an old woman who sells pittu, a sweet dish of rice flour, alone and poor.
- **Shiva:** a young labourer, with a basket, humble and smiling.
- **The Pandya king:** an imperious ruler with a cane.
- **The villagers.**

## The storyline

### 1. The flood (20 sec)
The Vaigai swells; water laps at the village. The king's order: every household must raise a share of the embankment.
**Narration:** "The river Vaigai was rising. Every house had to build its share of the bank."

### 2. Vandi (20 sec)
Old Vandi has no family to send. She sits with her small stall, in tears.
**Narration:** "Old Vandi had no one."

### 3. The labourer (25 sec)
A young man with a basket comes to her: "I will work for your share, for a plate of your pittu."
**Narration:** "A young labourer came. He asked only for her pittu."

### 4. The pittu (20 sec)
She makes the pittu; he eats it with delight. He picks up his basket.
**Narration:** "He ate, and went to work."

### 5. The sleep (25 sec)
At the river bank, he does nothing; he lies down in the shade and sleeps.
**Narration:** "But he did not work. He slept."

### 6. The cane (25 sec)
The king rides by and finds him asleep. He strikes the labourer with a cane.
**Narration:** "The king struck him with a cane."

### 7. The blow (20 sec)
The blow lands; and at the same moment every creature feels it: the king, the fish, the birds, the people.
**Narration:** "And every creature felt the blow."

### 8. The voice (10 sec)
A voice from heaven; Vandi is carried to the Lord.
**Narration:** "It was the Lord himself. And Vandi went to his side."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 756, 960 and 1317 recall it.

## Production notes

- **A tender film.** Vandi's loneliness is the centre.
- **The cane scene** should be stylised: no pain to the labourer or anyone; a flash of light.
- **Verify the ending** against the Tiruvilaiyadal Puranam before animation.
