# The Crocodile Gives Back the Child (Sundarar at Avinashi)

`n-sundarar-crocodile` | Saints | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: grief, hope, a miracle

## One-line story

At Avinashi, Sundarar sings a hymn, and the Lord makes a crocodile give back the child it swallowed.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| The songs say Sundarar sang at Avinashi and the Lord made a crocodile give back a child it had swallowed | Thiruppugazh song 1320 |
| Sundarar's own hymn at Thirupukkoliyur Avinashi (Tevaram 7.92, v. 4) asks the Lord to command Kala to make the crocodile on the bank give back the child | Tevaram 7.92 |
| The full telling is not in the Periya Puranam | research |

**Honesty note:** the story is alluded to in Sundarar's hymn; the fuller details (such as the parents and the time that passed) belong to later tradition and are not read in the texts here. Scenes 2 and 3 are the tradition's telling.
**Added for the screen:** the parents' grief, the pond scene, the narration.

## Characters

- **Sundarar:** a gentle saint, calm and loving.
- **The child:** a boy, shown first small, then alive.
- **The parents:** a devout couple, grieving.
- **The crocodile:** in the water, not frightening on its release.
- **Kala (Yama):** a distant presence in the hymn.

## The storyline

### 1. The town (20 sec)
Avinashi at sunrise; the temple, the tank, women carrying water.
**Narration:** "At the temple town of Avinashi, there was a tank with a crocodile."

### 2. The loss (30 sec)
Two boys play at the tank; one slips; the crocodile swallows him. The other runs home screaming.
**Narration:** "A crocodile swallowed a child. His parents mourned."

### 3. The wait (20 sec)
Seasons pass; the parents light a lamp each night.
**Narration:** "They never forgot him."

### 4. The saint arrives (25 sec)
Sundarar arrives at the temple. The parents come to him. He nods.
**Narration:** "Then Sundarar came to Avinashi."

### 5. The hymn (25 sec)
At the bank of the tank, he sings: "Command Kala to make the crocodile give back the child."
**Narration:** "He sang, and he asked the Lord to command Death himself to make the crocodile give back the boy."

### 6. The return (25 sec)
The water stirs; the crocodile rises and gently sets a child on the bank, alive.
**Narration:** "The crocodile gave him back."

### 7. The reunion (15 sec)
The parents run; the child lies in their arms.
**Narration:** "The boy was alive."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Song 1320 names the story.

## Production notes

- **The crocodile is not a villain.** Show the release without menace.
- **Do not show the swallowing;** show the boy slipping and a ripple.
- **Say that the parents' scene belongs to tradition.**
