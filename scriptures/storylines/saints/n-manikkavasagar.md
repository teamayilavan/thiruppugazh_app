# The Minister, the Guru and the Horses (Manikkavasagar at Perunthurai)

`n-manikkavasagar` | Saints | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: purposeful, then surrendered, then comic

## One-line story

A Pandya minister sent to buy horses meets Shiva as a guru under a kurundam tree and gives all the money to build a temple; the horses that arrive later are foxes turned into horses.

**Tradition note:** this story has a fuller telling in the Shiva set's `saints-kurundam`. Beats here follow that file and the Tiruvilaiyadal Puranam; the details of the foxes turning back to foxes at night are the tradition, not read verse by verse here.
**Added for the screen:** every visual; the narration.

## Characters

- **Vathavurar (Manikkavasagar):** a young minister, intelligent, in rich robes, then humble.
- **The guru:** Shiva in the guise of a teacher, calm and radiant, under the tree.
- **The Pandya king:** stern, impatient.
- **Foxes and horses.**

## The storyline

### 1. The commission (20 sec)
The Pandya king gives the minister a chest of gold: "Go to the coast and buy horses."
**Narration:** "A king sent his minister to buy horses."

### 2. The road (15 sec)
The minister's caravan travels the south.
**Narration:** "He travelled with the royal gold."

### 3. The tree (20 sec)
At Perunthurai, under a kurundam tree, a teacher sits with disciples. The minister is drawn to him.
**Narration:** "At Perunthurai, under a tree, he saw a teacher."

### 4. The teaching (20 sec)
The minister kneels; the teacher touches his head; a light passes through him. He weeps.
**Narration:** "The teacher touched him, and everything changed."

### 5. The gold (25 sec)
He gives the gold to build a temple under the tree. Bricks rise, a shrine takes shape.
**Narration:** "He gave all the gold, and built a temple."

### 6. The king's anger (20 sec)
A messenger from the king demands the horses. The minister says: "They will come."
**Narration:** "The king demanded the horses."

### 7. The horses arrive (25 sec)
A herd of splendid horses enters Madurai with the dawn. The king is delighted.
**Narration:** "And the horses came."

### 8. Foxes (15 sec)
That night, they leave the stable: foxes running into the dark.
**Narration:** "But they were foxes, turned into horses for a day."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Song 512 names the story.

## Production notes

- **Comedy, but with dignity.** The minister is not a fool: he was given a gift.
- **Pair with the Shiva set's `saints-kurundam`,** which tells the same saint's life.
