# Two Friends Go to Kailasa (Sundarar and Cheraman Perumal)

`n-cheraman-kailasa` | Saints | running time 2:40 (7 scenes) | tone: friendship, wonder, ascent

## One-line story

The Tamil saint Sundarar and the Chera king Cheraman Perumal are close friends; when Sundarar is called to Mount Kailasa on a great elephant, the king rides his horse after him.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Cheraman Perumal (Kazharitrarivar) is a close friend of Sundarar | Periya Puranam, Kazharitrarivar Puranam, vv. 112-118, 173-175 |
| Sundarar goes up to Tirukkailai (Kailasa) on a great elephant; the Puranam then turns to Cheraman's act of riding his horse | PP v. 174 |
| Cheraman's own poem, the Tirukkailaya Gnana Ula (Tirumurai 11.8), is the poem the song calls the "Adi Ula" | Tirumurai 11.8 |
| Song 170's commentary says the king rode a horse to Kailasa in Sundarar's company | Thiruppugazh song 170 |

**Tradition note:** the Ula itself does not name Sundarar. The film follows the Puranam for the ascent and the song's commentary for the king's company.
**Added for the screen:** the visuals of the ascent, the music, the narration.

## Characters

- **Sundarar:** a handsome young saint, gentle, in a white garment, radiant.
- **Cheraman Perumal:** a king, strong, loyal, with a crown.
- **The white elephant:** a heavenly mount.
- **The king's horse.**
- **Shiva's attendants** (a bright escort).

## The storyline

### 1. Two friends (25 sec)
In the Chera court the king embraces the saint; they walk in a garden together, laughing.
**Narration:** "The Chera king was the friend of Sundarar, and the two were never far apart."

### 2. A call (25 sec)
A sudden light in the sky. Shiva's attendants descend with a great white elephant: Sundarar is called to Kailasa.
**Narration:** "Then a white elephant came down from heaven for Sundarar."

### 3. The saint rises (20 sec)
Sundarar mounts the elephant and rises into the clouds. The king watches.
**Narration:** "He rode it up to Kailasa."

### 4. The king decides (25 sec)
The king mounts his horse: he will not be left. He urges it upward.
**Narration:** "The king would not stay behind."

### 5. The ride (25 sec)
The horse gallops across clouds, the elephant ahead, the king reaching.
**Narration:** "He rode his horse into the sky, behind his friend."

### 6. Kailasa (25 sec)
Snow peaks, a golden gate, the Lord. The two enter together.
**Narration:** "And both came to the Lord's mountain."

### 7. The poem (15 sec)
The king, kneeling, offers a poem: the Ula of Kailasa.
**Narration:** "The king sang of what he saw."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Song 170 names the king and his poem.

## Production notes

- **Dignity.** Two friends, not a chase. Let the sky do the work.
- **Check scene 4:** the Puranam names the horse ride; the exact route in the song's commentary should be read before animation.
