# The Elephant in the Millet Field (Vinayaka Helps His Brother)

`g-valli-elephant` | Ganesha | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: comic, quick, affectionate

## One-line story

When Valli will not listen to Murugan in disguise as an old ascetic, he thinks of his elder brother Vinayaka, who comes roaring as a wild elephant; Valli runs back to the old man, and Murugan thanks his brother.

**Added for the screen:** the visuals, the narration.

## Characters

- **Vinayaka:** shown as a great wild elephant, comic and gentle at heart.
- **Murugan** as the old ascetic.
- **Valli:** quick and brave.

## The storyline

### 1. The old man's trouble (20 sec)
In the millet field, the old ascetic looks at Valli, who has turned away in anger.
**Narration:** "Valli would not listen to the old man."

### 2. A thought (20 sec)
The old man closes his eyes and thinks of his elder brother.
**Narration:** "So he thought of his brother, Vinayaka."

### 3. The roar (25 sec)
From the hills comes a roar. A great elephant rushes through the millet, trumpeting.
**Narration:** "And a wild elephant came, roaring."

### 4. The flight (25 sec)
Valli screams, runs, and clings to the old man's arm: "Save me!"
**Narration:** "Valli ran to the old man, and held on to him."

### 5. The thanks (25 sec)
The old man smiles. He gives a small bow to the elephant, who winks and trots away.
**Narration:** "The old man thanked his brother, who went away."

### 6. The true form (25 sec)
The old man is gone, and Murugan stands in his true form.
**Narration:** "And then he showed her who he was."

### 7. A brother's gift (10 sec)
Far off, the elephant looks back, amused.
**Narration:** "A brother's gift, for a brother's wedding."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Songs 24, 146, 446 and 715 recall the elephant.

## Production notes
