# After the Contests (the Jains and the King's Turning)

`sam-jains` | Sambandar | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: grave, sombre, a turning

## One-line story

After the Jains lose the debates and the tests, the king takes the holy ash and turns to Shiva; the songs also repeat a tradition that the Jains were impaled, which this film treats with restraint.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| After the contests the Jains are defeated | Periya Puranam, Sambandar Puranam, vv. 853-856 (856 checked) |
| The king takes the holy ash | vv. 857-858 |
| **Songs only:** the Jains were impaled on stakes, as the tradition the Thiruppugazh repeats | Thiruppugazh songs (21 songs beyond the 20 in the Shiva set `saints-sambandar`) |

**Honesty note:** the research keeps the impalement as the tradition the songs repeat. The Periya Puranam verses read here (853-858) carry the defeat and the king taking the ash.
**Added for the screen:** the framing, the visuals, the narration.

## The storyline

### 1. The court after the tests (20 sec)
The court of Madurai, hushed. The Jain ascetics stand, their faces lowered.
**Narration:** "The tests were over, and the Jains had lost."

### 2. The king looks at his hands (20 sec)
The king looks at the ash on his own chest, and at the saint.
**Narration:** "The king looked at the saint, and at the ash."

### 3. The ash (25 sec)
He takes holy ash from Sambandar's hand and puts it on his forehead.
**Narration:** "He took the holy ash."

### 4. The queen (20 sec)
The queen and the minister smile with relief.
**Narration:** "The queen was glad."

### 5. A hard tradition (25 sec)
A long, slow shot of the empty court. A single narration line: "The songs also say that the Jains were put to death. It is a hard tradition; and the poet repeats it." The camera does not show it.
**Narration:** "The songs say the Jains were put to death. We do not show it."

### 6. The saint's way (25 sec)
Sambandar walks out of the court, holding the ash.
**Narration:** "But the saint's way was song and ash."

### 7. The city (15 sec)
Dawn over Madurai.
**Narration:** "And Madurai turned to Shiva."
