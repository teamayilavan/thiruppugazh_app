# The Golden Bowl (Uma Feeds the Child)

`sam-milk` | Sambandar | running time 2:35 (7 scenes) | tone: tender, hushed, glowing

## One-line story

A small child, left at the temple tank by his father, cries for him; Shiva and Uma appear, and the Goddess feeds him the milk of wisdom from a golden bowl.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| The child is left on the bank of the temple tank at Sirkazhi while his father bathes, and weeps | Periya Puranam, Sambandar Puranam, vv. 59-62 |
| Shiva and Uma appear; Uma feeds him the milk of Shiva-wisdom from a golden bowl | vv. 64-69 (v. 68 checked) |
| The hymns of the Tevaram are said to have flowed from that milk | Thiruppugazh songs (10 songs); Tevaram 1.1.1 is sung right after |

**Added for the screen:** the colour and light of the bowl, the father's anxiety, the narration.

## Characters

- **The child:** a toddler on the steps of the tank, crying.
- **The father, Sivapadahridayar:** in the water, with his back to the child, absorbed in his bath.
- **Shiva and Uma:** serene on a bull, in soft light.

## Places

The temple tank at Sirkazhi, its stone steps and pillars.

## The storyline

### 1. The tank at morning (20 sec)
Mist over a stone tank. Priests bathing. A father steps into the water and begins his rites.
**Narration:** "At Sirkazhi, a father went down into the temple tank, and left his small son on the steps."

### 2. The cry (25 sec)
The child looks at the water and cannot see his father. He weeps. The sound rings against the stone.
**Narration:** "The child could not see him, and he cried out."

### 3. The light (20 sec)
A soft light touches the child. Above the temple, Shiva and Uma look down.
**Narration:** "And someone heard."

### 4. The Goddess (25 sec)
Uma comes down to the child, kneels, and takes a golden bowl in her hands.
**Narration:** "Uma came to him with a golden bowl."

### 5. The milk (25 sec)
She lifts the bowl and feeds him; the child's tears stop, and his eyes glow.
**Narration:** "She fed him the milk of wisdom."

### 6. The father returns (25 sec)
The father climbs the steps and sees milk on the child's lips. "Who gave you this?" The child looks up toward the sky.
**Narration:** "When his father came back, there was milk on the child's lips."

### 7. A first song (15 sec)
The child points at the sky and a song begins to rise from him.
**Narration:** "And a song was already waiting in him."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Ten songs say the Tevaram flowed from this milk. This film leads straight into `sam-todudaiya`.

## Production notes

- **Do not show the child alone for long.** Keep the father close to the frame edge.
- **Uma is warm and unhurried.** The milk is light, not liquid.
