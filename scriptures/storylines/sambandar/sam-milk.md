# The Golden Bowl (Uma Feeds the Child)

`sam-milk` | Sambandar | running time 2:35 (7 scenes) | tone: tender, hushed, glowing | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

A small child, left at the temple tank by his father, cries for him; Shiva and Uma appear, and the Goddess feeds him the milk of wisdom from a golden bowl.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| The child is left on the bank of the temple tank at Sirkazhi while his father bathes, and weeps | Periya Puranam, Sambandar Puranam, vv. 59-62 |
| Shiva and Uma appear; Uma feeds him the milk of Shiva-wisdom from a golden bowl | vv. 64-69 (v. 68 checked) |
| The hymns of the Tevaram are said to have flowed from that milk | Thiruppugazh songs (10 songs); Tevaram 1.1.1 is sung right after |

**Added for the screen:** the colour and light of the bowl, the father's anxiety, the narration.

## Characters

- **The child:** a toddler on the steps of the tank, crying.
- **The father, Sivapadahridayar:** in the water, with his back to the child, absorbed in his bath.
- **Shiva and Uma:** serene on a bull, in soft light.

## Places

The temple tank at Sirkazhi, its stone steps and pillars.

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; tender and hushed, with a glow of wonder.

### 1. Three years old (20 sec)
**Visual:** Morning in Sirkazhi. A little boy of three, with tinkling anklets, follows his father, Sivapadahridayar, out of the house toward the great temple tank. The father turns and gently waves him back; the boy stamps his small feet and will not go.
**Narration:** "When Sambandar was three years old, his father set out one morning to bathe in the temple tank. The little boy followed him, crying. His father tried to send him home, but the child would not go."
**Dialogue:** Sivapadahridayar: "Well then, if that is how it must be, come."
**Sound:** small anklet bells; morning birds.

### 2. On the bank (20 sec)
**Visual:** The wide temple tank, the Thoni shrine rising beyond it. The father sets the boy on the stone steps, bows toward the shrine, and steps down into the water.
**Narration:** "At the great tank beside the temple, he sat the child on the bank and told him to wait. Then he bowed to the shrine and went down into the water to say his prayers."
**Sound:** water lapping; a soft drone.

### 3. The father goes under (20 sec)
**Visual:** The father dips beneath the surface for a long prayer. The water closes over him. Ripples settle. The boy looks; there is no one there.
**Narration:** "He went under the water for a long prayer, and did not come up. The little boy looked, and looked, and could not see his father anywhere."
**Sound:** the water goes quiet; one heartbeat-like drum.

### 4. The cry (25 sec)
**Visual:** The child rubs his eyes with his fists; his lips tremble; tears fall. He turns, not to the town, but to the top of the shrine, and calls out. The father's hand is visible at the edge of the frame, in the water, close by.
**Narration:** "He began to cry. He rubbed his eyes with his little hands. And then he looked up at the top of the temple, and called out."
**Dialogue:** Sambandar: "Amma! Appa!" (Mother! Father!)
**Sound:** the cry echoes softly; strings rise.

### 5. They come (25 sec)
**Visual:** Light gathers above the shrine. Shiva and Uma appear on the white bull, the crescent moon in Shiva's hair, and come down to the edge of the tank. Shiva looks at the crying child, then at Uma, and nods.
**Narration:** "The Lord of that temple heard him. Shiva came down with Uma, riding the bull, to the side of the tank. He looked at the weeping child with love, and asked Uma to feed him."
**Sound:** a deep, warm chord; bells.

### 6. The golden bowl (25 sec)
**Visual:** Uma kneels, unhurried. She holds a golden bowl; it fills with a soft white light. She wipes the child's tears with her fingers and puts the bowl into his hands. He drinks; the light spreads through him.
**Narration:** "Uma filled a golden bowl with her own milk, mixed with the wisdom of Shiva. She wiped his tears and placed the bowl in his hands. The child drank, and stopped crying. He was no longer alone, and he would never be afraid again."
**Sound:** a single sustained note, very soft.

### 7. A new name (20 sec)
**Visual:** The divine pair fade into the light over the shrine. The boy sits very still, a drop of milk on his lips, his eyes wide and clear. The father begins to rise from the water.
**Narration:** "From that moment, the Puranam says, he knew all that the wise know. So he was called Thirugnana Sambandar, the child joined to holy wisdom. The Thiruppugazh says his hymns flowed from that milk."
**Sound:** the music settles into a quiet, glowing chord.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

In about ten songs Arunagiri recalls the child who drank Uma's milk of wisdom and then sang the hymns. Songs to show: 426 (Thiruvannamalai), 737 (Thiruvathigai), 822 (Thiruvarur), 837 (Thirukkudavayil), 1287 (general songs).

## Production notes

- **Do not show the child alone for long.** Keep the father close to the frame edge.
- **Uma is warm and unhurried.** The milk is light, not liquid.
