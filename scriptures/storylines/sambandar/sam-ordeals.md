# The Leaf in the Fire and the Leaf Against the River

`sam-ordeals` | Sambandar | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: suspense, then wonder

## One-line story

To settle which faith is true, two palm-leaf scriptures are put in a fire and then in a flooded river: Sambandar's leaf stays green in the fire and swims upstream against the flood.

## Characters

- **Sambandar:** young and calm.
- **The king and queen of Madurai.**
- **The Jain ascetics.**
- **The minister Kulachchirai.**

## Places

Madurai; the court; the river Vaigai in flood; Tiruvedagam on the bank.

## The storyline

### 1. The challenge (20 sec)
In the court the Jains propose a test: let each side's scripture go into a fire.
**Narration:** "The Jains proposed a test: let the scriptures be put into fire."

### 2. The fire (25 sec)
Two palm-leaf bundles are placed in a blazing fire. The Jains' leaf curls and blackens.
**Narration:** "Their leaf burned."

### 3. The green leaf (25 sec)
Sambandar's leaf lies in the flames, green and whole. The crowd gasps.
**Narration:** "His leaf stayed green in the fire."

### 4. The river test (20 sec)
They go to the Vaigai, in flood, brown and fast. Both leaves are held over the water.
**Narration:** "Then they went to the river, in flood."

### 5. The Jains' leaf (20 sec)
The Jains' leaf drops, caught by the current, and is swept away.
**Narration:** "Theirs was carried away."

### 6. The leaf against the flood (30 sec)
Sambandar's leaf touches the water, then turns and moves upstream against the flood, steady, glowing.
**Narration:** "His leaf swam against the river."

### 7. The king (15 sec)
On the bank, the king watches; his bent back slowly straightens.
**Narration:** "And as it swam, the king's back became straight."

### 8. The halting place (10 sec)
The leaf stops at the bank of Tiruvedagam, the crowd cheering.
**Narration:** "It stopped at Tiruvedagam."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Ten songs refer to the palm-leaf stories: the leaf in the fire and the leaf against the river.

## Production notes

- **A test, not a battle.** Keep the Jains' side respectful: they lose a test, they are not mocked.
- **Link with `sam-jains`,** which is about what followed.
