# The Dance at Thiruvalangadu

`sam-thiruvalangadu` | Sambandar | running time 2:35 (7 scenes) | tone: mystic, grand, a vision

## One-line story

The songs say Murugan came as Sambandar to Thiruvalangadu, where Shiva danced for the gods; Sambandar sang a hymn there, and the temple's tradition holds the dance.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Song 674 says Murugan came as Sambandar to Thiruvalangadu, where Shiva danced for the celestials | Thiruppugazh song 674 |
| Sambandar's own hymn at Thiruvalangadu exists: Tevaram 1.45, twelve verses; verse 1 names Alangadu and the story of Neeli at Pazhaiyanur | Tevaram 1.45 |
| The hymn does **not** describe the dance | Tevaram 1.45 |
| The dance (Shiva and Kali) is the temple's tradition | `devi/d-kali-dance` |

**Tradition note:** the dance is shown as the temple's tradition and the song's image, not as something the hymn describes. The details of the contest between Shiva and Kali are not in any text read here.
**Added for the screen:** every visual; the narration.

## Characters

- **Sambandar:** a young saint, singing.
- **Shiva:** dancing, with fire.
- **The gods:** watching from the sky.
- **Murugan** (a golden figure in the first and last shots).

## The storyline

### 1. The forest of banyans (25 sec)
A wild forest of banyan trees at Alangadu, mist and silence. A faint golden figure watches.
**Narration:** "At Thiruvalangadu, a forest of banyans, the songs say Murugan came as Sambandar."

### 2. The saint (20 sec)
Sambandar walks in with his devotees.
**Narration:** "He came to sing."

### 3. The hymn (25 sec)
He sings a hymn of twelve verses; the first names Alangadu and a woman called Neeli.
**Narration:** "His hymn named the forest, and a woman of Pazhaiyanur, Neeli."

### 4. A vision (25 sec)
The air changes: stars come out; the gods gather in the sky.
**Narration:** "The people of this place remember a dance."

### 5. The dance (30 sec)
Shiva, a tall figure, dances in fire and shadow; the gods watch in awe.
**Narration:** "The Lord danced there, for the gods."

### 6. The echo (20 sec)
The vision fades; the forest is quiet again.
**Narration:** "The hymn does not tell it. The temple does."

### 7. The song (10 sec)
A single line of the Thiruppugazh over the forest.
**Narration:** "And the poet remembered it in a line."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Song 674 names the place and the visit.

## Production notes

- **Do not claim the hymn describes the dance.** The narration says the temple does.
- **Pair with the Devi film** that tells the dance contest in full.
