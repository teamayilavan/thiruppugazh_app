# A Child Is Born at Sirkazhi

`sam-birth` | Sambandar | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: calm, devotional, quietly joyful

## One-line story

In the temple town with twelve names, to a pious Brahmin couple, a child is born who will sing the Tamil Veda.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Sambandar is born at Sirkazhi, the town with twelve names (Pugali, Kazhumalam, Kochchai and others) | Periya Puranam, Sambandar Puranam, vv. 1-26 |
| In the Kavuniya Brahmin line, to Sivapadahridayar and Bhagavathiyar | vv. 1-26 |
| The songs name the town and the lineage | Thiruppugazh songs (9 songs) |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals of the town, the household scenes, the expressions; the text gives names and place, not the details of the household.

## Characters

- **Sivapadahridayar:** the father, a devout Brahmin in white, gentle.
- **Bhagavathiyar:** the mother, kind and radiant.
- **The child.**
- **Townspeople.**

## Places

Sirkazhi: a river-side town of temples; its tank; a home with a courtyard.

## The storyline

### 1. The town with twelve names (25 sec)
Aerial shot over a river-side town of gopurams. As the camera passes over roofs, twelve names appear on the screen: Pugali, Kazhumalam, Kochchai.
**Narration:** "Sirkazhi is a town of temples, and it has twelve names."

### 2. A town of learning (20 sec)
Brahmins chanting at dawn; students on the steps of the tank; temple bells.
**Narration:** "It was a town of chanting, and of learning."

### 3. The family (25 sec)
A courtyard home. Sivapadahridayar bows at the household shrine; Bhagavathiyar lights the lamp.
**Narration:** "In one house lived Sivapadahridayar and Bhagavathiyar, devoted to Shiva."

### 4. A birth (25 sec)
Night, a lamp, a new cry. The mother holds her son; the father bows.
**Narration:** "And to them a child was born."

### 5. The golden watcher (25 sec)
In the corner of the room, a faint golden figure with a spear stands watching.
**Narration:** "The songs say that Murugan himself had come to be this child."

### 6. The town learns (20 sec)
Dawn: neighbours and priests gather at the door with flowers.
**Narration:** "The whole town came to see him."

### 7. The cradle (10 sec)
The child's eyes open; a smile.
**Narration:** "A saint had been born at Sirkazhi."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Nine songs name the town and the lineage; for example, songs naming Sirkazhi among Murugan's temples.
