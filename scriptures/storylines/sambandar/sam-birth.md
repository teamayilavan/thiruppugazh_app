# A Child Is Born at Sirkazhi

`sam-birth` | Sambandar | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: calm, devotional, quietly joyful | **script level (second pass)**

## One-line story

In the temple town with twelve names, to a pious Brahmin couple, a child is born who will sing the Tamil Veda.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Sambandar is born at Sirkazhi, the town with twelve names (Pugali, Kazhumalam, Kochchai and others) | Periya Puranam, Sambandar Puranam, vv. 1-26 |
| In the Kavuniya Brahmin line, to Sivapadahridayar and Bhagavathiyar | vv. 1-26 |
| The songs name the town and the lineage | Thiruppugazh songs (9 songs) |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals of the town, the household scenes, the expressions; the text gives names and place, not the details of the household.

## Characters

- **Sivapadahridayar:** the father, a devout Brahmin in white, gentle.
- **Bhagavathiyar:** the mother, kind and radiant.
- **The child.**
- **Townspeople.**

## Places

Sirkazhi: a river-side town of temples; its tank; a home with a courtyard.

## The script

Narration is one calm, warm voice, about two words a second; hushed and joyful, like a lullaby that becomes a celebration.

### 1. The town of many names (25 sec)
**Visual:** Dawn over a temple town by the sea: paddy fields, flower gardens, tanks full of water, tall white houses with flags. In the middle rises the temple, and on a hill-like shrine within it, the Lord of the Boat (Thoniappar). Names appear one by one in English letters and fade: Brahmapuram, Venupuram, Pugali, Sirkazhi, Kazhumalam, Kochchaivayam.
**Narration:** "On the coast of the Chola country there was an old temple town. It had twelve names. People called it Pugali, Kazhumalam, Kochchai, and Sirkazhi. Its streets rang with the chanting of the Vedas, and children played at being priests."
**Sound:** soft waves, temple bells far off, children's voices.

### 2. A good household (20 sec)
**Visual:** A modest Brahmin house. Sivapadahridayar, a gentle scholar with holy ash on his brow, worships. His wife Bhagavathiyar lights a lamp at the threshold.
**Narration:** "In that town lived Sivapadahridayar, of the Kavuniya family, and his wife Bhagavathiyar. Both came from families that loved Shiva and wore the holy ash. Their home was simple, and their days were full of prayer, study and kindness to every devotee who came to their door."
**Sound:** a single lamp-flame flicker; a low drone.

### 3. A worried father (25 sec)
**Visual:** Sivapadahridayar walks through a town where some shrines stand empty and few wear the ash. He looks troubled. He returns home, sits before a small Shiva image and prays.
**Narration:** "But the old ways of worship were fading in the land, and Sivapadahridayar grieved to see it. So he prayed for a son, a son who would bring the people back to Shiva's feet."
**Dialogue:** Sivapadahridayar: "Lord, give us a child who will make your name shine again."
**Sound:** the music thins, then a warm chord as he prays.

### 4. The promise (20 sec)
**Visual:** The couple worship together at the Thoni shrine. Months pass in quick images: the seasons change on the temple tank; the family performs the rites for the coming child.
**Narration:** "Together they worshipped at the shrine of the Lord of the Boat. In time Bhagavathiyar carried a child, and through all the months the family kept the rites with joy."
**Sound:** a gentle rising melody.

### 5. The birth (25 sec)
**Visual:** Night. A star of the Ardra constellation glows over the town. A soft light fills the house. We see only the mother's smiling face and a tiny hand. Outside, flowers open in the dark gardens.
**Narration:** "Then, on the day of the star Thiruvathirai, the child was born. The Puranam says the whole town felt a sudden joy, before anyone even knew why. A cool breeze blew, and the gardens burst into flower."
**Sound:** a hush, then a clear temple bell and a baby's first cry.

### 6. The town rejoices (20 sec)
**Visual:** Neighbours step out of their houses, smiling without knowing why. Then the news runs from door to door. Petals fall gently from the sky; priests begin a fire ritual.
**Narration:** "Then they heard the news: a son is born in the Kavuniya house. The Puranam says the gods rained flowers, and the priests of the town lit the sacred fires."
**Sound:** conch shells, drums, happy voices.

### 7. A cradle by the lamp (15 sec)
**Visual:** Quiet again. The child sleeps in a cradle by the lamp. The temple tower stands outside the window under the stars.
**Narration:** "This child would be called Thirugnana Sambandar. And the Thiruppugazh says it was Murugan himself who was born that night in Sirkazhi."
**Sound:** a soft lullaby on the flute.

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Arunagiri names Sambandar's town and family again and again, and says that Murugan himself was born there. Songs to show: 134 (Palani), 261 (Thiruthani), 758 (Thiruvarathurai), 764 and 774 (Sirkazhi), 837 (Thirukkudavayil).

## Production notes

- **Keep it quiet.** This is an opening film, a hush before the miracles.
- **Watch the "twelve names" graphic,** which should be spelled in English letters only.
