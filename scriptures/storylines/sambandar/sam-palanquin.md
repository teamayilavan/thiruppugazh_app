# The Pearl Palanquin (Thiruvarathurai)

`sam-palanquin` | Sambandar | running time 2:30 (7 scenes) | tone: gentle, glowing, a gift

## One-line story

At Thiruvarathurai, tired from walking, the child saint is given by Shiva a pearl palanquin, a pearl umbrella and the insignia of a king, and the local Brahmins carry them to him.

**Added for the screen:** the setting of the road and the dusk, the reaction of the child, the narration. The verses give the gift and the carrying; they are not read here for the Brahmins' dialogue.

## Characters

- **Sambandar:** a small child, tired from walking.
- **His father,** carrying him on his shoulders.
- **Shiva** (a voice and a light).
- **The temple Brahmins:** respectful, carrying the gifts.

## The storyline

### 1. The long road (20 sec)
A father carries his small son on his shoulders along a dusty road under the sun, toward Thiruvarathurai.
**Narration:** "The child saint was travelling to the temple at Thiruvarathurai."

### 2. The tired child (20 sec)
The boy looks tired. His father walks slowly.
**Narration:** "He was small, and the road was long."

### 3. A dream (25 sec)
That night the temple's Brahmins dream: the Lord tells them to bring a palanquin of pearls, an umbrella, and the signs of a king, to the child.
**Narration:** "That night the Lord spoke to the temple's keepers in a dream."

### 4. The procession (25 sec)
At dawn, Brahmins carry a pearl palanquin, a pearl umbrella and a canopy out of the temple, toward the road.
**Narration:** "They carried a palanquin of pearls to the road."

### 5. The meeting (20 sec)
They meet the child; he looks in wonder; they bow.
**Narration:** "They found the child on the road."

### 6. The ride (30 sec)
He is lifted into the pearl palanquin, and the umbrella and canopy are held over him. The procession moves into the town.
**Narration:** "They carried him, like a king, into Thiruvarathurai."

### 7. The glow (10 sec)
The palanquin glows white in the sun.
**Narration:** "The gift was from the Lord."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Song 758 names the gift.
