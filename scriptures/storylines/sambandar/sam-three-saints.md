# Three Saints and the Doors of Vedaranyam

`sam-three-saints` | Sambandar | running time 2:45 (8 scenes) | tone: friendly, a little playful, wondrous

## One-line story

At Vedaranyam, the temple doors have been shut for ages; Appar sings them open, and then the child Sambandar sings them shut again.

## What the story rests on

| Beat | Source |
|---|---|
| Sambandar meets Appar at Sirkazhi and Tiruvarur and travels with him | Periya Puranam, Sambandar Puranam, vv. 274, 576-592 |
| At Vedaranyam (Thirumaraikkadu), Appar's hymn opens the temple doors and Sambandar's closes them | vv. 582, 587 checked; 597-609 (Maraikkadu) |
| The songs only name the places or the three saints together; they do not narrate the doors | Thiruppugazh songs (3 songs) |

**Added for the screen:** the visuals of the doors, the narration. Sundarar, the third of the three great saints, lived later than Appar and Sambandar, so he does not appear in the film; he is named only on the end card, as the songs name the three together.

## Characters

- **Appar:** an old saint, thin, with a staff, deeply gentle.
- **Sambandar:** a young saint, quick and merry.
- **The temple priests.**
- **Villagers.**

## Places

Sirkazhi; Tiruvarur; Vedaranyam, with its great shut doors.

## The storyline

### 1. A meeting (20 sec)
Sambandar, carried in a palanquin, meets the old saint Appar on the road. He steps down and bows.
**Narration:** "The young saint met the old one, and they became friends."

### 2. Two pilgrims (20 sec)
They travel together town to town, singing, one old voice and one young.
**Narration:** "They travelled together, and sang in every temple."

### 3. The doors (25 sec)
At Vedaranyam, the temple doors are shut; the villagers say they have been closed for ages, and the Lord is worshipped by a side door.
**Narration:** "At Vedaranyam, the great doors had been shut for ages."

### 4. Appar sings (25 sec)
Appar begins a hymn; the doors tremble, and slowly swing open.
**Narration:** "Appar sang a hymn, and the doors swung open."

### 5. The wonder (20 sec)
Crowds pour in. Villagers weep.
**Narration:** "The crowds went in, and wept."

### 6. A request (20 sec)
Appar asks Sambandar to sing them shut again, so that the doors can be opened by the people in the usual way.
**Narration:** "Then Appar asked the child to sing, so the doors might be closed."

### 7. Sambandar sings (20 sec)
Sambandar sings a hymn, and the doors gently close.
**Narration:** "The child sang, and they closed."

### 8. Two friends (15 sec)
They walk on together, sharing a laugh, down a long road under the evening sky.
**Narration:** "And so the saints walked on, each with his own gift."

## The Thiruppugazh connection (end card)

Three songs name the places or the three saints (Appar, Sundarar and Sambandar) together. Sundarar's own story is told in the Saints set.

## Production notes

- **Check the reason for the closing.** The research does not say why Sambandar closed the doors; the film's scene 6 is an addition and should be checked against the Periya Puranam verses 582-592 before release.
- **No rivalry.** The story's spirit is two friends, and two kinds of grace.
